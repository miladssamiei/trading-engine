# Local research inputs and published records

Raw market data is not distributed. Acquire it from an authorized source and
place it at the study's documented input path. See [data conventions](../docs/DATA.md).

The public `results/` tree contains selected original SPEC, GATE and REPORT text
documents, not the underlying market tapes, account exports or trade databases.
The [study index](../docs/STUDIES.md) lists the documents actually included.
Reports may reference local files that are intentionally absent from this repository.

Frozen documents are historical observations. Their spread assumptions, dates,
tick models and verdicts must be read together; they are not current broker terms.
