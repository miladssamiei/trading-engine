ENGINE A PORTFOLIO MONTE CARLO - PREDECLARED SPEC
Date: 2026-09-26. Written before prop_firm_monte_carlo.py exists or runs.
This is a stress diagnostic on an exposed, in-sample CFD-proxy ledger.

INPUT: data/results/futures_prop_validation/trades.csv. Filter engine exactly
A_KC and asset exactly in GC, BTC, ETH. Preserve all qualifying rows. Net
is the ledger's round-turn-cost-inclusive price-unit PnL. Initial_Risk is
abs(entry - initial_stop) in the same price units. Require finite positive
risk and finite net. R = net / Initial_Risk exactly, without clipping or
per-asset renormalization. Pool one R observation per trade; source mix is
therefore frequency weighted by historical counts.

FREQUENCY: Calendar span is 2020-01-01 through 2026-09-23 inclusive, the
union of the source tapes' full 2020-2026 window; days = inclusive date
difference. Average portfolio trades/day = qualifying trade count / days.
Calendar-time conversion is trade-count / that historical average. It is a
frequency projection, not a simulation of actual trade arrival times,
overlap, weekends, holidays or changing opportunity rates.

SIMULATION: Base closed equity 100000 dollars, initial closed peak 100000.
Phase-1 pass target 108000. Trailing closed-equity floor is max historical
closed peak minus 10000 dollars, updated after every completed trade. At
each step draw an R independently with replacement from the unified pool.
For each risk fraction f in {0.0025,0.0050,0.0075,0.0100}, trade PnL dollars
= drawn R * (pre-trade equity * f); new closed equity = prior equity + PnL;
new peak = max(prior peak, new equity). PASS if equity >= 108000; otherwise
RUIN if peak - equity >= 10000. The first reached boundary terminates the
path. Run 10000 independent paths per fraction with separate reproducible
PCG64 seeds 20260926, 20260927, 20260928, 20260929 respectively.

COMPUTATIONAL STOP: Cap each path at 50000 drawn trades to prevent an
unbounded simulation. Capped paths are CENSORED, never silently counted as
pass or ruin. Report censored fraction, and verify path counts sum to 10000
at every risk. No tuning of the cap or seeds after observing outcomes.

OUTPUT: For each risk, report pass/ruin/censored fraction, median and 95th
percentile number of trades among PASS paths only (linear empirical quantile),
and those two counts divided by the historical average trades/day. If no
paths pass, time quantiles are N/A. Save a path-level CSV and machine-readable
metadata plus REPORT_MONTE_CARLO.txt. Input and script hashes are pinned.

HONESTY: "Time to funded" here means only simulated Phase-1 target time;
there is no second phase, payout, account rule, intratrade equity or actual
funding event. The previous A_KC M1 per-position prop liquidation is already
in the ledger. This Monte Carlo adds only a closed-equity 10000-dollar
trailing check, not the firms' live unrealized-equity rule. Trade R values
come from bid-only CFD proxies with assumed futures cost floors and unequal
asset contract units. IID replacement breaks streaks, regimes, concurrent
positions and time variation; probabilities are conditional on the observed
R pool and must not be called prospective pass chances. No optimization.
