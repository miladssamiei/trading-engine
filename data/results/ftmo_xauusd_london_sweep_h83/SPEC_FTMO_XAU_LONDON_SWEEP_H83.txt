3. FIXED ENTRY STATE MACHINE
For each London weekday require four real M15 bars with starts 08:00,08:15,08:30,08:45 London. Opening range high H=max(BID highs), low L=min(BID lows), width W=H-L; require W>0.82 gold units. Scan real bars starting 09:00 through 11:45 London, in timestamp order. The first bar whose high strictly exceeds H arms an upper sweep; the first whose low strictly falls below L arms a lower sweep. If both edges are crossed in the same first-break bar, skip the date as ambiguous. After an upper sweep, track the maximum high through each scanned bar; if any lower-edge breach happens before a signal, skip the date. A short signal occurs on the first completed scanned bar whose BID close is strictly between L and H. Lower sweep is symmetric: track minimum low, skip if upper edge breached first, signal long on first close strictly inside. The first-break bar may itself close inside. If no return inside by the 11:45 bar close, no trade. The decision is only at that completed close; enter at the BID open of the immediately following real M15 bar, which must start exactly 15 minutes later. Skip any missing entry bar. One earliest qualifying entry per London date, no second attempt and no concurrent position.

4. FIXED STOP, TARGET, FLAT AND COST
Long BUY fills at entry BID+0.41; stop is tracked sweep minimum BID minus 0.25W and target is opening-range high H on BID. Short SELL fills at entry BID; stop is tracked sweep maximum BID plus 0.25W as an ASK threshold and target is opening-range low L as an ASK threshold. Require positive planned stop distance and positive target distance from the entry fill; otherwise skip the entry. Check stop and target on every real M15 bar from entry through the final 15:45-16:00 London bar. For long stop: BID low<=stop, adverse-gap fill=min(stop,BID open); long target: BID high>=H, fill H. For short stop: BID high+0.41>=stop, adverse-gap fill=max(stop,BID open+0.41); short target: BID low+0.41<=L, fill L. If both touch in a bar, stop wins. If neither touches, exit at the final real 15:45 M15 BID close for long or BID close+0.41 for short. If that final bar is absent after entry, mark unresolved using the last available earlier quote/bar only as a diagnostic, count it, and fail coverage. Do not use future final-bar availability to skip an entry. All entries/exits occur before 16:00 London and on the same Europe/Prague date. Fixed 0.41 variable-cost proxy charged through BID/ASK fills; extra 0.41 units per trade stress. Actual variable spread, commission, order delay and slippage require later tick/order execution evidence.
