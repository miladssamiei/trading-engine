SECTION 1 -- DATA
1.1  Instrument US500-tick (custom symbol). M5 export
     data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv (364,330 bars,
     2021-01-03 .. 2026-04-30, terminal-native, verified in prior
     studies). Time: epoch UTC -> America/New_York for wall minutes
     (project convention). The tape is extended-hours (roughly 00:00
     .. 16:1x and 18:00 .. 23:59 NY), so the 08:00-12:00 reference
     block below is fully populated on normal trading days.
1.2  Cash day = NY date whose 09:30 (wall 570) bar exists. A day is
     ELIGIBLE for signal generation iff it is a cash day AND has a
     valid reference candle (2.2).
1.3  Costs: 0.51 pt = the Model=4-confirmed real-tick median
     round-trip spread floor. INFORMATIONAL ONLY in this study
     (section 6.3); no net-of-cost gates are run.

SECTION 2 -- CONSTRUCTS
2.1  Reference 4H candle (per the source video, US indices): the
     08:00 .. 12:00 NY wall-minute block [480, 720) on each trading
     day, EST/EDT aware (tz_convert, DST handled). ref_high = max(high)
     and ref_low = min(low) over the M5 bars whose wall minute lies in
     the block.
2.2  Reference validity: the block must contain >= 24 of the 48
     possible M5 bars. Fewer -> the day is skipped for BOTH sides and
     tallied. The bar-count distribution is reported.
2.3  Execution timeframe: 15-minute candles aggregated from M5 buckets
     (three M5 bars per candle), aligned to :00/:15/:30/:45 NY. Bucket
     OHLC = first open / max high / min low / last close of the M5
     bars present in the bucket. NY offsets are whole hours, so UTC
     900-second bucketing is NY-aligned in both EST and EDT.
2.4  ATR14 (Wilder) on the CONTINUOUS 15m tape: overnight bars feed
     it, no per-day reset, seeded with the SMA of the first 14 TRs.
     The binding ATR for a signal is the value at Candle 2's close --
     the last completed candle at decision time (no lookahead past the
     entry decision). A signal whose Candle 2 has fewer than 14 prior
     15m candles, or a degenerate ATR (NaN or <= 0), is dropped and
     tallied.

SECTION 3 -- CRT "MODEL 1" STATE MACHINE (long side; short mirrored)
3.1  Sweep (Candle 1): the first 15m candle of the day whose close is
     strictly after 12:00 NY and whose Low is STRICTLY below ref_low.
     (The 11:45-12:00 candle lies inside the reference block, so its
     low cannot be strictly below ref_low; scanning effectively starts
     at the 12:00 bucket.)
3.2  Turtle-soup trigger (Candle 2): a SUBSEQUENT 15m candle, closing
     within 3 hours after Candle 1's close (c2_close - c1_close <=
     180 min), whose Low sweeps STRICTLY below Low(Candle 1) but whose
     Close is STRICTLY above Low(Candle 1).
3.2.1 Deepening rule: while armed, a subsequent candle with
     Low < Low(C1) AND Close <= Low(C1) becomes the new Candle 1
     (its close restarts the 3-hour window). A shallower candle
     (Low >= Low(C1)) changes nothing.
3.2.2 Window expiry: if 3 hours elapse with no Candle 2, the state
     returns to SEARCHING; a fresh qualifying sweep later the same day
     may re-arm it, time permitting. All scanning is same-NY-day only.
3.3  Entry: Long at the OPEN of the 15m candle immediately following
     Candle 2, i.e. the adjacent bucket c2_start + 15 min. If that
     bucket does not exist (tape gap), the trigger dies and is
     tallied. Signal accepted ONLY if the entry candle starts at or
     before 15:00 NY (wall 900) -- "no signals accepted after 15:00".
     Entry at exactly 15:00 is allowed.
3.4  At most ONE long signal and ONE short signal per day (first
     valid trigger per side). Both sides may fire on the same day and
     are independent samples.
3.5  Exit context: every position is flat by the session close = the
     last M5 bar close before 16:00 NY (the 15:55 bar, project
     convention). Nothing is held past it.

SECTION 4 -- MEASUREMENT (per signal)
4.1  Window: the M5 bars (finer tape, per project exit-fidelity
     discipline) from the entry bar through the session-close bar
     inclusive, same NY day.
4.2  Long: MFE = max(high) - entry; MAE = entry - min(low).
     Short: MFE = entry - min(low); MAE = max(high) - entry.
     All in index points.
4.3  Scaled: MFE_atr = MFE / ATR14, MAE_atr = MAE / ATR14.
4.4  Expectancy proxy e = MFE - MAE (raw points). MFE/MAE are
     excursion maxima, NOT simultaneously achievable PnL; e is the
     project-standard Phase 1 signal-quality proxy only.

SECTION 5 -- FUNNEL (every stage tallied in the report)
     days in tape -> cash days -> eligible days (cash AND valid
     reference) -> per side: days with >= 1 Candle-1 arm -> days with
     a Candle-2 trigger -> triggers killed by the 15:00 entry cutoff
     -> triggers killed by a missing adjacent next candle -> ATR
     warmup/degenerate drops -> measured signals.

SECTION 6 -- GATES (frozen here; GATE_CRT_EXPLORATION.txt is the
             verbatim extraction, written before any script ran)
6.1  G-C1 SAMPLE SIZE: total measured signals (long + short)
     >= 150 over the 5.3-year tape.
6.2  G-C2 GROSS EDGE -- ALL three clauses required:
     (i)   mean(MFE) > mean(MAE) on raw points (binding);
           mean(MFE_atr) > mean(MAE_atr) reported alongside;
     (ii)  threshold dominance: count(MFE >= t * ATR14) >
           count(MAE >= t * ATR14) at EVERY t in {0.50, 0.75, 1.00};
     (iii) day-block bootstrap P(mean(MFE - MAE) <= 0) <= 0.20:
           DAY MEANS of (MFE - MAE) resampled with replacement,
           10,000 draws, seed 20260909. The ATR-normalized variant
           (day means of (MFE - MAE)/ATR14) is reported alongside,
           non-binding.
     A clause that cannot be evaluated (missing data, degenerate
     case) counts as FAIL.
6.3  INFORMATIONAL COST CHECK (non-binding): if the raw point
     expectancy mean(MFE - MAE) < 0.51 pt (the confirmed real-tick
     spread floor), the report flags the strategy ECONOMICALLY DEAD
     BEFORE COSTS. Clearing 0.51 pt would still not make it tradeable:
     MFE/MAE maxima overstate achievable PnL, and this study is
     post-termination exploratory (section 0).
