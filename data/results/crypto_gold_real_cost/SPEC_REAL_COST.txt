CRYPTO/GOLD ENGINE A/B FINANCING PORTFOLIO - PHASE 1 SPEC
Frozen 2026-09-28 before evaluate/crypto_gold_real_cost_portfolio.py exists or any phase-1 run.

PURPOSE: Apply the owner's fixed spread, entry-price round-trip commission and annual swap stress assumptions to frozen Engine A/B on BTCUSD, ETHUSD and XAUUSD. This is a retrospective BID-tape proxy screen, not a reconstruction of the historically changing FTMO fee schedule or an account-level return. Null results and failures are complete deliverables. No parameter search or post-result threshold changes.

DATA: BTCUSD data/results/virgin_assets/btc_m1.csv; ETHUSD data/results/virgin_assets/eth_m1.csv; XAUUSD data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv. Sources contain UTC M1 BID OHLC. Verify sorted unique M1 minute timestamps, positive finite OHLC and valid envelopes, record SHA256/row counts/range. Remove explicit vendor carried O=H=L=C=prior close bars before execution; do not execute on synthetic minutes. Build continuous forward-filled 1H grid, NY-wall 4H [00,04), [04,08), etc. and 17:00 ET D1 anchors by the frozen evaluate/futures_prop_validation_phase1.py grids()/engine_a()/engine_b() functions. Keep all three sources to a common cutoff: last observed XAUUSD M1 time, 2026-09-01 20:59 UTC, so crypto's later September quotes do not create noncomparable pooled years. Earlier BTC/ETH data before XAUUSD begins may warm indicators but scored entries require timestamps >= 2020-03-01 UTC. Report source gaps, first/last observations, and missing-minute caveats. No broker quote/tick tape is available.

ENGINE A: Frozen 4H Keltner EMA29 +/-2.77 Wilder ATR29, last completed 17:00 ET D1 close vs EMA50 direction, first real M1 open of next 4H bucket, initial BID-coordinate stop distance max(abs(entry BID-prior EMA29),1.5*prior ATR29), EMA29 ratchet at each completed 4H close, one position per asset engine.
ENGINE B: Frozen 1H close beyond Donchian20 from t-2, prior 1H ADX14>=20, last completed NY-wall 4H Supertrend(10,3) direction, first real M1 open in next real H1 bar, initial 4*prior H1 ATR14 stop, ratchet at each real H1 close to close +/-4*current ATR14, one position per asset engine. M1 stop checks have adverse priority, open gaps fill at adverse M1 open; no TP or time exit. Preserve each frozen engine's short-stop BID/ASK convention exactly. Shadow signals while occupied. Open-end positions remain open and are excluded from closed-return statistics.

FRICTION: Quote BID tape. BTCUSD spread 3.0, ETHUSD spread 0.60, XAUUSD all-in spread 0.585, in USD price units per one base asset unit. Long entry ASK=BID+spread, exit BID; short entry BID, exit ASK=BID+spread. Thus gross BID-coordinate PnL minus spread is the fill PnL once. BTC/ETH additionally deduct a single round-trip commission equal to 0.00065*entry BID price per one BTC/ETH, independent of exit price or side, as specifically requested. XAUUSD adds no further commission. These fixed owner assumptions are applied to all historical years as a counterfactual; the crypto percentage is not the actual historical two-sided exit-notional schedule.

SWAP: Both long and short positions pay the specified negative annual rate, per one base asset unit, without compounding. At each rollover mark price is the last observed BID M1 close STRICTLY BEFORE the rollover; in no-quote gaps this is a stale mark and is disclosed. Each crypto UTC calendar midnight uses weight 1 and charges -mark*0.15/365, including Saturday/Sunday. Each gold NY 17:00 rollover with NY weekday Monday-Friday charges -mark*0.035/365, Wednesday weight 3 and other weekdays weight 1. No separate Saturday/Sunday gold charge. A rollover counts if entry_time < rollover_time <= exit_time; an open-end trade accrues to the final observed M1 timestamp. Precompute boundary arrays with DST-aware NY conversion. Record charge count, weighted count and total swap USD per asset and engine; report total charged on closed trades and separate open-end accrual. This annualized notional-charge interpretation is a model of the owner-specified rates, not an independently verified FTMO swap schedule.

NORMALIZATION: For every executed trade, initial risk in USD per one base asset unit is the positive difference between the entry executable fill and the executable initial-stop fill, including spread according to the frozen engine's stop coordinate. Gross USD=side*(exit BID-entry BID). Fill USD=gross USD-spread. Net USD=fill USD-commission USD+swap USD. Gross R=gross USD/initial risk; net R=net USD/initial risk. Commission and swap are not added to the initial-risk denominator. Gap losses can be less than -1R. Report gross and net USD expectancy by asset/engine and normalized net R expectancy and per-trade net R Sharpe (unannualized). Dollars mean USD per one BTC, one ETH or one troy ounce of gold; no lot size, contract conversion or account leverage is inferred.

POOLED SERIES: Combine closed trades from six independent asset/engine ledgers in chronological exit-time order (tie by asset then engine). Treat each trade as one initial-risk unit; cumulative net R is a fixed-1R-per-entry bookkeeping curve, not a cash account equity path because positions can overlap and no risk budget is specified. Report pooled trades, cumulative/mean net R, per-trade Sharpe and chronological MBB P(mean net R<=0): 10000 resamples, 10-trade overlapping blocks with replacement, seed 20261025, trimmed to n trades. Save pooled ledger and curve; count maximum simultaneous open positions. Six individual cells remain separately visible. No walk-forward refit or real-tick broker execution is claimed.
