HYPOTHESIS 85: FTMO XAUUSD LONDON HIGH-VOLUME BAR RETEST
Declared 2026-09-25 before H85 code, signals, returns or runs.

1. OBJECTIVE AND MECHANISM
Test one strictly intraday long-only gold rule on the confirmed EUR10k
FTMO-Demo target. A London opening advance that later tests the price
area of its most active M15 bar might have continuation demand.
Highest M15 tick volume is only a broker activity proxy; the bar
midpoint is NOT a true volume-profile point of control. The public
Gold POC Retest author's results motivate this separate hypothesis,
but its proprietary signal and published returns are NOT replicated.
No parameter/side/time rescue after the first run. Null is complete.

3. FIXED ONE-TRADE-PER-DAY RULE
For each London weekday, require exactly eight real consecutive M15
bars opening 08:00, 08:15, ..., 09:45 London, all tick_volume>0.
Define opening O as 08:00 BID open, opening close C as 09:45 BID close,
opening H and L as eight-bar extremes, width W=H-L. Require W>0.82
gold units and C>O. Pick the opening bar with largest tick_volume;
ties choose earliest. Reference P=(that bar's BID high+low)/2.
Require C>P. Scan M15 bars opening 10:00..13:45 London in order.
The first completed bar with BID low<=P and BID close>P is the sole
signal; do not take another setup that day. Require the next real bar
to open exactly 15 minutes later and no later than 14:00 London.
Buy at next bar BID open+0.41 assumed Ask. Require entry BID>P;
otherwise skip this date without another attempt. Initial BID stop
is P-0.5W. Require entry Ask>stop and risk=entry Ask-stop>0.
Target BID is entry Ask+2*risk. No discretionary filter, no shorts,
no pyramiding and no carry past London 16:00.

4. EXIT, COST AND COVERAGE
From entry bar through 15:45 London bar inclusive: long stop hits
when BID low<=stop, fill min(stop, BID open); target hits when BID
high>=target, fill target. If both hit in one bar, stop wins. Require
consecutive 15-minute printed bars along an open trade; gaps mark
the trade unresolved. If no bracket hits, flat at 15:45 bar BID close.
If that bar is absent, trade is unresolved and counts against gate.
Never pre-filter a signal using future exit-bar availability. Net
gold units=exit BID-entry Ask; extra-cost stress=net-0.41. Compute
floating worst from the minimum BID low of every bar used and charge
the extra 0.41 stress to it. Native historical variable Bid/Ask,
commission, slippage, EUR conversion and 0.01-lot rounding are not
available from this M15 source. These limitations prevent deployment
or claimed broker-executed performance after a Phase-1 pass.

Save every eligible-day status and every signaled trade including
unresolved ones. Report base/stressed net, gross BID movement, R

For EUR10k paid 2-Step first-phase proxy, use every gated trade-date

Also report 14-calendar-day EUR10k Free Trial proxy at EUR10.5k,
