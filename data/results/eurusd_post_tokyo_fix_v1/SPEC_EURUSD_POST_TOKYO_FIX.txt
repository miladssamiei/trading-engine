HYPOTHESIS 69: EURUSD POST-TOKYO-FIX DOLLAR REVERSAL
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. EXTERNAL MECHANISM AND FIXED RULE
Krohn et al., Foreign Exchange Fixings and Returns around the Clock,
Journal of Finance 2024, https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13306,
Table I reports EURUSD midpoint appreciation of +2.34 basis points
from the 09:55 Tokyo fixing to 02:00 New York in 1999-2019 data.
The paper explicitly accounts for New York DST because Tokyo has no
DST, and reports that Tokyo-fix reversal disappears on Japanese
holidays. Ex ante broker-CFD rule: BUY EURUSD at 09:55:05 Asia/Tokyo
on every Monday-Friday Tokyo civil date; sell at 02:00:00
America/New_York on the same civil date. Five seconds after the
fix allows reaction to the known clock instant. Include Japanese
holidays rather than sourcing a possibly revised calendar; this
dilutes the published effect and avoids a post-hoc date filter.
This is the final third prepublished fix leg considered here.
Failed #67 and #68 verdicts remain final. Three legs are a small
part of many earlier project hypotheses; historical significance
alone cannot validate a live edge after broad research selection.

3. EXECUTION PROXY AND COST
Take first valid quote at or after target within 10 seconds
inclusive for both entry and exit. Valid: finite, positive Ask
and Bid with Ask>=Bid. Count and exclude invalid sampled quotes;
skip and log dates missing either quote. No holiday, news,
volatility, spread, or day-of-week filter; no stop, target, extra
attempt, or alternate exit. Enter long at Ask, close at Bid.
Midpoint gross pips = (exitMid-entryMid)/0.00010. Executable quote
net pips = (exitBid-entryAsk)/0.00010. Actual quote spread is
charged; extra-cost stress net pips = quote net - 0.3 to buffer
additional slippage/latency equal to the user's spread floor.
No swap expected during this exact hold. First quote is an
optimistic fill proxy: live latency, rejection and market impact
are untested.

4. PARTITION AND INFERENCE
2020-2023 is descriptive only; 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-18
is the gated chronological historical block. Published source
sample ended 2019; no parameters are estimated from this broker

Bootstrap gated Tokyo dates with replacement 10000 times, seed
