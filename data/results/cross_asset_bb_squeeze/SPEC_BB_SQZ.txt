HYPOTHESIS #48 SPEC: MULTI-TIMEFRAME BOLLINGER SQUEEZE TREND (4h anchor, 30m execution)
Study ID: cross_asset_bb_squeeze
Date frozen: 2026-09-23, before any evaluation code was written or run.
Status: frozen. Corrections or re-measurements require a NEW study name.
Lineage: sibling of #47 (cross_asset_vol_squeeze, FAIL final). #47 entered 15m Donchian
breakouts while the 4h squeeze was STILL ON; this study tests a materially different
arming mechanic - the squeeze FIRE (compression >= 4 consecutive 4h bars, then release)
with a 4h ADX trend-strength filter and an 11-bar slope direction gate - executed on
30m bars with 4h-ATR-sized initial and trailing stops. Parameterization (29/1.82 BB,
29EMA/1.56 KC, ADX 14 >= 19.11, LR 11, 1.74/3.71 multipliers) is owner-supplied and
frozen as given; no parameter search, one run per instrument.

1. DATA
1.1 Tapes (verified M1, epoch-UTC time, columns time,open,high,low,close,
    tick_volume; sorted ascending defensively before aggregation):
    - US500 : data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv  (points; cost floor 0.51)
    - EURUSD: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (pips, 1 pip = 0.0001; cost floor 0.3)
    - XAUUSD: data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv (price units; cost floor 0.41)
1.2 Timezone America/New_York (EST/EDT) for all wall-time logic.
1.3 Cost floors are round-trip, subtracted once per trade from gross.

2. MULTI-TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION
2.1 30m bars: M1 epochs floored to 1800s slots; open=first, high=max, low=min,
    close=last, volume=sum per slot. NY wall 30m boundaries align exactly with the
    epoch-1800 grid because the NY UTC offset is always a whole number of hours.
2.2 Continuity: the 30m series is reindexed to the FULL continuous 1800s grid from
    first to last slot; missing bars are forward-filled with OHLC = prior close,
    volume = 0 (synthetic flat bars). This fills no-tick minutes, maintenance
    breaks, holidays and weekends alike.
2.3 4h anchor bars: built FROM the continuous 30m grid by NY WALL-clock bucket
    [00:00-04:00), [04:00-08:00), ... (open=first, high=max, low=min, close=last).
    Wall bucketing means DST-transition anchor buckets span 3 or 5 real hours;
    buckets are contiguous by construction. The two series are strictly aligned.

3. STATE MACHINE (causal 4h indicators; all on completed anchor bars)
3.1 For a 30m bar t, the 4h input is the most recently completed 4h bar relative
    to the open of t: the wall bucket strictly before the wall bucket containing t.
    Its indicators include that 4h bar's own close, known at its end.
3.2 On the 4h series (close c, high h, low l):
    - SMA29 and population stddev (ddof=0) -> BB = SMA29 +/- 1.82*std29.
    - EMA29 (span=29, adjust=False) and Wilder ATR(14) -> KC = EMA29
      +/- 1.56*ATR14. Wilder/RMA: TR=max(H-L,|H-cPrev|,|L-cPrev|),
      ATR(13)=mean(TR 0..13), ATR(i)=(ATR(i-1)*13+TR(i))/14.
    - Squeeze ON at 4h bar k iff BB_upper(k) < KC_upper(k) AND
      BB_lower(k) > KC_lower(k). Squeeze is defined only where all four band
      inputs are non-NaN.
    - ADX(14), Wilder double smoothing: +DM/-DM with tie->0; TR, +DM, -DM
      RMA-smoothed (first = mean of the first 14 terms from index 1);
      +DI=100*sDM/sTR, -DI=100*sDM/sTR; DX=100*|+DI-(-DI)|/(+DI+(-DI));
      ADX(27)=mean(DX 14..27), ADX(i)=(ADX(i-1)*13+DX(i))/14 after.
    - Linear-regression slope (11): least-squares slope of the last 11 4h
      closes vs x=0..10 (closed form; exactly 0.0 on a constant window).
    - ATR(14) Wilder as above - used for stop sizing.
3.3 SQUEEZE FIRED at 4h bar k: squeeze ON at each of k-4, k-3, k-2, k-1 (at
    least 4 consecutive completed ON bars) AND squeeze OFF at k (all five bars
    defined). "At least 4" is checked on the trailing four bars; longer ON runs
    qualify automatically.
3.4 Armed 4h bar k: k >= 33 (0-based; covers BB(29) warmup plus the 4-bar
    squeeze history) AND ADX, LR slope, ATR non-NaN AND squeeze defined at
    k, k-1, k-2, k-3, k-4.
3.5 The fired flag of anchor bar k applies to EVERY 30m bar whose most recently
    completed 4h bar is k (the whole following 4h window). If a trade exits
    inside that window, a later 30m bar in the same window may re-enter
    (declared; no once-per-fire cap beyond the 1-open-trade cap).

4. SIGNALS AND EXECUTION
4.1 Long signal at 30m bar t: 4h Squeeze Fired TRUE, 4h ADX >= 19.11, 4h LR
    slope > 0. Short: Fired TRUE, ADX >= 19.11, slope < 0.
4.2 Execution cap: max ONE open trade per instrument; signals while a trade is
    open are counted (ignored-while-open) and discarded. A trade occupies bars
    [entry_bar, exit_bar] inclusive; the next actionable signal is at
    exit_bar + 1 or later.
4.3 Entry: Open price of the signal bar t.

5. EXCURSION WALK AND TRAILING-STOP EXIT
5.1 ATR4 = Wilder ATR(14) of the mapped anchor bar (strictly prior 4h state),
    FROZEN AT ENTRY and used for all distance math of that trade.
5.2 Initial SL = Entry - 1.74*ATR4 (long) / Entry + 1.74*ATR4 (short).
5.3 Walk 30m bars j = entry_bar, entry_bar+1, ... in order (long case; short
    mirrored):
    a) Open(j) <= SL -> exit at Open(j), reason SL_GAP (honest fill);
    b) Low(j)  <= SL -> exit at SL, reason SL (worst-case default on same-bar
       dual conditions);
    c) at the close of j, ratchet: SL = max(SL, max Close over
       [entry_bar..j] - 3.71*ATR4), with ATR4 frozen per 5.1.
5.4 If no stop is hit by the final 30m bar, exit at that bar's close, reason
    FORCED_EOD, included in results.
5.5 Per-trade record includes MFE/MAE excursions over [entry_bar..exit_bar]
    (long MFE = max High - Entry, MAE = Entry - min Low; short mirrored),
    in instrument units, descriptive only.

6. ECONOMICS
6.1 Gross = side*(Exit - Entry) in instrument units: points for US500 and
    XAUUSD, pips (x10,000) for EURUSD. Net = Gross - cost floor (0.51 / 0.3 /
    0.41 respectively), one round trip per trade.
6.2 Net Sharpe (per-trade convention): mean(Net)/std(Net, ddof=1) * sqrt(252)
    over executed trades of the instrument.

7. PHASE 1 GATES (applied INDEPENDENTLY to each instrument; frozen verbatim in
   GATE_BB_SQZ.txt)
    G-BB1: Total executed signals >= 30.
    G-BB2: Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
    G-BB3: Moving-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=5 trades,
           seed 20260926) P(mean Net PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
    VERDICT RULE: an instrument PASSES only if G-BB1 AND G-BB2 AND G-BB3 all
    PASS. OVERALL verdict PASS only if ALL THREE instruments PASS. Any FAIL is
    final for this artifact; corrections require a new study name. Null
    results are complete, expected deliverables.

8. OUTPUTS (data/results/cross_asset_bb_squeeze/)
    SPEC_BB_SQZ.txt (this file), GATE_BB_SQZ.txt (written before the run),
    REPORT_BB_SQZ_PHASE1.txt (funnel, net expectancy, exit mix), bb_sqz_signals.csv
    (all instruments, asset column), run_meta.json (sha256 lineage of tapes and
    script, funnel, gates, verdict).

9. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
9.1 All 4h inputs strictly completed; entries only at 30m bar opens; no
    intrabar lookahead; gap fills at the open; worst-case same-bar stop.
9.2 Single frozen parameterization; no optimization, no re-runs, no post-hoc
    filtering; sides, years and instruments reported separately and pooled.
9.3 CONSEQUENCE OF 2.2, declared honestly: synthetic flat bars feed the
    indicators; LR slope is exactly 0.0 on constant windows (arms neither
    side), while Wilder ATR/ADX decay across long gaps, so post-gap fires can
    carry mechanically tightened stops. Accepted under the declared continuity
    convention; no bar-skipping or ATR-freezing fix is applied.
9.4 Phase 2 (MT5 execution) only on overall PASS; in-sample character of this
    screen acknowledged; MFE/MAE are exploratory descriptors, never results.
