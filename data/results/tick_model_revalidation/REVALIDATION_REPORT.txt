3. WHAT THE REAL-TICK TAPE CHANGED (honest decomposition)

  - Children's own executed results shrink on real ticks: ungated
    ORB +114.91 (vs +470.67 validated under Model=0) and ungated
    SP2L +139.21 (vs +281.84). Roughly 8-9% of trades differ by
    entry/exit key (ORB 38+48 of ~486, SP2L 107+101 of ~1450): the
    real tape's wicks and variable spread trigger (and miss) fills
    the synthetic M1-derived stream handled differently - the
    diag_20240419 TP class, now in bulk.
  - Real median spread 0.516 vs the synthetic constant 0.41: with
    ~880 gated round trips, ~0.1-0.2 pt extra per round trip is a
    material, honestly-reported cost drag (~100-180 pts of the
    -303 pt net gap vs Model=0); the remainder is different tick
    paths at stops/targets.
  - The 2025-05 label flip persists (D1 source), and its May-2025
    SP2L trades are now WORSE on the real tape (the disabled-child
    clause count dropped 31 -> 30 but the DD damage grew with the
    generally wider real-tick outcomes).

Conclusion: the controller's offline Phase-1 quality (0.720) does
not survive real-tick execution on this platform AT ALL levels -
tick path, spread, and the unchanged D1 label source. It remains
NOT validated, exactly as the original verdict, with a now
real-tick-grounded and LARGER G1 failure.
