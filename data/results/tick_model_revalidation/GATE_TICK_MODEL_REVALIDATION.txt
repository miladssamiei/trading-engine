GATE_TICK_MODEL_REVALIDATION
Gates for the tick-model revalidation (Model=4 real-tick re-measurement
of hypothesis #13's Phase-2 execution layer)

Status: written 2026-08-30 before any script. Verbatim extraction of
every gate in SPEC_TICK_MODEL_REVALIDATION sections 3 and 4. Nothing
here may be relaxed after revalidation output exists.

    Part 1 - full-range probe gates (infrastructure; pass/fail)

P1. Real-tick sync succeeds for the ENTIRE 2021-01..2026-04 range with
zero months falling back to synthetic generation:
  - the "generating based on real ticks" tester log line appears for
    every batch covering the range, AND
  - "real ticks begin from" never lags the requested start date by
    more than the documented probe tolerance of 1 trading day (the
    tape's first tick date is 2021-01-03/04).

P2. Total synchronized tick count across the probe batches is within
1% of the independently computed sum of ticks across the 64 monthly
.tkc files in the terminal Custom base (edge effects at month/batch
boundaries included in the tolerance).

P1 or P2 failure -> STOP. No Part 2. The affected sub-periods keep the
old synthetic-tick caveat; partial failures are not averaged away.

    Part 2 - regime rotation Phase 2 re-reconciliation gates
    (reproduced verbatim from GATE_REGIME_ROTATION.txt G1/G2; the ONLY
    change from the original run is Model=4 instead of Model=0)

G1. Consistency: MT5 ensemble Sharpe within +/- 0.15 (absolute) and
MT5 max drawdown within +/- 20% (relative) of the offline Phase-1
reference numbers (Sharpe 0.720, maxDD 1.80%) for Arm P, over the same
window.

G2. Execution integrity:
  - zero trades by a child disabled under the OFFLINE schedule on any
    day (by trade open date),
  - MT5 month-decision schedule matches the offline reference on
    >= 95% of months, every mismatch classified,
  - every trade attributed to exactly one child,
  - automatic ungated-reproduction check: embedded children must
    reproduce their validated trade sets exactly (any difference is
    an automatic G2 failure).

PASS = G1 AND G2, both exactly as originally defined. PASS means the
regime-rotation controller (Arm P) is VALIDATED under Model=4
real-tick execution. FAIL keeps the original Model=0 verdict standing:
controller remains NOT validated, with the new failure mode reported
precisely.
