G-P1 (Structural Mean Reversion)
  The Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) test p-value of the 252-day rolling
  spread must be < 0.05 on at least 40% of the rolling windows.

G-P2 (Gross Economic Edge)
  The gross expectancy (total points captured across both legs before
  costs) must be > 0.

G-P3 (Net Viability)
  Annualized Net Sharpe Ratio (after the 0.51/2.28 spreads and both
  instrument swaps) must be > 0.

VERDICT RULE
  PASS iff G-P1 AND G-P2 AND G-P3 all pass. Any failure is final for
  this artifact; null results are complete deliverables.
