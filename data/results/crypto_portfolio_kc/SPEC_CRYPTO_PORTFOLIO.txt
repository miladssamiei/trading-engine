1. DATA
1.1 Tapes (standard schema; onboarded 2026-09-24 by
    evaluate/virgin_assets_onboarding.py, sha256 lineage in
    data/results/virgin_assets/onboarding_stats.json):
    - BTCUSD: data/results/virgin_assets/btc_m1.csv  (3,260,989 M1 bars, price units; cost floor 15.0 RT)
    - ETHUSD: data/results/virgin_assets/eth_m1.csv  (3,375,260 M1 bars, price units; cost floor  2.0 RT)
    Both span 2020-01-01 00:00 UTC .. 2026-09-23 23:59 UTC.
1.2 Timezone America/New_York (EST/EDT) for all wall-time logic.
1.3 Cost floors remain OWNER ESTIMATES (no ask side in the M1 BID exports;

1.4 tick_volume = 0 (schema placeholder, never interpreted).

2. MULTI-TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION (identical machinery to #50/#51/#54)
2.1 1h base: M1 epochs floored to 3600s slots; OHLC first/max/min/last per
    slot; reindexed to the FULL continuous 3600s grid; missing hours
    forward-filled OHLC = prior close, volume = 0.
2.2 4h execution bars: NY WALL buckets [00:00-04:00), [04:00-08:00), ...
    from the continuous 1h grid; contiguous; DST buckets span 23 or 25
    real hours.
2.3 D1 anchors: NY WALL [17:00 -> next 17:00) buckets from the continuous
    1h grid; D1 close = last 1h close of the bucket; completes at its
    17:00 ET boundary; mapped to each 4h bar strictly from completed
    buckets only.

3. STATE MACHINE AND EXECUTION (frozen #51/#54 mechanics verbatim)
3.1 D1 EMA(50) (ewm span=50, adjust=False; first 49 NaN); Bullish iff
    D1 Close > EMA(50); Bearish iff <.
3.2 4h EMA(29) (first 28 NaN); Wilder ATR(29); Upper/Lower = EMA29
    +/- 2.77*ATR29.
3.3 Armed at 4h bar t iff Close, EMA29, ATR29, bands at t-1 are non-NaN
    AND the mapped completed D1 bar exists with non-NaN EMA(50). Warm-up
    (~Feb 2020) trades are simply absent from the ledger.
3.4 LONG  iff Close(t-1) > Upper(t-1) AND D1 Bullish;
    SHORT iff Close(t-1) < Lower(t-1) AND D1 Bearish.
    Entry = Open(t), market. Max ONE open trade per INSTRUMENT (the
    portfolio may hold up to two concurrent positions, one per asset);
    trades occupy [entry_bar, exit_bar]; signals while that instrument is
    open are counted (ignored-while-open) and not acted on.
3.5 Initial stop: SL = Entry - max(|Entry - EMA29(t-1)|, 1.5*ATR29(t-1))
    for LONG; Entry + same for SHORT. This distance is
    Initial_Risk_Distance, frozen at entry, NEVER re-based when the trail
    moves.
3.6 EMA-riding trail: on every 4h bar j of the walk (entry bar included),
    AFTER that bar's exit checks, SL ratchets to EMA29(j) (LONG: max;
    SHORT: min; only tightens; NaN skipped).
3.7 Exit walk (long case; short mirrored) per bar j in order:
    a) Open(j) <= SL -> exit at Open(j), SL_GAP (honest);
    b) Low(j) <= SL -> exit at SL, reason SL (worst-case same-bar);
    c) apply 3.6 and continue.
    No take-profit; FORCED_EOD at the final 4h bar's close if nothing
    hits, included in results.

4. R-MULTIPLE NORMALIZATION AND PORTFOLIO POOLING
4.1 Per trade: Gross = side*(Exit - Entry) in native price units;
    Net = Gross - cost floor (native units);
    Net_R = Net / Initial_Risk_Distance (dimensionless).
    Gross R = side*(Exit-Entry)/Initial_Risk_Distance is also recorded.
    R-normalization is what makes heterogeneous units poolable; each
    asset's cost burden enters in its own units before normalization.
4.2 Portfolio ledger: ALL executed trades from both instruments, sorted
    chronologically by 4h entry bar time; deterministic tie-break on
    asset name (BTCUSD before ETHUSD) when both enter the same 4h bar.
    The equity curve is EVENT-INDEXED cumulative Net R in ledger order
    (concurrent overlapping trades appear sequentially by entry) - a
    declared simplification, not calendar marking.
4.3 Portfolio statistics reported: n, mean/median/total Net R, win rate,
    per-trade R Sharpe (mean/std(ddof=1)*sqrt(252), informational only),
    max drawdown of the cumulative Net R curve (peak-to-trough, R units),
    per-asset n/mean/total Net R, per-year pooled Net R, side mix, exit
    mix.

5. PHASE 1 GATES (applied to the POOLED PORTFOLIO LEDGER, Net R units;
   frozen verbatim in GATE_CRYPTO_PORTFOLIO.txt)
    G-PORT1: Total pooled executed trades >= 300.
    G-PORT2: Portfolio Net R-Multiple Expectancy > 0.15 R per trade.
    G-PORT3: Moving-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=10 trades,
            seed 20261003) P(mean Net R <= 0) <= 0.10.
    VERDICT RULE: OVERALL PASS only if G-PORT1 AND G-PORT2 AND G-PORT3 all
    PASS. Any FAIL is final for this artifact; corrections require a new
    study name. Null results are complete, expected deliverables.
    Declared expectation (from #54, before this run): identical mechanics
    should reproduce ~342 pooled trades, so G-PORT1 has a thin margin;
    G-PORT2/G-PORT3 thresholds are stricter than any prior study
    (0.15 R; block 10; p <= 0.10) and are taken as given.

7.1 Mechanics, parameters, walk logic copied verbatim from #54 (frozen
    from #51/#50); prior-bar indicator inputs only; completed D1 anchors
    only; exit checks before ratchet; worst-case same-bar stop; honest
    gap fills. No adaptation beyond the pooled R normalization declared
    here.
7.2 The trade set is NOT fresh data: #54 already reported these
    instrument-level results (section 0). Pooling is the pre-declared
    aggregation step; a PASS upgrades nothing to "validated result"

    gates declared before the run).
7.3 Cost floors are owner estimates (1.3): R-expectancy margins within
    plausible floor error are flagged by reporting gross R alongside net
    R everywhere; no floor is re-tuned after seeing results.
7.4 Continuous forward-fill consequences as declared in #50/#51/#54
    (EMA flattens, ATR decays, flat closes cannot print breakouts, D1
    direction persists); mostly moot on 24/7 crypto printing (BTC 7.9% /
    ETH 4.6% synthetic 1h bars).
7.5 Single frozen parameterization; no optimization, no re-runs, no
    post-hoc filtering; assets, years, sides and exit reasons reported
    as run. MFE/MAE are exploratory descriptors, never results.
