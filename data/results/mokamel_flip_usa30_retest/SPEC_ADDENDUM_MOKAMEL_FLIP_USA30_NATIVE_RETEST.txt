hypothesis #16 (originally SPEC_MOKAMEL_FLIP.txt, 2026-09-03, US500-tick),
not a new hypothesis and not a rule change. The pattern's presenter claimed
a Dow-Jones-only domain; the original test ran on US500 and closed NULL with

removes that caveat by re-running the EXISTING, UNMODIFIED state machine
and stage scripts on native USA30 data:

40.00 / 80.00 index points (STOP/TARGET). Dollar convention: $1 per point
per lot (project convention), binding sizing 2.5 lots => $100 risk at the
40-pt stop, identical economics to the original arm.

>= WARMUP 200 AND ny_date in the binding universe; binding population =
