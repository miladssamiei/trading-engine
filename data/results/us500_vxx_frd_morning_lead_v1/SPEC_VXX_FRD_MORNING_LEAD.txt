Convert UTC epoch seconds to America/New_York. Require real
09:30 cash-open, 09:45 entry and 10:45 exit M5 bars. Enter at
09:45 BID open and exit at 10:45 BID open, one same-day trade.
Gross points = direction times exit-minus-entry BID. Base net
subtracts the owner's 0.51-point round-trip spread proxy; stress
subtracts another 0.51 point. No stop, target, alternative fill,
overnight hold, session filter or source substitution. Fixed-spread
BID opens omit actual order execution and account floating loss.
