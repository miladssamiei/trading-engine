SPEC_SESSION_TRANSFER
Session-structure mechanism on US500: block-to-block return transfer
(hypothesis #11, project hypothesis numbering)

Status: SPEC written 2026-08-29 BEFORE any code or run. The companion
GATE_SESSION_TRANSFER (artifact list, section 9) must be created as a
verbatim extraction of every gate number in sections 6 and 7 BEFORE the
first Phase-1 script runs. Nothing in this document may be changed
after Phase-1 output exists; any addition is exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MECHANISM AND DISTINCTNESS

The signal is the relationship BETWEEN fixed NY time blocks in a day,
not patterns inside one block. Two arms:

  A. CARRY - a strong, volatile pre-open block (08:00-09:30) predicts
     same-direction movement in the cash block (09:30-16:00).
  B. FADE - a large post-close block (16:00-20:00) move that opposes
     the cash block predicts mean reversion over the overnight window
     (20:00 -> next 09:30) and, secondarily, in the next cash block.

Distinct from every prior hypothesis, explicitly:

  - ORB trades the FIRST MINUTES of the cash session against the rest
    of the day (opening-range breakout levels). Carry uses only the
    PRE-OPEN block return as predictor; no opening range, no breakout
    levels, no intra-cash structure is consulted for the signal.
  - VWAP MR and Bollinger MR trade INTRA-session deviation from a
    session mean or band. Neither block arm uses any intra-block mean,
    band, or VWAP.
  - trend-pullback / H1/H2 / FVG trade continuation after intra-session
    pullbacks marked by EMAs, leg counting, or gaps inside one session.
    Session transfer uses daily block OPEN-TO-CLOSE returns only.
  - SP2L trades a spike/gap candle structure AT the NY open with a
    pending-order mechanism. Carry instead aggregates the full
    08:00-09:30 return; no candle pattern, no pending orders, no gap
    threshold against the prior close.
  - multi-horizon momentum (daily and intraday) votes trend direction
    from overlapping trailing windows at one decision time. Session
    transfer compares non-overlapping adjacent blocks of one day.

If either arm passes, its daily P&L must be correlated against all
prior streams (section 8) to verify it is not another lens on the same
edge; a pearson near the continuation family (> +0.35) is a red flag
that the mechanism claim is false even if gates pass.

2. DATA AND TIME DEFINITIONS

Instrument: US500 index CFD, project symbol US500-tick (MT5 internal
US500.c for D1 only, section 5). All intraday work uses the verified
US500-tick exports; TimeCurrent is UTC for this symbol (proven in the
ORB work; NY offsets computed with generic US DST, never hardcoded).

Primary data: verified M5 export
data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv (364,330 bars,
2021-01..2026-04, byte-exact vs terminal in prior studies).
M15 is derived from M5 (floor(epoch/900)) only if ever needed; the
Bollinger-MR Step-0 finding applies verbatim: 121,486 M15 buckets, 107
partial (0.09%, kept as-is, tallied), and the verified M1 export must
NOT be used as an aggregation source (it skips no-tick minutes,
13,883 partial M15 buckets). This study needs only M5.

Timezone: epoch UTC converted to America/New_York (wall minutes =
hour*60 + minute of the bar OPEN). Same convention as ORB/VWAP/SP2L.

Trading calendar: a date is a CASH DAY if a bar with wall 570 (09:30)
exists on that NY date. Verified baseline: 1,370 cash dates
2021-01..2026-04. Holidays and evening-only dates are excluded
automatically (no 09:30 bar). No other calendar filter exists.

Session blocks (NY wall minutes, bar OPEN time):

  PRE   08:00-09:30   walls [480, 570)
  CASH  09:30-16:00   walls [570, 960)
  POST  16:00-20:00   walls [960, 1200)

Tape reality, documented (verified in prior work): no ticks exist
16:15-17:59 NY on any day (maintenance). The POST block therefore
contains the 16:00-16:14 bars, a structural 105-minute gap, and the
18:00-20:00 bars (which open the next trading day's Globex session but
carry this date's wall clock). The block is USED AS SPECIFIED; the gap
is inside the block return by construction and every statistic that
uses r_post inherits it. The final M5 bucket of each day (16:10-16:14)
is structurally partial; it is kept as the tape's close (Bollinger-MR
convention) and not "fixed".

Block open / close: open = first bar OPEN inside the block; close =
last bar CLOSE inside the block (the 09:25 bar close for PRE, the
15:55 bar close for CASH, the 19:55 bar close for POST).

Day eligibility per block:
  PRE  valid if bars at wall 480 and wall 565 both exist.
  CASH valid if bar at wall 570 exists (the cash-day definition).
  POST valid if bars at wall 960 and wall 1195 both exist.
An analysis that needs a block uses only days where that block (and,
for fade, the next day's CASH or PRE block) is valid; excluded days are
tallied in the funnel, never silently dropped. Cross-day fade signals
into a weekend (Friday POST -> Monday CASH) are INCLUDED and counted;
Phase 2 financing treats them per the swap convention below.

3. CORE DAILY VARIABLES (computed per cash day D)

  r_pre   = ln(P_close_pre  / P_open_pre)     signed log return
  r_cash  = ln(P_close_cash / P_open_cash)
  r_post  = ln(P_close_post / P_open_post)
  vol_pre  = sum of |ln(close_i/open_i)| over PRE M5 bars (simple
             realized volatility; the std of M5 log returns is computed
             and stored as a secondary column but NOT used in gates)
  vol_cash = same definition over CASH M5 bars
  hl_cash  = (max_high_cash - min_low_cash) / P_open_cash
  r_ovn    = ln(P_open_cash(D+1) / P_close_post(D))   overnight window
  All percent figures below are 100*exp(x)-1 or 100*x consistently;
  document the chosen form in the CSV header (use percent-of-price =
  100*(P_end/P_start - 1) for all reported MFE/MAE).

Thresholds are CAUSAL percentile rules: P80(x, 252) = the 80th
percentile of x over the TRAILING 252 valid days strictly before D
(no same-day or future data; the first 252 days of the tape can
produce no signals and are burn-in, consistent with the 2021 burn-in
convention).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HYPOTHESES (signal definitions, both pre-declared)

HYPOTHESIS A - CARRY
  Signal day D (evaluated at 09:30, the cash open):
    |r_pre| >= P80(|r_pre|, 252)  AND  vol_pre >= P80(vol_pre, 252)
  Direction: +1 if r_pre > 0, -1 if r_pre < 0 (r_pre = 0 impossible in
  practice; if it occurs, no signal).
  Position window (Phase 1, exit-free): from cash open (wall 570 bar
  open) to cash close (15:55 bar close), M5 path.
  Claim tested: same-direction transfer of pre-open imbalance into the
  cash session, net of range/volatility controls (section 5).

HYPOTHESIS B - FADE
  Signal day D (evaluated at 20:00, the post-block close):
    |r_post| >= P80(|r_post|, 252)  AND  sign(r_post) = -sign(r_cash)
  Direction: -sign(r_post).
  Primary window (Phase 1, exit-free): 20:00 bar close -> next cash
  open (09:30 bar open) on the next valid cash day, M5 path.
  Secondary window (informational only in Phase 1): the FULL next cash
  block, same direction.
  Claim tested: reversion of an outsized counter-cash drift after the
  close, over the overnight.

The two arms are independent tests; each is gated separately. Neither
shares machinery with ORB/VWAP/SP2L/trend-pullback beyond the tape,
calendar, cost, and bootstrap conventions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. FILTERS AND REGIMES

Applied IN Phase 1 (pre-declared, part of the signal itself):
  - The percentile conditions of section 4 (these ARE the filters).
  - Nothing else. No range-day filter, no max-move filter, no session
    slope. Adding any filter after seeing Phase-1 output is exploratory
    and cannot rescue a failed gate.

Pre-declared DESCRIPTIVE splits (computed and reported in Phase 1, but
NOT gates and NOT filters):
  - hl_cash split at its full-sample median: signals on below-median
    ("normal") vs above-median ("wide") days, per arm.
  - vol_cash split at its full-sample median, same treatment.
  - D1 regime label from US500.c (data/results/momentum_multihorizon/
    us500c_d1.csv): up = close > EMA(200d), down = close < EMA(200d),
    computed causally on completed D1 bars only. Signals split
    up/down/range are reported per arm.

Only if BOTH arms FAIL Phase 1: a single follow-on is permitted and
must be pre-declared before running it - regime-conditional carry
(carry signals only in the up/down D1 regime), one test, gates
unchanged. It may not be iterated.

6. PHASE 1 - SIGNAL QUALITY (no exits, no tuning)

Per arm, per signal day: direction-native MFE and MAE measured on the
M5 path over the arm's window, as percent of the entry price (carry:
entry = cash open; fade: entry = 20:00 close), SP2L E-XTM first-bar
convention (entry-bar close/extremes first, then running extremes over
the remaining bars of the window). Fade windows that span weekends use
the M5 bars that exist; the maintenance gap contributes nothing.

Window-scaled MFE/MAE thresholds (pre-declared; the standard
0.10/0.15/0.20% set is calibrated for 90-minute windows and is
degenerate at block scale, where both sides routinely exceed 0.20%):
  - CARRY (cash-block window): t in {0.25, 0.50, 0.75}%
  - FADE   (overnight window): t in {0.10, 0.20, 0.30}%
Rationale: cash-block median high-low is ~0.8-1.0% and the overnight
median range ~0.4-0.5% on this tape; the chosen sets sit at comparable
quantiles of their windows.

Statistics per arm: n, mean/median MFE, mean/median MAE, mean and
median of (MFE - MAE), count dominance at the arm's thresholds,
after-cost expectancy per signal (gross median favorable move minus
0.8 pt round trip expressed in percent of entry - descriptive only in
Phase 1), yearly mean diffs, funnel (eligible days -> block-valid days
-> percentile pass -> joint-condition pass).

Phase 1 GATES (per arm; all must hold for that arm to advance):

  G1  n >= 150 signal days AND n >= 5% of eligible days for the arm.
  G2  count(MFE >= t) > count(MAE >= t) at ALL arm thresholds AND
      mean(MFE - MAE) > 0.
  G3  median(MFE) - median(MAE) >= +0.05%.
  G4  Day bootstrap (10,000 resamples of per-day MFE-MAE, iid over
      days): P(mean <= 0) <= 0.40. Reported alongside and required as
      robustness: 10-day MOVING-BLOCK bootstrap P(mean <= 0) <= 0.45
      (adjacent-day correlation from regimes; slightly relaxed bound).
  G5  No single year contains > 40% of the arm's signal days AND the
      yearly mean diff is > 0 in >= 4 of 5 full years (2021-2025;
      2026 partial, reported only).

PASS = G1-G5 for at least one arm -> Phase 2 for that arm ONLY. Any
failure -> REPORT_SESSION_TRANSFER null write-up and STOP: no Phase 2,
no EA. Honest stop-and-report is a complete deliverable.

7. PHASE 2 - SIMPLE EXITS (only for a passing arm; all parameters
fixed here in advance; no search)

CARRY exit (one variant, no alternatives):
  Entry: market at the cash-open bar open (wall 570), direction
  sign(r_pre), 1 lot.
  Exit: forced time exit at the 15:55 bar open (project convention:
  VWAP MR / SP2L forced-exit timing).
  Stop: none (time-only variant is the pre-declared primary).
  The hl_cash-based stop variant (stop = cash open -/+ 0.75 x trailing
  20-day median of hl_cash, force-close at 15:55 unchanged) is run as
  a SINGLE pre-declared secondary variant, labeled; it cannot replace
  the primary unless the primary passes gates and the secondary passes
  the same gates with a HIGHER net Sharpe - one comparison, no grid.

FADE exit (one variant, no alternatives):
  Entry: market at the first bar open at/after 20:00, direction
  -sign(r_post), 1 lot.
  Exit: fixed time at the next cash-open bar open (wall 570), the
  overnight window end.
  The reversion-target variant (TP = entry +/- 0.5 x |P_close_post -
  P_close_cash|, SL = entry -/+ 1.0 x the same distance, whichever
  hits first, time exit unchanged) is the single pre-declared
  secondary variant under the same one-comparison rule.

Walk-forward: 2021 burn-in; folds 2022H1..2026H1 (the standard project
FOLD_BOUNDS); the rules are fixed so the folds are a stability check,
not a selection. Costs 0.8 pt round trip, $1/pt/lot, $10k base, no
compounding. Financing reported ex-financing headline; the documented
swap ambiguity (long -20 / short +2 $/day/lot vs strict MT5 points)
noted once; fade positions span exactly one overnight (weekend signals
span three calendar days; the swap convention's Wednesday x5 applies).

Phase 2 GATES (S1-S4, project standard):
  S1 net expectancy > 0 AND net Sharpe > 0 (daily P&L zero-filled over
     cash days, sqrt(252), BARS_PER_YEAR = 252).
  S2 >= 150 trades.
  S3 >= 4 of 5 full years net positive AND no year > 60% of total net.
  S4 net-of-0.8pt profitability (S1 restated as the explicit cost
     gate).
Any tuning after Phase-2 output = exploratory, labeled, cannot rescue.

8. RECONCILIATION AND INTEGRATION

Sharpe definition: exactly the pipeline standard - mean per-day P&L
over std (ddof=1) x sqrt(252) on the zero-filled cash-day series.
Correlations (pearson_zf: zero-filled union of days, >= 30 common
days) of the arm's daily P&L against every prior stream with one:
  ORB (data/results/orb_2021_present/py_trades.csv),
  SP2L spike-gap (data/results/sp2l_2021_2026/py_trades.csv),
  VWAP MR (data/results/vwap_2021_2026/py_days.csv),
  VWAP trend-pullback (data/results/vwap_trend_pullback_rr/wf_trades.csv),
  intraday momentum (data/results/momentum_intraday_rr/wf_trades.csv),
  daily momentum P (data/results/momentum_multihorizon/variant_p_daily_n5.csv).
  H1/H2, H2-morning and Bollinger MR have no P&L streams (stopped at
  Phase 1); stated in every report, not silently omitted.
Expected: near zero. |rho| > 0.35 vs any stream triggers a written
investigation of whether the "new mechanism" claim survives.

Integration, only if S-gates pass: EITHER a separate native EA with
its own position limits (Step-4 conventions verbatim: NormPrice
tick-grid rounding on every order and close, DST-aware session code,
Model=0 yearly batches, Python-vs-MT5 reconciliation at the
established tolerances, classify-don't-chase on tester-tape residual
differences), OR an input to a meta-coordinator EA that switches
strategy families by regime. The choice is made only after S-gates and
correlations are on the table; this SPEC does not pre-commit to either.
