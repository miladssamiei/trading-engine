0.50%: 79 vs 85; 0.75%: 57 vs 66; G2 fails).

Finding (reported, not traded): strong, volatile pre-open blocks do
NOT carry into the cash session on this tape - they REVERSE. The
reversal is uniform across D1 regimes (descriptive splits: down

single pre-declared follow-on (regime-conditional carry) is NOT

one permitted follow-on would be wasted; it stays unspent and this
report documents why. Trading the reversal instead ("reverse carry")
would be a post-hoc hypothesis swap - barred by the project's
honesty rules and by this SPEC's own stop conditions.

  it fails like the rest: the carry direction is actively wrong and
  the fade direction is real-but-too-small-and-too-rare (n=66, median
