GATE_SESSION_TRANSFER
Session transfer gates for Hypothesis 11, carry and fade arms

Status: written 2026-08-29 before any Phase 1 script. This file is a
verbatim extraction of all gates in sections 6 and 7 of
SPEC_SESSION_TRANSFER. Nothing in this file can be relaxed after output
exists. Any new gate is exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 GATES, PER ARM (carry and fade separately)

G1. Sample size and coverage
    n >= 150 signal days for that arm
    AND
    n >= 5 percent of the arm's eligible days.

G2. Count dominance and mean sign
    For all thresholds t in the arm's set
        carry: t in {0.25, 0.50, 0.75} percent
        fade:  t in {0.10, 0.20, 0.30} percent
    count(MFE >= t) > count(MAE >= t)
    AND
    mean(MFE minus MAE) > 0.

G3. Median gap
    median(MFE) minus median(MAE) >= +0.05 percent.

G4. Bootstrap robustness
    Day bootstrap with iid resampling over signal days,
    10,000 resamples of mean(MFE minus MAE):
        P(mean <= 0) <= 0.40
    AND
    moving block bootstrap with 10-day blocks:
        P(mean <= 0) <= 0.45.

G5. Year balance and consistency
    No single year contains more than 40 percent of that arm's signal
    days
    AND
    yearly mean(MFE minus MAE) > 0 in at least 4 of 5 full years
    2021 to 2025. 2026 is partial, reported only, not counted in the
    4-of-5 rule.

PHASE 1 PASS CONDITION FOR AN ARM
    All G1 to G5 hold for that arm.
    If at least one arm passes, that arm advances to Phase 2.
    If both arms fail, the project stops after REPORT_SESSION_TRANSFER
    and no Phase 2 or EA is allowed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 GATES, PER PASSING ARM

S1. Net expectancy and Sharpe sign
    Daily PnL zero filled over cash days:
        mean(PnL) > 0
    AND
        Sharpe > 0
    Sharpe defined as mean divided by standard deviation (ddof=1)
    times sqrt(252).

S2. Trade count
    Number of trades >= 150.

S3. Yearly profit shape
    At least 4 of 5 full years net positive
    AND
    no single year contributes more than 60 percent of total net
    profit.

S4. Cost adjusted profitability
    Net profitability after a fixed 0.8 point round trip cost per
    trade remains positive. This is S1 restated with explicit cost.

PHASE 2 PASS CONDITION FOR AN ARM
    All S1 to S4 hold for that arm under the primary exit variant.
    A single secondary exit variant may be run. It can only replace
    the primary if it also passes S1 to S4 and has higher net Sharpe.
    No other exit tuning is allowed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORRELATION RED FLAG

After Phase 2, for any passing arm:
    Compute pearson correlation of daily PnL versus each prior stream
    listed in section 8 of SPEC_SESSION_TRANSFER, using zero-filled
    union of days and at least 30 common days.
    If absolute correlation > 0.35 against any prior stream, the
    mechanism claim is flagged as suspect and a written investigation
    is required in REPORT_SESSION_TRANSFER.
    This is not a hard stop gate but a mandatory explanation gate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STOP AND HONESTY RULES

If Phase 1 fails for both arms, the project stops at
REPORT_SESSION_TRANSFER. No Phase 2 scripts may be written.
If Phase 2 fails S gates for a passing arm, that arm is rejected.
No rescue attempts or relaxed gates are allowed.
Any follow-on, such as regime-conditional carry, is labeled
exploratory and cannot change a failed verdict.

END OF GATE_SESSION_TRANSFER
