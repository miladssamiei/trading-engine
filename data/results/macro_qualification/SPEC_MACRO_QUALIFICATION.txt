SPEC_MACRO_QUALIFICATION.txt
Written 2026-09-06, BEFORE any qualification script was written or run.

0. WHAT THIS IS

Data-and-feasibility qualification of the XAUUSD Scheduled Macro-Surprise
mechanism (Rank 1 conditional lead of the 2026-09-06 deep-research triage,
data/results/project_status/INTRADAY_DEEP_RESEARCH_2026-09-06.md and its
source ledger report-source.md). NOT a strategy backtest: no trade rule,
no parameter search, no EA work, no MT5 tester runs, no signals. The
single question: can an event dataset of provable pre-release provenance
be joined to the verified XAUUSD tick tape, and does a strictly
POST-LATENCY directional move of at least 2x the measured cost floor
exist? This enforces the project's twice-confirmed rule that
contemporaneous event jumps are not executable trade expectancy. A
failure at any gate G-Q1..G-Q4 abandons the mechanism BEFORE any trading
rule is designed.

1. FROZEN UNIVERSE AND WINDOW (never widened after this document)

Event families (exactly three; headline series fixed):
- NFP: BLS Employment Situation, "total nonfarm payroll employment"
  change (thousands), FIRST release for the reference month.
- RETAIL: Census Advance Retail Sales, retail sales m/m percent,
  advance estimate.
- DURABLE: Census Advance Report on Durable Goods, manufacturers' new
  orders m/m percent, advance estimate.
No other headline inside a report (unemployment rate, ex-auto retail,
core capital goods, etc.), no revised or later-vintage re-releases, and
no other event families: CPI, FOMC, PCE, GDP, ISM and everything else
are BARRED substitutes even on failure.

Primary window: 2021-01-01 .. 2026-04-30 UTC (task-owner-frozen; note
for the record that the verified XAUUSD tape is longer, 2020-01-01
23:00 UTC .. 2026-09-01 20:59:59.911 UTC, per data/results/
xauusd_onboarding/REPORT.md). ONE pre-declared power fallback, declared
HERE and therefore not post-hoc: if and only if the G-Q3 count in the
primary window is < 150 usable events, the window extends once to the
full verified tape 2020-01-01 .. 2026-08-31 and G-Q3 is re-evaluated
once; still < 150 -> G-Q3 FAIL terminates. If used, the extension is
stated in the REPORT as a declared fallback, never as a rescue.

3. G-Q1 - EVENT PROVENANCE (verdict BEFORE any price is read)

Sourcing. Identify and fetch one PRIMARY machine-readable external
source carrying, per event: exact scheduled release timestamp, consensus
(forecast) value, and first actual value. Acceptable candidates
(highest preference first): a historical economic-calendar API with
per-event archived forecasts (e.g., Fin2Dev), a documented CSV archive
of the historical calendar (e.g., a pinned Kaggle Global Economic
Calendar dataset), FXStreet calendar exports, or another reputable
archive that is event-keyed and retrievable in bulk. One SECONDARY
independent source of the same kind is named for audit cross-checks
only. MT5's internal CalendarValueHistory is BARRED as a source: its
records carry no historical value-arrival timestamp usable as an event
clock and calendar functions are unavailable in the Strategy Tester
(MQL5 documentation, verified 2026-09-06). Verification anchors (not
consensus providers): BLS Employment Situation schedule and archived
press releases (bls.gov), Census retail and M3 advance-release
schedules and press releases (census.gov), and ALFRED vintages (PAYEMS,
RSAFS, DGORDER) for first-published actuals.

Provenance rules:
- Consensus must be the value archived FOR THAT EVENT (event-date
  keyed, as known before release) by the primary source; a consensus
  recomputed or restated later is unacceptable. The primary source's
  methodology/fields documentation is fetched and archived with the
  data.
- Actual must be the FIRST published value. If a source can only
  provide the revised value for an event, that event is EXCLUDED and
  counted in the exclusion table (never patched from memory).
- One primary source for ALL events. No mixing of providers per event.
  The primary source is frozen at G-Q1 completion; it may not be
  switched after any return has been computed.
- Every raw vendor payload is archived under this study's raw/ with
  retrieval date and content hash; nothing is re-fetched silently.

GATE G-Q1. PASS requires ALL of:
(a) primary source frozen and documented (name, access method,
    retrieval dates, hashes); secondary audit source named;
(b) field coverage >= 95% of the OFFICIAL schedule count of the three
    families in the window (schedule counts from the BLS/Census
    schedule pages, logged) with all three required fields present;
    every missing/excluded event listed with a reason code (delayed,
    shutdown-affected, missing consensus, revised-only, other);
(c) provenance audit: 12 stratified sampled events (4 per family, at
    least one per year of the window) checked against agency primary
    documents - scheduled date/time vs the agency schedule page, first
    actual vs the agency press release / ALFRED first vintage. Each
    discrepancy (wrong period, wrong unit, wrong value) removes that
    event from the dataset; more than 3 discrepant events of 12 ->
    G-Q1 FAIL (source untrustworthy; the mechanism dies here unless a
    DIFFERENT primary source is qualified in a NEW, separately
    documented pass BEFORE any price data is read - and only if the
    original failure was a sourcing failure, not a coverage failure of
    the universe itself);
(d) the archived dataset is complete and loadable (macro_event_dataset
    _raw.csv) with per-field provenance columns.
Any of (a)-(d) failing -> G-Q1 FAIL, STOP: complete deliverable.

4. G-Q2 - CLOCK SYNCHRONIZATION

Mechanics:
- Scheduled times are converted America/New_York -> UTC epoch with a
  DST-aware tz library (zoneinfo). Mechanical invariant checked for
  EVERY event: the three families release at 08:30 ET, so the UTC time
  must be 12:30 (EDT) or 13:30 (EST) consistent with that date's DST
  calendar. Violations are timestamp-suspect: isolated -> event
  EXCLUDED (logged); systematic (> 10% of events) -> G-Q2 FAIL.
- Officially rescheduled events (e.g., government-shutdown delays that
  moved releases off the usual pattern) are kept only if the primary
  source timestamp matches a verifiable agency notice or archived
  schedule; otherwise excluded.
- Join: T=0 is the scheduled release epoch. Entry tick for a buffer B
  in {30s, 90s} = FIRST tick with ts >= T+B; if that tick's ts > T+120s
  the event is EXCLUDED as non-quoting. Exit tick for horizon H in
  {300s, 900s} = LAST tick with ts <= T+H; if no tick exists in
  [T+H-60s, T+H] the event is EXCLUDED. Missing exact-second ticks are
  handled ONLY by this next-available-tick rule: no interpolation, no
  bar-derived prices, no backfill.
- Activity-burst sanity: for NFP events the tick rate in [T, T+5s) must
  exceed the [T-300s, T-60s) baseline by >= 5x; a missing burst flags
  the event for manual audit (and exclusion if unexplained). For RETAIL
  and DURABLE the burst check is informational only (weaker signature)
  and cannot exclude by itself.
- Manual spot-check (hard-fail rule): 6 events (2 per family) with
  printed tick windows (last 5 ticks before T, first 10 after T)
  eyeballed against the audit log. ANY timezone ambiguity, DST offset
  mismatch, or unexplained gap found -> G-Q2 HARD FAIL.

GATE G-Q2. PASS requires: zero unresolved timestamp-suspect events
beyond the logged isolated exclusions, the DST invariant holding for
every retained event, the 6 manual spot-checks clean, and every
retained event having valid entry/exit ticks under the rules above.
Anything else -> G-Q2 FAIL, STOP: complete deliverable.

5. G-Q3 - STATISTICAL POWER (counted BEFORE any return is computed)

Usable event = frozen-family event inside the window that passed G-Q1
field/provenance requirements and G-Q2 join rules, and is not a
simultaneous release. If two frozen families release at the same
timestamp, BOTH events are excluded (attribution ambiguity on a single
tape); such days are logged. Events with surprise exactly zero
(consensus == actual) are excluded from directional measurement and
counted separately.

GATE G-Q3. PASS requires: >= 150 usable events AND >= 150 unique NY
calendar dates carrying at least one usable event, in the primary
window (with the single pre-declared fallback extension of section 1
if needed). The count script prints the counts BEFORE computing any
price difference, and the counts are logged. Fewer than 150 after the
fallback -> G-Q3 FAIL, STOP: complete deliverable. No event family may
be added to raise the count.

6. G-Q4 - POST-LATENCY MEASUREMENT (only if G-Q1..G-Q3 all PASS)

Definitions (fixed here):
- Surprise s_i = first_actual - consensus, family-native units,
  standardized per family by the full-window StdDev of surprises over
  that family's usable events (a scale computation only; sign and any
  threshold are untouched by it).
- Direction d_i = -sign(standardized surprise): positive growth
  surprise -> SHORT gold, negative -> LONG (literature sign of Elder,
  Miao & Ramchander 2012; Smales & Yang 2015 for the timing caveat).
- mid = (bid+ask)/2 from the tick tape. GROSS directional move of a
  cell = d_i * (mid_exit - mid_entry), in $/oz.
- Cells: PRIMARY = entry T+30s -> exit T+300s. SECONDARY (reported,
  non-binding) = (T+30s -> T+900s), (T+90s -> T+300s), (T+90s ->
  T+900s). All four are computed and reported.
- No price movement from T=0 to the buffer end is EVER credited, in
  any cell, in any reported number that feeds a gate.

GATE G-Q4. PASS requires ALL of:
(i) PRIMARY-cell mean gross directional move across all usable events
    >= $0.82/oz (2.0x the $0.41 binding floor). If the entire
    published move occurs inside the un-tradeable 30-second jump
    window, the executable edge is zero and the hypothesis FAILS -
    explicitly the expected possible outcome per Smales-Yang (majority
    of the gold reaction complete within ~90 seconds);
(ii) family robustness: the PRIMARY-cell mean is positive in >= 2 of
    the 3 families AND the pooled mean remains > 0 after removing the
    single best family;
(iii) year robustness: no single calendar year contributes > 50% of
    the total positive PRIMARY-cell move.
Failure of any clause -> G-Q4 FAIL: the mechanism is abandoned.

Reported alongside, never binding: the T0 -> T+30s contemporaneous
jump per family (a tape-vs-literature sanity check - if the tape's own
total-window response is ~zero, the failure is recorded as decay
evidence); MAE/MFE of the directional position inside the primary
cell; a net-of-spread variant (long entries at ask, short entries at
bid, exits opposite) as execution context.

7. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK / STOP RULES

- A failure at any gate G-Q1..G-Q4 is a COMPLETE deliverable and
  terminates the hypothesis. Null qualification = valid result.
- No substituting other event families (CPI, FOMC, PCE, ...) if the
  frozen three fail or lack power. No new entry buffers (30s/90s
  fixed), no new horizons (300s/900s fixed), no surprise thresholds
  (all eligible events traded by sign), no re-denomination after
  numbers are seen.
- No measuring the jump from T=0 and claiming it as executable.
- Post-G-Q4 discovery of a provenance ERROR (fact, not choice):
  affected events removed, counts and gate verdicts restated ONCE,
  original and corrected verdicts both documented; no events may be
  added in this correction pass.
- If this qualification fails at any gate, the project accepts the
  2026-09-06 triage verdict: single-instrument CFD-tape intraday edges
  are exhausted; no 19th pattern will be invented to fill the gap; the
  remaining shortlist paths are the daily-horizon candidates of
  LITERATURE_EDGE_REVIEW.txt (gold-macro conditioned holds, index-pair
  relative value, VRP-conditioned exposure).
- No signals generated, no orders, no EA, no tester. A Phase-1
  strategy study is permitted ONLY on a four-gate PASS and requires
  its own pre-declared SPEC; nothing here authorizes one.
