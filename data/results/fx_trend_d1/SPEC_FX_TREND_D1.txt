SPEC: EURUSD D1 TREND FOLLOWING - PHASE 1 SIGNAL QUALITY SCREEN (HYPOTHESIS #42)
Declared: 2026-09-23, before any code for this study existed.

0. CONTEXT
  - First EURUSD D1 (daily-horizon) study; prior EURUSD work (#34/#35/#36/#38)
    was intraday. Classic Donchian-20 breakout with Wilder-ATR trailing stop
    (trend-following archetype at the daily horizon). Null result
    pre-accepted as a complete deliverable.

1. DATA
  1.1 Tape: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_d1.csv (1,748 D1 bars,
      2020-01-02 .. 2026-09-18, strict Forex (D-1 17:00, D 17:00] ET close
      convention, verified onboarding artifact). Sorted chronologically by
      date; pips convention 1 pip = 0.00010.

2. STATE MACHINE (CAUSAL INDICATORS)
  2.1 Donchian-20 at day t: max High and min Low over the STRICTLY PRIOR 20
      bars [t-20, t-1]; day t is never included (no lookahead).
  2.2 ATR: classic Wilder recursion. TR_i = max(H_i - L_i, |H_i - C_{i-1}|,
      |L_i - C_{i-1}|), TR_1 = H_1 - L_1; ATR_14 = mean(TR_1..TR_14);
      ATR_i = (13*ATR_{i-1} + TR_i)/14 for i > 14. ATR_i is the value
      through completed bar i (causal).
  2.3 Warmup: signals armed from bar index 21 onward (21-day warmup per
      tasking; covers the 20-bar Donchian and the 14-bar ATR seed).

3. SIGNAL GENERATION (MOMENTUM BREAKOUT)
  3.1 LONG: Close_t > DonchianHigh_t. SHORT: Close_t < DonchianLow_t.
  3.2 Execution cap: at most ONE open trade at a time; signals firing while
      a trade is open are ignored (counted in the funnel, no queueing);
      while flat, the first signal triggers entry at the Open of day t+1
      (no t+1 bar -> no-entry drop).
  3.3 Initial stop: LONG SL = entry_open - 2.0*ATR_t; SHORT SL =
      entry_open + 2.0*ATR_t, with ATR_t the signal day's completed-ATR
      (known before the t+1 open).

4. EXCURSION WALK AND RISK MANAGEMENT
  4.1 From entry day E (entry at open): at each walk day i's close, ratchet
      the stop - LONG: SL = max(prev SL, C_i - 2.0*ATR_i); SHORT:
      SL = min(prev SL, C_i + 2.0*ATR_i). The first ratchet happens at E's
      close on top of the initial stop.
  4.2 Exit check on the FOLLOWING day j: LONG exits if L_j <= SL at the SL
      price; SHORT exits if H_j >= SL at the SL price. HONEST-FILL RULE
      (documented deviation from the tasking letter): if day j OPENS beyond
      the stop (O_j < SL long / O_j > SL short), fill at O_j (gap-through),
      consistent with the #40 gold convention. Exit day flag STOP / STOP_GAP.
  4.3 EOD: a trade whose stop is never hit by data end is force-closed at
      the last bar's close (flag EOD).
  4.4 MFE/MAE in pips over walk days E..exit day inclusive (entry-day
      extremes included; the exit day's full bar included as the standard
      OHLC approximation - intraday exit timing is not observable from D1).
      Gross exit PnL pips = direction * (exit - entry) * 10000. Pre-cost;
      the verified 0.3-pip London/NY overlap median spread floor is
      reported alongside, never netted.

5. GATES (frozen verbatim in GATE_FX_TREND_D1.txt)
  G-FX1 total executed signals >= 30.
  G-FX2 aggregate mean MFE > mean MAE (strict, pips).
  G-FX3 day-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=5 trades, seed
      20260924) P(mean Gross PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
  Operationalization of G-FX3 (frozen here): the tasking's "day-block
  bootstrap, block=5 trades" is internally mixed; implemented as a
  moving-block bootstrap over the EXECUTED-TRADE sequence - draw
  k = ceil(n/5) blocks of 5 consecutive trades with replacement
  (resampled length k*5 >= n, not trimmed), pooled mean gross PnL per
  resample, P(mean <= 0) over 10,000 resamples, rng seed 20260924.
