HYPOTHESIS #47 SPEC: CROSS-ASSET MULTI-TIMEFRAME VOLATILITY SQUEEZE BREAKOUT (4h anchor, 15m execution)
Study ID: cross_asset_vol_squeeze
Date frozen: 2026-09-23, before any evaluation code was written or run.
Status: frozen. Corrections or re-measurements require a NEW study name.
Lineage: first cross-asset study of the program (US500 + EURUSD + XAUUSD). The
4h BB-inside-KC squeeze with a 15m Donchian trigger is a NEW pre-declared
mechanism (TTM-squeeze-style construction); it is not on the closed-family list
(Bollinger MR closed is a mean-reversion rule, not a squeeze breakout). EURUSD
D1 Donchian breakout (#42/#46) is closed; this is a different timeframe pair and
a different arming condition. Single pre-declared parameterization, no parameter
search, one run per instrument.

1. DATA
1.1 Tapes (verified M1, epoch-UTC time, columns time,open,high,low,close,
    tick_volume; sorted ascending defensively before aggregation):
    - US500 : data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv  (points; cost floor 0.51)
    - EURUSD: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (pips, 1 pip = 0.0001; cost floor 0.3)
    - XAUUSD: data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv (price units; cost floor 0.41)
1.2 Timezone America/New_York (EST/EDT) for all wall-time logic.
1.3 Cost floors are round-trip and subtracted once per trade from gross.

2. MULTI-TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION
2.1 15m bars: M1 epochs floored to 900s slots; open=first, high=max, low=min,
    close=last, volume=sum per slot. NY wall 15m boundaries align exactly with
    the epoch-900 grid because the NY UTC offset is always a whole number of
    hours.
2.2 Continuity: the 15m series is reindexed to the FULL continuous 900s grid
    from first to last slot; missing bars are forward-filled with OHLC = prior
    close, volume = 0 (synthetic flat bars). This fills no-tick minutes,
    overnight/maintenance breaks, holidays and weekends alike.
2.3 4h anchor bars: built FROM the continuous 15m grid by NY WALL-clock bucket
    [00:00-04:00), [04:00-08:00), ... (open=first, high=max, low=min,
    close=last per bucket). Wall bucketing means DST-transition anchor buckets
    span 3 or 5 real hours; buckets are contiguous by construction.
2.4 The two series are strictly aligned (4h built from the same continuous 15m
    grid).

3. STATE MACHINE (causal indicators)
3.1 For a 15m bar t, all 15m inputs use bars <= t-1; the 4h input is the most
    recently completed 4h bar relative to bar t's open: the wall bucket
    strictly before the wall bucket containing t. Its indicators include that
    4h bar's own close, which is known at its end, hence at t's open.
3.2 4h indicators (on close c, high h, low l):
    - SMA20 and population stddev (ddof=0) -> Bollinger BB = SMA20 +/- 2.0*std20.
    - EMA20 (span=20, adjust=False) and Wilder ATR(14) -> Keltner
      KC = EMA20 +/- 1.5*ATR14. Wilder: TR=max(H-L,|H-cPrev|,|L-cPrev|),
      ATR(13)=mean(TR 0..13), ATR(i)=(ATR(i-1)*13+TR(i))/14.
    - Squeeze ON at 4h bar k iff BB_upper(k) < KC_upper(k) AND
      BB_lower(k) > KC_lower(k).
    - Linear-regression slope (20): least-squares slope of the last 20 4h
      closes vs x=0..19 (closed-form weights; slope is exactly 0.0 on a
      constant window, so fully-flat windows arm neither side).
3.3 15m indicators: Donchian(20) strictly prior: dch(t)=max High over
    [t-20, t-1]; dcl(t)=min Low over [t-20, t-1]. Wilder ATR(14) on 15m
    (same recursion as 3.2).
3.4 Armed: 15m index >= 20 AND dch, dcl, prior ATR(14 at t-1) non-NaN AND the
    mapped 4h bar index >= 19 (BB/EMA/LR warmup) AND the mapped 4h squeeze
    state is defined.
3.5 CONSEQUENCE OF 2.2, declared honestly: synthetic flat bars feed the
    indicators. Flat windows make Donchian breakouts impossible (close equals
    channel) and set LR slope to exactly 0, but Wilder ATR decays across long
    gaps (weekends), so early post-gap signals carry mechanically tightened
    stops. This is accepted as part of the declared continuity convention; no
    bar-skipping or ATR-freezing fix is applied.

4. SIGNALS AND EXECUTION
4.1 Long signal at 15m bar t: 4h squeeze ON, 4h LR slope > 0, AND
    Close(t-1) > dch(t). Short: squeeze ON, slope < 0, AND Close(t-1) < dcl(t).
4.2 Execution cap: max ONE open trade per instrument; signals while a trade is
    open are counted (ignored-while-open) and discarded.
4.3 Entry: Open price of the signal bar t. A trade occupies bars
    [entry_bar, exit_bar] inclusive; the next actionable signal can only fire
    at exit_bar + 1 or later.

5. EXCURSION WALK AND TRAILING-STOP EXIT
5.1 Initial risk unit: ATRsig = 15m Wilder ATR(14) at bar t-1 (strictly
    prior). Initial SL = Entry - 2.0*ATRsig (long) / Entry + 2.0*ATRsig
    (short).
5.2 Walk 15m bars j = entry_bar, entry_bar+1, ... in order (long case; short
    mirrored):
    a) Open(j) <= SL -> exit at Open(j), reason SL_GAP (honest fill);
    b) Low(j)  <= SL -> exit at SL, reason SL (worst-case default on same-bar
       dual conditions);
    c) at the close of j, ratchet: SL = max(SL, max Close over
       [entry_bar..j] - 2.5*ATR15(j)), ATR15(j) known at that close; if
       ATR15(j) is NaN the ratchet is skipped (SL unchanged).
5.3 If no stop is hit by the final 15m bar, exit at that bar's close, reason
    FORCED_EOD, included in results.
5.4 Per-trade record includes MFE/MAE excursions over [entry_bar..exit_bar]:
    long MFE = max High - Entry, MAE = Entry - min Low (short mirrored),
    in instrument units, descriptive only.

6. ECONOMICS
6.1 Gross = side*(Exit - Entry) in instrument units: points for US500 and
    XAUUSD, pips (x10,000) for EURUSD. Net = Gross - cost floor (0.51 / 0.3 /
    0.41 respectively), one round trip per trade.
6.2 Net Sharpe (per-trade convention): mean(Net)/std(Net, ddof=1) * sqrt(252)
    over executed trades of the instrument.

7. PHASE 1 GATES (applied INDEPENDENTLY to each instrument; frozen verbatim in
   GATE_CROSS_SQZ.txt)
    G-SQ1: Total executed signals >= 30.
    G-SQ2: Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
    G-SQ3: Moving-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=5 trades,
           seed 20260926) P(mean Net PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
    VERDICT RULE: an instrument PASSES only if G-SQ1 AND G-SQ2 AND G-SQ3 all
    PASS. OVERALL verdict PASS only if ALL THREE instruments PASS. Any FAIL is
    final for this artifact; corrections require a new study name. Null
    results are complete, expected deliverables.

8. OUTPUTS (data/results/cross_asset_vol_squeeze/)
    SPEC_CROSS_SQZ.txt (this file), GATE_CROSS_SQZ.txt (written before the
    run), REPORT_CROSS_SQZ_PHASE1.txt (funnel, net expectancy, win rate,
    excursion asymmetry, per-instrument gates), cross_sqz_signals.csv (all
    instruments, asset column), run_meta.json (sha256 lineage of tapes and
    script, funnel, gates, verdict).

9. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
9.1 All indicator inputs strictly prior (15m) or completed (4h) as defined in
    section 3; entries only at 15m bar opens; no intrabar lookahead; gap fills
    at the open, worst-case same-bar stop default.
9.2 Single frozen parameterization; no optimization, no re-runs, no
    post-hoc filtering; sides and instruments reported separately and pooled.
9.3 Phase 2 (MT5 execution) only on overall PASS; in-sample character of this
    screen is acknowledged; MFE/MAE reconstructions are exploratory
    descriptors, never results.

ERRATUM 2026-09-23 (pre-verdict, appended; sections above left as originally frozen).
The first execution of the Phase-1 script returned ZERO signal candidates on all three
instruments. Root cause: sections 3.3/4.1 as originally worded define dch(t) = max High
over [t-20, t-1] and require Close(t-1) > dch(t); since High(t-1) >= Close(t-1), the long
trigger is unsatisfiable by definition (short mirrored). This is a degenerate spec clause,
not a market null. Corrected causal reading, matching the declared intent (signal state
completed at bar t-1, channel strictly prior to the compared close):
  dch(t) = max High over [t-21, t-2]; dcl(t) = min Low over [t-21, t-2];
  Long  : Close(t-1) > dch(t); Short: Close(t-1) < dcl(t); armed from 15m index >= 21.
All other clauses, parameters, gates and the verdict rule are unchanged. No results
existed at correction time (0 trades); the single corrected run is the run of record.
