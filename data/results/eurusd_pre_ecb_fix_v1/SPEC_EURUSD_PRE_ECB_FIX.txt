1. EXTERNAL MECHANISM AND RULE
Krohn et al., Foreign Exchange Fixings and Returns around the Clock,
Journal of Finance 2024, https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13306,
Table I reports an average EURUSD return of -3.53 basis points
from 02:00 New York to the ECB fixing at 14:15 Frankfurt in
1999-2019 interdealer data. Ex ante rule: SELL EURUSD at 02:00
America/New_York and cover at 14:15 Europe/Berlin on each weekday.
The ECB fixing time is localized with Europe/Berlin, including
weeks when U.S. and European DST switch dates differ. This is a
separate published pre-fix mechanism from Hypothesis 67's failed
post-London-fix drift. #67 remains final. Selecting another paper
leg after #67 entails multiple-testing risk; a historical economic
PASS is not yet a verified live-tradable system.

3. QUOTE EXECUTION PROXY AND COST
First valid quote at or after each target within 10 seconds inclusive.
Valid: finite positive Ask and Bid, Ask >= Bid. Count invalid quotes
in each sampled window, exclude only those rows; skip/log a date
when either target has no valid quote. No order replacement, stop,
target, side flip, spread filter, or holding-time adjustment.
Short at entry Bid; cover at exit Ask. Midpoint gross pips =
(entryMid-exitMid)/0.00010. Executable quote net pips =
(entryBid-exitAsk)/0.00010. Actual Bid/Ask spread is charged;
extra-cost stress net pips = quote net - 0.3 to buffer execution
friction beyond the user's stated EURUSD spread floor. No swap
expected in this intraday window. First-quote fills still omit
rejection, queueing, live latency, and market impact.
