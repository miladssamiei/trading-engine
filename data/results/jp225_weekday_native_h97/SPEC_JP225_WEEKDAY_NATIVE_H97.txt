plus seven hours (the 17:00 NY boundary); require prior completed
Thursday or Friday trading day before Monday 00:00 entry. No synthetic
daily bars, no price forward-fill. ATR20 is Wilder TR=max(high-low,
abs(high-prev close),abs(low-prev close)), initial 20-day arithmetic
mean and subsequent (19*prior+TR)/20. Use only the completed day before
Monday; require finite positive ATR20.
Each eligible NY Monday, buy at first native M1 first-tick ASK in
[00:00,00:05). Initial hard stop=entry ASK - 2*prior ATR20.
From the entry minute onward, if real M1 BID open<=stop fill at the
worse BID open; otherwise if real M1 BID low<=stop fill at the stop.
One trade per week; no short, target, trailing, reentry or overlap.
On a normal week, unstopped exit at first native M1 first-tick BID
Thursday NY [00:00,00:15). If that Thursday is Jan1, Jul4, Dec25 or
fourth Thursday of November, unstopped exit at first native M1 BID

If no required exit quote, mark UNRESOLVED with no PnL. Exclude the
incomplete final tape week. Flat before Friday on every resolved trade.
