SCOPE AND HISTORICAL LABEL RESOLUTION
The requested "#52/#53 15m SMC order-block" engine does not exist as one
frozen strategy. H52 is a 15m Bollinger limit pullback with a 4h trend anchor;
H53 is a 1h prior-day range sweep and inversion FVG with a D1 anchor. Neither
defines an order-block threshold. To preserve exact lineage without inventing
a rule, Engine D is reported as two separate, unselected rows: D52 (the 15m
timeframe match) and D53 (the sweep mechanism match). Neither is called 15m
SMC. The three engines run independently per asset; no row is selected for
the verdict or optimized after observation.

ENGINE C - H56 EXACT GOLD-NATIVE PARAMETER TRANSFER
Use the frozen H56 three-candle pattern on completed M1 bars: P-gap > 1.0
price unit, spike-body ratio > 1.5. A single pending order updates the maximum
one consecutive opposite move rule, pullback trigger and risk on later M1
bars; invalidate risk > 10 price units; require close versus EMA60, Wilder
ADX14 >= 20, and NY entry open time [01:00,05:00). Pending expires at NY date
end. Entry executes at the trigger bar's own low (long) or high (short), as
H56's known optimistic M1 extreme fill. Structural stop is pre-spike low/high;
1:1 TP uses H56's asymmetric spread-aware bracket (long TP = ask entry +
initial risk on bid; short TP = bid entry - initial risk on ask). Same-bar SL
before TP; subsequent stops/targets gap at M1 open. No other filter or
timeframe substitution, including on NQ and ES.

ENGINE D52 - H52 15M BOLLINGER LIMIT PULLBACK
Build continuous epoch 15m OHLC by fieldwise forward fill; build NY wall 4h
bars from that grid. Use H52's frozen prior completed 4h RSI14/Supertrend
(10,3)/ADX14 gates and prior 15m ADX14 >20; limit at prior BB20 +/- 2

bracket: stop 6x prior 15m Wilder ATR14 from fill, target prior opposite BB
band. No changes to H52 indicators, level definitions or fixed brackets.

ENGINE D53 - H53 1H SWEEP / INVERSION FVG
Build continuous epoch 1h OHLC by fieldwise forward fill; build D1 [17:00,
17:00) NY anchors. Track most recent bullish/bearish three-candle 1h FVG
and each current session's prior-day-high/low sweeps and extremes exactly as
H53. Enter next real 1h bar at first M1 open after prior close-through of
the opposing FVG. Short stop = swept PDH extreme, target = prior-day low;
long stop = swept PDL extreme, target = prior-day high. Static brackets and
no signal recency or wall-hour filters. Synthetic 1h bars update indicators
and sweep state but cannot execute an entry.

M1 EXECUTION AND PROP GUARD
For D52/D53, scan M1 from the first executable minute. A standing stop has
priority over target when both touch the same M1 bar. On later M1 bars,
adverse stop gaps and favorable target gaps fill at the M1 open. On a limit
entry minute, the touch must precede exit; if both bracket sides touch,
score the stop first. This is an OHLC proxy, with unknown intraminute order.
For each position, initial risk is the positive distance from reference entry
to initial structural stop. Peak_Open_Equity starts at zero net PnL and
updates with favorable M1 high/low marked net of the round-turn floor.
Liquidation floor = peak - 2.5 * initial risk, fixed for that position.
After bracket checks on each M1 bar, if M1 close net open equity <= floor,
exit at that M1 close as PROP_LIQUIDATION. Shadow all new signals and setups
until exit UTC epoch + 86400 inclusive. Signals while occupied are discarded;
next actionable higher-timeframe signal is strictly after the exit bar.
Engines and assets are independent. This per-position price-unit overlay is
not an account-wide Apex or Topstep dollar threshold. A real firm threshold
persists across trades and may fail the account at breach; this diagnostic
cooldown is the owner-requested counterfactual.

STATS AND GATES
Each of nine asset-engine rows is gated on its full tape: executed trades >=
30, event-indexed per-trade Net Sharpe = mean / sample std(ddof=1) > 0, and
moving-block bootstrap P(mean net <= 0) <= 0.20. Bootstrap: 10,000 resamples,
five-trade contiguous blocks with replacement, seed 20260926. If fewer than
five trades or undefined Sharpe, gate FAIL. Overall PASS only if all nine
rows PASS. No optimization, ablation, winner-picking or refitting. CAL/OOS
and yearly views are diagnostics only. A FAIL is final for this artifact;
a PASS remains an in-sample proxy, not a confirmed live result.
