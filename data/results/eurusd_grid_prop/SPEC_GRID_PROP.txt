Purpose
Test the owner's proposed small sizing, minus1% bailout, basket flatten and restart policy on the existing EURUSD volume/EMA grid. Preserve the entire original eurusd_grid experiment and frozen evaluator. Null or failed controls are final for this artifact. This is a bounded Phase 1 design; a failed candidate cannot be adopted as a prop-ready strategy. No live orders are authorized or sent by this research script.

Source and base signals
Reuse the verified EURUSD BID M1 source and the original evaluator's continuous UTC H1 grid and causal EMA5/three-rising-hour-volume signals. Record both evaluator and source hashes. Real M1 quotes drive all risk checks; only the first real M1 minute of an H1 bucket can submit one new position. Basket target is still net>30 base-size pip equivalents at a real completed H1 close, not an intraminute profit target. Single directional basket, same-direction 30-pip adverse spacing, opposite signals ignored. A cap blocks further additions without inventing a cap-triggered close or changing existing positions.

Volume, depth and margin
Ideal lot plan at depth d is base_lots*1.5^(d-1); each actual order is rounded DOWN to the0.01 step. Preserve the ideal geometric plan rather than compounding previously rounded volumes into a0.01-lot fixed-size deadlock. Minimum0.01 is already met by all candidate bases. Maximum5 positions, maximum0.30 total lots, and estimated margin <=15% of current equity. EUR margin estimate =100000*total_lots/30. Reject any candidate addition failing one cap, leaving current basket managed by the guards. No increase to rescue a losing basket or override a cap is permitted.

Equity guards and loss accounting
Every real M1 OPEN is checked before any new order; after an entry/addition, check risk including its immediate cost; then check that minute's adverse LOW for longs/HIGH for shorts. Basket bailout at floating net <=EUR-100. Daily personal floor is balance at midnight Europe/Prague minusEUR100; includes existing floating loss, new costs and swap, not a freshEUR100 allowance per basket. Internal total buffer floorEUR9,100 is static; its trigger permanently stops that simulated account. Also monitor official-model daily floor =Prague-midnight balance minusEUR500 and static firm floorEUR9,000. A single actual firm-model breach fails safety even if an internal guard simultaneously closes the basket. Equality is treated conservatively as a trigger. Account profit is not a license to ignore theEUR100 basket cap.

Intrabar execution and gaps
All current basket PnL is monotone in EURUSD BID for a fixed inventory. Solve the BID price at the tightest of basket, daily-personal and total-buffer equity floors. If the adverse M1 OPEN is already past the floor, liquidate at that OPEN (overshoot allowed and recorded). Otherwise, if the adverse M1 extreme touches the floor, liquidate at the solved boundary as an idealized virtual stop. Stop checks always precede any H1 profit exit in that minute. Record bailout cause, adverse gap, basket loss, day loss and residual overshoot. Do not record the later full M1 adverse extreme as held exposure after liquidation. This idealized floor fill excludes latency, variable spread and slippage; it does not guarantee a live1% cap. All positions flatten as one basket at the same modeled BID.

Cooldown and daily latch
After ANY basket/daily bailout, no order may open for the remainder of that Europe/Prague calendar day. NEXT_DAY permits a fresh completed H1 signal after the next Prague midnight. 24H/72H also require24/72 elapsed UTC hours from bailout, whichever is later than next Prague midnight. Real market quotes and a new eligible H1 bucket are required; no stale signal is replayed after unlocking. Restart from the base lot and empty basket. Profit exits do not impose a cooldown. A total-buffer halt is permanent for that simulated account, without resetting losses or automatically purchasing a replacement account.

Swaps enter basket net target, loss guards, marks and eventual realized PnL. No extra commission beyond the all-inclusive floor. Log net accrued swap separately.
