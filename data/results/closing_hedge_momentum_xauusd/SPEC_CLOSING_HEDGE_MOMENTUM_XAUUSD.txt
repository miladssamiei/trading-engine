HYPOTHESIS 60: XAUUSD COMEX-CLOSE HEDGING MOMENTUM
Declared 2026-09-25 before gold strategy code or any gold Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND SOURCE
Baltussen, Da, Lammers, and Martens, Journal of Financial Economics 142
(2021) 377-403, https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf , report
that the prior close to final-half-hour move predicts the last-half-hour
move across futures. Their gold COMEX GC trading window is 08:20-13:30
New York time and their individual GC result is positive in data ending
2020. Close-related hedging or rebalancing is the proposed mechanism.
This tests transfer to the broker's XAUUSD CFD in 2020-2026, not an
exact GC futures replication. The US500 transfer test in hypothesis 59
has already failed; its result supplies no gold parameter or direction
choice. This gold rule uses the gold hours printed in the paper.

2. DATA AND CLOCK
Use verified Bid OHLC M5 at data/results/xauusd_onboarding/
xauusdtick_m5.csv. Convert epoch UTC to America/New_York. A gold
session date requires an exact printed 08:20 bar. To trade, require
exact printed 12:55, 13:00, 13:05, 13:10, 13:15, 13:20, and 13:25
bars and the immediately preceding gold session date's exact 13:25
close. Do not substitute an older date if the prior session's close
is missing. Missing or early-close days are skipped and counted.

3. CAUSAL RULE AND COST
At 13:00, compare the just-completed 12:55 Bid close with the prior
gold session's 13:25 Bid close. Higher means LONG, lower means SHORT,
ties mean no trade. Enter one lot at the printed 13:00 Bid open and
exit at the 13:25 Bid close, flat before 13:30. One trade per date;
no stop, target, filter, re-entry, overnight position, or swap.
Gross = direction * (exit Bid - entry Bid) in gold price units.
Base net = gross - 0.41 (the project's verified full-tape median CFD
spread); double-cost stress net = gross - 0.82. Bid M5 prices are
bar proxies, not verified executable fills; variable actual spreads,
slippage, and market impact are not modeled. Report gross and both
net measures, win share, drawdown, side and year breakdowns.

4. PARTITION AND INFERENCE
Use 2020-01-01 through 2023-12-31 as diagnostic calibration and
2024-01-01 through the tape end (2026-09-01) as the frozen test block.
New York dates determine year. The 2026 year is partial. There is no
training, tuning, or subset search. This is a fixed historical block,
not virgin data: other project studies exposed the gold tape.
Resample test-block session dates with replacement 10,000 times,
seed 20260926, to estimate P(mean base net <= 0); one trade/date
means each date is a block. Report descriptive bootstrap 95% quantiles.

5. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-GH1: At least 150 executed trades in the 2024-2026 test block.
G-GH2: Test-block mean net gold units under double 0.82-unit cost is strictly greater than zero.
G-GH3: Test-block bootstrap P(mean base net <= 0) is at most 0.05, using 10,000 date-block resamples and seed 20260926.
G-GH4: Each complete test-block calendar year, 2024 and 2025, has strictly positive total base net gold units.
Overall Phase-1 PASS requires all four gates. FAIL is final for this
artifact. No direction subset, price-move threshold, alternate close
window, or different cost can rescue a FAIL without a new SPEC.

6. STOP AND HONESTY
No MT5 tester run unless this Phase 1 passes; a PASS would require a
new execution SPEC/GATE and Model=4 tick validation before any system
can be called a walk-forward-confirmed result. Offline positive mean
alone is not deployability. Freeze this SPEC and GATE before writing
gold Phase-1 code. Always write a REPORT, including for a null result.
