HYPOTHESIS 74: WEEKEND PAXG DIRECTION -> SUNDAY XAUUSD INTRADAY CONTINUATION
Declared 2026-09-25 before data acquisition code, evaluation code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND LIMITING RESEARCH
PAX Gold trades through the traditional gold weekend closure and can
incorporate news before XAUUSD reopens. Cong, Mayer and Rabetti (2026),
https://wifpr.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/WIFPR-Tokenizing-Real-World-Assets-Cong-Mayer-and-Rabetti.pdf,
describe off-hours token price discovery and reopening convergence.
Castello (2026), https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7174537,
reports only weak weekend-token prediction of gold's reopening move in
a short February-June 2026 sample. Neither paper establishes tradable
post-reopen continuation after CFD costs. This study tests the simple,
distinct implication that the weekend token move predicts the first
liquid Sunday-evening XAUUSD intraday return after the CFD has reopened.
The null is expected and is a complete deliverable.

2. PUBLIC SOURCE AND CAUSAL SIGNAL
Download Binance's public SPOT PAXGUSDT monthly klines at interval 1h
for every available month from 2020-08 through 2026-08 inclusive from
https://data.binance.vision/data/spot/monthly/klines/PAXGUSDT/1h/.
Keep each original zip and a SHA256 manifest in this new study
directory. The Binance public-data schema provides bar-open epoch
timestamps, close, quote-asset volume, and trade count. Epochs in
2025+ may be microseconds; earlier epochs milliseconds. Reject bad
timestamps or duplicate hourly opens. A NY weekend signal needs two
completed 1h bars with positive traded volume and trade count:
Friday [16:00,17:00) America/New_York and following Sunday
[16:00,17:00) America/New_York. Both are known at Sunday 17:00 NY,
before the XAUUSD entry order. BUY if Sunday PAXG close is strictly
above Friday close, SELL if strictly below, zero-change skip. No
premium level, magnitude threshold, holiday/event, year, or side
filter. USDT risk/basis noise is retained as a source limitation.

3. XAUUSD SAME-NY-DATE BID-BAR PHASE-1 EXECUTION PROXY
Use the verified native-gold BID M1 export at data/results/
xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv, SHA256
a8c4c1d6551d27f3796f8d9b88d332172c41c76c151844e759492837152e7b1f.
Interpret its epoch seconds as UTC. For every eligible Sunday, require
REAL M1 bars timestamped exactly 18:30 and 23:30 NY on that Sunday.
Enter at the 18:30 BID bar open and exit at the 23:30 BID bar open.
This is a five-hour same-New-York-date hold. Long uses entry Ask
proxy = entry BID+0.41 and exit BID; short uses entry BID and exit
Ask proxy = exit BID+0.41, so net gold units equal signed BID
open-to-open move minus 0.41. An extra 0.41 gold unit per trade is
charged as execution stress. One trade per Sunday, no other entries,
stop, target or alternate exit. A missing endpoint is logged and
excluded, never forward-filled or replaced by a later favorable bar.
This proxy ignores variable Sunday spread, slippage and floating
losses; any Phase-1 PASS permits only new native Bid/Ask and prop-risk
studies, not execution or FTMO qualification.
