VWAP MEAN REVERSION - EURUSD PHASE 1 SIGNAL-QUALITY SCREEN
Hypothesis #35 (owner-tasked, 2026-09-22). Phase-1 MFE/MAE excursion screen
ONLY: no trade rule beyond first-touch excursions, no EA, no tester, no
Phase 2 implied by any outcome.

1. TASKING AND LINEAGE
- EURUSD transplant of the VWAP mean-reversion family per task-owner
  instruction. The family is a frozen null on US500-tick (vwap_rr_verdict:
  walk-forward 273 trades, expectancy -$0.075, Sharpe -0.048; 2025-2026
  negative under every configuration). EURUSD is a new instrument with a
  different session anchor (08:00 ET London/NY overlap open vs the US500
  studies' 09:30 cash open), percentage bands scaled to FX, and a new cost
  floor. This is a NEW pre-declared artifact, NOT a re-litigation of the
  US500 verdicts. Null result pre-accepted as a complete deliverable.

2. DATA
- Tape: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (verified
  onboarding 2026-09-22: 2,490,896 M1 bars, 2020-01-01 17:01 NY ..
  2026-09-18 16:59 NY, Bid OHLC + tick_volume, epoch-UTC open times,
  no-tick buckets skipped).
- Timezone: America/New_York (DST-aware), computed from epoch UTC.
- Trading day = NY calendar date. Session = M1 bars of that NY date with
  open wall minute in [08:00, 16:00) ET (wall in [480, 960)). Session open
  price = open of the session's first printed bar (wall >= 480).

3. STATE MACHINE (PER NY DATE, SESSION BARS IN ORDER)
- VWAP: TP = (High + Low + Close) / 3; VWAP[t] = cumsum(TP * tick_volume) /
  cumsum(tick_volume) over session bars from the 08:00 anchor; cumulative
  sums reset at the session's first bar. VWAP keeps updating through the
  walk bars (state machine never pauses inside the session).
- 30-minute slope: slope[t] = VWAP[t] - VWAP[ref(t)] where ref(t) = the
  LAST session bar with open time <= t_open - 1800 s. If no such bar
  exists (sparse morning), slope is UNDEFINED and no signal can fire at t.
  (A bar strictly after 08:30 always satisfies the 30-minutes-elapsed
  condition relative to the 08:00 anchor; the reference-bar rule handles
  sparse tapes.)
- Deviation bands: upper = VWAP * 1.0025, lower = VWAP * 0.9975 (0.25%).
- Session range: rolling Max High / Min Low over session bars so far
  (inclusive of the current bar).
- Max-move (trending-day) filter: if (rolling Max High - Min Low) >
  0.0040 * session open price, the session is INVALIDATED for mean
  reversion from that bar onward (signal generation only; already-open
  walks are unaffected). Strictly greater-than; evaluated sequentially
  and inclusively at each bar.

4. SIGNAL GENERATION
- Signal window: M1 bars with open wall strictly inside (08:30, 15:30) ET
  (510 < wall < 930), slope defined, max-move filter not yet triggered
  (range through the signal bar inclusive <= 0.40%).
- SHORT (revert down to VWAP): Close > upper band AND slope <= 0.
- LONG (revert up to VWAP): Close < lower band AND slope >= 0.
- First-touch cap: only the FIRST valid signal per direction per NY date
  (max one SHORT + one LONG per day; later candidates of an already-fired
  direction are counted in the funnel and dropped).
- Entry: the OPEN of the next printed M1 bar after the signal bar, if it
  has the same NY date and open wall < 960; otherwise the signal is a
  no-entry (dropped, counted).

5. EXCURSION WALK (PRE-COST)
- From the entry bar (entry price = its open) over session bars (same NY
  date, wall < 960), in order, until the FIRST of:
  TARGET: VWAP touched - SHORT: bar Low <= VWAP[bar]; LONG: bar High >=
          VWAP[bar] (inclusive; the entry bar itself can be the target).
  TIME:   session end (last session bar with wall < 960; 16:00 ET close).
- MFE = (max High - entry) for LONG / (entry - min Low) for SHORT;
  MAE = (max High - entry) for SHORT / (entry - min Low) for LONG; over
  the walk bars in pips (1 pip = 0.00010). The target bar counts toward
  both excursions.
- Descriptive cost context ONLY (Phase 1 stays gross): the verified EURUSD
  London/NY overlap median spread is 0.3 pips (onboarding 2026-09-22);
  mean/median MFE, MAE and MFE-MAE are compared against this floor in the
  report. No net gate exists at Phase 1.

6. GATES (FROZEN IN GATE_VWAP_MR_EURUSD.TXT BEFORE ANY CODE EXISTS)
G-VW1   total session-valid signals >= 150.
G-VW2   aggregate mean MFE > mean MAE (strict, pips).
G-VW3   day-block bootstrap: resample NY dates with replacement (10,000
        resamples, numpy.random.default_rng(20260921)); statistic = pooled
        mean of (MFE - MAE) in pips over resampled signals;
        P(statistic <= 0) <= 0.20.
OVERALL PASS requires all three. Any FAIL is final for this artifact.

7. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
- All state is causal: VWAP, bands, rolling range and filter status at bar
  t use session bars up to and including t only; entries use only bars
  after the signal bar; the walk uses the evolving VWAP strictly forward.
- No parameter search: band width, slope span, filters, caps, thresholds,
  seed are frozen in this SPEC; nothing tuned after seeing results.
- Null pre-accepted; US500 VWAP-MR nulls documented as lineage context.
- Phase-1 excursions are signal-quality measurements, not tradable
  results (no spread/swap/slippage modeling beyond the descriptive floor
  comparison; no walk-forward claim).
