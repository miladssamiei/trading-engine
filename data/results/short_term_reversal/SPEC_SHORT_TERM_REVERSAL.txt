SPEC_SHORT_TERM_REVERSAL
Study dir: data/results/short_term_reversal/
Pre-declared: 2026-09-08, BEFORE any script exists.
Lineage: third leg of the daily-horizon program. Tests whether the
anti-prediction signature found by the daily directional model
(REPORT_DAILY_DIRECTIONAL_MODEL.txt: all 8 CPCV configs negative OOS IC,
lgbm t-stats -6..-10) reflects genuine short-term mean-reversion structure
that a model trained on a DISJOINT macro era would learn and carry forward.
This is NOT a sign-flip of the prior study: predictions are evaluated
one-sided positive here; a negative-IC outcome is a FAILURE, not an edge.

SECTION 0. SCOPE AND TERMINAL STAKES

0.1 Hypothesis: extreme macro/intermarket deviations (the same 5 orthogonal
features) combined with short-horizon price exhaustion predict 1-3 day
mean reversion in US500, with structure learned on 2015-2019 that
generalizes across the 2020 structural break to 2021-2026.

0.2 This is the FINAL strategy-research artifact on the US500/XAUUSD CFD
tapes. If G-R1 or G-R2 fails, the report writes "TRANSITION TO PATH C:
RESEARCH EXHAUSTED" and the project ceases pattern/ML strategy research on
these tapes. No further SPEC may be proposed for them.

SECTION 1. DATA (frozen sources)

1.1 US500 prices, full span: data/results/momentum_multihorizon/
us500c_d1.csv (broker US500.c D1 export, verified 2014-06-23..2026-08,
epoch UTC, OHLC). ONE price series for the entire study - no seam between
eras. Daily close P_t = the D1 bar close. Informational reconciliation on
the 2021-2026 overlap vs the tick-tape cash closes: report median and 95th
percentile of |close difference| (different feeds; economics never touch
the tick tape in this study).

1.2 USA30 leg (for ffd_log_spdj): broker USA30.c D1 export fetched at
script stage via the MT5 API into this study dir (usa30c_d1.csv), required
2014-06..2026-04. PRE-DECLARED CONTINGENCY: if the broker D1 history does
not cover 2015-01-01, ffd_log_spdj is EXCLUDED from this study (4 features
remain; recorded in the report; not a failure, not a substitution).

1.3 XAUUSD: no XAUUSD-level feature exists in the frozen 5-feature set;
XAUUSD series are not part of this study (the "US500/XAUUSD tapes" in the
Path C clause refers to the project's research program, not this
artifact's inputs).

1.4 FRED: VIXCLS, T10Y2Y, DFII10 re-downloaded 2014-01-01..2026-08-31
(fredgraph, archived raw/ with provenance). Same availability rules as
SPEC 1.3 of the feature pipeline: VIX same-day; T10Y2Y/DFII10 published s
usable s+1; forward-fill <= 5 rows with publication-date tracking;
current-vintage limitation accepted once, documented.

1.5 Calendar: US500.c D1 trading dates. Features/targets indexed on them.

SECTION 2. TEMPORAL ISOLATION (frozen)

2.1 Train window: 2015-01-01..2019-12-31. Effective start = first date
ALL surviving features are non-NaN (with l=252 FFD warmup from the
2014-06-23 tape start, FFD features begin ~mid-2015; the dropped head
rows are reported). Estimated ~1,100 usable rows.

2.2 Gap: 2020-01-01..2020-12-31 dropped entirely. The COVID shock year
is a structural embargo. NOTHING (no statistic, no scaling, no d*, no
imputation) may read 2020 rows.

2.3 Test window (strict OOS): 2021-01-01..2026-04-30. Hard end.

2.4 Every parameter - FFD d*, standardization mean/std, hyperparameters,
sigma for target standardization (train-side), any quantile - is fit on
train rows only and applied frozen to test rows.

SECTION 3. FEATURES (same definitions, re-calibrated to train)

3.1 The 5 features of SPEC_DAILY_FEATURE_ENGINEERING section 3, rebuilt
on the US500.c D1 calendar (and USA30.c D1 for the ratio if available):
ffd_log_us500, ffd_log_spdj, vrp, tslope_d21, dfii10_d21. Formulas, l=252,
21-row differences, VR21 prior-returns, FRED shifts: identical.

3.2 FFD d* search: grid 0.05..0.95 step 0.05, ADF (constant, AIC lag)
statistic < -2.86, smallest passing d, computed EXCLUSIVELY on train
rows (post-warmup); d* locked, applied unchanged to the test window.
Full grid diagnostics to fracdiff_reversal_d.json. No passing d ->
feature excluded (recorded; no substitution).

SECTION 4. MODELS (trees barred)

Model A - ElasticNet (regression on y_{t,h}): train-fold
standardization; hyperparameters alpha x l1_ratio on the SAME grid as
SPEC_DAILY_DIRECTIONAL_MODEL section 2; internal sklearn
TimeSeriesSplit(n_splits=5) over train rows only; selection metric =
mean validation-fold Spearman IC; refit on full train.

Model B - RidgeClassifier (on sign of the raw forward log return):
alpha in {0.1, 1.0, 10.0, 100.0, 1000.0}; the SAME TimeSeriesSplit(5)
procedure with selection by mean validation-fold Spearman IC of
decision_function; features standardized with train statistics.

Global seed 42. Exactly these two estimators; no trees, no ensembles,
no per-horizon feature selection.

SECTION 5. TARGETS AND EVALUATION

5.1 y_{t,h} = ln(P_{t+h}/P_t) / (sigma_t * sqrt(h)), sigma_t = trailing
20-return std (ddof=1, includes r_t) on US500.c D1 closes, h in {1,2,3};
trailing rows lacking P_{t+h} dropped per horizon. Classifier label =
sign(ln(P_{t+h}/P_t)).

5.2 Evaluation: predictions on test rows only; Spearman IC per
(model, horizon) = 6 configurations.

SECTION 6. GATES (verbatim-extracted into GATE_SHORT_TERM_REVERSAL.txt)

G-R1 (OOS IC FLOOR): on the 2021-01-01..2026-04-30 test window, the
Spearman IC between predictions and y_{t,h} must be >= 0.025 for at
least one (model, horizon) of the 6 configurations. One-sided positive:
negative or near-zero IC is a failure. If no configuration passes,
the study FAILS at G-R1 and G-R2 is not evaluated.

G-R2 (NET ECONOMIC VIABILITY): among G-R1 passers, the configuration
with the highest test IC is selected (selection by IC only, BEFORE any
economics are computed). Signal s_t = sign(prediction) (+1/-1/0 at
exactly zero); NON-OVERLAPPING trades t0, t0+h, ... from the first test
row; gross = s_t * (P_{t+h} - P_t) in D1-close points; costs = 0.51 pt
round-trip spread + financing at the documented convention (swap_long
-20 pt/night, swap_short +2 pt/night, Wednesday-night starts x5, on
calendar nights of the actual hold); annualized net Sharpe =
mean/std(ddof=1) * sqrt(252/h) must be > 0 on the test window. Gross
Sharpe, total costs, and per-year net points are reported alongside.

SECTION 7. HONESTY AND TERMINAL STOP RULES

7.1 No sign inversion. If the best test IC is strongly NEGATIVE, that is
a FAILURE (the anti-prediction signature did not invert into a tradable
edge under temporal isolation). Flipping signs after viewing test
results is the forbidden post-hoc rescue, and this pre-declaration
closes that door explicitly.

7.2 Failure at G-R1 or G-R2 -> the script writes into the report:
"TRANSITION TO PATH C: RESEARCH EXHAUSTED" - the absolute termination
of strategy research on the US500/XAUUSD CFD tapes; no further pattern
or ML SPEC may be proposed for them.

7.3 No re-runs with different windows, grids, costs, seeds, or feature
subsets after any test-window metric is viewed. A gate failure is final.

7.4 INFEASIBLE (distinct from Path C): only if US500.c D1 data cannot
support the study (missing/short tape). Feature-level contingencies
(3.2 exclusions, missing USA30.c) are recorded and the study proceeds
with the surviving features.
