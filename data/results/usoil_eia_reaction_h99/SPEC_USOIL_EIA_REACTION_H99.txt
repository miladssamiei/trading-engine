[11:00,11:01), and [15:00,15:01); otherwise mark week unresolved.

Bid tick at/after 10:30. Positive -> buy first Ask tick at/after 11:00;
negative -> sell first Bid tick at/after 11:00; zero -> no trade. The
10:30-11:00 observed Bid high minus low defines range. Require range>0.
Long stop = entry Ask minus that range; short stop = entry Bid plus range.

Bid<=stop and short on first Ask>=stop at that real executable quote,
