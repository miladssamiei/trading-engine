HYPOTHESIS 64: DELAYED GOLD RESPONSE TO U.S. GROWTH SURPRISES
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND LIMIT
A positive surprise in U.S. employment or consumption can raise
expected rates and the dollar, pressuring gold; a negative surprise
can do the reverse. Prior research reports a negative gold-futures
response to positive nonfarm-payroll and advance-retail-sales
surprises in the first five minutes. This study asks whether the
same signed information has an executable residual return AFTER
one minute, not whether the untradeable initial jump exists.
JOLTS and ADP employment are predeclared labor-demand analogues to
reach adequate event count; their predictive strength is unproven.
The failed Hypothesis 63 price-reaction-follow result stays failed:
this signal uses an external actual-minus-forecast, not price.

2. HISTORICAL EVENT SOURCE AND HONESTY LIMIT
Use only data/results/xauusd_macro_reaction_follow_v1/
fred-us-macro-events-source.json, sha256
b9d8a2256ecc61fda3f68efbc243b407db5b19b0f2288c44f827d03854861201.
It is the public FRED US Macro Core Data release archive, enriched
after the fact with Investing.com historical consensus records.
The event source has release-date fields but NO verifiable captured-
before-release timestamp for each forecast. Therefore even a price
economics PASS is PROVISIONAL and may not be called a validated or
deployable result. A later source-vintage qualification would need
archived pre-release forecasts and actual receipt/publication times.
Never use FRED's currently revised observation value as actual;
use only metadata.consensus.actual and .forecast in the archived
event record. Both must be finite numeric values. Do not use the
stored surprise value or later return data to choose events.

3. FROZEN EVENT UNIVERSE
Four U.S. release families: PAYEMS Employment Situation at 08:30
America/New_York; RSAFS advance retail sales at 08:30; ADPMNUSNERSA
ADP employment at 08:15; JTSJOL job openings at 10:00. Accept an
event only when its seriesId matches, releaseDateApproximate is false,
createdAt UTC converts to that exact NY wall release minute, and
actual and forecast are finite and unequal. Actual-minus-forecast
positive means SELL XAUUSD; negative means BUY. No surprise-size
threshold or model fitting. At most one trade per NY date: choose
the earliest eligible event by release instant. If two eligible
families share that earliest instant, skip that date entirely to
avoid attributing a joint release to one sign. Later same-day
events are not trades. Calendar dates are provisional third-party
roster entries; an economics PASS would require official schedule
reconciliation before execution confirmation.

4. TICK EXECUTION AND COST
Use the verified raw XAUUSD timestamp, askPrice, bidPrice CSVs
listed in data/results/xauusd_onboarding/onboarding_stats.json.
For each event, enter at the first valid quote timestamp from
release+60 seconds through release+65 seconds inclusive. BUY at
Ask or SELL at Bid. Exit at the first valid quote from release+
15 minutes through release+15 minutes+10 seconds inclusive: long
exits Bid, short exits Ask. Skip and log missing or invalid quotes.
No stop, target, order retry, size filter, or overnight holding.
Gross midpoint-reference move = direction*(exit mid - entry mid).
Executable quote net = exitBid-entryAsk for long; entryBid-exitAsk
for short. Extra-cost stress net = quote net - 0.41 gold unit on
the SAME trades, as a buffer for additional slippage/latency.
Actual Bid/Ask spreads are already charged by executable quote net.
One price unit equals $1/oz; no compounding or swap. Quote fills
at the first recorded tick remain optimistic about order latency,
market impact, and rejection.

5. PARTITION AND UNCERTAINTY
2020-2021 NY event dates are descriptive calibration. The gated
historical block is 2022-01-01 through the event archive end in
April 2026, with 2026 partial. Before quote-validity exclusions,
the frozen filter yields 184 distinct 2022+ dates, including two
simultaneous earliest-release ambiguities to skip. Report roster,
all skips, entry/exit spread, gross/quote/stress economics, annual
and family breakdowns, win rate, max drawdown, and hold duration.
Resample gated entry dates with replacement 10,000 times, seed
20260930, retaining all trades from each date; report one-sided
P(mean executable quote net <= 0) and descriptive 95% quantiles.

6. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-MSD1: At least 150 executed trades on distinct 2022-2026 NY event dates.
G-MSD2: Strictly positive mean extra-cost stress net per trade in the gated block.
G-MSD3: Date-bootstrap P(mean executable quote net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10,000 resamples, seed 20260930.
G-MSD4: Strictly positive executable quote-net total in each complete gated year 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025.
All four are required for an economic-proxy PASS. A FAIL is final
for this artifact. No post-hoc family/side/year/threshold/window
selection is permitted. A PASS remains PROVISIONAL because the
forecast vintage and actual receipt timestamps are not proven.

7. STOP AND ARTIFACTS
On economic-proxy FAIL, stop with no MT5 tester. On PASS, first
obtain independently archived before-release forecasts, first
actual/publication timestamps, and official release-date checks;
then freeze a separate source-qualification SPEC/GATE. Only after
that can a separately frozen Model=4 execution test be considered.
Save SPEC, verbatim GATE, exact source hash, event roster, quote
trades, metadata, and REPORT regardless of result. Never alter
prior SPEC/GATE/REPORT documents.
