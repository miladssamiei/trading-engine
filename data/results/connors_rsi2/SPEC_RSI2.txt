CONNORS RSI(2) PLUS BOLLINGER BAND INDICES PHASE 1
Frozen 2026-09-28 before evaluate/connors_rsi2_indices.py exists or any Phase 1 execution.

Question: Does one fixed RSI(2) and Bollinger mean-reversion rule survive US500 and USTECH retail cost floors on the supplied retrospective BID M1 proxies? This tests the specified mechanical interpretation, not a claim that it reproduces every Larry Connors discretionary rule. A null result is a complete deliverable.

Inputs: US500 data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv (2021-2026); USTECH data/results/virgin_assets/ustech_m1.csv (2020-2026). Times are UTC epoch seconds converted to America/New_York for session eligibility. Input times must be unique, sorted M1 opens with finite well-formed positive OHLC. USTECH's entire tick_volume column is a zero placeholder from onboarding, so it cannot mark synthetic candles; every recorded USTECH OHLC row is treated as an observed vendor M1 bar. US500 recorded rows are treated the same way. Missing UTC M1 minutes have no executable quote.

Build epoch-aligned UTC M15 and H1 grids by observed M1 OHLC aggregation, then fieldwise forward-fill missing whole buckets. Missing buckets may affect indicators and indexing but cannot issue an M15 signal or provide an M1 entry/exit. M15 RSI(2) is Wilder's average gain/loss seeded with the mean of the first two changes; a flat/flat seed gives RSI=50, gain-only RSI=100 and loss-only RSI=0. M15 Bollinger middle is SMA20 and bands are SMA20 +/- 2.0 times population rolling standard deviation (ddof=0), with 20 bars required. M15 ATR14 is Wilder true-range average seeded from the first 14 continuous bars. H1 EMA200 uses recursive alpha=2/201 from the first H1 close, hidden until 200 continuous H1 bars exist. Signals require finite indicators and positive ATR.

At the end of each real M15 bar, use its completed close for RSI2 and bands and the last H1 bar completed at or before that M15 close. H1 close > EMA200 permits only long; H1 close < EMA200 permits only short; equality permits neither. Long signal requires RSI2<10 and M15 close<=lower band. Short requires RSI2>90 and close>=upper band. The completed M15 signal time must be in New York wall-clock [03:00,16:00); DST is handled by America/New_York. No current incomplete H1 value is used.

The entry must be the observed M1 open with timestamp exactly equal to the signal M15 end, not a later open. Skip signals without that exact M1 quote. At most one position per asset. Any signal whose scheduled entry open occurs during an existing trade, including its exit M1, is shadowed. Long entry fills at BID open+cost; short entry at BID open. Cost is fixed ASK-BID 0.55 US500 points or 1.66 USTECH points, embedded once through entry and exit quotes. No separate cost subtraction in realized fills. Long target is the signal M15 SMA20 in BID coordinates; short target is that SMA20 in ASK coordinates. Reject targets that are nonpositive net rewards after spread. Long stop BID=entry ASK-1.5*signal M15 ATR14; short stop ASK=entry BID+1.5*ATR14. Reject a nonpositive fill-to-stop risk. These targets and stops are static. Long stops check M1 BID low; short stops check M1 ASK high. Long targets check M1 BID high; short targets check M1 ASK low. Same-M1 stop/target collision takes the adverse stop. Stops gap adversely to the current M1 open; favorable target gaps fill at the better M1 open. Brackets check the entry M1 as well, with adverse stop priority. The time stop occurs at the first observed M1 open at or after entry timestamp+120 minutes; at that bar's open, the position closes before its high/low can trigger a bracket. A market closure may delay this exit beyond 120 wall-clock minutes, and such cases are counted. Any position still open at end of tape is marked OPEN_END and excluded from closed-trade gate statistics. No session exit beyond this time rule; no swap or slippage is charged.

Metrics for each asset: number of closed trades, win rate by net PnL, TP/SL/TIME counts, gross mean using BID entry/exit, net mean from executable fill-to-fill PnL, unannualized per-trade net mean/std(ddof=1), moving-block bootstrap fraction of resampled net means<=0 using exactly 10,000 draws, block length 5 and seed 20261010. Ordered trade outcomes form the bootstrap series. Fewer than five closed trades give undefined p and fail; fewer than two or zero variance give undefined Sharpe and fail. Save annual entry-cohort counts/totals, full ledgers, input hashes and result metadata. Report both assets even if either fails.

GATES (verbatim copied into GATE_RSI2.txt):
G1: US500 and USTECH each require at least 150 closed trades.
G2: US500 and USTECH each require net expectancy > 0 after their fixed cost floors.
G3: US500 and USTECH each require unannualized per-trade net Sharpe > 0.
G4: US500 and USTECH each require MBB P(mean net <= 0) <= 0.05 using 10000 resamples, block length 5 and seed 20261010.
G5: The overall Phase 1 screen passes only if both assets pass G1 through G4; a pass is eligible for separately predeclared execution/forward validation, not validated execution.

No post-result threshold tuning, alternate RSI definitions, cost relaxation, asset deletion, or select-only-best reporting. Frozen files must not be overwritten; corrections require a new named study. BID-only OHLC lacks true ASK path, variable spread, limit fill probability and within-minute ordering. The proxy histories have been used in earlier project research, so favorable retrospective outcomes are exploratory.
