3. PATTERN DETECTION (3-CANDLE EXPANSION / IMBALANCE), ON COMPLETED BARS

Let t be the live forming M1 candle; detection is evaluated at the close of
completed candle t-1 (the detection bar d = t-1), using only completed bars.
Pattern bars are t-4 (pre-spike), t-3 (spike), t-2 (post-spike). body(x) =
abs(close(x) - open(x)).

BUY setup at d (all required):
  B1  close > open on each of t-4, t-3, t-2 (three consecutive bullish)
  B2  strictly higher closes across t-4 -> t-3 -> t-2
  B3  strictly higher opens across t-4 -> t-3 -> t-2
  B4  P-gap imbalance: low(t-2) > high(t-4) + 1.0
  B5  spike body: body(t-3) > 1.5 * body(t-2) AND body(t-3) > 1.5 * body(t-4)
  Valid -> register pending BUY. Structural SL = low(t-4).

SELL setup is the exact mirror (bearish candles, strictly lower opens/closes,
high(t-2) < low(t-4) - 1.0, same spike-body clauses). Valid -> pending SELL.
Structural SL = high(t-4).

If a BUY and a SELL setup fire at the same detection bar, NEITHER is taken
(source: buy and not sell).

Declared deviations from the source bot's live detection: the source's
pullback-break condition on the forming candle (its buy0/sell0) and its spike
ratio clause against the forming candle are NOT part of detection here; in
this spec the pullback is exclusively the entry trigger of Section 4. This is
the #56 mechanism as pre-declared by the owner.

4. PENDING ORDER VALIDATION AND ENTRY

A pending is a state machine on bars strictly after the detection bar d, on
the same NY calendar date as d (a pending EXPIRES at its NY date's last bar;
documented deviation from the source's indefinite overnight lifetime, adopted
from the parent US500 port, and outcome-equivalent for entries because the
session window of Section 5 closes at 05:00 ET inside every NY date).

Single pending at a time; no new setup detection while a pending exists or a
trade is open. Setups on bars <= a pending's outcome bar (entry exit bar,
INVALID bar, or date end) are shadowed; the machine resumes strictly after.

On each bar u > d, in this order:
  E1  Trend structure (all bars d+1..u inclusive, updated every bar): for
      BUY, a bar with high <= prior bar's high is an opposite move; the run
      of consecutive opposite moves must not exceed 1. Mirror for SELL
      (low >= prior low). Once a run of 2 occurs the pending can never enter
      (the window only grows) - it lingers to its date end (tallied
      trend-dead). This is the source MAX_OPPOSITE_MOVES=1 rule.
  E2  Entry trigger / pullback: BUY low(u) < low(u-1); SELL high(u) >
      high(u-1). No trigger -> pending persists.
  E3  Trigger_Price = the breaking bar's own extreme (BUY low(u); SELL
      high(u)) - the level the source bot records as its entry.
      risk = Trigger_Price - SL (BUY) / SL - Trigger_Price (SELL).
      risk <= 0 -> pending persists (not reached deep enough).
  E4  risk > 10.0 -> pending KILLED (source INVALID), occupancy ends at u.
  E5  EMA filter: BUY close(u) > EMA60(u); SELL close(u) < EMA60(u); else
      persists.
  E6  ADX filter: Wilder ADX14(u) >= 20.0 (finite), else persists.
  E7  Session filter: entry bar's open-time wall minute in [01:00, 05:00)
      ET (60 <= minute-of-day < 300). Else persists. Bars at/after 05:00 ET
      are not evaluated further (entry impossible that date); the pending
      expires at its date end.
  E8  All pass -> EXECUTE at bar u. Pending clears.

All indicator values are strictly causal, computed as continuous full-tape
recursions (EMA span 60; source-exact Wilder ADX: TR/DM ewm alpha=1/14, DX,
ewm alpha=1/14) evaluated on completed bars. The source recomputes indicators
over its last 10,000 bars per poll; the continuous recursion is the
deterministic closed-bar equivalent (same as the parent port).

5. FILTER / SESSION CONFIGURATION (ALL ENABLED, NO ABLATION)

EMA60 direction, ADX14 >= 20, trend-structure max 1 opposite move, NY session
[01:00, 05:00) ET - all ENABLED for every entry, exactly as Section 4. One
configuration, one pass; no sensitivity sweep is part of this screen (the
fixed 0.41 spread is the pre-declared cost model; it is applied inside the
execution mechanics of Section 6, which is the entirety of the
spread-sensitivity treatment for #56 Phase 1).

6. SPREAD-AWARE EXECUTION AND EXITS

LONG: entry executes at Ask = Trigger_Price + 0.41.
  SL triggers on Bid at the structural level: SL_Bid = SL.
  TP triggers on Bid at TP_Bid = Ask_Entry + 1.0 * risk.
  (Consequences, declared: TP gain = +risk; SL loss = -(risk + 0.41), the
  spread is paid adversely at the structural stop.)
SHORT: entry executes at Bid = Trigger_Price.
  SL triggers on Ask >= SL, i.e. on Bid >= SL - 0.41.
  TP triggers on Ask <= Bid_Entry - 1.0 * risk, i.e. on
  Bid <= Bid_Entry - risk - 0.41.
  (Consequences, declared: both TP gain and SL loss = risk exactly.)
These bracket geometries are the #56 spec verbatim, including its long/short
asymmetry, and are implemented literally.

Exit walk starts ON the entry bar (after the fill at the bar's extreme) and
continues bar by bar until resolution; worst-case same-bar priority: SL is
checked before TP on every bar, including the entry bar. On the entry bar
fills are at the exact trigger levels (the entry is at the bar's extreme; a
BUY cannot touch its SL on the entry bar since risk > 0). On later bars,
honest gap fills: a stop that the bar opens beyond fills at the open
(adverse), a target the bar opens beyond fills at the open (favorable), else
at the level. A trade still open at the tape's end closes at the last bar
close (exit type END; bid for longs, ask = close + 0.41 for shorts). No
time-based, session-based, or trailing exit exists: brackets only.

Max 1 open trade at a time; no new setups while a trade is open. Position
size fixed at 1.0 lot; no compounding.

7. STATISTICS AND GATES

Population: all executed trades of the single machine pass, chronological.
Net PnL per trade in price units as defined in Section 6 (the 0.41 spread is
embedded in the fills; no separate cost line). Win = Net PnL > 0. Profit
factor = sum(wins) / abs(sum(losses)). Net Sharpe = mean(trade Net PnL) /
std(trade Net PnL, ddof=1), event-indexed per trade, NOT annualized (the
convention of #47-#55). Moving-block bootstrap: with n trades, ceil(n/5)
blocks per resample, block start drawn uniformly from {0..n-5}, blocks
concatenated and truncated to n; 10,000 resamples; seed 20261004;
P = fraction of resample mean Net PnL <= 0. Gates: GATE_SP2L_M1.txt.
Reported alongside (not gated): funnel tallies, direction split, exit mix,
same-bar-exit share, holding bars, median risk, per-year table.
