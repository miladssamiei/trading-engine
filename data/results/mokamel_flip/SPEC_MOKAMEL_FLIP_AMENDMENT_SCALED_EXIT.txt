SPEC AMENDMENT 1 — MOKAMEL FLIP SCALED-EXIT ARMS (US500-NATIVE ADOPTION)
=========================================================================
Hypothesis #16, amendment to SPEC_MOKAMEL_FLIP.txt / GATE_MOKAMEL_FLIP.txt
(frozen 2026-09-03). Written 2026-09-03 AFTER Stage B, BEFORE any scaled
Stage-B re-run. The original SPEC and GATE files are historical records
and are NOT modified by this amendment; this file references them by
name and adds exactly what its title says — nothing else reopens.

MOTIVATION (the flaw being addressed)
The literal 40/80-point binding arm was calibrated for Dow Jones'
price level (the presenter's instrument; roughly 7-9x US500's level
over 2021-2026, per the task brief that commissioned this amendment).
Stage B showed the direct symptom: 78% of trades (1,059/1,355) exit
at the 15:55 forced exit, only 26 ever reach the 80-pt target, and
the day-close rule never fires. This amendment derives a properly
scaled stop/target pair from FIXED, EXTERNAL, NON-PERFORMANCE-BASED
anchors and tests it under the identical Stage-B protocol.

DOW DATA SOURCING DECISION (open question resolved 2026-09-03)
No Dow Jones / US30 / DJI data exists in this project: the MT5
terminal bases hold only EURUSD, GBPUSD, US500.c, USDCHF, USDJPY,
XAUUSD (broker) and US500-tick / USA500_Tick (custom); the data tree
contains no US30/DJI series (config.py / ExportM1Data.mq5 mentions
are legacy plumbing around a symbol that was never exported). Task
owner decision: ADOPT THE NUMBERS FOR US500 — derive the scaled
exits from US500's own validated data plus the public price-level
knowledge already stated in the commissioning brief. No Dow series
is sourced; no Dow tick/bar data is fabricated. Rigor flag: the
price-level anchor is task-brief-stated public knowledge, which is a
LOWER-RIGOR anchor than this project's usual measured-and-reconciled
data; the volatility anchor is US500-native and fully measured. Both
are declared here before any scaled run.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
- Stage A (signal quality, GATE A1 PASS) is untouched and is NOT
  re-run; it does not depend on stop/target values.
- The original binding 40/80 arm's Stage B result stands as recorded
  (complete, valid, null-leaning: the literal video numbers largely
  fail to engage US500's actual range).
- The unit ring {20/40, 30/60, 50/100, 60/120} remains non-binding,
  non-promotable context. It is NOT the source of the new values: the
  derived 5-pt stop lies OUTSIDE the ring's value space (ring stops
  are 20-60 pt). No ring cell is promoted or informed by this
  amendment. The Stage-B ring numbers were acknowledged only as the
  SYMPTOM motivating the amendment (as pre-stated in the brief), not
  as a selection criterion.

THE TWO DERIVATION METHODS (both pre-registered, neither chosen by
performance; both computed and reported even though they converge)

METHOD 1 — PRICE-LEVEL RATIO (public-anchor variant):
Dow/US500 price ratio anchor R_price = 8.0, the MIDPOINT of the
7-9x band stated in the commissioning brief (the least-informative
choice inside the stated bounds; no Dow series is used).
  stop_M1 = 40 / R_price = 5.00 pt
  target_M1 = 80 / R_price = 10.00 pt
Untested, non-binding sensitivity context only: the 7x and 9x band
edges give 5.7 -> 5.5 and 4.4 -> 4.5 pt stops under the rounding rule
below; they are NOT run as arms (no third method, no band mining).

METHOD 2 — VOLATILITY ANCHOR (US500-native ATR):
  stop_M2 = 1.0 x median(ATR14(M5) at trigger candles, Stage-A
           binding population) = 1.0 x 4.782 = 4.782 pt
  target_M2 = 2 x rounded stop (shape rule below) = 10.00 pt
The 1.0x multiple is the canonical "one typical five-minute bar of
movement" volatility stop, fixed here BEFORE any scaled run; the
4.782 median is a volatility statistic (not a performance statistic)
already cited in the commissioning brief. Method 2 is the US500-native
replacement of the brief's Dow-ATR-ratio method, which is unavailable
without Dow bar data (stated plainly, not substituted silently).

ROUNDING RULE (both methods): round the STOP to the nearest 0.5 pt,
then set TARGET = 2 x rounded stop (preserves the video's 1:2 shape
exactly). stop_M1 5.00 -> 5.0; stop_M2 4.782 -> 5.0 (nearest 0.5).
CONVERGENCE: both methods land on STOP 5.0 pt / TARGET 10.0 pt.
The two arms are therefore numerically identical; they are still
reported as two separately-labeled arms (their derivation chains are
independent evidence, and convergence is itself the evidence the
brief asked for). No tie-break, no choice to make.

TESTING PROTOCOL (identical to the original Stage B in every respect
except the stop/target distances)
- Population: the SPEC 6.1 signal set (3,224 eligible).
- Money machine: SPEC section 5 verbatim (aggressive/conservative
  I-6 mode logic, Q3 day-close 3-total/3-total, I-10 neutrality,
  occupancy, filter exits).
- Exits: M1-tape walk, first-crossing, SL-first same-bar ambiguity,
  gap fills at open, 15:55-bar-open forced exit, no overnight;
  validation invariants asserted per trade.
- Sizing: lots = $100 risk / stop = 100 / 5.0 = 20.0 lots on the
  fixed $10,000 base, no compounding (I-11 generalized).
- Costs: 0.51 pt binding per round trip, 0.80 pt informational.
- Window: full 2021-01..2026-04, in-sample Stage-B context.

GATE B1-AMENDED (applied identically to each scaled arm, separately):
  (i) expectancy_after_cost > 0 (0.51 pt cost);
  (ii) n_trades >= 150;
  (iii) exit-reason sanity: share of trades exiting at the forced
        15:55 exit (reason "time_exit") < 60%. The original arm's 78%
        is the mismatch symptom; a properly scaled arm must show the
        pattern's stops/targets engaging. filter_exit trades count in
        the denominator but not the numerator (they are a distinct,
        pre-declared exit class).
If BOTH scaled arms fail clause (iii), that is strong evidence the
mismatch is not explained by simple price/volatility scaling, and NO
third ad hoc scaling will be attempted — the amendment says so and
stops.

HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK RULES (in addition to the original SPEC's)
- Both derivation anchors were fixed before any scaled Stage-B
  number existed. No third method may be introduced after seeing
  results; neither method may be preferred retroactively.
- This amendment does not reopen the original 40/80 result.
- The M1 anchor's lower rigor (brief-stated public band, not
  measured Dow data) is flagged here and in the report.
- A null result for the scaled arms is a complete, expected,
  valuable deliverable. Given the known economics (0.51 pt cost on a
  5-pt stop = 10% of risk per trade; the scalping-screen family died
  at exactly this scale), a clause-(i) failure is a likely outcome
  and will be reported plainly if it occurs.
