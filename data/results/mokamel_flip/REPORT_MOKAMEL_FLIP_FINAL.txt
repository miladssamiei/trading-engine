REPORT — MOKAMEL FLIP FINAL VERDICT (HYPOTHESIS #16) — CONSOLIDATED
=====================================================================
Date: 2026-09-04. This report consolidates Stage A (signal quality),
Stage B (literal 40/80 exit arm + money machine + unit ring), and
Amendment 1 (scaled-exit arms) into the single final verdict for
hypothesis #16. All prior artifacts in data/results/mokamel_flip/
remain untouched; this file adds the closure only.

1. VERDICT: NULL. The mokamel flip mechanism has no exploitable
gross edge on US500 at any tested exit calibration. Stage C is NOT
run, and the study is closed.

2. WHY STAGE C IS SKIPPED (explicit reasoning, per the closure
brief): Stage C would walk the literal 40/80 arm forward over
2022-01..2026-04 with no parameter selection. The scaled-exit
amendment already answered the deeper question — does the flip
trigger have real gross edge, independent of exit calibration? —
with a clear no: at properly scaled 5/10-pt brackets (two
independently derived methods converging), gross expectancy is
-0.0223 pts/trade, statistically indistinguishable from zero, and
decisively negative (-0.5323) after the confirmed 0.51-pt real cost.
Meanwhile the 40/80 arm's Stage-B pass is already fully diagnosed as
a 2021-only drift-capture artifact (2022+ net -$101; 2025 -$1,849).
Running Stage C would only reconfirm that documented finding —
procedural box-ticking, not investigation. This project's honesty
rules favor a complete, well-diagnosed null over ceremony.

3. THE BRACKETING LOGIC (the study's actual finding):
- WIDE exit (40/80 pt, the video's literal claim): 78% of trades
  time out at the 15:55 forced exit; the arm becomes an
  enter-on-flip, hold-to-close drift capture; its thin positive
  expectancy (+0.2121 pts after cost) is concentrated entirely in
  the 2021 burn-in year and is negative from 2022 onward. The
  pattern's own stop/target logic never engages.
- SCALED exit (5/10 pt, price-level ratio 8.0x and 1.0x median
  ATR14(M5) converging on the same cell): the levels now engage
  properly (time-exit share 2.7%; the day-close rule fires; the
  aggressive/conservative machine activates) — and the edge
  vanishes: gross expectancy ~0, after-cost -0.5323 pts/trade,
  Sharpe -1.54, negative in 5 of 6 years.
- Conclusion: what looked like signal at wide exits was session
  drift, and what survives calibration to the instrument's actual
  volatility is nothing. The two ends bracket zero.

4. THE Q1 NOTE (Dow-Jones-only domain claim): this test was always
  OUTSIDE the pattern's claimed domain — the presenter states the
  pattern is taught and only claimed to work on Dow Jones, and no
  Dow data exists in this project (verified; Amendment 1 documents
  the sourcing check). The null result on US500 is therefore
  CONSISTENT WITH, not contradictory to, the presenter's own stated
  limitation. This must NOT be read as "the video was right": no Dow
  test was run, and consistency with an untested claim is not
  evidence for it. The accurate statement is the narrow one: a
  US500 failure is unsurprising given the presenter's own claim,
  and nothing more.

5. WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE PROJECT: mechanism #16 is now the Nth
  video/course-sourced pattern — alongside the VWAP trend-pullback,
  Al Brooks H1/H2, Al Brooks H2/IBS, and the 50-combination
  scalping screen — to fail under rigorous pre-declared testing on
