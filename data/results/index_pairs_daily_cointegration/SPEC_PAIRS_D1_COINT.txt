1. DATA AND D1 AGGREGATION
  1.1 Tapes: data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv (US500) and
      data/results/usa30_onboarding/usa30tick_m1.csv (USA30; substitution as
      in #39 - the tasked path vwap_2021_2026/usa30tick_m1.csv does not
      exist). Both epoch-UTC open-time Bid OHLC.
  1.2 Timezone America/New_York. D1 bar dated D covers the strict interval
      (D-1 17:00 ET, D 17:00 ET]; assignment rule: a M1 bar opening at wall
      time t belongs to D1 date (t - 17:00h).date() + 1 day (a bar opening
      exactly at 17:00:00 belongs to the NEXT D1 bar, matching the onboarding
      D1 convention). D1 open = first M1 open in the interval, D1 close =
      last M1 close in it, high/low = extremes; days with no M1 bars do not
      exist.
  1.3 Inner join of the two D1 series on the exact D1 date.

2. STATE MACHINE (ROLLING OLS COINTEGRATION)
  2.1 For each joined D1 bar t with at least 252 trailing observations
      (window INCLUSIVE of t): OLS of US500 close (Y) on USA30 close (X) in
      PRICE LEVELS. beta_t = cov(Y,X)/var(X), alpha_t = mean(Y) - beta_t*
      mean(X) (rolling least squares).
  2.2 Daily residual spread series: Spread_t = Y_t - (beta_t*X_t + alpha_t),
      each day using THAT day's fitted (beta_t, alpha_t). First valid Spread
      observation = joined day 252.
  2.3 Z-score: Z_t = (Spread_t - Mean_252(Spread)) / StdDev_252(Spread)
      where Mean_252/StdDev_252 roll over the trailing 252 Spread
      observations inclusive of t, sample std (ddof=1). Two-stage warmup:
      Z armed at joined day 503 (252 for the regression + 252 spread obs).
      StdDev = 0 -> unarmed.

3. SIGNAL GENERATION
  3.1 SHORT_SPREAD (US500 overpriced): Z_t > +2.0 -> short US500 / long
      USA30. LONG_SPREAD (US500 underpriced): Z_t < -2.0 -> long US500 /
      short USA30.
  3.2 Two independent per-direction state machines, evaluated
      chronologically: a breach on day t opens a trade at the NEXT D1 bar's
      opens (day t+1) ONLY if no trade is currently open in that direction
      (no re-entry while open; the opposite direction is unaffected). No
      next bar -> no-entry drop.

4. EXCURSION WALK AND ECONOMICS (GROSS)
  4.1 From entry day E (entry at D1 opens) evaluate daily closes until the
      FIRST of: (a) REVERSION - Z_j crosses to the entry-opposite side of
      zero (Z_j <= 0 for SHORT_SPREAD, Z_j >= 0 for LONG_SPREAD; the entry
      day's own Z is evaluable); exit at that day's close; (b) TIME - 45
      trading days elapsed after entry; exit at the close of bar E+45;
      (c) EOD - end of data forces a close at the last bar (flagged).
  4.2 Leg notionals: $10,000 on the US500 leg; USA30 leg = $10,000 x beta_s
      where beta_s is the SIGNAL-day beta. Daily pair PnL:
      pnl_j = d_y*$10,000*(Y_j/Y_E - 1) + d_x*$10,000*beta_s*(X_j/X_E - 1),
      with (d_y, d_x) = (-1, +1) for SHORT_SPREAD, (+1, -1) for LONG_SPREAD,
      Y_E/X_E the entry-day D1 opens.
  4.3 MFE = max pnl_j over the walk (daily closes), MAE = -min pnl_j.
      Exit PnL = pnl at the exit close. All GROSS (no costs); descriptive
      cost floor alongside: CFD spreads 0.51 pt US500 / 2.28 pt USA30 ->
      $10,000*0.51/Y_E + $10,000*beta_s*2.28/X_E per round trip.

5. GATES (frozen verbatim in GATE_PAIRS_D1_COINT.txt)

  G-DC2 aggregate mean Pair MFE > mean Pair MAE.
  G-DC3 mean gross PnL > 0.

    not evidence.
