3. QUOTES, ENTRY, EXIT, AND COST
Use verified raw EURUSD tick CSV C:/Users/Milad/download/
eurusd-tick-2020-01-01-2026-09-20.csv with epoch-ms timestamp,
askPrice, bidPrice. Source hash and integrity are recorded in
data/results/eurusd_onboarding/onboarding_stats.json. Exclude an
invalid individual quote (nonpositive, nonfinite, or Ask<Bid)
and count it; do not repair or interpolate. Enter at the first
valid quote from release+60 through release+65 seconds inclusive:
BUY at Ask or SELL at Bid. Exit at the first valid quote from
release+15 minutes through +15 minutes+10 seconds inclusive:
long at Bid, short at Ask. Skip and log absent entry or exit.
No stops, targets, second attempts, or overnight positions.
Gross pips = direction*(exitMid-entryMid)/0.00010.
Executable quote net pips = (exitBid-entryAsk)/0.00010 for long
or (entryBid-exitAsk)/0.00010 for short. Extra-cost stress net
pips = quote net - 0.3 on the SAME trades. Actual Bid/Ask spread
is already paid in executable quote net; extra 0.3 pip buffers
latency/slippage. No compounding or swap. First-quote fills remain
optimistic about live order latency, rejection, and market impact.
