HYPOTHESIS 65: EURUSD FIRST-MINUTE U.S.-NEWS RESPONSE LEADS GOLD
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND SEPARATION FROM PRIOR STUDIES
At scheduled U.S. macro releases, EURUSD gives a fast observable
directional read on the U.S.-dollar component of the information.
If gold incorporates that common dollar component more slowly,
EURUSD's first-minute direction could predict gold's later return.
EURUSD up implies a weaker dollar and BUY XAUUSD; EURUSD down
implies a stronger dollar and SELL XAUUSD. This cross-market
lead-lag signal differs from Hypothesis 63, which followed gold's
own first-minute response, and Hypothesis 64, which used unverified
actual-minus-forecast data. Both earlier FAILs remain final. No
same-day gold price feature sets direction. There is one fixed
signal, entry, exit, and event universe; no threshold search.

4. GOLD EXECUTION AND ECONOMICS
At 08:31:05..08:31:10 inclusive, enter on the first valid gold
quote: BUY at Ask for EURUSD-up, SELL at Bid for EURUSD-down.
Exit on the first valid gold quote at 08:45:00..08:45:10
inclusive: long at Bid, short at Ask. Skip and log a missing
entry/exit quote. No stop, target, retry, holiday subset, or
overnight position. Gold gross midpoint-reference move is
direction*(exitMid-entryMid). Executable net is exitBid-entryAsk
for long or entryBid-exitAsk for short. Extra-cost stress net is
executable net minus 0.41 gold unit on the SAME trades. Actual
Bid/Ask spread is already charged in executable net. One unit
equals a $1/oz gold move; no swap or compounding. First-quote
fills omit live latency, impact, and rejected orders.

5. PARTITION AND UNCERTAINTY
NY dates 2020-2021 are descriptive calibration. Dates 2022-01-01
through the source-roster end in April 2026 form the gated
historical block, with 2026 partial. The price tapes and release
dates have been available in this project, so a PASS is not virgin
out-of-sample evidence. Report all date/source/quote skips,
trade count, gross/quote/stress means and totals, entry/exit
spreads, win rate, drawdown, year and release-family breakdowns,
and hold time. Resample gated entry dates with replacement
10,000 times, seed 20261001, retaining all trades per date;
report one-sided P(mean executable net <=0) and descriptive
95% quantiles.

6. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-EXL1: At least 150 executed trades on distinct 2022-2026 release dates.
G-EXL2: Strictly positive mean extra-cost stress net per gold trade in the gated block.
G-EXL3: Date-bootstrap P(mean executable quote net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261001.
G-EXL4: Strictly positive executable quote-net total in each complete gated year 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025.
Overall Phase-1 PASS requires every gate. Otherwise FAIL is final
for this artifact. No post-hoc side inversion, family, year,
reaction-size threshold, entry delay, or exit-window adjustment.
