1. FEATURE ENGINEERING (GOLD)
  1.1 D1 series: XAUUSD M1 aggregated by America/New_York calendar date;
      day close = last printed M1 close of that ET date. Log returns
      ln(C_t/C_{t-1}) on consecutive available D1 dates.
  1.2 Realized volatility: RV(d) = sqrt(252) * std(ln-returns over the 21
      observations t = d-20..d, sample std ddof=1).
  1.3 Raw VRP: VRP_Gold(d) = GVZ(d) - RV(d)*100, computed on the inner join
      of GVZ trading dates (ET) and gold D1 dates.
  1.4 Causal publication shift (s -> s+1): VRP(d) is known only at d's
      close, so the feature dated d+1 equals VRP(d). For a session on day d
      the conditioning value is VRP(d-1), fully known before the 08:20 ET
      COMEX open.

2. CAUSAL ROLLING PERCENTILES (per #31 convention)
  2.1 On the shifted series S(d) = VRP(d-1): trailing_P50(d) = median of
      S over the trailing 252 observations INCLUSIVE of d; fewer than 252
      observations = warmup, day not arm-eligible (matches #31: the shift
      makes S(d) known by the day-d open, so inclusive is causal).
  2.2 Arm A (High VRP): S(d) > trailing_P50(d). Arm D (inverted control):
      S(d) < trailing_P50(d).

3. ORB EXTRACTION
  3.1 GOLD (executable leg): Opening Range = extremes (High max / Low min)
      over printed M1 bars with ET minute-of-day in [500, 515) (08:20..
      08:34, the [08:20, 08:35) COMEX/NY liquidity open). Trigger = the
      FIRST M1 close strictly above the OR High (LONG) or strictly below the
      OR Low (SHORT), scanned from OR completion to the 15:59 bar; first
      breakout per day wins, max 1 trade/day, both directions eligible, no
      re-arm. Entry = the trigger bar's close (signal-bar-close fill).
      Stop = opposite OR boundary. Walk from the bar after the trigger bar:
      if a bar OPENS beyond the stop, fill at that open; else if the bar
      touches the stop (Low <= SL long / High >= SL short), fill at the SL;
      otherwise continue to the time exit = close of the last printed M1 bar
      with ET minute-of-day <= 959 (16:00 ET session close).
      Days with no printed OR bar, no trigger, or degenerate risk (risk in
      price units <= 0) produce no trade.
  3.2 COSTS: Gold net = gross (direction * (exit - entry)) - 0.41 (verified
      round-trip spread floor in price units). US500 stream net reused as-is
      (#31 convention). No swap (intraday).
  3.3 USA100: infeasible per 0(a); no trades extracted; VXN not used.

4. METRICS AND PORTFOLIO
  4.1 Per-instrument baseline metrics on the arm-eligible population (all
      gold ORB trades on arm-eligible dates = Arm A + Arm D): trade count,
      win rate, total and mean net, per-trade Net Sharpe = mean/std(ddof=1)
      of per-trade net RETURN ON NOTIONAL (net / entry price) x sqrt(252)
      (#31 convention). Full-history (incl. warmup) reported descriptively.
  4.2 Edge concentration: Arm A mean net return vs baseline mean net return.
  4.3 Combined portfolio: streams = US500 #31 Arm A + Gold Arm A (if it
      qualifies; else gold excluded and G-XV3 evaluated on US500 alone).
      Each trade -> daily net return on notional (net / entry price); daily
      stream = sum over that date's trades, zero-filled on the union trading
      calendar of the member streams. Reported: combined daily Sharpe
      (mean/std(ddof=1) x sqrt(252)), trades/year = n_trades / (years
      between first and last combined trade date, /365.25), trades/week,
      and the Pearson correlation matrix of the member daily streams.

5. GATES (frozen verbatim in GATE_CROSS_ASSET_VRP.txt)
  G-XV1 at least one new instrument (USA100 or Gold) Arm A retains >= 100
      trades post-warmup.
  G-XV2 the qualifying instrument's Arm A Net Sharpe strictly exceeds its
      unconditioned baseline Net Sharpe.
  G-XV3 combined portfolio (US500 + passing instruments) achieves an annual
      trade frequency >= 120 trades/year (>= 2.5 trades/week average).
