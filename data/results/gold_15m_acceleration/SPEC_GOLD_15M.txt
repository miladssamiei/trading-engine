Question: Do fixed M15/H1 transfers of the established Keltner and Donchian gold breakout engines clear a 0.41 gold-unit CFD round-turn cost and generate more than +5.33 net R per calendar month over the full available gold tape?
Input: data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv, bid-only XAUUSD M1 OHLC proxy, full available range. Label the instrument GC proxy only; no CME GC quote, roll, or futures execution claim. Hash source and evaluator. No optimization or variant selection.
Grids: Build epoch-aligned continuous M15 and H1 OHLC grids from real M1 minutes; each field forward-fills across absent buckets. A bar is REAL only when an M1 observation belongs to that bucket. Indicators include filled bars to match parent convention. Entries require a REAL M15 bucket and use its first real M1 open. At M15 bucket i, all execution indicators use bucket i-1 or earlier; H1 anchor uses bucket floor(start_i/3600)-1, the last strictly completed hour.
Engine E: Prior M15 close exceeds EMA29 +/- 2.77 Wilder ATR29 in the directional sense; last completed H1 close is above/below H1 EMA50. Enter next real M15 open. Initial stop at entry bid minus/plus max(abs(entry bid - prior M15 EMA29), 1.5*prior M15 ATR29). After every completed REAL M15 bar with no stop, ratchet bid-reference stop to its current EMA29, never widen. One position per engine. The H1 EMA is seeded and first 49 H1 values are unavailable.
Engine F: Prior M15 close strictly breaks Donchian20 high/low of M15 bucket i-2. Last completed H1 Supertrend(10,3) direction agrees. Prior M15 Wilder ADX14 >=20. Enter next REAL M15 open. Initial stop at entry bid minus/plus 4*prior M15 Wilder ATR14. After each completed REAL M15 bar with no stop, ratchet to current M15 close minus/plus 4*current ATR14, never widen. One position per engine.
Fills: Single fixed 0.41 gold-unit round-turn CFD cost inserted once: long ask entry=bid-proxy M1 open+0.41 and bid exit; short bid entry=bid-proxy M1 open and ask exit=bid-proxy exit+0.41. Long stop trips if M1 low<=bid stop; short stop trips if M1 high+0.41>=ask stop. Engine E's short parent stop is bid-reference, so its ask stop is bid-reference stop+0.41; Engine F's short parent stop is ask-reference, so its bid trigger is ask stop-0.41. Adverse gaps fill at worse M1 open. Check entry M1 for stop. If no stop by tape end, close at last M1 close. Signals while a position is open or within the M15 exit bucket are discarded. No prop unrealized trailing guard and no cooldown. Swaps fixed at zero, with overnight-position counts disclosed. No forced daily flat and no take profit.
R: For each trade, net R equals net bid-proxy price PnL after cost divided by the absolute all-in loss from executed entry to the initial stop (including any spread impact), which must be positive. R uses the initial stop, not the subsequently ratcheted stop. Total R is the arithmetic sum across sequential trades within each engine. Total tape months is the number of distinct inclusive UTC calendar months from the first through last M1 timestamp; partial boundary months count as whole months. Monthly R velocity=total net R/total tape months. Target is strictly >+5.33 R/month. Also report annual and calendar-month R sums, full 2024-25 monthly velocity, trade counts, mean net price-unit expectancy, unannualized per-trade net Sharpe mean/std(ddof=1), and max trade-sequence R drawdown.
Gate: For each engine independently, at least 50 trades, net Sharpe >0, and moving-block bootstrap P(mean net price-unit PnL<=0)<=0.20. Bootstrap uses 10,000 trade-order resamples of five-trade blocks, fixed seed 20260926. Gate pass and velocity target are distinct verdicts. Neither alone proves a 45-day FTMO pass.
