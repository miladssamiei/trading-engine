HYPOTHESIS 71: US500 PRICE-ONLY NOISE-AREA INTRADAY MOMENTUM
Declared 2026-09-25 before this hypothesis's code or Phase-1 run.

1. SOURCE AND DISTINCT MECHANISM
Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon, Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday
Momentum Strategy for S&P500 ETF (SPY), first version 2024, author
PDF https://concretumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Beat-the-Market.pdf
sections 3 and 4: prior-14-session time-of-day absolute return defines
an intraday noise area; at HH:00/HH:30 the first price outside a band
signals continuation, with opposite-band crossover and daily close
exits. Test its published BASE price-only opposite-band version on
US500-tick CFD cash-session BID bars. Do not import the paper's
later VWAP/volume variant, because US500 tick-count is not traded
share volume. Do not use its volatility-based dynamic size, any VIX
filter, threshold search, side filter, stop tightening or timing
variant. This is distinct from the repo's ORB and last-half-hour
closing momentum tests. The 2024+ tape has been seen in many studies;
historical PASS would remain selected evidence requiring fresh forward
confirmation. No existing frozen study is altered.

2. DATA, CLOCK AND CAUSAL SIGNAL
Input only data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv, SHA256
a8ee68f1a0010a1be9704c0d59fd5382aa798846cdae4d519b799ae21ec66c2d;
M5 is built directly from verified real imported ticks. Epoch UTC to
America/New_York wall time. Cash [09:30,16:00), a cash day iff a real
09:30 M5 bar exists; require a real 15:55 M5 bar to trade that day.
Today's Open is the real 09:30 M5 BID open; previous Close is the
previous valid cash day's final real 15:55 M5 BID close. At each NY
clock H in {10:00,10:30,...,15:30}, use ONLY the preceding real M5
bar ending at H, i.e. 09:55 at 10:00, to determine the BID signal;
execute at the real M5 bar OPEN stamped H. If either bar is absent,
skip that clock and count it. For each of the previous 14 valid cash
days d-k, require the real preceding M5 bar at the identical H and
that day's real 09:30 M5 open; otherwise skip that current clock.
sigma(H)=mean of 14 absolute [(prior_day_preH_BID_close /
prior_day_0930_BID_open)-1] values. Upper(H)=max(today_open,
previous_close)*(1+sigma(H)); Lower(H)=min(today_open,
previous_close)*(1-sigma(H)). All inputs known before H. A signal
is +1 if today's real pre-H BID close > Upper; -1 if < Lower;
otherwise 0. Equality is inside the band. This pre-H sampling is the
causal interpretation of the paper's half-hour decisions; do not
execute using an unfinished H bar or a same-minute final price.

3. POSITION STATE, EXIT AND COST
Start every NY date flat. At H, flat +1 signal -> BUY at H bar
ASK = BID open +0.51; flat -1 -> SELL at H BID open. One position
max. Long remains open until a sampled -1 opposite-band signal;
short remains until +1. At opposite signal close old and enter new
opposite at the same H bar open, each trade charged its own 0.51
round-trip spread. Same-side and neutral signals hold. Every open
position force exits at real 15:55 M5 BID close for long or
ASK=BID+0.51 for short, before 16:00 NY. No overnight financing.
All entries/exits on same NY date, max hold <6 hours. Initial
planned-stop risk for later R diagnostic: BUY entry Ask minus Lower
at entry H, SELL Upper at entry H plus 0.51 minus entry Bid.
This is a sampled decision stop rather than an exchange stop;
actual gap and intrabar adverse excursions may exceed planned risk.
Report gross BID-point move and fixed-spread net = gross -0.51;
extra cost stress = net -0.51 (total 1.02). M5 bar opens and final
closes are optimistic execution proxies, not real Bid/Ask fills.

Bootstrap gated New York dates with replacement 10000 times, seed

Risk per trade = fixed $100 = 1% of initial balance divided by

first $11000 closed balance after >=4 distinct Prague entry dates

This close-only check ignores intra-trade floating equity and is
optimistic. No policy or actual-account authorization is inferred.
Report pass/breach/timeout rates across all eligible starts.

G-NA2: Strictly positive gated mean extra-cost-stress net US500 points per trade.
G-NA3: Date-bootstrap P(mean fixed-spread net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10000 resamples, seed 20261008.
G-NA4: Strictly positive fixed-spread net US500 point totals in each complete gated year 2024 and 2025.
G-NA5: Every executed trade exits on its New York entry date before 16:00 and has positive initial planned-stop risk distance.
G-NA6: In the gated 180-day Challenge proxy at 1% planned risk, at least 70% of eligible starts pass and at most 10% breach a close-based floor.
All six required for Phase-1 PASS. A FAIL is final for this exact
artifact; no post-hoc side, stop, time, size, 14-day-window or
cost adjustment. If G-NA1..5 FAIL, G-NA6 is not used to rescue the
rule; its diagnostic rate can still be reported when computable.
