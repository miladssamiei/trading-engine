1.3 Timezones: epoch UTC -> America/New_York wall time for all session logic.

2. LONG MODEL (ATR LIMIT TOUCH)

2.1 Indicator: 14-period ATR on the continuous M5 tape, Wilder smoothing
    (TR_i = max(H-L, |H - C_prev|, |L - C_prev|); ATR seeded with the mean of the
    first 14 TRs; first valid ATR index = 13; first entry candidate index = 14).

2.2 Setup and entry: for each M5 candle i with valid ATR[i-1]:
      LowerBand = Close[i-1] - (3.1 * ATR14[i-1])
    If Low[i] <= LowerBand -> Long entry recorded at exactly LowerBand (limit
    fill; gap-through bars also record the band price per tasking, no open-price
    improvement is taken).

2.3 Exits:
      Target     = Entry + (2.0 * ATR14[i-1])
      Stop Loss  = Entry - (1.5 * ATR14[i-1])
    Exit scan starts on the entry bar itself and walks forward bar by bar:
      - if Low[j]  <= Stop  -> exit at Stop   (checked FIRST: pessimistic)
      - elif High[j] >= Target -> exit at Target
    If one bar satisfies both Target and Stop (any bar, entry bar included),
    assume the Stop was hit first (tasking pessimistic collision rule).
    If the tape ends before either level is hit -> mark-close at the final M5
    close, exit_reason=end_of_tape (flagged in the trades CSV).

2.4 Position rule: single Long position per instrument; entry signals occurring
    while a Long trade is open are skipped (not queued). After an exit on bar j,
    new signals are scanned from bar j+1.

2.5 Filter: no entries on the exact NY cash-session opening candle
    (wall-minute 570, 09:30-09:35 ET). No other session filter: the tape is the
    full ~24h day, overnight band touches are valid signals.

3. SHORT MODEL (SHOOTING STAR & TIME EXIT)

3.1 Daily filter: short signals on NY date d are accepted only if the previous
    D1 candle (definition 1.2) closed red (Close < Open).

3.2 Pattern trigger on M5 candle i:
      body       = abs(Close - Open)
      upper_wick = High - max(Close, Open)
      lower_wick = min(Close, Open) - Low
      condition  : upper_wick >= (2.0 * body)  AND  lower_wick <= (0.10 * upper_wick)
    Zero-body candles: body=0, condition reduces to upper_wick >= 0 (true) and
    lower_wick <= 0.10*upper_wick; evaluated mechanically as written.

3.3 Entry: market Short at the exact Open price of the next M5 candle (i+1).
    Exit: market Buy-to-cover at the exact Close of that same entry candle —
    exactly a 5-minute hold. Pattern on the final tape bar (no i+1) is skipped.
    No session filter for shorts (declared by tasking; daily filter only).

4. COST MODEL & PERMUTATIONS

4.1 Per instrument, three combinations: Long-Only, Short-Only, Combined
    (Combined = the union of both independent trade streams; the two models run
    concurrently and do not block each other).

4.2 GROSS: 0 spread, 0 slippage. NET: subtract the verified median round-trip
    spread from EVERY trade's point PnL regardless of direction:
      US500 = 0.51 pt, USA30 = 2.28 pt, XAUUSD = 0.41 pt.
    No swap/financing is modeled (tasking specifies spread-only decay; Long
    trades may hold overnight and are still charged spread only — documented
    limitation).

4.3 Metrics: expectancy per trade (points) and win rate, each reported GROSS and
    NET. Trade PnL in points: Long = exit - entry; Short = entry - exit.

5. GATES (verbatim in GATE_YOUTUBE_HYBRID_SCALPING.txt, frozen before any run)

G-Y1 (Sample Size): The combined strategy must generate >= 150 trades on each
    instrument.
G-Y2 (Cost-Floor Survival): The NET expectancy per trade for the Combined
    strategy must be > 0 on at least one of the three instruments.

Overall verdict rule: G-Y1 AND G-Y2 must both pass for a PASS; any failure is a
FAIL and is final for this artifact. No Phase 2 (MT5 execution) is authorized by
this study regardless of verdict.

7. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES

- All numeric constants (3.1, 2.0, 1.5, 2.0x body, 0.10x upper wick, 14 ATR,
  spread medians) are fixed by the tasking before the run; no parameter search,
  no threshold tuning, no instrument cherry-picking beyond reporting all three.
- Entry prices are the mechanical rule prices (band / next open); no look-ahead:
  entries use only bar i-1 information (Long) or bar i information (Short);
  exits use only subsequent bar extremes with the pessimistic collision rule.

- This is a descriptive spread-decay study; a FAIL verdict closes the mechanism
  and it joins the closed families list.
