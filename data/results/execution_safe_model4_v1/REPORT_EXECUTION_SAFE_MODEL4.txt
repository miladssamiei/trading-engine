Purpose and scope

This study corrected the two defects that stopped Hypothesis #21 before modeling: 54 ORB trades carried into a later New York date after off-grid force-close requests were rejected, and one SP2L trade opened on a nonstandard session that had no tradable tick at the configured exit. The frozen parent EAs and their historical results were not modified. Two new versioned experts were created and tested:

ea/ORB_ExecutionSafe_v1.mq5
ea/SP2L_ExecutionSafe_v1.mq5

The allowed changes were limited to tick-grid-normalized explicit close requests with local result logging and retry state, SL/TP reason priority over force-close state, and the fixed 45-date historical nonstandard-session entry block. Inputs other than the identifying magic numbers remained equal to the frozen parents. Manual diff review found no change to signal, direction, stop, target, volume, intended exit time, or day-accounting formulas outside the approved corrections.

Descriptive comparison with frozen Model=4 streams

Frozen ORB had 437 trades, +$114.91 PnL, 0.0745 Sharpe, $791.59 maximum drawdown, 54 later-date closes, and two blocked-date entries. The corrected stream matched 435 frozen trade keys, removed two, and added 15 after same-day closes released occupancy for subsequent opportunities. PnL increased by $268.23 and maximum drawdown fell by $211.17. These changes are consequences of the predeclared execution correction and were not used as a selection gate.

Frozen SP2L had 1,349 trades, +$138.65 PnL, 0.1329 Sharpe, $513.18 maximum drawdown, one later-date close, and seven blocked-date entries. The corrected stream matched 1,342 trade keys and removed the seven blocked-date trades, with no added trades. PnL increased by $44.10 and maximum drawdown fell by $34.87. These changes were not used as a selection gate.

Known limitations

The PASS is narrow. The tester logs retain two entry-side rejection episodes from the frozen entry logic: ORB made two rejected retries with invalid price retcode 10015 on 2022-10-14 before entering on the next M5 bar, and SP2L made two rejected retries with invalid stops retcode 10016 on 2025-12-29 and did not enter that setup. X-E3 explicitly gates the corrected close helper, so these entry failures do not change this verdict. They require a separately predeclared correction study before any claim of general order-execution robustness.

The fixed date list is based on incomplete-session dates observed in the available 2021-01 through 2026-04 historical tape. It is not a future exchange calendar or proof of the broker's live CFD schedule. The new EAs therefore remain historical research artifacts.

The corrected performance is still weak and unstable. ORB has three negative calendar segments, including 2024 and 2025. SP2L loses $306.81 in 2025. Neither corrected stream has a performance gate in this study, and no new walk-forward predictive-edge claim was tested. The result does not repair the incomplete FTMO account identity, live quote, tick-value, fill, margin, or reconciliation evidence.

Conclusion

The specific Model=4 cross-date close defects are corrected on the tested historical tape, so the execution_safe_model4_v1 artifact passes its technical gates. This PASS does not qualify either EA for the FTMO 10k Challenge and does not reopen the failed HAR study retroactively. Any continuation must use a new pre-declared study. The next justified work is a separate entry-order normalization/stops correction, followed by a new risk-model eligibility study only after its own data gates pass. Live or FTMO execution remains prohibited by this research verdict.
