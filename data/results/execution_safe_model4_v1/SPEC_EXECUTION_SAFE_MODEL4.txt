Study: execution_safe_model4_v1
Date frozen: 2026-09-11
Status: pre-declared before implementation, compilation, or tester execution

Purpose
Correct two execution defects observed in the frozen Model=4 ORB and SP2L exports, without changing either strategy's entry signal, stop, target, sizing, or intended regular-session exit time. The corrected experts are new artifacts. The frozen ORB_Strategy.mq5 and SP2L_SpikeGap.mq5 remain untouched.

Observed defects motivating this study
1. ORB recorded 54 positions closing on a later New York date. Tester logs show repeated TRADE_RETCODE_INVALID_PRICE (10015) when PositionClose submitted raw bid/ask prices that were not aligned to the symbol's 0.01 tick grid.
2. SP2L recorded one later-date close: ticket 328 opened on 2025-07-04 and had no tradable tick at its intended 15:55 New York exit. It eventually closed by stop loss on 2025-07-07. Its helper also reports the CTrade object's stale result instead of the helper's local MqlTradeResult, and force-close state can override the actual SL/TP exit reason.

Artifacts
New experts only:
- ea/ORB_ExecutionSafe_v1.mq5
- ea/SP2L_ExecutionSafe_v1.mq5
New evaluation/runner and tests may be added under evaluate/ and tests/. All outputs belong under data/results/execution_safe_model4_v1/.

Frozen-behavior constraint
The new experts must retain the frozen parents' strategy inputs and entry eligibility, signal calculation, direction, entry order construction, stop, target, volume, intended 15:55 New York force-exit time, and day-export semantics, except for the three explicitly approved corrections below. Version strings, comments, output filenames, and magic numbers may change to identify the new artifacts.

Approved correction A: grid-safe explicit close
Both experts must close an owned position with an explicit opposite TRADE_ACTION_DEAL request. The request price must use current bid for a long close or current ask for a short close and must pass through the existing tick-grid normalization. The helper must expose and log its own MqlTradeResult retcode/comment and GetLastError. A failed attempt must leave the position eligible for another attempt on each subsequent tick. Force-close state is complete only after the owned position is actually absent.

Approved correction B: exit-reason priority
When recording a closing deal, DEAL_REASON_SL and DEAL_REASON_TP must take priority over any force-close state. A time/force exit is recorded only when the deal reason is not SL or TP and the position was under the force-close path.

Approved correction C: historical nonstandard-session entry block

Tester protocol
Compile both new experts with MetaEditor. Run one yearly batch per expert for 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 through the available April endpoint: 12 batches total. Use US500-tick and Model=4, every tick based on real ticks. ORB uses M5 and SP2L uses M1, matching their frozen tester configuration. Preserve batch logs, reports, copied sandbox CSVs, sources, executable hashes, config hashes, and stitched outputs.

Evaluation rules
The audit must parse exported trades, day files, and tester logs. It must report trade counts, PnL, daily Sharpe under the established common-universe convention, maximum drawdown, year totals, later-New-York-date closes, negative durations, entries on blocked dates, exit-reason counts, close-helper failure retcodes, invalid-price messages, market-closed messages, and whether each batch ended flat. It must also compare the corrected and frozen Model=4 streams by added, removed, and matched trades and by performance metrics. That comparison is descriptive and has no optimization or performance gate.

Stop rules
Stop and report FAIL if either source fails compilation, any batch lacks Model=4 real-tick evidence, any batch fails, outputs cannot be reconciled, or any eligibility gate below fails. Do not revise a gate, the date list, or strategy behavior after observing results. Any further correction requires a new study name and new pre-declared SPEC/GATE.

Honesty and deployment limits
This is an execution-eligibility correction, not a claim of predictive edge and not FTMO qualification. PASS does not make either strategy profitable, walk-forward-confirmed, prop-safe, or deployable. The FTMO account and quote/fill evidence remain incomplete. downstream_model_allowed and live_execution_allowed remain false regardless of outcome.
