HYPOTHESIS 81: US500 OVERNIGHT GAP TO FIRST-HALF-HOUR REVERSAL
Declared 2026-09-25 before this hypothesis's code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND SCOPE
Iwanaga and Sakemoto, Does overnight return predict the first half-hour
return for U.S. market indices?, North American Journal of Economics
and Finance 86 (September 2026), article 102707,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.najef.2026.102707, report a negative relation
between overnight SPY return and the next first-half-hour return. They
also report that the relation weakened after the 2010s. This study
tests one deliberately simple high-gap CFD translation, not their
exact ETF investment strategy or a claimed CFD replication. It is
distinct from prior US500 ORB, noise-area momentum and closing-drift
studies. Prior US500 test years have been seen by this project, so even
a PASS is selected historical evidence, not untouched out-of-sample
or live proof. A null result is a complete deliverable.

2. SOURCE AND CLOCK
Only verified native-tick-derived BID M5 input
data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv, SHA256
a8ee68f1a0010a1be9704c0d59fd5382aa798846cdae4d519b799ae21ec66c2d.
Epoch seconds UTC to America/New_York wall time with DST. A valid cash
date has one real 09:30 M5 bar and one real 15:55 M5 bar. Cash close is
the latter bar's BID close. Gap on date d is today's real 09:30 BID
open minus the immediately preceding valid cash date's 15:55 BID close.
Require all six real M5 bars 09:30..09:55 and a real 10:00 M5 bar for
the trade. Reject duplicate clock bars, nonfinite/nonpositive prices,
or impossible OHLC. Holidays and missing bars do not get synthetic
fills. Prior valid cash dates, not calendar days, define rolling inputs.

3. SIGNAL, STOP, EXIT AND COST
At the 09:30 NY open, use the 20 preceding valid cash dates only.
Threshold is their median absolute overnight cash gap. Trade if
abs(today gap) is strictly greater than this threshold and gap is
nonzero. Gap up => SELL; gap down => BUY. If any prior gap required for
the 20-date window is unavailable, skip. Initial stop distance D is
the median of the same 20 prior dates' first-half-hour real M5
high-max minus low-min across 09:30..09:55. Require each of those 20
prior dates to have all six real bars and D>0. No parameter search,
volatility regime, weekday, news or side filter.

Enter at today's 09:30 BID open (BUY ASK modeled as BID+0.51). Long
stop is entry BID-D; short stop is entry BID+D-0.51, triggered by
ASK= BID+0.51 reaching entry BID+D. Examine each real M5 bar including
the entry bar. On a stop, fill at the worse of stop level or that
bar's open, accounting for an adverse gap; SELL exit ASK adds 0.51.
No TP, trailing stop, re-entry or same-day second trade. If still
open, exit at the 10:00 real BID open (SELL exit ASK=BID+0.51). Report
gross directional BID points; net after 0.51 total round-trip spread;
stress net after another 0.51. One position max; no financing.
M5 OHLC cannot establish actual order fill, variable spread or intra-
bar gap path. A historical PASS only licenses a separately declared
Model=4 real-tick order study and actual FTMO-feed validation.

4. PARTITION, INFERENCE AND CHALLENGE PACE
2021-2023 is descriptive; 2024-01-01 through 2026-04-30 gated. Use
10,000 NY-date bootstrap resamples of gated net mean, seed 20261016.
Report both complete 2024 and 2025 net totals. Separately simulate
every gated entry date with a complete next 180 calendar days. Each
trade's fixed dollar planned risk is $100 (1% of initial $10,000):
position units=$100/D, fractional lots assumed. Actual stopped loss
can exceed $100 from a gap. No compounding. At each trade, track worst
M5 BID low for BUY or worst BID high+0.51 for SELL before exit,
translated to floating equity. Start $10,000, static equity floor
$9,000, and daily equity floor=prior Prague-midnight balance-$500.
Pass at first closed balance >=$11,000 after >=4 distinct Prague entry
days, provided floors never breached first. An eligible start must
have 180 calendar days of source follow-up before 2026-04-30. Report
pass, breach, timeout counts; this remains an optimistic CFD/FTMO
proxy because fractional lots and actual FTMO feed/margin/fills are
unknown. The 180-day horizon operationalizes the owner's desire not
to wait 8-9 months. It is a research screen, not a firm rule.

5. VERBATIM PHASE-1 GATES
G-OG1: At least 150 executed gated trades on at least 150 distinct New York cash dates with complete real entry, path and exit M5 bars.
G-OG2: Strictly positive gated mean US500 points per trade after the 0.51 spread proxy and an additional 0.51 cost stress.
G-OG3: New York date bootstrap P(mean base 0.51-cost net <= 0) at most 0.01, 10000 resamples, seed 20261016.
G-OG4: Strictly positive base 0.51-cost net US500 point totals in each complete gated calendar year 2024 and 2025.
G-OG5: Every executed trade is same-New-York-day, enters at 09:30, exits no later than 10:00, and has a strictly positive causal initial stop distance.
G-OG6: In the gated 180-calendar-day first-Challenge proxy at fixed 1% planned risk, at least 70% of eligible starts pass and at most 10% breach an equity floor.
All six required for Phase-1 PASS; a FAIL is final for this exact
artifact. G-OG6 is a pace diagnostic if economic gates fail, not a
way to rescue them. No post-hoc threshold, timing, side, stop, size
or year change to this artifact.

6. STOP RULE AND OUTPUTS
Save all eligible-date signals/skips, trade CSV, source hash, annual
statistics, bootstrap, pace tally, metadata and immutable REPORT.
If any gate fails, do not run MT5 Phase 2 for this rule. On PASS,
freeze a separate Model=4 real-tick execution SPEC/GATE before EA or
tester work, then require FTMO account costs and floating-risk proof.
Append SPEC/GATE, run and verdict stages to PROGRESS.md. Never alter
any existing frozen SPEC/GATE/REPORT.
