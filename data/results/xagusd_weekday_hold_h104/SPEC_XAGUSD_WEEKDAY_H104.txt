all 20 must be finite. No short, optimization, or re-entry.

Thursday15:55 NY. Entries use first real minute ASK open exactly at
entry wall time. Scheduled exits use real minute BID open exactly at

signaled exit becomes unavailable, count UNRESOLVED and FAIL gate.

1.5*prior ATR20. Starting in entry M1 through minute strictly before
scheduled exit, if Bid open<=stop fill at Bid open, else if Bid low

No target or trailing adjustment. If no stop, use scheduled BID open.
Any missing real M1 within the hold is retained as a data gap;

but do not synthesize quotes at their boundaries.
