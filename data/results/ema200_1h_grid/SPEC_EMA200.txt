EMA200 1H ANCHOR BIAS WITH FIXED ATR BRACKET GRID - FROZEN SPEC - 2026-09-30

Purpose
Test the owner's Hypothesis #94 as a complete, untuned 3 x 4 stop/target matrix on US500 and XAUUSD BID M1 proxy tapes. A null result is complete. Every cell is exploratory because all 12 brackets per asset are measured on the same historical tape; a gate PASS is not walk-forward validation.

Data and calendar
US500 source: data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv; fixed round-turn price cost 0.51. XAUUSD source: data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv; fixed round-turn price cost 0.41 as specifically requested here. No additional commission, slippage or swap. Require strictly increasing 60-second-aligned positive OHLC data, hash source files, retain true no-tick gaps. Convert epoch UTC to America/New_York for anchor times and NY calendar dates, including DST. Build continuous H1 OHLC grids by fieldwise forward-fill of missing buckets; indicators use these continuous bars and never use an incomplete bar. US500 H1 buckets are offset 30 minutes so 09:30 ET is an H1 open in both standard and daylight time; because US DST shifts UTC by whole hours, a fixed UTC epoch half-hour offset preserves the NY half-hour alignment. XAUUSD H1 buckets use epoch clock hours so 08:00 ET is an H1 open. Require an actual M1 bar at the precise anchor minute; skip a date with no exact anchor instead of entering late.

Indicator and signal rules
EMA200 is recursively seeded from the first H1 close with alpha=2/201 and becomes eligible only after 200 completed H1 bars. Wilder ATR14 is SMA-seeded with the first 14 H1 true ranges, then recursively updates as (13*previous ATR+current TR)/14. At the asset's exact anchor M1 open, read only the prior completed H1 EMA200 and ATR14. If anchor M1 BID open > prior EMA200, buy; if <, sell; if equal or indicators unavailable, no trade. One entry candidate at most per NY calendar date and asset. The anchor M1 BID open is the reference Entry Price and must equal the containing real H1 bar open.

Execution and brackets
Test all SL multipliers {1.0,2.0,3.0} crossed with TP multipliers {0.5,1.0,2.0,3.0}, independently per asset. Initial reference risk R is SL multiplier times prior H1 ATR14. Long BID stop/target = reference entry BID minus/plus SL/TP times ATR; long fill entry ASK = BID+cost and exit BID. Short ASK stop/target = reference entry BID plus/minus SL/TP times ATR; short entry BID and exit ASK=BID+cost. This places stop/target on the executable exit side and embeds the cost exactly once. Gross PnL uses BID entry and BID exit reference; net PnL uses executable entry and exit, so gross-minus-net must equal exactly one fixed cost per closed trade. Search real M1 bars chronologically from the entry minute. Stop is tested before target in each M1 bar; a same-minute collision is a stop. Adverse stop gaps fill at the M1 opening BID/ASK if beyond the stop; favorable target gaps fill at the fixed target. Positions may hold overnight and overlap across different NY entry dates because the request sets only a one-entry-per-day rule. No time stop: positions lacking a bracket exit by tape end are OPEN_END and excluded from closed-trade metrics; no forced profitable mark is used. No financing is modeled, so overnight outcomes are conditional on zero swap.

Statistics and artifacts
Per asset and SL/TP cell, save full trade ledger including OPEN_END rows, source/indicator metadata, exact cost and exit reason. Report closed-trade count, OPEN_END count, net win rate, gross and net mean PnL in native price units, net mean R (net PnL divided by initial R per trade), and moving-block bootstrap one-sided P(mean net <= 0). Bootstrap: 10,000 draws, five consecutive trades per block, seed 20260930 plus deterministic asset/cell identifiers. Bootstrap samples to the original number of closed entry-ordered trades. Gate per cell: at least 150 closed trades, net mean price-unit PnL > 0, and MBB p <= 0.05. Flag cells satisfying all three PASS; all others FAIL. No multiple-testing correction or walk-forward claim is implied. Save plain-text REPORT_EMA200.txt regardless of outcome.

Do not change the 24-cell grid, indicator definitions, cost floors, fill rules, bootstrap or gates after viewing results. Report all cells, including losses and censored positions.
