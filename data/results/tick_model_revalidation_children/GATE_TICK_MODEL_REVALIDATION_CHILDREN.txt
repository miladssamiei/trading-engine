GATE_TICK_MODEL_REVALIDATION_CHILDREN
Gates for the standalone Model=4 revalidation of ORB and SP2L
(two independent execution-layer re-measurements; not new hypotheses)

Status: written 2026-09-02 before any script. Verbatim extraction of
every O and S gate in SPEC_TICK_MODEL_REVALIDATION_CHILDREN sections

    Part 1 - ORB revalidation gates

O1. Reconciliation agreement between the Model=4 MT5 run and the SAME
Python reference, at the SAME standard used originally: >= 90%
trade-level full agreement (entry <= 1.5 pts, exit <= 1.5 pts, exit
time <= 15 min, mapped reason equal), every residual classified, zero
unexplained residuals tolerated without a class label.
(Original Model=0 reference point: 93.1% = 416/447.)

O2. Trade count and net PnL reported explicitly against BOTH the
Python reference (442 trades, +531.13) AND the original Model=0 MT5
result (447 trades, +470.67, Sharpe 0.337, maxDD 5.13%), with the
magnitude of change (percentage change in net PnL, Sharpe, maxDD)
stated honestly. DESCRIPTIVE - no pass/fail threshold; the result
becomes ORB's new real-tick reference baseline.

O3. Does ORB under Model=4 still beat its own buy-and-hold comparison
on the real tape? Sharpe and return/DD reported side by side for
ORB-Model-4 vs BH recomputed on the real-tick tape (the verified M5
export derived from the same Custom-base real ticks the tester
replays). DESCRIPTIVE - no pass/fail threshold; reported plainly.

    Part 2 - SP2L revalidation gates

S1. Reconciliation agreement between the Model=4 MT5 run and the
Python reference at the same standard (original: 89.7% = 1,144/1,275
matched): report the new percentage and whether the documented
"two-tapes" residual class (~10% knife-edge OHLC-inequality trades)
SHRINKS, persists, or is replaced by a different residual class under
Model=4. Threshold: >= 85%; a materially worse agreement is itself a
finding and is reported as such, never hidden by a lowered bar.

S2. Net PnL, Sharpe, and maxDD reported explicitly against the
original Model=0 MT5 result (1,343 trades, +281.84, Sharpe 0.275
common-universe, maxDD 4.17%), magnitude of change stated honestly.
DESCRIPTIVE - no pass/fail threshold.

S3. Does SP2L's net expectancy per trade remain economically
distinguishable from realistic costs under Model=4's real variable
spread, re-measured specifically over SP2L's own entry windows (the
validated 09:30-15:30 NY session)? Explicit numbers required:
per-trade net expectancy vs per-trade real spread cost, against the
project's 0.8 pt round-trip / 0.41 pt assumptions. If the real spread
over SP2L's windows is higher than assumed, state explicitly whether
the prior honesty note ("net expectancy of the order of transaction
costs") gets WORSE. Verdict stated plainly.

Year-by-year net PnL recomputed for both strategies (2021-2025 full
years, 2026 partial) with specific attention to SP2L's 2025 (documented
bad year under Model=0): does 2025 remain negative, improve, or worsen
under real ticks?

    Honesty and continuity rules

    - Re-measurements of ALREADY-VALIDATED hypotheses' execution
      layer; no renumbering; ORB/SP2L EAs, SPECs, GATEs, REPORTs and
      RECONCILEs untouched.
    - The full-range Model=4 probe (P1/P2 PASS) is reused, not
      repeated.
    - If real ticks make either strategy's Sharpe negative or
      economically dead, this is reported in full with the same
      directness as every null result in this project.
    - VWAP MR, meta-coordinator, calendar-effects, and meta-labeling
      Model=4 revalidations remain OUT OF SCOPE (separate future
      work). No downstream SPEC is re-run or re-adjudicated here;
      the combined-implications section only flags stale-vs-robust.
