REVALIDATION_REPORT_CHILDREN
Standalone Model=4 (real-tick) revalidation of ORB and SP2L
(two independent execution-layer re-measurements; not new hypotheses)

Status: written 2026-09-02 after the pre-declared runs, per
SPEC_TICK_MODEL_REVALIDATION_CHILDREN.txt and
GATE_TICK_MODEL_REVALIDATION_CHILDREN.txt (O1-O3, S1-S3 extracted
before any script ran). All 12 batches (6 ORB M5 + 6 SP2L M1) ran on
real ticks ("generating based on real ticks", "real ticks begin from
2021.01.03" in every batch) and passed. The EAs, original
REPORTs/RECONCILEs, and all prior revalidation artifacts are
untouched.

VERDICT IN ONE LINE: ORB and SP2L remain NET-POSITIVE under real
ticks but retain only a fraction of their documented Model=0 edge
(ORB net -75.6%, Sharpe 0.337 -> 0.074; SP2L net -50.8%,
common-universe Sharpe 0.275 -> 0.133); reconciliation agreement
drops below the pre-declared bars (ORB 70.4% < 90%; SP2L 84.3% <
85%), with SP2L's documented bad 2025 WORSENING (-260.25 -> -341.68)
and its per-trade expectancy now ~5x SMALLER than measured real
execution costs. The strategies' "validated" status was a
synthetic-tick-execution statement; under real-tick execution both
are marginal.

different stop/target fill paths on real wicks (the diag_20240419
class, in bulk) - exactly the classes the reconciles now show.

4. COMBINED IMPLICATIONS - STALE vs ROBUST (no re-adjudication here)

New real-tick-grounded baselines this revalidation establishes:
  ORB : +114.91, 437 trades, Sharpe 0.0745, maxDD 7.92%, ret/DD 0.145
  SP2L: +138.65, 1,349 trades, common-universe Sharpe 0.133,
        maxDD 5.13%, 2025 -341.68

POTENTIALLY STALE (verdict depended on Model=0 ORB/SP2L numbers that
this revalidation shows were inflated by synthetic-tick execution):
  - The ORB and SP2L validation REPORTs' headline stats (ORB 0.337/
    5.13%; SP2L 0.275/4.17%) as statements of EXECUTED performance.
    They remain accurate as Model=0 records; as realism baselines
    they are superseded by the numbers above.
  - Static portfolio baselines S1/S2/S3 and the portfolio study's
    combined book (built on Model=0 child streams).
  - Meta-coordinator Phase-1 E-gates (E1's "> every child's Sharpe >
    0.337" bar and its child baselines were Model=0 products; its
    Phase-1 numbers were Python-engine-based but its CHILD inputs
    and eligibility rationale lean on the Model=0 validations).
    Note: the meta-coordinator FAILED anyway on E4 concentration -
    see robust list.
  - Regime rotation's Phase-2 G1 reference framing ("ORB 0.337 the
    current best") and any future work citing 0.337/0.275 as "the
    strategies' Sharpe".
  - SP2L ablation/sensitivity conclusions insofar as they are quoted
    as executed (they are offline Python-engine results and stand as
    such).

ROBUST (verdict survives the correction; no re-litigation needed):
  - Regime rotation Phase-2 FAIL: already failed under Model=0 AND
    the Model=4 re-run (worse). Unchanged.
  - Meta-coordinator FAIL: failed on E4 (2023 concentration ~73%),
    a structural concentration failure, not a close-call against the
    0.337 bar. A lower real baseline would not rescue it.
  - Calendar effects NULL: OVERNIGHT failed O1/O2 outright (not
    significant, financing-dominated); DOW failed D1 decisively
    (Monday REVERSED). The O4 gate's 0.337 bar never activated.
  - Meta-labeling NULL: failed M5 (total improvement NEGATIVE -
    degenerate half-sizing); the M1 bar (0.275) change is
    irrelevant to that failure mode.
  - VWAP MR NULL (negative expectancy), session-transfer NULL,
    multi-horizon momentum NULL, trend-pullback NULL, Al Brooks
    signal-quality null: all failed decisively under conditions at
    least as favorable as Model=0; a worse real baseline cannot
    rescue them.
  - The OFFLINE Python-engine results generally: the Python engine
    always ran on the real-tick-derived M1/M5 exports, so its
    results (including regime rotation Phase 1) are unaffected by
    the tester tick-model correction - only EXECUTED (MT5) numbers
    change meaning.

Bottom line, stated plainly: the project's only two individually
validated strategies do NOT retain their documented edge under
real-tick execution. Both remain slightly net-positive (ORB +114.91,
SP2L +138.65 over 5.3 years on a $10k base) with Sharpe 0.07-0.13 -
economically marginal, with SP2L's edge smaller than its measured
execution-cost noise and ORB's edge concentrated in 2022-2023. Any
future deployment, portfolio, or coordination work must use the
Model=4 numbers above as the executed-performance baseline, and the
out-of-scope revalidations (VWAP MR, meta-coordinator, calendar,
meta-labeling) remain available as separate pre-declared work -
though every one of their verdicts already fails or nulls under
conditions no better than Model=0.
