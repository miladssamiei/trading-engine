LUCID GC H56 50K FLEX - PREDECLARED ACCOUNT TRANSFER SPEC
Date: 2026-09-26. Written before this transfer evaluator or its outputs.

PURPOSE
Test whether the frozen, corrected C_H56_M1 gold proxy ledger could clear a
Lucid 50K Flex evaluation under optimistic account arithmetic. This is a
diagnostic, not a native GC backtest, live recommendation or new H56 verdict.
Preserve all prior H56 and futures proxy SPEC/GATE/REPORT artifacts.

INPUT AND RULE
Read only the GC / C_H56_M1 rows in the immutable V2 futures intraday
remeasurement trades.csv, in entry-time order. H56 is the three-completed-M1-
candle aligned expansion with a >1.0 gold-unit gap and middle body >1.5x
neighbors. Later pullback at the M1 bar extreme, EMA60 direction, ADX14 >=20,
NY entry [01:00,05:00), structural pre-spike stop, 1R target, at most one
position, no overnight hold. Do not refit or select parameters on this ledger.
All rows must have entry/exit on the same New York weekday and exit strictly
before 16:45 America/New_York; any violation fails the time gate.

DATA LIMITS
The signal and exits were measured on XAUUSD bid-only M1 bars, not a CME GC
contract, quotes, market orders, depth, roll-adjusted continuous future or
Lucid platform fills. M1-bar-extreme entry is optimistic and intraminute
path is unknown. The proxy is fully exposed 2020-2026 and not a prospective
walk-forward confirmation. These missing sources fail the native evidence
gate regardless of favorable account arithmetic.

ACCOUNT AND COST
Assume Lucid 50K Flex evaluation, no optional daily-loss limit. Starting
balance $50,000; profit target $3,000; max loss $2,000; 50% largest positive
day / account profit consistency; max 4 minis or 40 micros. EOD closing
balance high-water mark trails by $2,000 until it locks at $50,100 when a
closing balance exceeds $52,100. Evaluate the active MLL after each trade and
at each day close. Conservative pretrade safety check: if balance minus the
trade's structural stop loss reaches the active MLL, count as breach before
entry. This checks risk reserve, not actual historical floating equity.
The true floating path and margin remain unverified.

GC = 100 troy ounces and MGC = 10. Both tick by $0.10/oz. Lucid lists
commission $2.30/GC side, $0.80/MGC side. Assume exactly one tick bid/ask
spread, zero slippage and no extra fee: GC cost 0.146 gold units/contract
round trip, MGC 0.26. This is a scenario floor, not observed spread. Derive
proxy net dollars as (ledger gross - scenario cost - extra adverse cost) x
contract ounces x count. Gross is only the proxy ledger price change; this
does not re-run brackets under new costs. Run the complete fixed grid:
  1 GC, 1-tick spread, 0 extra; 1 GC, 1-tick, 0.5 extra gold units;
  2 GC, 1-tick, 0 extra; 5 MGC, 1-tick, 0 extra.
The 0.5 extra case represents five additional gold price ticks round trip;
it is a diagnostic stress, not an empirical fill estimate.

ROLLING EVALUATION
Use every unique entry day from 2020 through the last entry day that leaves
a complete 180-calendar-day horizon before the data end. A start uses all
trades with entry day >= start and < start+180 calendar days, chronological.
Start balance and MLL reset for each start. Pass at the first closed trade
where balance >=$53,000 and the largest positive daily closed PnL divided by
total account profit is <=0.5. A breach at or below active MLL occurs first.
Loss check uses dollar stop bound: long risk = initial_risk+scenario cost+
extra cost; short risk = initial_risk+extra cost (the structural ask stop
already includes the scenario spread), multiplied by ounces and count.
Track session daily PnL for consistency. Update EOD MLL only after the last
trade of the NY day. Zero-trade days do not change its high watermark.
If neither pass nor breach by horizon end, label timeout. Also report full-
ledger mean, annual sums, and 180-day pass/breach/timeout for all four rows.
No order, vendor purchase or historical artifact overwrite is authorized.

GATES AND HONESTY

runner. A row can only be called a diagnostic PASS if all numerical gates
pass. Overall GO additionally needs genuine CME point-in-time GC Bid/Ask or
