ALFONSO SUPPLY AND DEMAND PHASE 1, REQUESTED HYPOTHESIS #94
Frozen before the evaluation script or any Phase 1 run, 2026-09-28.
The project already uses #94 for us100_weekday_hold_h94. This study's unique identity is alfonso_snd; the reused number is only the owner's requested label.

Purpose: Test a fixed algorithmic interpretation of Alfonso Moreno-style first-touch supply and demand on three retrospective BID M1 proxy tapes. This is exploratory proxy research, not a claim of discretionary-method fidelity or MT5 execution validation. Null results are final for these artifacts.

Inputs: XAUUSD data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv, US500 data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv, EURUSD data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv. Costs embedded once as constant BID/ASK differences: 0.585 gold units, 0.51 index points, 0.00007 FX price units (0.70 pip). All results use native price units; EURUSD displays pips. No swap, slippage, commission beyond the given all-inclusive floors, or position sizing. Source date bounds are the available verified tapes, no extrapolation.

Grids: Epoch-aligned UTC M5/M15/H1/H4, plus D1 only to support the H4 baseline curve/trend. Aggregate observed M1 OHLC, then fieldwise forward-fill missing buckets. Filled buckets influence causal indicators and time indexing but cannot be base, departure, pivot or trade-signal bars. A bar closes at the start of its next UTC bucket; any action starts at the next available observed M1 open. ATR14 is Wilder EWM with first finite seed after 14 true ranges on the continuous execution grid.

Zone definition: A real base is an immediately preceding maximal run of 1 through 6 real candles with body/range <=0.50; a zero-range candle is not a base. Runs above six invalidate the entire run. Its immediately following real departure candle has body/range >=0.80 and directional close beyond the base's opposite wick (demand close above base high, supply close below base low). The departure close also must break either the latest confirmed opposing 2-left/2-right swing pivot (strict inequality, pivot available only from its second following completed bar) or the distal of a previously validated, still unmitigated opposing zone. A candidate needs finite positive ATR14 at departure. Demand proximal=max of base real-body highs, distal=min base wicks; supply proximal=min real-body lows, distal=max base wicks. Reject nonpositive width. Zone validation is at departure close. No retroactive use of its base or departure for mitigation. Each zone is unmitigated until its first later observed bar reaches proximal: BID low<=proximal for demand, BID high>=proximal for supply. First touch retires it, regardless of order fill. A close beyond distal also retires it as broken. Prior live opposing zones are checked before processing a new departure.

Sequences: SWING H4 curve/H1 trend/M15 execution; INTRADAY H1 curve/M15 trend/M5 execution; H1_BASE H4 curve and trend/H1 execution; H4_BASE D1 curve and trend/H4 execution. For each execution zone at validation, use only completed HTF bars strictly preceding validation. The curve requires nearest live HTF demand with proximal below current BID close and nearest live HTF supply with proximal above it; their proximal prices define [demand,supply], positive width. A long requires position <=0.33; short >=0.67. No curve means no order. HTF trend is event based: up requires at least one prior demand respect (first touch with completed close back above demand proximal) and one prior supply break (completed close above supply distal), each more recent than the latest bearish event; down reverses supply respect and demand break. A first touch closing through distal is a break, not respect. All other states are flat. Event times are completed trend-grid bar times; event state is carried to later execution-zone validations. For H1_BASE/H4_BASE curve and trend share the same HTF; no current incomplete HTF state is used.

Order and replay: At zone validation, create one independent pending order per R:R profile 1,2,3,4,5 only if curve and trend permit the side. Long entry ASK=proximal, so observed BID low must reach proximal-cost; short entry BID=proximal, so BID high must reach proximal. A proximal BID touch that does not fill the long ASK limit cancels the order as mitigated. The first eligible observed M1 bar after validation determines touch, and filled entries use the limit price, except a favorable gap at M1 open fills at the better open quote. Entry stop is distal minus 0.20*execution ATR for long, distal plus 0.20*ATR for short, in executable BID/ASK coordinates. Initial risk is fill-to-stop distance, including the entry spread; reject nonpositive risk. Target is entry plus/minus R:R*initial executable risk. Reject an order if its target would cross the nearest opposing execution-timeframe zone proximal available at validation; a missing opposing zone imposes no cap. Targets and stops remain fixed. M1 adverse stops take priority if both bracket levels are inside the same bar; adverse gaps fill at the M1 open. On the entry M1, an adverse stop may execute but a target is not credited because the OHLC order is unknown. End-of-tape open positions are marked but excluded from closed-trade gates. Only one open position per asset/sequence/R:R cell; zones whose first fill would occur while occupied are shadowed and never reused. Pending orders can coexist; each zone has at most one first touch. No fill before zone validation.

Statistics: Per asset/sequence/R:R: closed trade count, win rate, profit factor (infinite if no losses and positive gains; undefined if no trades), gross and net mean per trade, unannualized net mean/std(ddof=1), 10,000 moving-block bootstrap resamples of the ordered trade net PnL with block length 5 and seed 20260928. P is the fraction of bootstrap means <=0. Report yearly entry-cohort counts and net totals, full years through 2025 and partial 2026 separately. If fewer than five closed trades, MBB p is undefined and gate fails. If fewer than two or zero variance, Sharpe undefined and gate fails.

GATES (verbatim copied to GATE_ALFONSO_SND.txt):
G1: Each asset/sequence/R:R cell requires at least 150 closed trades.
G2: Each asset/sequence/R:R cell requires net expectancy > 0 after the fixed retail cost floor.
G3: Each asset/sequence/R:R cell requires unannualized per-trade net Sharpe > 0.
G4: Each asset/sequence/R:R cell requires MBB P(mean net <= 0) <= 0.05 with 10000 resamples, block length 5, seed 20260928.
G5: Each asset/sequence/R:R cell requires no negative net entry-cohort total in a full calendar year covered by the tape (XAUUSD/EURUSD 2020-2025; US500 2021-2025); partial 2026 is disclosed, not gated.
G6: No cell is called validated on this retrospective Phase 1 proxy alone. A cell passing G1-G5 is eligible only for a separately predeclared execution/forward validation.

Honesty and stop rules: No model-family, pivot-window, threshold, cost, ratio or timeframe search after seeing results. Report all 60 cells, every null result and all entry/exit ledgers. Never overwrite this SPEC, GATE or final REPORT. A correction requires a new named study. BID M1 candles cannot establish within-minute path or broker-specific fill probability; results are proxy estimates. The same data were previously used in project research, so positive cells are not independent discovery evidence.
