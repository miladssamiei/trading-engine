HYPOTHESIS 62: EURUSD INTRADAY ATR LOWER-BAND LIMIT TRANSFER
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. PROVENANCE AND CLAIM
Transfer the exact long-only H22 ATR lower-band buy-limit rule as audited
in Hypothesis 61 to EURUSD without tuning its ATR, band, stop, target,
side, order lifetime, occupancy, or M1 path convention. Hypothesis 61
failed its frozen US500 and XAUUSD gates and stays failed. This is a
new-instrument replication, not a rescue of either earlier artifact.
EURUSD's imported tick tape and 08:00-11:00 America/New_York overlap
session have verified M1/M5 integrity and a median 0.3-pip spread.
The historical tape has been available to the project: a PASS is a
historical proxy finding, not a virgin or deployable result.

2. DATA, SESSION, AND UNITS
Read Bid OHLC M5 from data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m5.csv
and Bid OHLC M1 from data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv.
Convert epoch UTC to America/New_York, including DST. No forward-fill.
A session date requires an exact 08:00 M5 bar and an exact 10:59 M1
bar. Entry orders may originate on M5 bars opening [08:00,10:55]
wall time inclusive and expire before the next M5 open. Exit by the
exact 10:59 M1 close. Fixed spread S=0.00003 price = 0.3 pip;
1 pip=0.00010 price. Report all PnL in pips, one unit of EURUSD,
no compounding and no swap because trades are intraday.

3. CAUSAL INDICATOR AND ENTRY
From the complete M5 tape compute TR[0]=H[0]-L[0], later TR as the
maximum of H-L, |H-prior close|, |L-prior close|. Replicate the H22
source seed ATR[14]=mean(TR[0:15]); thereafter Wilder
ATR[i]=(13*ATR[i-1]+TR[i])/14. For eligible M5 bar i, use only the
completed i-1 close and ATR: Ask buy-limit L=CloseBid[i-1]
-3.1*ATR[i-1]. A fixed-spread Ask proxy touches only if a printed
M1 LowBid+S<=L in that M5 bar. Fill at L, even when the minute opens
below L (no favorable gap improvement). No printed M1 touch means
unfilled order. At most one open trade and one pending order;
skip signals during occupancy. After an exit minute, the earliest
new signal M5 open is strictly later than that exit M1 open.

4. EXIT AND COST
Initial Bid stop=L-1.5*entry ATR; Bid target=L+2.0*entry ATR.
Walk printed M1 bars from fill through the forced-exit minute.
Check stop before target. Entry-minute stop fills at stop; later
gap-through stops fill at min(stop, M1 OpenBid). Target fills at its
level. On the entry minute, allow the target only when OpenBid+S<=L;
otherwise the high may predate the limit fill, so defer target.
If no bracket fires, exit at exact 10:59 M1 Bid close. Gross pips
are (exitBid-(L-S))/0.00010; base net pips=(exitBid-L)/0.00010;
double-cost stress pips=base net-0.3 on the same trades. This fixed
Ask proxy cannot prove exact real Ask fills or within-minute path.

5. PARTITION AND UNCERTAINTY
Dates before 2024-01-01 are diagnostic only. The gated historical
block is 2024-01-01 through the tape end (2026-09-18), by NY entry
date; 2026 is partial. Report funnel, all years, trade count,
gross/base/double-cost mean and total, win rate, max drawdown,
exit types, holding minutes, and same-entry-minute exit share.
Resample gated trade dates with replacement 10,000 times, retaining
all trades on each sampled date, RNG seed 20260928. Report one-sided
P(mean base net <=0) and descriptive 95% bootstrap quantiles.

6. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-EAB1: At least 150 executed trades in the 2024-2026 gated block.
G-EAB2: Strictly positive mean double-cost net pips per trade in the gated block.
G-EAB3: Date-bootstrap P(mean base net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10,000 resamples, seed 20260928.
G-EAB4: Strictly positive base-net total pips in each complete gated year, 2024 and 2025.
Overall Phase-1 PASS requires every gate. Otherwise FAIL is final
for this artifact. No post-hoc month, year, minute, exit type, or
trade-direction subset can rescue a FAIL.

7. STOP RULE AND ARTIFACTS
On FAIL, stop without an MT5 tester run. On PASS, separately freeze
a Model=4 real Bid/Ask tick execution SPEC/GATE before any tester
run. It must require >=150 reconciled trades, positive net expectancy,
year-sign agreement, and order-state reconciliation. A Phase-1 PASS
alone is not a validated intraday system. Save this SPEC, verbatim
GATE, code, trade CSV, metadata, and REPORT even on FAIL. Never edit
existing historical SPEC/GATE/REPORT documents.
