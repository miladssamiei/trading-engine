FTMO TWO-STEP GOLD TRADE-R MONTE CARLO - PREDECLARED SPEC - 2026-09-28
Purpose: simulate the owner's simplified FTMO 10k two-step evaluation and a 30-trade first payout window from the already-executed strict-cost gold A/B proxy ledgers. This is an expected-value scenario, not an estimate of actual FTMO qualification odds. The engines are retrospective BID-proxy studies with failed year-sign robustness gates, not live-verified account edges. No account is purchased or traded.

INPUTS: Read data/results/gold_ensemble_real_cost/trades_A_KC_strict.csv and trades_B_DONCHIAN_strict.csv, the 0.585 all-inclusive cost M1 replay. Verify 190 A and 564 B records only as minimum historical expectations, and preserve actual counts and SHA256. Require finite net, side, entry_fill, entry_bid, initial_stop, cost and exact fill accounting; cost=0.585 once. Use all 754 executed trades, 2020-2026, pool uniformly at the trade level (therefore A/B selection weights equal their observed counts, not 50/50 engine weights). Sampling with replacement is IID and ignores calendar/regime dependence, simultaneous positions, account leverage, and strategy decay.

R DEFINITION: Exact initial stop-fill risk is available, so do not approximate from an average risk distance. Long risk in gold units = entry_fill - initial_stop (BID stop). A short risk = (initial_stop + 0.585) - entry_fill because A stores a BID-coordinate stop converted to an ASK stop. B short risk = initial_stop - entry_fill because B stores the ASK stop itself. Require all denominators >0. Empirical R=trade net gold units / that positive initial loss. Gap losses may have R<-1. Report per-engine and pooled R mean/median/range, denominator mean and frequency of R<-1. This R normalization replaces unknown variable lot size while retaining asymmetric trade outcomes.

ACCOUNT MODEL: Owner-defined $10,000 initial simulated capital; fee $175 paid once per purchased challenge; 80% profit split; fee refunded only on first positive funded payout. In all phases use fixed initial-capital risk cash per trade: 0.5%=$50, 1%=$100, 2%=$200, with no compounding; trade cash PnL = risk cash * sampled R. Closed-equity cumulative PnL starts at zero separately in Phase 1, Phase 2 and funded phase. Phase 1 passes at >=+$1,000 and fails at <=-$1,000. After a Phase 1 pass, Phase 2 resets to zero, passes at >=+$500 and fails at <=-$1,000. After both pass, funded PnL resets to zero; draw exactly 30 trades unless closed equity first reaches <=-$1,000, which means zero payout. At trade 30, if funded PnL >0 and no blow, gross received = 0.80*positive funded PnL + $175 fee refund; otherwise received=0. Net EV per challenge purchased = mean(gross received across all initial paths)-$175. Profit targets and loss thresholds are checked after each closed trade, never on intratrade equity. A phase that has not hit either boundary after 20,000 trades is unresolved and counted as no pass; report the count. Seed 20260928 and exactly 10,000 independently sampled paths for EACH risk scenario.

STATISTICS: Report Phase 1 pass probability; Phase 2 pass probability conditional on Phase 1 and unconditional from purchase; probability of a first positive payout from purchase; funded positive-payout conditional probability; gross expected receipt, fee, net EV; Monte Carlo standard error and 95% normal interval for net EV conditional on the empirical distribution; phase trade-count medians/p90s, funded blow rate and unresolved paths. Calculate model-implied minimum account count n=ceil(log(0.05)/log(1-p_first_payout)) when 0<p<1, with p=0 -> no finite n and p=1 ->1. This attains at least 95% in the IID Bernoulli model only; it is not a guarantee or a purchase recommendation. Report binomial Monte Carlo uncertainty for p and note account outcomes will be correlated if the same strategy/regimes are traded.

OFFICIAL-RULE GAP: FTMO 2-Step's official objectives include a 5% maximum DAILY equity loss, a 10% static maximum loss, 10%/5% phase targets and minimum trading days. The user specified the static loss and targets but no daily sequencing; an IID closed-trade R sampler cannot enforce daily equity or intratrade floating-loss limits, minimum trading days, news/holding restrictions, payout timing/eligibility, variable swap or actual fees. Consequently all pass, payout and EV numbers are for this simplified scenario and may be materially optimistic for a real FTMO account. The owner-specified $175 fee is a scenario input, not a verified current price. FTMO official references: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/ and https://ftmo.com/en/faq/is-the-entry-fee-refunded/ .
