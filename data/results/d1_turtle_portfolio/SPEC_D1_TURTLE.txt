Null results and failed gates are complete results. No parameter search or post-result changes to this SPEC, GATE or REPORT.

DAILY GRID: Interpret M1 timestamps as bar opens in UTC; convert to America/New_York wall time with DST. Bar labeled day D contains M1 opens in [D-1 17:00 ET,D 17:00 ET). This is the M1-open implementation of the Hypothesis #42 17:00 ET close. Group real M1 OHLC by this session; exclude weekend labels and daily buckets with fewer than 300 observed M1 bars. Open=first real M1 open, high=max BID high, low=min BID low, close=last real M1 close. A weekday with missing/short activity is not forward-filled into an executable daily bar. Record first/last UTC and day counts. The first/last partial source days are retained only if they meet this threshold; disclose it.

COSTS AND PIPS: USDJPY 0.009 (0.9 pip), GBPJPY 0.025 (2.5 pips), EURJPY 0.015 (1.5 pips), EURUSD 0.00003 (0.3 pip). JPY pip=0.01, EURUSD pip=0.0001. BID tape; fixed ASK=BID+cost. Long entry ASK and exit BID; short entry BID and exit ASK. Cost embedded once; net pips must equal gross BID-coordinate pips minus floor pips. No swap, variable spread, slippage, lot sizing, cross-currency conversion or FTMO account guards; overnight holding is disclosed.

INDICATORS: EMA50 on completed daily closes, pandas adjust=False, first valid after 50 daily closes. Wilder ATR20: TR=max(H-L,abs(H-prior C),abs(L-prior C)), first TR=H-L, seed with mean of first 20 TR values, then (19*prior ATR+TR)/20. Entry Donchian20 for completed signal day t uses strictly prior 20 daily highs/lows t-20..t-1, excluding signal day. Long if Close_t > prior20High and Close_t > EMA50_t; short if Close_t < prior20Low and Close_t < EMA50_t. Signal at t close, entry at first real M1 open of next accepted daily bucket t+1. No trade at a synthetic bar, one position per asset, overlapping signals ignored while occupied.

INITIAL STOP AND TRAILING EXIT: Entry prices use the BID/ASK fill above. Long initial BID stop=entry ASK-2*ATR20_t; short initial ASK stop=entry BID+2*ATR20_t. Thus tradable-fill initial risk is exactly 2*ATR20_t. Stop stays fixed. Walk M1 chronologically from the entry minute: long BID low<=stop, short ASK high>=stop; adverse opening gaps fill at the M1 open BID/ASK, otherwise at the stop. This hard stop takes priority over a pending daily trailing exit at the same M1 open. After a completed daily bar j closes with no hard stop, trigger Turtle harvest if long Close_j < the low of strictly prior ten daily bars j-10..j-1, or short Close_j > their high; fill at first real M1 open of next accepted daily bucket j+1. The trailing level is a signal threshold, not a limit/stop fill price. If a hard stop and daily close breach occur in the same day, the M1 stop wins. No TP, stop ratchet, optional exit, or forced end-of-tape close. Unclosed last trade is OPEN_END, excluded from closed-trade statistics but retained in its ledger. Any trailing signal with no next executable daily open remains OPEN_END.

SCORING: Replay the available full source chronology to preserve warmup and occupancy. Primary metrics and annual matrix use trades entered from 2020-01-01 through the available 2026 source end, cohort by NY session entry day. This makes JPY 2010-2019 warmup and earlier trades unscored, and EURUSD 2020 initial EMA warmup explicit. Report each source's differing last date. Closed-trade count, net win rate, net expectancy and total in pips, exit mix (initial hard stop vs ten-day daily-close trailing), unannualized per-trade net Sharpe=mean/std(ddof=1), and moving-block bootstrap P(mean net<=0): 10000 resamples, block=5 consecutive closed trades, seed=20261020, sampling enough overlapping blocks with replacement then trim to n trades. Fewer than five trades gives undefined p. Full calendar-year sign gate applies only 2020-2025; 2026 is partial. Save full scored ledgers, annual and summary CSVs, provenance metadata and a plain-text REPORT.
