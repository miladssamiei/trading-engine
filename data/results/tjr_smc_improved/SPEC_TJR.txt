Requested label Hypothesis #95 collides with existing us100_vix_weekday_h95. Unique study identity is tjr_smc_improved; the reused number is only the owner's requested label.

Purpose: Test one fixed causal interpretation of the supplied TJR Smart Money Concept rule on two retrospective BID M1 proxy tapes. This is not a claim that discretionary chart annotation, actual ASK quotes or broker fills have been reproduced. Null results are final for these artifacts.

Inputs: XAUUSD data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv (2020-2026), US500 data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv (2021-2026). Verify sorted unique finite well-formed M1 BID OHLC. Cost floors are XAUUSD 0.585 price units and US500 0.51 index points, embedded once as constant ASK=BID+cost. No separate cost deduction after fill. No swap, slippage or account sizing; report NY-date overnight holds so the zero-financing limit is visible.

Grids: Aggregate observed M1 bars into UTC-epoch-aligned XAUUSD M5 and US500 M15 OHLC. Reindex to a continuous grid and fieldwise forward-fill missing whole buckets to preserve time spacing, but a filled bucket cannot be a swing pivot, sweep or BoS bar. Swing High at completed pivot bar p requires its high strictly greater than each of the six immediately preceding and two immediately following grid-bar highs; Swing Low requires its low strictly below the corresponding eight lows. Require all nine bars to be real. A pivot is first available after the second following bar has completed, so a sweep bar uses only the latest pivot available at its open. Ties are not swings.

Sweep: On a real completed grid bar, bullish sweep is low strictly below the last available swing low and close strictly above that pivot low; bearish sweep is high strictly above the last available swing high and close strictly below that pivot high. Freeze the opposing pivot level available at the sweep bar open as the later BoS threshold. A missing opposing pivot prevents arming. Wick stop is the sweep bar's actual extreme. If a bar sweeps both sides, cancel pending setups and do not arm either. A new same-direction sweep replaces an older pending setup; an opposite-direction sweep cancels the older setup. Pending bullish setup invalidates if a later bar's low pierces below its sweep wick before BoS; pending bearish setup invalidates if a later high pierces above its sweep wick. Invalidation has priority on a BoS bar. No arbitrary bar-count expiry for XAUUSD; pending setups remain until replaced, invalidated or confirmed. US500 pending state resets at each New York cash-session open.

BoS: Earliest one real grid bar after the sweep. Bullish BoS is a close strictly above the frozen most recent opposing swing high; bearish BoS is a close strictly below the frozen opposing swing low. US500 permits only bullish sweep/BoS, while XAUUSD permits both. A confirmed BoS generates one entry candidate and consumes the pending setup. On US500, both the sweep and BoS bar must be entirely inside cash [09:30,16:00) America/New_York. The next M1 entry timestamp at the BoS bar end must also be strictly before 16:00 NY. The pivot source may include prior real overnight M15 bars. XAUUSD has no session filter.

Execution: Enter exactly at the observed M1 open whose timestamp equals the BoS bar end; if that exact bar is missing, skip (do not move the signal to a later minute). Long fill is ASK open=BID open+cost, short fill is BID open. Long stop is the sweep BID low, short stop is the sweep high in ASK execution coordinates. The short stop triggers when M1 BID high+cost reaches that sweep-high level, making the strict spread effect explicit. Reject nonpositive executable initial risk; long R=entry ASK-long BID stop, short R=short ASK stop-entry BID. Maximum one open trade per asset; candidates whose exact entry M1 is on/before the existing trade's exit M1 are shadowed. The initial stop and future ratchets are fixed executable quote levels.

M1 walk and ratchet: Check the existing stop first on each real M1 bar, including entry bar. Long stop checks BID low and fills at min(stop,BID open); short stop checks ASK high and fills at max(stop,ASK open). This gives adverse stop priority over any favorable excursion in the same M1. If not stopped, update MFE using long BID high-entry ASK or short entry BID-ASK low. When floor(MFE/R)>=1 after that M1 completes, ratchet the stop to entry executable fill plus direction*(floor(MFE/R)-1)*R, never loosening it. The new level is effective only on the next observed M1 bar; there is no within-M1 favorable-first assumption. Thus step 1 is net break-even, step 2 locks +1R, step 3 locks +2R, and so on. No fixed target or time exit. A still-open position at the final M1 is marked OPEN_END and excluded from closed-trade gates. Record maximum ratchet step, MFE, stop reason, M1 hold time, year and overnight status.

Statistics per asset: closed count, net win fraction, gross mean using BID entry/exit, net mean from executable fills, net total, unannualized per-trade net Sharpe=mean/std(ddof=1), 10,000 moving-block bootstrap resamples of ordered trade net PnL with five-trade blocks and seed 20261012; p is fraction with mean<=0. Less than five closed trades yields undefined p; less than two/zero variance yields undefined Sharpe. Report annual entry-cohort counts/net PnL, signal funnel and full trade ledger. A positive Sharpe is described but is not an extra gate beyond the owner's thresholds. No post-result threshold or cost change.
