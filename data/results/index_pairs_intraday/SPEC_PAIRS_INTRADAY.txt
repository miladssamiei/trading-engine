1. DATA AND ALIGNMENT
  1.1 Inner join of the two M1 tapes on the exact epoch minute timestamp; a
      joined bar exists iff BOTH instruments printed that minute. Minutes
      where either feed is silent are dropped (counted in the funnel).
  1.2 Cash day = NY date with a printed joined 09:30 bar (minute-of-day 570),
      per project convention. Session bars: minute-of-day [570, 955]
      (09:30..15:55). Bars after 15:55 are not used by this study.
  1.3 Session-open anchor = the 09:30 bar opens of both legs. All metrics
      reset daily at the 09:30 open.

2. STATE MACHINE (INTRADAY SPREAD DIVERGENCE, per NY day)
  2.1 Cumulative session returns: Ret_500(t) = Close_500(t)/Open_500(09:30)-1;
      Ret_30(t) likewise.
  2.2 Raw spread: Spread(t) = Ret_500(t) - Ret_30(t).
  2.3 Rolling statistics over the trailing 60 PRINTED joined session bars
      (inclusive of the current bar, min_periods 60; the tape skips silent
      minutes so window length is bar count, not wall minutes):
      Mean_60, StdDev_60 (sample, ddof=1).
  2.4 Z(t) = (Spread(t) - Mean_60)/StdDev_60 where StdDev_60 > 1e-12; bars
      with StdDev_60 <= 1e-12 or fewer than 60 in-window observations are
      UNARMED (no Z, cannot signal).
  2.5 Arming: Z first exists at the 60th printed session bar (~10:29 under a
      complete tape); the signal window opens strictly after 10:30 regardless.

3. SIGNAL GENERATION (RELATIVE VALUE MEAN REVERSION)
  3.1 Signal window: joined session bars with minute-of-day in [631, 929]
      (strictly between 10:30 and 15:30 ET).
  3.2 SHORT_DIV (US500 outperforming): Z(t) > +2.0 -> short US500 / long
      USA30. LONG_DIV (USA30 outperforming): Z(t) < -2.0 -> long US500 /
      short USA30.
  3.3 Execution cap: the FIRST armed |Z| breach in the window wins; maximum
      1 pair trade per day; drops never re-arm. Days without a breach
      produce no trade.
  3.4 Entry: the opens of the next PRINTED joined bar after the signal bar,
      both legs simultaneously. If that bar lies beyond minute-of-day 955,
      the candidate is dropped as no-entry.

4. EXCURSION WALK AND ECONOMICS (GROSS, PRE-COST)
  4.1 Walk forward from the entry bar until the FIRST of:
      (a) REVERSION: the first bar t (entry bar evaluable) where the evolving
      Z(t) crosses to the entry-opposite side of zero - Z(t) <= 0 for
      SHORT_DIV, Z(t) >= 0 for LONG_DIV; exit at that bar's close;
      (b) TIME: the 15:55 bar (minute-of-day 955); exit at its close.
  4.2 Leg returns from entry: r_500(t) = Close_500(t)/EntryOpen_500 - 1;
      r_30(t) likewise.
  4.3 Pair PnL (unlevered, $10,000 allocated per leg): pnl(t) =
      $10,000 * (d500*r_500(t) + d30*r_30(t)) with d = +1 long / -1 short.
  4.4 Pair MFE = max pnl(t) over walk bars; Pair MAE = -min pnl(t). Computed
      on per-bar CLOSES: synchronous intrabar extremes of two instruments
      are not observable from M1 OHLC, and the reversion exit is close-based.
  4.5 Exit PnL = pnl at the exit bar close, signed.
  4.6 Cost floor (descriptive only, never netted into gates): 0.51 pt US500
      and 2.28 pt USA30 CFD spreads -> round-trip pair cost in $ on the
      $10k-per-leg basis, computed at the median signal-date closes of each
      leg, reported alongside the gross edge.

5. GATES (frozen verbatim in GATE_PAIRS_INTRADAY.txt)
  G-P1 total session-valid signals >= 150.
  G-P2 aggregate mean Pair MFE > mean Pair MAE (strict, $).
  G-P3 day-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 20260924) over NY-date
      blocks of P(pooled mean(MFE - MAE) <= 0) <= 0.20.
