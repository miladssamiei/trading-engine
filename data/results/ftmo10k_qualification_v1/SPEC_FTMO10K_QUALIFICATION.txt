FTMO 10k qualification and offline accounting foundations v1
Frozen 2026-09-11 before new implementation. Infrastructure milestone; no alpha hypothesis number, model fit, performance study, or execution authorization.

PURPOSE AND BOUNDARY
Build a local-file import/audit CLI and pure offline risk/accounting functions for the owner's stated FTMO USD 10000 2-Step Swing first Challenge. Stated account type is not authenticated broker evidence. No MT5 initialize/login, network connection, order, process management, dependency install, EA change or deployment. Existing studies and historical verdicts stay unchanged. Current milestone cannot promote a strategy or automatically authenticate a supplied file.

ARTIFACTS AND INTERFACES
configs/prop/ftmo_2step_swing_10k_v1.json is an offline policy and unresolved identity manifest. evaluate/ftmo10k_qualification_v1.py implements snapshot/quote import, hashes and audits. evaluate/ftmo_account_replay_v1.py implements independent pure functions with tests in tests/test_ftmo10k_qualification_v1.py and tests/test_ftmo_account_replay_v1.py. Both modules use Python standard library only; installed timezone data may be used by zoneinfo. Required timezone availability failure is explicit, never a fixed-offset fallback.
CLI qualification inputs: --manifest PATH, optional --snapshot PATH, optional --quotes PATH, required --output-dir NEW_PATH, and optional --as-of ISO8601_UTC. Missing snapshot yields a written INCOMPLETE audit with exit 2. Invalid supplied data yields FAIL with exit 1. Structurally valid but independently unverified evidence is INCOMPLETE with exit 2. Output directories and archival artifacts must never be overwritten. Raw bytes are archived with SHA256 when supplied, subject to credential-field rejection before archival. No full account IDs, passwords, tokens or login details are emitted to console or audit output; retain only a supplied non-secret account fingerprint and server identity. Snapshot schema documentation/template is separate from authentic evidence.
Snapshot JSON schema version 1: provenance.source_kind is ftmo_export or synthetic_fixture; source and received_at UTC; account has account_fingerprint, server, broker, platform, currency, initial_balance, program, account_type, phase, captured_at UTC, balance, equity; symbol has name, contract_id, quote_currency, profit_currency, tick_size, tick_value_profit, tick_value_loss, contract_size, volume_min, volume_max, volume_step, margin_initial, stops_level, freeze_level, filling_modes, trading_sessions, swap_mode, swap_long, swap_short, rollover_multipliers. All numerical inputs are finite, booleans do not count as numbers; prices/volume/tick economics are positive where meaningful; unknown fields needed for economics never receive legacy defaults. Snapshot captures actual equity; commission/swap are not subtracted again from it. Manifest identity fields initially null and must all match before identity can be qualified; first Challenge, Swing, 2-Step, USD, 10000 are fixed. ftmo_export is an assertion, not cryptographic authentication.
Optional quote CSV schema: timestamp_utc,received_at_utc,bid,ask,symbol,contract_id,source_kind. Quotes must have aware UTC times, receipt no earlier than observation, finite positive bid <= ask, matching symbol/contract, declared provenance, chronological observation and receipt order, no duplicate observation timestamp, and no observations after as-of. No synthetic fixture can qualify broker coverage. Report count, date range, unique New York cash dates, coverage per intended 09:35 entry/exit window, Prague rollover window and stress window, full-spread distribution, and receipt latency. Intended coverage windows are NY 09:30-09:40, Prague 23:55-00:05, and NY 15:55-16:05; rollover crossing midnight is assigned to the following Prague date. At least 20 distinct observed session dates and 20 observed dates in each window are necessary but not sufficient for qualification; sparse snapshots cannot establish uninterrupted execution quality. Unsupported historical availability, financing history, fill slippage, cost reconciliation and independent provenance remain explicit missing-evidence blockers. Never transfer TenTrade or Dukascopy costs as FTMO observations.

OFFLINE RISK CONTRACT
Use Decimal-compatible finite monetary arithmetic and explicit USD account currency. Firm policy: initial balance 10000; daily allowance fixed 500; daily equity floor is actual Prague-midnight balance minus 500; total floor is static 9000; Challenge profit target 1000, Verification 500; minimum four distinct Prague trading dates. Falling below either firm floor is a breach; equality is no headroom and must refuse entry. Target completion requires closed positions and pendings, balance target achieved, four observed trading dates, no known breach, and adequate history; balance/equity or day-count alone cannot certify qualification.
Use Europe/Prague civil dates and actual DST transitions, never broker midnight or a fixed UTC offset. Daily anchors can only come from an explicit exact-midnight balance observation or reconstruction using complete timestamped balance-changing ledger events and a known prior balance covering midnight; current equity/current balance must not be silently used as midnight balance. History gaps, missing timezone, stale/missing/corrupt persisted state, account/server/policy mismatch or out-of-order times block new risk. Persistent state serialization is local data only, namespaced by server, account fingerprint and policy version. Reconstruct missed midnight across restart from complete history or fail closed.
Internal limits are separate from firm rules: initial per-position planned risk 10, aggregate open plus pending risk 50, daily deterioration 100 from midnight balance, weekly decline pause 200 from an explicitly provided weekly anchor, strategy drawdown pause 300 from explicitly provided deployment equity high-water mark, and floor reserve 150. Do not replace the firm static floor with an equity high-water mark. All account open positions, partial fills, manual exposure and pending orders count; absent/unbounded stop risk blocks entries. Pending potential fills are additive even when correlated; no netting or unverified OCO reduction. Existing incremental risk from current equity to planned stop/liquidation plus future cost reserve is subtracted once from remaining daily/floor headroom and the aggregate allowance. Actual equity already includes current floating P/L and booked costs. No per-magic nominal position counts.
New risk allowance is max(0, min(per-position allowance, aggregate cap minus existing incremental open/pending risk, midnight balance minus internal daily stop distance headroom after existing risk, weekly remaining headroom after existing risk, strategy HWM remaining headroom after existing risk, equity minus daily floor minus reserve minus existing risk, equity minus total floor minus reserve minus existing risk) minus separately declared additional liquidation reserve). Missing required anchors/risk estimates or invalid identity/currency produce zero permission or a handled validation error, never a tradable default. Stop sizing uses account-currency risk per lot based on actual tick-size/tick-value-loss or a supplied verified account-currency profit conversion, plus declared costs; volume rounds DOWN to the broker volume step and respects minimum/maximum. Below-minimum volume is zero. Stops/gaps can exceed planned risk; functions do not promise guaranteed losses or perform closure reconciliation.
Cost helpers distinguish midpoint reference accounting, where crossed spread cost is half the sum of entry and exit full spreads, from actual bid/ask fill accounting, where no extra spread is subtracted. Include incremental commission, slippage and financing once with declared currency and units. Current swap snapshots are prospective scenarios, never historical schedules.

PREDECLARED GATES
G-I1: SPEC exists before new implementation; GATE is a verbatim extraction of every G- line before any implementation script or test run; historical SPEC/GATE/REPORT and frozen EAs are untouched.
G-I2: Standard-library offline implementation and meaningful tests pass with the project interpreter; no MT5 connection, process control, order, dependency installation, deployment or model fitting occurs.
G-D1: Authentic independently reviewed FTMO account evidence confirms account fingerprint, exact server, platform, USD, initial 10000, 2-Step, Swing and Challenge against a populated expected-identity manifest; absent evidence is INCOMPLETE and mismatch is FAIL.
G-D2: All symbol economics, volume constraints, session/swap metadata, source/receipt times and hashes are present and internally valid; absent critical fields is INCOMPLETE, contradictory or malformed fields is FAIL.
G-D3: Authentic quotes cover at least 20 distinct New York cash session dates and 20 distinct observed dates in each declared window, with zero unresolved quote-side/timing/provenance defects; absent or insufficient coverage is INCOMPLETE and invalid supplied observations is FAIL.
G-D4: Independent provenance, actual execution/slippage/commission and financing reconciliation, historical-coverage limitations and account-rule agreement are documented; unresolved evidence is INCOMPLETE. Synthetic fixtures and self-declared source_kind never satisfy this gate.
G-R1: Tests verify fixed daily allowance/static total floor, both targets/four-day condition, actual-equity costs, Prague midnight floating-loss reset, both DST transitions and mismatched US/Europe transition weeks, missing history/restart identity rejection and reconstruction without anchor laundering.
G-R2: Tests verify separate internal limits, all-account open/pending risk, unknown-risk refusal, zero headroom, down-rounded lot steps/minimum lots, invalid/nonfinite/currency inputs and no double-counted spread/costs.
G-F1: Final REPORT lists every gate and evidence; missing authentic snapshot or unresolved G-D gates gives overall INCOMPLETE and downstream_model_allowed=false regardless of infrastructure test PASS. Any observed implementation defect must be fixed and rechecked before infrastructure PASS; contradictory authentic data yields FAIL for this artifact. No statistical or trading-performance claim follows this infrastructure milestone.

STOP AND HONESTY RULES
Stop dependent model/feature/EA work while qualification is incomplete or failed. Do not fabricate account identity, fills, costs, history or independent review. Test fixtures are labelled synthetic and are never mixed with authentic archives. Tests prove bounded offline behavior, not live safety, broker authenticity, uninterrupted quote coverage or profitability. REPORT is written once after final checks, including unresolved inputs and exact commands. Future data acquisition and any order-bearing validation require separately scoped work; historical failures remain final.
