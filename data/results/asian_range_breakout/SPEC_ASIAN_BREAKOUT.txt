SPEC: ASIAN RANGE LONDON BREAKOUT - PHASE 1 SIGNAL QUALITY SCREEN (HYPOTHESIS #38)
Declared: 2026-09-23, before any code for this study existed.

0. CONTEXT
  - Fourth EURUSD study on the verified onboarding tape (after #34 DTE,
    #35/#36 VWAP-MR). New session-structure family: Asian consolidation ->
    London expansion breakout (classic session-breakout archetype). Null
    result pre-accepted as a complete deliverable.
  - Tape: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (2,490,896 M1
    bars, epoch-UTC open-time, Bid OHLC, tick_volume; onboarding HARD
    ASSERTs passed: monotonic, 0 duplicates). All session boundaries are
    evaluated on UTC wall-clock timestamps.

1. SESSION DEFINITIONS (UTC)
  1.1 Asian Range window: M1 bars with open time in [00:00:00, 07:00:00) UTC
      (minutes-of-day 0..419).
  1.2 London Breakout window: M1 bars with open time in [07:00:00, 10:00:00)
      UTC (minutes-of-day 420..599).
  1.3 Time exit: 17:00 UTC (minute-of-day 1020). Nothing is carried into the
      low-liquidity rollover.
  1.4 Day unit: the UTC calendar date of the bars. Funnel base = dates with
      at least one printed bar before 17:00 UTC. A day is range-definable
      iff it has >= 1 printed M1 bar in 1.1.

2. RANGE DEFINITION AND FILTER
  2.1 Asian_High = max High, Asian_Low = min Low over bars in 1.1.
  2.2 Asian_Range_Pips = (Asian_High - Asian_Low) * 10000; 1 pip = 0.00010.
  2.3 Tight-range filter: days with Asian_Range_Pips > 30.0 are INVALID
      (strict, comparison on the pipette grid to avoid float ties) - the
      expansion already happened during Asia. Only <= 30.0-pip ranges
      proceed to signal generation.

3. SIGNAL GENERATION
  3.1 On valid days, scan M1 bars strictly inside the breakout window (1.2).
  3.2 LONG candidate: the first M1 bar with Close > Asian_High. SHORT
      candidate: the first M1 bar with Close < Asian_Low. Maximum one
      candidate per direction per day (first close-breakout wins; a dropped
      candidate does NOT re-arm its direction).
  3.3 Entry: the Open of the next PRINTED M1 bar after the signal bar (the
      tape skips empty minutes). If no next printed bar exists before 17:00
      UTC of the same date, the candidate is dropped as no-entry.
  3.4 Stop: the opposite side of the Asian Range (LONG SL = Asian_Low,
      SHORT SL = Asian_High). Initial_Risk = |Entry - SL|. Candidates with
      Initial_Risk <= 0 are dropped as degenerate-risk.
  3.5 Both directions may fire on the same day; each is walked independently.

4. EXCURSION WALK (MFE/MAE)
  4.1 Walk printed M1 bars from the entry bar (inclusive) through the last
      printed bar with open time < 17:00:00 UTC of the same UTC date.
  4.2 LONG: MFE_pips = (max bar High - Entry) * 10000; MAE_pips = (Entry -
      min bar Low) * 10000. SHORT mirrored. Pre-cost, no adjustments.
  4.3 Risk-standardized: MFE_R = MFE_pips / Risk_pips; MAE_R likewise.
  4.4 Raw time-exit expectancy (descriptive only): Entry to the time-exit
      price = Open of the first printed bar with open time >= 17:00:00 UTC
      same date (fallback if the day ends early: Close of the last printed
      bar before 17:00). Signed by direction, in pips, compared
      descriptively against the verified 0.3-pip London/NY overlap median
      spread floor. This screen is pre-cost.

5. GATES (frozen verbatim in GATE_ASIAN_BREAKOUT.txt)
  G-AS1 total session-valid signals >= 150.
  G-AS2 aggregate mean MFE > mean MAE (strict, pips).
  G-AS3 day-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 20260923) over UTC-date
      blocks of P(pooled mean(MFE - MAE) <= 0) <= 0.20.

7. HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK RULES
  - Phase-1 signal-quality screen ONLY: pre-cost MFE/MAE excursions; no
    Phase-2 execution claim; no optimization or parameter search beyond the
    frozen windows/filters above; no target/exit tuning.
  - Causality: the Asian range is fully formed before the breakout window
    opens; signals use only the signal bar's own close vs pre-computed
    range levels; entries are at printed next-bar opens.
  - The Bid-only tape means spread is not modeled; the 0.3-pip overlap
    median floor is reported alongside, never netted inside gates.
  - Null result is final for this artifact; verdicts quote the frozen gates
    only.
