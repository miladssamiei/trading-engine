ENGINE A: Last completed 17:00 ET D1 close relative to EMA50 agrees with direction. Prior completed NY-wall 4H close beyond EMA29 +/-2.77 Wilder ATR29; first real M1 open of next 4H bucket. BID-coordinate initial stop distance max(abs(entry BID-prior EMA29),1.5*prior ATR29); EMA29 ratchet after every completed 4H bar without loosening. Maximum one open A trade.
ENGINE B: Last completed NY-wall 4H Supertrend(10,3) agrees. Prior H1 close beyond Donchian20 from t-2 and prior H1 Wilder ADX14>=20; first real M1 open of next real H1 bar. Initial stop 4*prior H1 ATR14; ratchet at each real H1 close to close +/-4*current ATR14 without loosening. Maximum one open B trade. Both engines run independently, can overlap each other, and shadow candidates while occupied. No TP, time exit or prop drawdown guard. Stop has adverse priority within an M1 bar; gap-through fills at adverse M1 open. Final open positions are OPEN_END, excluded from closed performance but charged accrued financing through tape end.

FRICTION: Fixed BID/ASK proxy spread+commission all-in floor 0.03 price units per round trip. Long entry ASK=BID+0.03 and exit BID. Short entry BID and exit ASK=BID+0.03. Apply once; closed gross BID-coordinate PnL minus 0.03 equals fill PnL. USD figures are per one barrel of oil, not an FTMO lot or account. No variable spread, slippage, commission beyond the floor, contract sizing or currency conversion.

SWAP: Owner scenario -5.0% annualized for both long and short open positions, without compounding. Each NY weekday Monday-Friday at 17:00 America/New_York (DST-aware) with Wednesday weight 3, other weekdays weight 1: charge -abs(last observed BID M1 close strictly before rollover)*0.05/365*weight USD per barrel. Absolute mark ensures a debit even if the underlying price were negative, though nonpositive source prices are rejected above. Count a rollover if entry_time < rollover_time <= exit_time; for OPEN_END accrue through final M1 timestamp. A rollover during a quote gap uses the latest stale BID close and its age is reported. Closed-trade net PnL = gross BID-coordinate PnL -0.03 + swap (negative). Report swap paid on closed trades and separately on OPEN_END accrual, plus the total for each engine. This annualized notional-charge model is the specified scenario, not verified historical FTMO financing.
