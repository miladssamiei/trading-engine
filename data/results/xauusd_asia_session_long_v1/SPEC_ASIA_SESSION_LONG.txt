Declared 2026-09-25 before this hypothesis's code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND PRIOR EVIDENCE
Wenling Wei, Who Moves the Price? Trading-Session Return Decomposition
and Cross-Session Momentum in Gold and Silver Markets, 2026,
https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7257240
reports that 00:00-08:00 UTC contributed much of XAUUSD spot's
2024-2026 return and that only a long-only session strategy remained
positive after the paper's cost assumption. The strong gold bull
market and the paper's same-sample selection limit transferability.
Test the simplest distinct implication: buy every eligible gold CFD
UTC date at 00:00 and sell at 08:00, regardless of news, previous
session return or any technical condition. No side/time filter, stop,
target, threshold, momentum overlay or selected weekday. Earlier
gold macro/closing studies are separate and remain frozen.

3. SPREAD, ECONOMICS AND PARTITION
Gross price units/trade = exit BID open - entry BID open. Fixed-spread
net = gross -0.41. Extra cost stress = net - another 0.41, so total
screen friction is 0.82 gold price units/trade. One trade per UTC
date, long only. Report trade count, eligible/unavailable dates,
gross/net/stress mean and sum, win rate, max drawdown, all-year
totals and mean/median entry spread assumption. 2020-2023 is
descriptive calibration; UTC entry dates 2024-01-01 through the tape
end 2026-09-01 are gated historical. 2026 partial separate.
Bootstrap gated UTC entry dates with replacement 10000 times, seed
20261009; report P(mean fixed-spread net<=0) and 95% descriptive
quantiles. This sample has extensive prior gold research and overlap
with the cited paper, so even a historical PASS is selected evidence.
