The target is the EUR10,000 FTMO 2-Step Swing account. It charges or
pays swaps, allows news trading and weekend/overnight holds, and has
5% maximum daily and 10% maximum total floating-equity loss rules.

net-position increases predict higher next-week commodity returns.
Gold producers/merchants hedge physical exposure and may supply
liquidity to trend-following managed money. This is a transfer
hypothesis, not a claim that the panel result survives FTMO costs.

FROZEN SIGNAL AND EXECUTION PROXY
For each eligible Tuesday report t, net producer positions N_t equal
Prod_Merc_Positions_Long_All minus Prod_Merc_Positions_Short_All.
Q_t=(N_t-N_previous)/Open_Interest_All_previous. Q_t>0 means LONG
XAUUSD; Q_t<0 means SHORT. Q_t=0 means no trade. No percentile,
threshold, VIX, gold-price trend, stop, weekday or sign optimization
is permitted after seeing returns. One candidate position per week.
Skip a week if its Thursday is US Thanksgiving Day, July 4, January
1 or December 25, including observed Thursday market closures. All
other weeks require a real Monday 09:30 NY M1 open in [09:30,09:31)
and a real Thursday 15:55 NY M1 open in [15:55,15:56). Missing entry
or required exit is unresolved coverage, never filled synthetically.
Entry LONG at BID M1 open plus 0.41 gold units; SHORT at BID open.
Set a hard initial stop 1.5% adverse from executed entry price:
LONG stop on BID at ASK entry*(1-0.015); SHORT stop on ASK at BID
entry*(1+0.015). For stop checking, synthetic ASK equals BID+0.41
at every M1. Scan every real M1 from entry through the scheduled
Thursday exit. A touched stop fills at the stop or, if the first
quote of that minute is worse, at that adverse first quote. A stop
in the scheduled exit minute takes priority. If no stop, exit at
Thursday 15:55 BID for LONG or ASK for SHORT. Force flat by Thursday;
no weekend position. Source minutes absent inside a hold are counted
and reported; a gap over 30 consecutive minutes while the market
normally trades fails source integrity for that trade. Do not infer
unobserved stop behavior during such gaps.
