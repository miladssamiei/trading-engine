HYPOTHESIS #50 SPEC: DUAL-TIMEFRAME KELTNER CHANNEL BREAKOUT MOMENTUM (4h execution, D1 17:00 ET anchor)
Study ID: cross_asset_kc_breakout
Date frozen: 2026-09-23, before any evaluation code was written or run.
Status: frozen. Corrections or re-measurements require a NEW study name.
Lineage: fourth cross-asset study. #47/#48 (squeeze family) and #49 (Alligator
1:1 bracket) all FAILed. This study tests an execution-level KC(29/2.77)
breakout gated by a D1 EMA(50) direction filter, with an EMA-baseline riding
stop - a stop paradigm not yet screened (stop = channel baseline, not ATR
trail or fixed bracket). Parameterization (EMA 29/50, 2.77 multiplier, 1.5xATR
clamp) is owner-supplied and frozen as given; no parameter search, one run per
instrument.

1. DATA
1.1 Tapes (verified M1, epoch-UTC time, columns time,open,high,low,close,
    tick_volume; sorted ascending defensively before aggregation):
    - US500 : data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv  (points; cost floor 0.51)
    - EURUSD: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (pips, 1 pip = 0.0001; cost floor 0.3)
    - XAUUSD: data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv (price units; cost floor 0.41)
1.2 Timezone America/New_York (EST/EDT) for all wall-time logic.
1.3 Cost floors are round-trip, subtracted once per trade from gross.

2. MULTI-TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION
2.1 Continuous 1h base grid: M1 epochs floored to 3600s slots; open=first,
    high=max, low=min, close=last, volume=sum per slot; reindexed to the FULL
    continuous 3600s grid with missing bars forward-filled OHLC = prior close,
    volume = 0 (synthetic flat bars). NY wall hour boundaries align exactly
    with the epoch-3600 grid (NY UTC offset is always whole hours).
2.2 4h execution bars: built FROM the continuous 1h grid by NY WALL-clock
    bucket [00:00-04:00), [04:00-08:00), ... DST-transition buckets span 3 or
    5 real hours; buckets are contiguous by construction.
2.3 D1 anchor bars: strict forex/CFD daily bars ENDING 17:00 ET - built FROM
    the same continuous 1h grid by NY WALL-clock daily bucket [17:00, 17:00).
    DST-transition days span 23 or 25 real hours in wall bucketing; the first
    and last buckets of the tape may be partial. Because the [16:00-20:00) 4h
    bucket straddles the 17:00 boundary, D1 bars are aggregated at 1h
    resolution (never from the 4h series) so daily closes are true 17:00 ET
    closes. Both series derive from the same continuous grid (strictly
    aligned).
2.4 For a 4h execution bar t with wall start w: the most recently completed
    D1 bar is the [17:00,17:00) bucket whose end <= w (the daily bucket
    strictly before the bucket containing w). Its Close and EMA include that
    bar's own 17:00 close, known at its end.

3. STATE MACHINE (causal indicators)
3.1 D1 anchor trend filter: EMA(50) of D1 Close (span=50, adjust=False,
    masked NaN before index 49). Direction of the prior completed D1 bar k:
    BULLISH iff Close(k) > EMA50(k); BEARISH iff Close(k) < EMA50(k);
    equality arms neither side.
3.2 4h execution Keltner channels, evaluated on the strictly prior 4h bar
    t-1 (its EMA/ATR include bar t-1's close, known at its close):
    - Base: EMA(29) of 4h Close (span=29, adjust=False, masked NaN < 28).
    - Range: Wilder ATR(29) on 4h High/Low/Close (TR=max(H-L,|H-cPrev|,
      |L-cPrev|), ATR(28)=mean(TR 0..28), ATR(i)=(ATR(i-1)*28+TR(i))/29).
    - Upper(t-1) = EMA29(t-1) + 2.77*ATR29(t-1);
      Lower(t-1) = EMA29(t-1) - 2.77*ATR29(t-1).
3.3 Armed: bar t is armed iff Close(t-1), Upper(t-1), Lower(t-1), EMA29(t-1),
    ATR29(t-1) and the mapped D1 Close/EMA50 direction are all non-NaN
    (implicit warmup ~29 4h bars + ~50 D1 buckets).

4. SIGNALS AND EXECUTION
4.1 Long signal at 4h bar t: Close(t-1) > Upper(t-1) AND D1 anchor BULLISH.
    Short: Close(t-1) < Lower(t-1) AND D1 anchor BEARISH.
4.2 Execution cap: max ONE open trade per instrument; signals while a trade
    is open are counted (ignored-while-open) and discarded. A trade occupies
    bars [entry_bar, exit_bar] inclusive; the next actionable signal is at
    exit_bar + 1 or later.
4.3 Entry: Open price of the signal bar t.

5. EXCURSION WALK AND EMA-BASELINE RIDING STOP
5.1 Initial risk distance D = max(|Entry - EMA29(t-1)|, 1.5*ATR29(t-1))
    (EMA-baseline distance, clamped at a minimum of 1.5*ATR to prevent
    degenerate tight stops; both terms from the strictly prior bar t-1,
    frozen at entry). Initial SL = Entry - D (long) / Entry + D (short).
    R multiple = side*(Exit - Entry)/D.
5.2 Walk 4h bars j = entry_bar, entry_bar+1, ... in order (long case; short
    mirrored):
    a) Open(j) <= SL -> exit at Open(j), reason SL_GAP (honest fill);
    b) Low(j)  <= SL -> exit at SL, reason SL (worst-case default);
    c) at the close of j, ratchet to the EMA baseline: SL = max(SL,
       EMA29(j)) for longs (mins for shorts). EMA29(j) includes bar j's
       close, known at that close.
5.3 If no stop is hit by the final 4h bar, exit at that bar's close, reason
    FORCED_EOD, included in results.
5.4 Per-trade record includes MFE/MAE excursions over [entry_bar..exit_bar]
    (long MFE = max High - Entry, MAE = Entry - min Low; short mirrored),
    in instrument units, descriptive only.

6. ECONOMICS
6.1 Gross = side*(Exit - Entry) in instrument units: points for US500 and
    XAUUSD, pips (x10,000) for EURUSD. Net = Gross - cost floor (0.51 / 0.3 /
    0.41 respectively), one round trip per trade.
6.2 Net Sharpe (per-trade convention): mean(Net)/std(Net, ddof=1) * sqrt(252)
    over executed trades of the instrument.

7. PHASE 1 GATES (applied INDEPENDENTLY to each instrument; frozen verbatim in
   GATE_KC_BREAKOUT.txt)
    G-KC1: Total executed signals >= 30.
    G-KC2: Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
    G-KC3: Moving-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=5 trades,
           seed 20260928) P(mean Net PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
    VERDICT RULE: an instrument PASSES only if G-KC1 AND G-KC2 AND G-KC3 all
    PASS. OVERALL verdict PASS only if ALL THREE instruments PASS. Any FAIL is
    final for this artifact; corrections require a new study name. Null
    results are complete, expected deliverables.

8. OUTPUTS (data/results/cross_asset_kc_breakout/)
    SPEC_KC_BREAKOUT.txt (this file), GATE_KC_BREAKOUT.txt (written before
    the run), REPORT_KC_BREAKOUT_PHASE1.txt (funnel, net expectancy, win
    rate, MFE/MAE excursion asymmetry), kc_breakout_signals.csv (all
    instruments, asset column), run_meta.json (sha256 lineage of tapes and
    script, funnel, gates, verdict).

9. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
9.1 All 4h inputs strictly prior (bar t-1); all D1 inputs completed buckets;
    entries only at 4h bar opens; no intrabar lookahead; gap fills at the
    open; worst-case same-bar stop.
9.2 Single frozen parameterization; no optimization, no re-runs, no post-hoc
    filtering; sides, years and instruments reported separately and pooled.
9.3 CONSEQUENCE OF 2.1, declared honestly: synthetic flat bars feed the
    indicators - EMA flattens toward the last close, Wilder ATR decays across
    long gaps (narrowing the channels), and flat closes cannot print new
    breakouts. D1 direction persists unchanged across flat weekend buckets.
    Accepted under the declared continuity convention; no bar-skipping or
    ATR-freezing fix is applied.
9.4 Phase 2 (MT5 execution) only on overall PASS; in-sample character of
    this screen acknowledged; MFE/MAE are exploratory descriptors, never
    results.
