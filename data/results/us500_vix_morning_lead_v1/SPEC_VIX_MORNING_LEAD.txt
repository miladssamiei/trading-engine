MECHANISM AND SOURCE LIMIT
Sung, An Empirical Study of the Price Leadership of VIX (2014),
https://ir.lib.ncu.edu.tw/handle/987654321/65376,
reports VIX-to-S&P 500 lead-lag at 5-45 minute frequency. Tapiero,
The Information Content of Intraday VIX and its Expected Correlation
on the S&P 500 (2015),
https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2674086,
reports incremental one-step-ahead S&P return-density information.
Neither paper proves directional CFD profit or FTMO pace. Direct VIX
is a materially different signal from H76's inaccessible Dukascopy
VXX futures-ETN quote source; H76 stays INCOMPLETE and frozen. This
test asks whether the first 15 cash minutes of direct VIX predict
the next US500 hour in the inverse direction. Null is complete.
