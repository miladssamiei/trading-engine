HYPOTHESIS 82: FTMO-NATIVE XAUUSD INTRADAY DONCHIAN/SUPERTREND PORTABILITY AND 180-DAY CHALLENGE PACE
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code, data export, or Phase-1 run.

1. QUESTION AND FROZEN MECHANISM
Test whether the frozen H70 gold rule survives on the target FTMO-Demo XAUUSD broker feed and clears both native spread and a 180-calendar-day EUR 10,000 FTMO 2-Step Challenge proxy. No parameter, hour, side, year, stop, or risk-row optimization. Previous H70 replay is frozen FAIL and remains so. This is a new source and new evaluation, not a correction to H70. FTMO broker symbols differ from the imported research instruments.

2. SOURCE AND CLOCK
Read XAUUSD broker H1 BID OHLC bars with MT5 copy_rates_range from 2023-01-01 through the last complete broker hour no later than 2026-09-24 23:00 server time. Require connected server FTMO-Demo, broker FTMO Global Markets Ltd, non-custom symbol, and positive 100-unit contract size. Write a source CSV and SHA256, excluding all account identifiers. The MT5 Python timestamps on this connected terminal encode its GMT+2 plus daylight-saving server wall clock, observed +3 hours in September 2026. Interpret each returned epoch as a naive server wall hour and convert with Europe/Athens European DST to UTC. Since DST switches occur on market-closed Sundays, require converted UTC hours strictly increasing and unique; otherwise fail coverage rather than guessing. Audit sample first quote versus H1 open and report source counts, coverage, gaps and hash. A downloaded H1 bar can differ from order execution. M1 copy_rates_range is anomalous and forbidden as the source.

3. SIGNAL AND SAME-DAY BAR EXECUTION
Build continuous UTC-hour 1H grid from converted broker H1 BID OHLC, forward filling missing-hour fields and marking REAL bars. Aggregate REAL H1 bars into New York wall 4H buckets starting 00,04,08,12,16,20; use the exact H57 Wilder ATR, Wilder ADX, Supertrend and signal functions: Supertrend(10,3), previous completed 1H close outside Donchian(20) channel through t-2, ADX14(t-1)>=20, 4x ATR14 initial and completed-hour trailing stop, strict prior-completed 4H anchor. Entry at REAL H1 t open when NY wall hour 00..15. Single position, shadow signals through the exit H1 hour. Long entry BID+0.41, short entry BID; long stop on BID, short stop on BID+0.41, adverse bar-open gap rule unchanged. Stop check precedes trailing update; final flat uses the real 15:00-16:00 NY bar close. If the 15:00 real hour is absent after entry, flag unresolved, take a diagnostic last available BID mark only, and fail coverage. Do not use future final-hour existence to avoid an entry. Bar high/low first-touch and bar-open fills are optimistic versus real orders, so Phase 1 is only a screen.

4. PERIOD, STRESS AND INFERENCE
2023 is warmup only. Gated entries are NY 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-24. Charge fixed 0.41 gold units per round trip via Bid/Ask construction. Apply an additional 0.41 units per trade to stressed net. This extra cost stands in for unmeasured spread variation, commissions and slippage but does not prove they fit within it. Report annual 2024, 2025 and partial 2026, counts, side, exit type, mean, sum, max drawdown, stop distance and holding time. Bootstrap distinct NY entry dates with replacement 10000 times, seed 20260925; preserve all trades per sampled date and report P(mean unstressed net<=0). No data-dependent refit.

5. EUR 10,000 2-STEP STAGE-1 PACE SCREEN
Apply each fixed nominal initial-risk fraction 0.5%, 1.0%, 1.5%, 2.0% separately to the same trade sequence, without compounding, using stressed realized R=(net-0.41)/planned initial stop-distance. For conservative floating drawdown use the worst BID low (long) or BID high+0.41 (short) of all real H1 bars from entry through the exit bar, including the entire exit bar even if the stop would have happened earlier; subtract the extra 0.41 and divide by planned risk. An unresolved trade fails coverage and prevents an overall PASS. This ideal continuous-lot proxy omits 0.01-lot rounding, variable EURUSD conversion, margin, and actual fills; it cannot certify the live account. Reuse the fixed 180-calendar-day rolling-window method from xauusd_saleh_floating_pace_audit_v1: start on each gated NY trade date with a fully observed 180-day horizon, EUR 10,000 balance, target EUR 11,000 after at least four distinct Europe/Prague trading dates, static EUR 9,000 total equity floor, and Europe/Prague midnight daily floor of opening balance minus EUR 500. Apply a breach before a pass when either is touched on a trade; positions are intraday. Report PASS/BREACH/TIMEOUT counts, pass and breach rate, median pass days for every fixed risk. These FTMO 2-Step objectives are conditional until the target account's exact phase is independently confirmed.

G-FT1: At least 150 gated executed or marked trades on at least 150 distinct New York dates, with zero missing entries and zero unresolved exits.
G-FT2: Strictly positive mean extra-0.41-cost-stress net gold units per gated trade.
G-FT3: Date-bootstrap P(mean unstressed bar-model net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10000 resamples, seed 20260925.
G-FT4: Strictly positive unstressed net gold-unit total in each complete gated year 2024 and 2025.
G-FT5: At least 200 fully observed rolling 180-calendar-day starts and at least one predeclared risk row with pass rate at least 0.70 and breach rate at most 0.10.
G-FT6: Every executed trade has the same New York entry and exit date and a holding time below 16 hours; broker source is non-custom with strictly increasing unique UTC hours and a recorded SHA256.
All six must pass. No risk-row, calendar, source, cost, or exit variation after observing results. A FAIL is final for this artifact. No target-broker tick replay or MT5 EA on this exact rule after FAIL.
