SIGNAL-QUALITY SCREEN

1. TASKING AND LINEAGE
- Direct follow-up to Hypothesis #35 (vwap_mr_eurusd, FAIL 2026-09-22 by
  sample starvation: fixed 0.25% bands under a 0.40% max-move cap are
  nearly self-excluding because VWAP always lies inside the session
  range; 1 signal in 6.7 years). #36 replaces BOTH fixed scalars with
  volatility-standardized quantities: 2.0-sigma volume-weighted deviation
  bands and a 1.0 x ATR14(D1) dynamic session-range filter. This is the
  owner-tasked standardization fix, a NEW pre-declared artifact; the #35
  verdict stands and is not re-litigated. US500 VWAP-MR nulls
  (vwap_rr_verdict) remain frozen lineage context. Null result
  pre-accepted as a complete deliverable.

2. DATA
- Intraday: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (verified
  onboarding 2026-09-22: 2,490,896 M1 bars, Bid OHLC + tick_volume,
  epoch-UTC open times).
- Daily: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_d1.csv (1,748 rows;
  columns date,open,high,low,close,tick_volume; date = YYYYMMDD NY date
  of the 17:00 ET Forex close; D1 bar D covers (D-1 17:00 ET, D 17:00]).
- Timezone: America/New_York (DST-aware). Trading day = NY calendar
  date. Session = M1 bars of that NY date with open wall minute in
  [08:00, 16:00) ET (wall in [480, 960)). Session open price = open of
  the session's first printed bar.

3. DAILY VOLATILITY STATE (CAUSAL)
- True Range on D1 bars: TR_i = max(H-L, |H - C_prev|, |L - C_prev|).
- Wilder ATR14: ATR_14 = mean(TR_1..TR_14) at the 14th bar, then
  ATR_i = (ATR_{i-1} * 13 + TR_i) / 14. Computed causally along the D1
  tape in date order.
- Causal lookup: for session day D, the last COMPLETED D1 bar is the
  latest D1 row with date < D (the bar dated D is still in progress
  during D's session). ATR(D) = Wilder ATR through that bar. If fewer
  than 14 D1 bars precede D (warmup), ATR is undefined: no signals that
  day (tallied as days_no_atr).

4. INTRADAY STATE MACHINE (PER NY DATE, SESSION BARS IN ORDER)
- Cumulative VWAP: TP = (High + Low + Close) / 3; VWAP[t] =
  cumsum(TP * tick_volume) / cumsum(tick_volume) from the 08:00 ET
  anchor (reset per NY date). VWAP keeps updating through walk bars.
- Volume-weighted standard deviation:
  sigma[t] = sqrt(max(0, cumsum(TP^2 * tick_volume) / cumsum(tick_volume)
                       - VWAP[t]^2)).
- Bands: upper = VWAP + 2.0 * sigma[t]; lower = VWAP - 2.0 * sigma[t].
  Bands ARM only after 30 minutes of cumulative session data:
  t_open - session_first_bar_open >= 1800 s.
- 30-minute slope: slope[t] = VWAP[t] - VWAP[ref(t)], ref(t) = the LAST
  session bar with open time <= t_open - 1800 s; if no such bar exists,
  slope undefined -> no signal at t.
- Dynamic session-range filter: rolling (Max High - Min Low) over
  session bars so far (inclusive of the current bar); if it EXCEEDS
  1.0 * ATR(D) (strictly greater), the session is invalidated for
  signal generation from that bar onward (already-open walks are
  unaffected).

5. SIGNAL GENERATION
- Signal window: M1 bars with open wall strictly inside (08:30, 15:30)
  ET (510 < wall < 930), bands armed, slope defined, dynamic range
  filter not yet triggered.
- SHORT: Close > upper band AND slope <= 0.
- LONG: Close < lower band AND slope >= 0.
- Execution cap: FIRST valid signal per direction per NY date (max one
  SHORT + one LONG per day; later candidates of an already-fired
  direction are counted pre-cap and dropped).
- Entry: the OPEN of the next printed M1 bar after the signal bar, if it
  has the same NY date and open wall < 960; otherwise no-entry (dropped,
  counted). Same cap semantics as #35: the first candidate consumes the
  direction even if its entry is missing.

6. EXCURSION WALK (PRE-COST)
- From the entry bar (entry price = its open) over session bars (same NY
  date, wall < 960), in order, until the FIRST of:
  TARGET: evolving VWAP touched - SHORT: bar Low <= VWAP[bar]; LONG:
          bar High >= VWAP[bar] (inclusive; the entry bar itself can be
          the target).
  TIME:   session end (last session bar with wall < 960).
- MFE = (max High - entry) for LONG / (entry - min Low) for SHORT;
  MAE = (max High - entry) for SHORT / (entry - min Low) for LONG; over
  the walk bars inclusive of the exit bar, in pips (1 pip = 0.00010).
- Descriptive cost context ONLY (Phase 1 stays gross): verified EURUSD
  London/NY overlap median spread 0.3 pips; mean/median MFE, MAE and
  MFE-MAE compared against it in the report. No net gate at Phase 1.

7. GATES (FROZEN IN GATE_VWAP_MR_EURUSD_STD.TXT BEFORE ANY CODE EXISTS)
G-VW1   total session-valid signals >= 150.
G-VW2   aggregate mean MFE > mean MAE (strict, pips).
G-VW3   day-block bootstrap: resample NY dates with replacement (10,000
        resamples, numpy.random.default_rng(20260921)); statistic =
        pooled mean of (MFE - MAE) in pips over resampled signals;
        P(statistic <= 0) <= 0.20.
OVERALL PASS requires all three. Any FAIL is final for this artifact.

8. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
- All state causal: VWAP/sigma/range/slope at bar t use session bars up
  to and including t only; ATR(D) uses D1 bars completed strictly
  before day D; entries use only bars after the signal bar; the walk
  uses the evolving VWAP strictly forward.
- No parameter search: band multiple (2.0), ATR multiple (1.0), ATR
  span (14), slope span (30 min), caps, window, seed are frozen here;
  nothing tuned after seeing results. The standardization motive comes
  from #35's pre-diagnosed starvation, not from peeking at #36 outcomes.
- Null pre-accepted; US500 and #35 EURUSD verdicts stand untouched.
- Phase-1 excursions are signal-quality measurements, not tradable
  results.
