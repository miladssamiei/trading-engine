SIGNAL AND ONE WEEKLY POSITION
For each NY Monday with at least one M1 quote in [00:00,00:05), use
only the latest fully completed daily bar before Monday 00:00 NY.
Require that bar's trading date is the preceding Thursday or Friday,
so stale earlier prices cannot drive a signal. If its close exceeds
the close 21 completed trading days earlier, LONG. If below, SHORT.
If equal or prerequisites absent, no trade. Enter at the first real
M1 open in Monday [00:00,00:05): long at BID+0.41 synthetic ASK,
short at BID. Exactly one entry per calendar week. No re-entry.

EXIT AND COVERAGE
Initial stop distance is 2*the preceding completed daily ATR20,
measured from executable entry. Long stop is entry minus distance;
short stop is entry plus distance. From entry M1 onward, test stop
gap at executable open first, then touch at that M1 BID low (long)
or BID high+0.41 (short); stop fills at worse gap open or stop level.
Same-minute stop takes precedence. No trailing stop or profit target.
If no stop, exit at the first real M1 open in Thursday NY
[14:00,14:15). If no such M1 exists, mark UNRESOLVED; never carry to
Friday/weekend or use a synthetic close. If the tape ends before the
required exit, mark UNRESOLVED. Skip a Monday with no real entry
minute and log its coverage. If a trade would open after the tape's
last complete Thursday, exclude that incomplete week and log it.

ECONOMICS AND BENCHMARK
Per-ounce USD gross is direction*(executable exit-entry), with
long exit on BID and short exit on synthetic ASK=BID+0.41.
Commission is 0.000007*entry plus 0.000007*exit per ounce, using
FTMO's published current metal rate; actual target fees require
account reconciliation. Base net deducts that commission, zero
swap per owner instruction. Stress net deducts a further 0.41
spread plus 0.25 slippage per round trip (0.66 in total).
Report a matched-week always-LONG Monday-to-Thursday benchmark
without a stop, on exactly the same executed weeks, with identical
base and stress cost formulas. This tests whether the momentum
direction adds value to a gold long exposure; benchmark has no
prop-pass gate and is descriptive only. No news blackout.
