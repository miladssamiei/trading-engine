HYPOTHESIS 76: VXX MORNING MOVE LEADS ONE-HOUR US500 CASH RETURN
Declared 2026-09-25 before source download, Phase-1 code or run.

MECHANISM AND SOURCE QUALITY
Sung, An Empirical Study of the Price Leadership of VIX (2014),
https://ir.lib.ncu.edu.tw/handle/987654321/65376,
reports some 5-45 minute VIX-to-S&P 500 lead-lag at high frequency.
Tapiero, The Information Content of Intraday VIX and its Expected
Correlation on the S&P 500 (2015),
https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2674086,
reports incremental one-step-ahead S&P return-density information.
Those studies do not establish directional CFD profit, and VXX is
a short-term VIX-FUTURES ETN, not the VIX index. This study tests
the distinct, uncertain transfer that the first 15-minute VXX
move predicts the next one-hour US500 return in the inverse
direction. Earlier price-only morning and intraday-momentum FAILs
remain final; no market, clock, side, threshold or exit search.

DATA AND CAUSAL CLOCK
Obtain Dukascopy vxxususd BID M5 CSV for 2021-01-01 through
2026-05-01 (end exclusive) using the published dukascopy-node CLI;
save the unmodified export and SHA256 before any evaluation.
Dukascopy lists VXX quote history from 2017:
https://www.dukascopy-node.app/instrument/vxxususd.
The instrument is an indicative Dukascopy VXX quote, not an
exchange audit-trail trade/option feed. Epoch timestamps are UTC;
convert to America/New_York. For each NY cash date require REAL
VXX M5 bars at 09:30, 09:35, 09:40, all with positive tick volume.
The VXX signal is sign of (09:40 bar close - 09:30 bar open), fully
known at 09:45. A positive sign means SELL US500, a negative sign
means BUY US500; zero skips. No VXX level, magnitude, gap, year,
US500 prior return, news, volatility or other filter.

Use the verified US500-tick real-tick-derived BID M5 export at
data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv, SHA256
a8ee68f1a0010a1be9704c0d59fd5382aa798846cdae4d519b799ae21ec66c2d.
Require a real 09:30 NY cash open bar, and real 09:45 entry and
10:45 exit M5 bars on the same NY date. Enter at the 09:45 BID
bar open; exit at the 10:45 BID bar open. Signed BID open-to-open
move is gross US500 points. Subtract 0.51 point per trade for the
CFD spread and a second 0.51 for execution stress. One trade per
cash date; no stop, target, alternate fill or overnight position.
M5 open fills and fixed spread are optimistic execution proxies;
native Model=4 and FTMO quote/risk confirmation would follow only
an economic Phase-1 PASS.

through 2026-04-30 NY dates. Report source coverage and skips,
annual and side gross/base/stressed point totals, number of trades,
win rate, and date-bootstrap P(mean base net<=0). Bootstrap gated
cash dates 10,000 times with seed 20261012. This is the 76th
historical project study, so require p<=0.01 rather than the usual
0.05 as a selection caution, but even a historical PASS is not
independent forward confirmation. A FAIL is final and stops before
native tick replay or prop Challenge pace evaluation. Null expected.
