REPORT — SCALPING / CLASSICAL TECHNICAL-INDICATOR SCREENING FUNNEL (Hypothesis #14)
US500-tick, 2021-01-03 .. 2026-04-30. Written 2026-09-03, after the full
funnel completed. Readable without opening any CSV.

VERDICT: NULL RESULT — ZERO SURVIVORS. 50 pattern+filter combinations
entered the pre-declared funnel; 45 failed the Stage-A signal-quality
gate, the 5 survivors all failed the Stage-B R:R grid gate (not one of
their 80 grid cells was both positive after the 0.51 pt real-spread cost
AND strict-neighborhood stable). Stage C (walk-forward) therefore had no
candidates and Stage D (MT5 Model=4 execution check) was not run — both
outcomes are exactly what the SPEC pre-declared for an empty survivor
set. No classical scalping/indicator pattern in this universe is a
candidate for further consideration on this instrument. Model=0 was used
nowhere; in fact no MT5 tester run happened at all.

THE FUNNEL (all 50 fates in funnel_master.csv, nothing pruned):
- Stage A (exit-free, direction-native MFE/MAE at 0.5/0.75/1.0 x ATR14,
  day-block bootstrap p<=0.20, n>=150, entries restricted to the
  pre-declared [08:00,11:30) NY liquid window): 45/50 DROP-A.
  PASS: P1b (EMA8/21 cross on M5) with ALL FOUR filter arms F0/F1/F2/F3
  (n 2,481-3,052, p_boot 0.000) and P5_F3 (RSI divergence + combined
  RSI/MACD filter; n=904, p_boot 0.008). The count condition
  (MFE>MAE at EVERY threshold) was the binding failure for most
  drop-outs (e.g., P7 stoch divergence had mean_e up to +0.62 ATR yet
  MAE dominated at one threshold; breakouts P4a-d and pin/engulfing
  P2/P3 were pure noise, p_boot 0.23-1.00).
- Stage B (16-cell stop/target ATR grid, SP2L/Bollinger bar-path rules,
  SL-first ambiguity, 0.51 pt cost): all 5 combos DROP-B. Best cell of
  the entire study: P1b_F0 at stop 1.0 / target 3.0 x ATR — gross
  expectancy +0.236 pts/trade (win rate 26.0%, Sharpe net of cost
  -0.69), i.e. after-cost -0.274 pts/trade. The 80-cell matrix
  (matrix_master.csv) shows EVERY cell of every Stage-B combination
  negative after the binding 0.51 pt cost (and worse under the legacy
  0.8 pt). Strict neighborhood never became binding: no cell even
  reached positive after cost.

THE ECONOMICS (the single sentence version): the best classical-pattern
gross edge found (+0.24 pts/trade) is less than half the confirmed real
median spread (0.51 pts) — the same "expectancy smaller than
transaction costs" lesson the SP2L/ORB children revalidation delivered,
now shown for the whole classical scalping family up front, at a
fraction of the MT5 compute.

MULTIPLE-TESTING LEDGER (pre-declared in the GATE): 50 tests at Stage A
with alpha 0.20 imply ~10 chance passes; 5 observed — fewer than chance
alone would produce, and none survived the cost-aware grid. The two
patterns that did pass Stage A passed with wide margins (p_boot 0.000,
n>2,400), which is why they were allowed their Stage-B grid; their
failure is economic (costs), not statistical noise.

SESSION DEPENDENCE (descriptive, stage_a_descriptive.csv): for both
Stage-A passers the direction-native excursion edge lives inside the
liquid window and disappears or inverts overnight (P1b mean e: +0.092
pre-open 08:00-09:30, +0.106 at the open 09:30-11:30, +0.039 late cash,
+0.012/−0.013 evening/overnight). The pre-declared session restriction
was therefore the right sampling frame — but no window made any pattern
net-positive after costs.

IMPLEMENTATION-BUG DISCLOSURE (honesty rule): the first Stage-A run
contained a sign error making RSI==100 everywhere; it produced zero P5
signals and degenerate F1/F3 filter arms. The bug was caught by the
zero-signal audit BEFORE any verdict was recorded, fixed, and the whole
funnel re-run with added degeneracy assertions. Gates, thresholds and
the SPEC were never touched; only funnel_master.csv / matrix_master.csv
regenerated. The final numbers above are from the corrected run.

WHAT WOULD HAVE COME NEXT (not run, by protocol): Stage-C walk-forward
(52-cell MM-expanded space, half-year folds 2022H1..2026H1, occupancy,
trailing-expectancy cell selection) and Stage-D MT5 Model=4
reconciliation (D1 Sharpe +/-0.15, D2 >=85% trade agreement) apply only
to Stage-B survivors; with none, both stages are vacuous and their
artifacts (stage_c_survivors.csv, stage_d_reconciliation.csv) ship as
header-only files.

DOWNSTREAM GUIDANCE: do not re-run any P1-P8 pattern/filter arm on this
instrument/timeframe without a genuinely new pre-declared mechanism
(new SPEC). The economics statement to carry forward: on US500-tick
with a 0.51 pt real spread, an intraday pattern needs gross expectancy
above ~0.5 pts/trade before anything else (execution, walk-forward,
regime) is even worth measuring — none of the classical patterns came
close.
