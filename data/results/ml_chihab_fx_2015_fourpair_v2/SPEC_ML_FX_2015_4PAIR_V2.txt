CHIHAB-STYLE FOUR-PAIR FX HOURLY RE-MEASUREMENT V2 - PREDECLARED SPEC - 2026-09-27
V1 SPEC/GATE were frozen but not coded or run; V2 supersedes them after the owner supplied all-inclusive cost ranges.
This is a new measurement, not an overwrite of data/results/ml_chihab_fx/REPORT_ML_FX.txt. It adapts the frozen seven-feature V2 RF+Probit architecture to EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY and EURGBP from 2015 onward. It is not an exact replication of Chihab et al. (2019), whose instruments, dates and horizon differ. Prior EURUSD research exposure means even walk-forward forecasts are not an untouched strategy discovery.
Inputs and common period: GBPUSD, USDJPY and EURGBP use the user-provided C:/Users/Milad/download/{pair}-m1-bid-2015-01-01-2026-09-27.csv. EURUSD uses C:/Users/Milad/download/eurusd-m1-bid-2015-01-01-2019-12-31.csv stitched chronologically to data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv. Only UTC [2015-01-01, 2026-09-20) is scored/executed, the common available period. Source CSVs are BID-price proxies; provenance beyond the files is not independently verified. Record source hashes, raw/kept counts, first/last timestamps and year coverage. Timestamps must be unique, sorted, minute-aligned; OHLC finite and positive.
Data cleaning declared before model runs: The three user-provided 2015-2026 CSVs and EURUSD 2015-2019 contain carried no-tick minutes without a volume field. Drop a row exactly when O=H=L=C=the previous row's close; retain other flat bars. This may also drop a genuine unchanged-price minute, so report the count. Audited source anomalies confined to October 2024 have at most 0.3 pip OHLC envelope error: repair high=max(source high,open,close) and low=min(source low,open,close), record affected count and maximum error; abort if any error exceeds 0.31 pip or occurs outside October 2024. EURUSD 2020+ existing tick-derived M1 has positive tick volume and is not flat-filtered. Do not invent M1 executions in missing minutes.
Pip and cost convention: EURUSD, GBPUSD and EURGBP pip size 0.0001; USDJPY pip size 0.01. Owner-provided all-inclusive round-trip cost ranges in pips: EURUSD 0.6-0.7, GBPUSD 0.7-0.9, USDJPY 0.7-0.9, EURGBP 0.79-1.19. Primary/gated runs use the conservative upper endpoints 0.7, 0.9, 0.9, 1.19 respectively. Secondary lower-endpoint runs use 0.6, 0.7, 0.7, 0.79 solely as sensitivity, not as gate substitution. These are scenario assumptions from the owner image, not independently verified historical executable costs. Embed the cost once: long entry BID open+cost and exit BID; short entry BID and exit BID+cost, equivalently side*(exit BID-entry BID)-cost. No variable spread or slippage measurement. No swaps are charged; count positions crossing a New York date and flag this optimistic assumption.
Features and target: Aggregate observed M1 bars to epoch-aligned H1 OHLC, then fieldwise forward-fill a continuous H1 grid. Reuse evaluate/ml_chihab_replication.py V2 feature_table exactly: (C-SMA20)/C, (C-SMA50)/C, Wilder RSI14, MACD(12,26) line/C, MACD(12,26,9) signal/C, Bollinger(20,2) population width/SMA20 and Wilder ADX14. Do not include MACD histogram. y_t=1 iff close[t+1]>close[t], else 0; train only where t and t+1 are real H1 bars. Score only where t+1 is real.
Walk-forward and trading: Reuse V2 walk_forward, accuracy_summary and execute definitions without changing models or thresholds. For each monthly test fold, fit on the prior six UTC calendar months with a strict label-time embargo before the test month. RF is 64 trees, max depth 5, min leaf 25, sqrt features, seed 42. Probit is standardized train-only Binomial GLM with probit link, maxiter 100. Enter at the next available real M1 open when both predict the same direction. Exit at the next available M1 open upon either model's contrary warning; suppress same-open reversal. One open trade per pair, no structural stop or invented time exit. End-open positions are marked and excluded from closed-trade gate statistics.
Statistics and gate: For each pair report scored H1 count, RF/Probit/both-agree/train-majority accuracy, agreement coverage, closed-trade count, win rate, mean and total net pips, Net Sharpe=closed net-pip mean/std(ddof=1), and moving-block bootstrap P(mean<=0) with 10,000 resamples of five-trade blocks, seed 20260927. Report accuracy minus trade win rate in percentage points as a diagnostic with distinct denominators. Report annual entry-cohort PnL and full-calendar-year 2016-2025 sign agreement. The predeclared per-pair numerical gate applies only to the upper-cost primary run: >=150 closed trades, Net Sharpe>0 and MBB p<=0.05. Overall numerical gate requires all four upper-cost runs. Separate research qualification requires positive mean and positive PnL in each full calendar year 2016-2025. No model search or post-hoc parameter changes.
Artifacts and integrity: Freeze this SPEC and a verbatim GATE before writing/running evaluate/ml_chihab_fx_2015_fourpair_v2.py. Store predictions, fold table, trade ledgers, source/cleaning manifest and REPORT in this new directory, whether pass or fail. Never overwrite prior study artifacts. Audit cost identity, no overlapping positions and fold embargo. This BID M1 fixed-cost proxy cannot prove account-specific ASK fills, financing, FTMO compliance or live profitability.
