FROZEN INPUT AND NORMALIZATION
Use exactly the frozen native_quote_trades.csv from

including every trade and the one non-executable diagnostic mark.
Initial planned stop risk units for BUY = entry Ask - initial SL;

per trade = initial $10,000 times the declared risk fraction, fixed
dollars rather than compounding. Closed PnL = R times planned risk.
This assumes fractional position sizing with no FTMO volume grid,
margin or fill constraints. It is optimistic because actual FTMO
contract economics and real floating-equity minima are unavailable.

ROLLING CHALLENGE SIMULATION
Use every distinct NY entry date in the 2024+ frozen list as a start
date if the full 90/180-calendar-day follow-up is observable before
the tape's last NY date. A window includes all trades from that date
through the day before start + horizon. At each Prague midnight reset
the daily loss floor to prior-day closed balance minus $500. Since
these trades enter/exit the same Prague date, closed PnL updates the
balance in time order, and the date of each entry counts once toward
the four-day minimum. Static floor $9,000. On each close check floor
breach first, then closed balance >=$11,000 and >=4 trading days.
Stop at first pass or breach; otherwise TIMEOUT at horizon. A close-
only breach check is an optimistic lower bound on true breaches;
unobserved adverse floating moves can turn a reported pass/timeout
into a fail. This audit does not claim FTMO execution evidence.
