HAR volatility risk scaling over fixed Model=4 child streams, hypothesis #21
Frozen 2026-09-11 before implementation or result computation. Owner explicitly requested continued backtesting from institutional-ml-trading-research.md. This is the document's risk-forecasting lane: it does not predict trade direction, change ORB/SP2L signals, reopen a failed alpha family, or authorize FTMO execution.

MECHANISM AND CLAIM
Test whether a fixed causal HAR-style forecast of next-session US500 cash-session realized variance can improve the risk-adjusted performance and drawdown of a fixed equal-notional ORB/SP2L portfolio by reducing both child sizes in forecast high-volatility states. The mechanism is risk estimation and exposure control, not conditional-return alpha. The no-overlay comparison is the fixed portfolio; a 22-session realized-variance forecast and identical sizing formula are the simple risk baseline. A PASS means only that the overlay merits later execution-parity work on this historical proxy. Current FTMO qualification remains incomplete, downstream_model_allowed=false and live_execution_allowed=false regardless of result.

IMMUTABLE INPUTS AND REPRODUCTION
Price input is data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv, epoch UTC OHLC, 2021-01-04 through 2026-04-30. Build cash sessions in America/New_York [09:30,16:00); a date is eligible only when a 09:30 wall-minute bar exists. The common zero-fill universe is the 1369 eligible dates in data/results/orb_2021_present/mt5_days.csv under the existing session_open>0 or prev_close>0 rule. Trading inputs are data/results/tick_model_revalidation_children/orb_model4_trades.csv and sp2l_model4_trades.csv, semicolon delimited, already executed with Model=4 real imported ticks at one lot. Reproduce ORB 437 trades and +114.91, SP2L 1349 trades and +138.65, and record SHA-256 for every input. Group profit and trade counts by the New York date of open_time, zero-fill the common universe, and require every close_time to have the same New York civil date as its open. A reproduction, date, parse, finite-value or same-day failure stops with FAIL. Never substitute the regime-controller embedded child exports or Model=0 streams.

REALIZED VARIANCE
For each eligible cash day, start the price path at the 09:30 bar open, then append every M5 bar close through the last bar before 16:00. Daily realized variance RV_t is the sum of squared log changes along that path. Require finite positive OHLC prices, strictly ordered timestamps, at least 70 cash M5 bars, a 09:30 bar and positive RV. The first price is the contemporaneous cash open only for measuring that day's outcome after it completes; every forecast for day t uses RV no later than t-1. Use floor 1e-12 before logarithms. No annualization is used, avoiding the prior 21x realized-volatility scaling defect.

FORECASTS AND CHRONOLOGY
The fixed HAR log-variance model for target day t is OLS on an intercept, log(RV_{j-1}), the mean log RV over j-5..j-1, and the mean log RV over j-22..j-1 for matured historical targets j. Fit expanding through t-1 with at least 252 eligible target rows, refit every forecast day, and apply exp(predicted log RV plus one-half the in-fit residual variance) as the variance forecast. There is no regularization, model/horizon/feature search, refit schedule choice or sign flip. Benchmark B1 is RV_{t-1}; benchmark B22 is the arithmetic mean RV over t-22..t-1. The primary benchmark is the better of B1 and B22 by outer-validation mean QLIKE. QLIKE is y/f - log(y/f) - 1 with positive y and f. Also report log-MSE, Spearman rank correlation and realized-RV top-versus-bottom forecast-quintile ratio.

POSITION RULE
The fixed no-overlay portfolio is 0.50 lot-equivalent ORB plus 0.50 lot-equivalent SP2L on every day their frozen logic trades. For HAR and B22 sizing, calculate the expanding median of matured historical RV targets available before t. The common multiplier is clip(sqrt(training_median_RV / forecast_RV_t), 0.25, 1.00); it can only reduce exposure and applies to both children for the whole day before either can enter. No forecast changes side, setup, entry, stop, exit or trade count. Continuous lot-equivalents are a research idealization because authentic FTMO minimum/step volume is unknown. PnL is linearly scaled from one-lot Model=4 net profit; this assumes negligible size impact and is not an MT5 execution replay.

COSTS AND ACCOUNTING
The recorded Model=4 trade profits are the baseline net executed PnL and already contain that tester's realized bid/ask execution. Do not subtract baseline spread again. The 1.5x spread diagnostic subtracts an additional 0.255 point per scaled one-lot round trip and the 2.0x diagnostic subtracts an additional 0.510 point, derived from the documented 0.510-point real-tick median. Each child's daily extra cost equals its trade count times its 0.50 base allocation times the day's multiplier times the incremental spread. Both strategies are required to open and close on the same New York date, so financing is zero in this study. Use $1 per point per one-lot custom-symbol convention, $10000 base, no compounding. Sharpe is mean daily PnL divided by std(ddof=1) times sqrt(252); max drawdown uses cumulative zero-filled daily PnL. Report totals, Sharpe, max drawdown dollars and percent, worst day, trade days, calendar years, best positive-year share, multipliers and cost stress for fixed, B22 and HAR.

VALIDATION AND HOLDOUT
All eligible forecasts through 2024-12-31 after the 252-row fit minimum form the outer validation period. Dates 2025-01-01 through 2026-04-30 are the sealed overlay holdout and are not scored or written unless every V- gate passes on outer validation. Model fitting may expand causally through each prior date; the algorithm and gates never change. Full validation years are calendar years wholly contained between the first eligible validation forecast and 2024-12-31. If validation passes, open the holdout once and report it separately and combined, with no rescue. For inference on validation, use 10000 circular moving-block bootstrap samples of 20 consecutive calendar rows with seed 20260911; recompute the paired HAR-minus-primary-benchmark Sharpe difference, and report the probability it is at most zero. Bootstrap rows preserve same-day alignment of forecasts and PnL.
