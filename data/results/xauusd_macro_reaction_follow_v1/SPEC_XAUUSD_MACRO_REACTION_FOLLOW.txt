HYPOTHESIS 63: XAUUSD POST-RELEASE FIRST-MINUTE REACTION FOLLOW
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND SCOPE
Scheduled high-impact U.S. statistics create a new public-information
shock in gold. If the first minute does not fully incorporate it, the
direction of the initial XAUUSD midquote move may persist for 14 more
minutes. Trade that observable direction, not the unseen actual or
consensus figure. This is a release-conditioned information test,
distinct from prior generic intraday momentum, calendar timing, or
close-hedge studies. The earlier studies retain their frozen verdicts.
There is no threshold, parameter search, side selection, or stop/target
optimization. A historical PASS would be a candidate only: executable
orders and prospective performance would still need confirmation.

2. EVENT ROSTER, DATA, AND PROVENANCE
Use only the three U.S. BLS release families: Employment Situation
(PAYEMS), CPI (CPIAUCNS), and PPI (PPIACO). The date roster comes from
the public FRED US Macro Core Data event JSON, main-branch download
sha256 b9d8a2256ecc61fda3f68efbc243b407db5b19b0f2288c44f827d03854861201,
URL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superpilot69/fred-us-macro-open-data/main/data/fred-us-macro-events.json.
Use only event records whose seriesId matches one of those three,
releaseDateApproximate is false, and createdAt converted from UTC to
America/New_York is exactly 08:30. Use createdAt solely to identify
scheduled release DATE/TIME; never read consensus, actual, or later
revisions into the trading signal. Deduplicate simultaneous releases
to one event/trade per NY date. BLS states release-calendar times in ET;
archive dates remain a provenance limit because the third-party FRED
roster has not been independently reconciled date by date to BLS.
Report this limitation explicitly; a PASS cannot be called validated
without an official-calendar reconciliation and prospective execution.

XAUUSD raw tick CSVs and file-hashes are those frozen by
data/results/xauusd_onboarding/onboarding_stats.json. Raw fields are
timestamp epoch milliseconds, askPrice, bidPrice; the onboarding
verified monotonic timestamps and zero spread inversions. Use actual
Bid and Ask for all reference, entry, and exit quotes. No bar-model
touches, no fixed-spread fill approximation, no synthetic ticks.
One gold price unit means a $1/oz change; no compounding, no swap.

3. CAUSAL SIGNAL AND EXECUTION
At each 08:30 ET roster date, take the last valid raw quote strictly
before 08:30:00 as the pre-release midpoint, provided it is at most
10 seconds old. Take the last valid quote strictly before 08:31:00
as the reaction midpoint, provided it is at most 10 seconds old.
Direction is sign(reaction mid - pre-release mid). Skip exactly zero.
Do not inspect any quote at or after 08:31:00 to form the signal.
Enter at the first valid quote timestamp >=08:31:05 and <=08:31:10:
BUY at its Ask if direction positive, SELL at its Bid if negative.
Exit at the first valid quote timestamp >=08:45:00 and <=08:45:10:
long exits at Bid; short exits at Ask. If any required quote is missing
or invalid, skip the date and report why. No other exits, stops,
positions, or event-day filters. This quote-entry convention has zero
additional modeled order latency after the first quote in the window;
the stress test below charges one extra 0.41 unit per trade, but cannot
reproduce market impact or rejected quotes.

4. ECONOMICS AND MEASUREMENTS
Gross midpoint-reference move is direction*(exit mid - entry mid).
Executable quote net is exitBid-entryAsk for long and entryBid-exitAsk
for short. Round-trip spread drag = gross mid move - quote net, which
includes half-spreads at each endpoint and is measured per trade.
Extra-cost stress net = quote net - 0.41 gold unit, representing one
more stated CFD median-spread equivalent on the SAME trades. Report
entry/exit spreads, their distributions, gross/quote/stress means and
totals, win rate, max drawdown, year and release-family breakdowns,
and the actual elapsed hold time. No favorable price improvement.

5. PARTITION AND BOOTSTRAP
2020-2021 event dates are descriptive calibration, never gated. The
gated historical block is 2022-01-01 through the source-roster end
(2026-04-23), by NY release date. 2026 is partial. The frozen roster
has 153 distinct 2022+ candidate dates before quote-validity skips:
2022 36, 2023 36, 2024 36, 2025 32, 2026 13. Resample gated entry
dates with replacement 10,000 times, seed 20260929; report one-sided
P(mean quote net <= 0) and descriptive 95% quantiles. One trade per
event date makes date blocks independent for this calculation; do not
replace it with a trade-level bootstrap if roster defects emerge.

6. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-MRF1: At least 150 executed trades on distinct 2022-2026 event dates.
G-MRF2: Strictly positive mean extra-cost stress net per trade in the gated block.
G-MRF3: Date-bootstrap P(mean executable quote net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10,000 resamples, seed 20260929.
G-MRF4: Strictly positive executable quote-net total in each complete gated year 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025.
Overall Phase-1 PASS requires all four gates. Otherwise FAIL is final
for this artifact; no post-hoc change of direction, event family,
year, signal threshold, entry buffer, or exit time can rescue it.

7. STOP RULE AND ARTIFACTS
On FAIL, no MT5 tester. On PASS, separately freeze a Model=4 execution
SPEC/GATE and first reconcile every event date with official BLS
archives. Require actual order-state/tick matching, >=150 trades,
positive net expectancy, year-sign agreement, and latency/slippage
sensitivity. Even that would be historical evidence, not live proof.
Save this SPEC, verbatim GATE, event roster, code, quote-level trades,
metadata, and REPORT regardless of outcome. Never edit historical
SPEC/GATE/REPORT records.
