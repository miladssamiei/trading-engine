CHIHAB-STYLE RF + PROBIT HOURLY FX ADAPTATION - PREDECLARED SPEC - 2026-09-27
The requested EURUSD, GBPUSD and EURGBP hourly test is not an exact replication of Chihab et al. (2019), https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2019/8342461. Their article tested intraweek FX decisions on USD/EUR, USD/JPY and USD/GBP during 2014-2016; the user's EURGBP cross, 2020-2026 dates, H1 target and fixed seven features are new. Preserve this distinction in the report.
Data acquisition: Reuse data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv, a documented Dukascopy BID tick-derived M1 export. For missing GBPUSD and EURGBP M1 BID, use Python subprocess.run to call the installed npx.cmd dukascopy-node CLI with instrument, -from 2020-01-01, -to 2026-09-02, -t m1, -p bid, -f csv, -dir data/raw/dukascopy_fx, explicit file names, retries and a finite timeout. Save raw CSVs and download command/source hashes. The end is exclusive; clip all pairs to UTC [2020-01-01,2026-09-02), including Sep 1. Do not download existing nonempty raw files. Parse CLI UTC timestamps to epoch seconds. Reject duplicate/unsorted timestamps, nonpositive or nonfinite OHLC. Require each pair cover calendar years 2020-2026, at least 1.5 million M1 rows, and a last real bar on or after 2026-08-31. Report source first/last, row count, hash and missing-hour coverage. No synthetic M1 executions.
Price and cost: One standard pip=0.0001 quote-price units for all three pairs. Round-trip BID/ASK proxy floor in price units is EURUSD 0.00003 (0.3 pip), GBPUSD 0.00005 (0.5 pip), EURGBP 0.00008 (0.8 pip). Long entry=next BID M1 open+cost, exit BID; short entry BID and exit BID+cost. Report all net PnL and expectancy in pips by dividing quote-price differences by 0.0001. One open position per pair. Swap=0 in the model, but positions may cross broker rollover because no forced close was requested; count NY-date overnight holds and disclose the omitted financing.
Features and target: Aggregate each real M1 tape to epoch-aligned continuous H1 OHLC with fieldwise forward-fill. Reuse the seven frozen V2 features exactly: (C-SMA20)/C, (C-SMA50)/C, Wilder RSI14, MACD(12,26) line/C, MACD(12,26,9) signal/C, Bollinger(20,2) population-width/SMA20, and Wilder ADX14. No MACD histogram. A model observation t must be a real H1 bar; training label requires both t and t+1 real. Target y_t=1 iff H1 close[t+1]>close[t], else0; ties are DOWN. Feature F_t uses only data through completed t. Test accuracy is scored only when t+1 is real; score eligibility cannot affect predictions or trades.
Models and folds: Reuse evaluate/ml_chihab_replication.py V2 feature_table, walk_forward and accuracy_summary without changing their definitions. One rolling six UTC-calendar-month train window, then one month of strictly OOS predictions, repeated monthly. Label close time for every training row is strictly before test-month start. Per fold, train-only StandardScaler; RF 64 trees, max_depth5, min_samples_leaf25, sqrt features, seed42; Probit statsmodels Binomial GLM with probit link, maxiter100, class threshold0.5. No hyperparameter search or model-family fallback. Aborted/nonconverged folds are a complete failure rather than being silently replaced.
Trading: Reuse V2 execute for model-consensus entries on the next available real M1 open and one-model contrary-warning exits on the next such open, no same-open reversal, no structural stop/TP or invented time exit. All three are CFD runs with static structural drawdown interpretation: no intratrade prop guard is added because the user specified none for this FX study. Any OPEN_END position is marked at the last M1 close and excluded from closed-trade gate/statistics. Save per-pair OOS prediction CSV, fold CSV, trade ledger and source manifest. Report maximum signal-to-next-open delay (weekend gaps can be long).
Statistics and gate: For each pair report RF, Probit, both-agree and train-majority OOS classification accuracy, agreement coverage, scored counts, closed-trade count, net mean and total pips, Net Sharpe=mean/std(ddof=1) on closed net pips, win rate, MBB P(mean net<=0) using 10,000 five-trade block resamples/seed20260927, and full calendar-year PnL signs. The predeclared per-pair gate is exactly >=150 closed trades, Net Sharpe>0 and MBB p<=0.05; overall pass requires all three. A separate research qualification also requires positive net expectancy and agreement of complete 2021-2025 year signs. Accuracy-minus-trade-win-rate in percentage points is a diagnostic with different denominators, not a PnL measure.
Honesty and records: Walk-forward model fitting is strictly OOS, but this architecture and EURUSD tape have prior project research exposure. Fixed spread can understate variable spread/slippage. BID M1 cannot prove account-specific ASK fills, swap-free status, or FTMO compliance. Freeze SPEC/GATE before code and Phase 1; output immutable REPORT_ML_FX.txt regardless of result, append PROGRESS, update graph. A null result is complete.
