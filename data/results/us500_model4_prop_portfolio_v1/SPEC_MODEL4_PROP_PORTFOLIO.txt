HYPOTHESIS 73: US500 MODEL=4 ORB + SP2L FROZEN EXECUTION PORTFOLIO
Declared 2026-09-25 before any code or run for this study.
This is a fixed combination of two pre-existing MT5 execution streams,
not a new entry signal or an approval for live/FTMO execution.

MECHANISM AND QUESTION
ORB and SP2L enter US500 by different signal mechanisms. Combining
their fixed executed results may diversify losses enough that scaling
both legs could hit the first FTMO 2-Step $10k Challenge target within
180 calendar days without a high loss-floor breach rate. This study
tests that precise proposition under a deliberately optimistic
close-based risk model. A null verdict is a complete outcome.

FROZEN INPUTS AND DATA DEFINITIONS
Read only the stitched final corrected Model=4 MT5 real-tick exports:
data/results/execution_safe_model4_v1/stitched/orb_execution_safe_trades.csv
data/results/execution_safe_model4_v1/stitched/sp2l_execution_safe_trades.csv
data/results/execution_safe_model4_v1/stitched/orb_execution_safe_days.csv
The trade files are semicolon-delimited and profit is USD at one lot
($1/point/lot). Parse close_time as UTC and convert to New York date.
Use the ORB day file rows with session_open>0 or prev_close>0 as the
common cash-session day universe. Assert that every trade's open and
close are on the same New York date, every close date is in that
universe, and no duplicate trade key exists within a leg. Do not
modify, rerun, reselect or refit either expert. Check the base streams
against their frozen execution-safe report's trade count and PnL.

PORTFOLIO AND COSTS
Hold exactly one lot of each stream before a common scalar k. If both
signal on a date, both execute; use the recorded fills and no cap or
cross-strategy shadowing. The saved MT5 profit already includes the
historical real variable spread and zero recorded swap/commission.
For a conservative extra-cost scenario deduct another 0.51 USD per
executed one-lot trade before scaling. Daily base and stressed PnL are
the sum of trade profits by New York close date, zero-filled on the
common universe. No compounding, financing or post-hoc leg weights.
The extra 0.51 is a stress, not the actual FTMO quote-cost estimate.

EVALUATION SPLIT AND STATISTICS
2021-01 through 2023-12 is descriptive calibration. 2024-01 through
last available 2026-04 date is the historical gated block; 2024 and
2025 complete-year totals are separately gated. Report trade counts,
yearly totals, net mean, daily Sharpe, maximum close-to-close drawdown
and extra-cost stressed equivalents. For the gated block, bootstrap
the 2024+ session dates with replacement 10,000 times, seed 20261009,
and compute P(mean stressed daily PnL<=0). This is a historical
description after prior strategy exposure, not virgin forward OOS.

FIRST-CHALLENGE PACE SIMULATION
For each 2024+ session-day start with a full 180-calendar-day observable
window, reset first-phase account equity to $10,000. Apply every
subsequent day's stressed closed PnL at both legs scaled together by
k in the frozen grid {1,2,4,8,16,32}. First closed-day equity at or
above $11,000 passes only after four distinct dates with at least one
opened/closed trade. Closed-day equity at or below $9,000 breaches;
daily closed PnL at or below -$500 also breaches. Check breach before
pass for each date, otherwise time out after 180 calendar days. Report
pass, breach, timeout and median days-to-pass for every k; count
each rolling start but do not claim independent trials. Same-day
intraday equity excursions, Prague rollover and FTMO broker fills are
not observed; the pass rate is optimistic, and breach is understated.

GATES FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-MP1: Both input streams reproduce the corrected Model=4 report's trade counts and USD totals, with every trade on one New York date inside the common session universe.
G-MP2: The 2024+ combined extra-0.51-per-trade stressed daily mean is strictly positive and its 10000-resample date-bootstrap P(mean<=0) is at most 0.05.
G-MP3: Both complete gated years 2024 and 2025 have strictly positive combined extra-0.51-per-trade stressed USD totals.
G-MP4: At least one fixed common scale k in {1,2,4,8,16,32} has at least 70% first-Challenge passes and at most 10% close-based breaches among all complete 180-calendar-day 2024+ rolling starts.
All four gates must PASS for the Phase-1 portfolio screen to PASS.
Even a PASS would permit only a separately predeclared intraday-risk,
FTMO quote/account and simultaneous-MT5 execution reconciliation;
it would not validate or authorize Challenge trading. Any FAIL is
final for this frozen artifact. No new k, window, leg weight, exit,
year selection or cost revision after seeing results.
