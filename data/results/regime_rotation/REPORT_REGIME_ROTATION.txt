CAUSALITY BUG FOUND AND FIXED BEFORE ACCEPTING THE PASS

The first run's month-label cutoff (NY-midnight of the month's first
day) wrongly included the D1 bar stamped 00:00 UTC of the month's
FIRST TRADING DAY - a one-day lookahead (that bar completes 17:00 ET
on the day it governs). Caught by independent hand-recomputation of
pivotal labels (2022-03: correct bar says RANGE at trend_rel -0.881,
lookahead bar said DOWN-VOL at -2.223; 2025-04: -2.035 vs -1.509).
The cutoff was corrected to UTC midnight of the month's first
calendar day (includes the last prior-day bar, excludes the month's
own first trading day) and EVERYTHING was recomputed. Note honestly:
the buggy version also passed R1-R4 for arm P (Sharpe 0.608); the
causal version is STRONGER (0.720), so the pass is not an artifact of
the bug - the corrected labels flipped 2022-03 to RANGE and 2025-05 to
RANGE, both risk-off months that dodged SP2L losses. Post-fix, the
pivotal labels match hand-recomputation exactly; arm-day arithmetic
verified (UP-VOL day ensemble = SP2L only; RANGE day = 0).

Related permanent note: US500.c D1 bars are stamped 00:00 UTC of
their NY trading date (tz-converts to the PRIOR EVENING's date). Any
"bars strictly before day D" filter must use UTC-midnight cutoffs,
not NY-midnight ones. (The session-transfer study's descriptive-only
D1 regime split used nydate < d with these stamps - descriptive,
non-gating there; flagged for awareness.)

HONEST ROBUSTNESS NOTES (descriptive, no new variants run)

1. The 2025 result does not hinge on the single April exclusion: with
   correct labels, April 2025 (DOWN-VOL, SP2L-only, -14.1) avoided
   ORB's -228.6 tariff-crash month. Descriptive arithmetic on the
   reported outputs: had April been an ORB month instead, 2025 would
   be about +37 - still positive, R4 would still pass. The May 2025
   RANGE month (flat) similarly excluded SP2L's -101.2; the year
   passes with or without these two luck-adjacent months, but they
   are visibly the largest single contributors.
2. DOWN-VOL (SP2L in down-market vol) is the weakest regime cell
   (Sharpe 0.313). Per the anti-swap rule this cannot be redesigned;
   it is the honest cost of the pre-declared mapping.
3. The mechanism contains no new edge - it is a causal calendar split
   of two validated children. Its Sharpe comes from each child
   trading only in its documented favorable environment, plus 67
   risk-off RANGE days.
4. Same-tape caveat applies: one instrument, one 5.3-year window, and
   the 2022/2025 regime episodes dominate the label variety. Phase 2
   (MT5) and G-gates are the required next check before any of this
   is treated as validated.
