GATE_REGIME_ROTATION
Slow regime-rotation gates for hypothesis #13 (monthly ORB/SP2L
controller)

Status: written 2026-08-29 before any Phase-1 script. This file is a
verbatim extraction of all R and G gates in sections 6 and 7 of
SPEC_REGIME_ROTATION. Nothing here may be relaxed after rotation
output exists. Any new gate is exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 GATES (OFFLINE REGIME-ROTATION QUALITY)

These gates apply separately to each arm (P and Q). An arm passes
Phase 1 only if ALL R-gates hold.

R1. Sharpe dominance
    Compute ensemble daily PnL over the common 1,369-day ORB
    session-day universe, zero-filled, and Sharpe as mean divided by
    standard deviation (ddof=1) times sqrt(252).
    Gate:
        Sharpe > 0
        AND Sharpe strictly greater than Sharpe of every child (with
            two children this means > max(0.337, 0.275))
        AND Sharpe strictly greater than the best static portfolio
            Sharpe (> 0.420).

R2. Drawdown bound
    Compute true max drawdown of the ensemble cumulative PnL curve at
    the 2.00-lot book size.
    Gate: ensemble max drawdown <= 11.29 percent (equal to 1.10 x the
    worst child's drawdown at 2 lots, 10.26 percent).

R3. Activity floor
    Gate: number of days with at least one trade in the ensemble
    >= 150.

R4. Year shape and 2025 stress case
    Over full years 2021-2025:
    - At least 4 of 5 years net positive.
    - No single year contributes more than 60 percent of total net
      PnL.
    - 2025 stress case: ensemble 2025 net must be >= 0 OR strictly
      better than every baseline's 2025 net; i.e. > -228.03 points,
      the best baseline 2025.
    2026 is partial; it is reported only and does not count toward the
    4-of-5 rule.

PHASE 1 PASS CONDITION
    An arm passes Phase 1 if R1, R2, R3, and R4 all hold for that arm.
    Rotation moves to Phase 2 only if at least one arm (P or Q) passes
    all R-gates.
    If both arms fail, the project must stop at REPORT_REGIME_ROTATION:
    no Phase 2 code, no EA, no mapping changes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 GATES (MT5 IMPLEMENTATION), ONLY ON PHASE-1 PASS

These gates apply to the single RegimeController EA implementing the
passing arm.

G1. Consistency between offline and MT5 rotation
    Run the RegimeController EA in MT5 over the same window
    (2021-01..2026-04) in Model=0 batch mode, computing labels
    internally from D1 and applying the same mapping and weights.
    Gate:
        MT5 ensemble Sharpe is within +/- 0.15 (absolute difference) of
        the offline Phase-1 Sharpe for the same arm.
        AND MT5 ensemble max drawdown is within +/- 20 percent
        (relative difference) of the offline Phase-1 max drawdown.
    These tolerances are sized by the documented SP2L cross-tape
    residuals, not chosen loosely.

G2. Execution integrity
    Gate: all of the following must hold over the test window:
    - Zero trades on any day by a child that is disabled under the
      offline mapping for that day's regime.
    - The MT5 month-decision schedule (which families are enabled per
      month) matches the offline reference for at least 95 percent of
      months; every mismatch must be classified and explained.
    - Every trade is attributed to exactly one child via magic
      numbers; there are no unattributed trades.

PHASE 2 PASS CONDITION
    The RegimeController is validated only if both G1 and G2 hold for
    the passing arm.
    If G-gates fail, the regime-rotation controller is rejected;
    children remain individually as validated and no live regime
    controller may be deployed under this SPEC.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STOP AND HONESTY RULES

    Child EAs remain frozen: no entry, exit, lot, or parameter changes
    are allowed under this SPEC in Phase 1 or Phase 2.

    Labels, thresholds, mappings, arms, and gates are fixed before any
    rotation metric is computed. Any variant added after viewing
    results must be labeled EXPLORATORY and cannot rescue a failed arm.

    Mapping inversion or reassignment after seeing results is
    forbidden (anti-swap rule). Per-regime child P&L cannot be used to
    redesign the mapping.

    If Phase 1 fails R-gates for both arms, no Phase 2 scripts or EAs
    may be written. REPORT_REGIME_ROTATION is the final deliverable.

    If Phase 2 fails G-gates, the RegimeController cannot be used in
    production; any further work on it is exploratory and cannot
    change the failed verdict.

END OF GATE_REGIME_ROTATION
