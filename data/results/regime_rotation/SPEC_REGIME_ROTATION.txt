SPEC_REGIME_ROTATION
Slow regime-rotation controller on US500: monthly D1-label switching
of strategy families (hypothesis #13, project hypothesis numbering)

Status: SPEC written 2026-08-29 BEFORE any code or run. The companion
GATE_REGIME_ROTATION (artifact list, section 8) must be created as a
verbatim extraction of every R and G gate in sections 6 and 7 BEFORE
any Phase-1 script runs. Nothing here may change after rotation output
exists; additions are exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GOAL, MECHANISM, DISTINCTNESS

A slow controller that decides, at each month boundary, which strategy
family may trade on US500 during the coming month, using only simple
causal D1 regime labels. The question: can MONTHLY family rotation
improve robustness and risk shape over "always run ORB" / "always run
SP2L" / the static portfolio combinations, without inventing a new
raw-tape edge and without touching child exits.

Distinctness and priors, stated honestly:

  - Not a new price-pattern mechanism: all edges are inherited from
    the children; the controller reads D1 features only, never
    intraday tape.
  - Not the meta-coordinator (hypothesis #12): that controller gated
    children DAILY on their own trailing P&L (Sharpe 0.631, DD 5.17%,
    but FAILED its concentration gate - 2023 = 73% of net, 2025 still
    negative; null, not validated). This controller is SLOWER
    (monthly), uses market-state labels instead of child performance,
    and is deliberately complementary rather than stacked. The
    coordinator's failure pattern is a warning: improving Sharpe
    without changing the profit's shape fails the year gates here too.
  - Not the HMM regime filter (orb_regime, 2026-08-24): that learned
    hidden states and failed 0/3. Here labels are three transparent
    D1 thresholds, fixed in advance, with no fitting.

3. REGIME LABELS (monthly, three thresholds, fully causal)

Data: verified US500.c D1 history
(data/results/momentum_multihorizon/us500c_d1.csv, 2014-06..2026-08;
daily-return correlation 0.969 vs the verified tape - the documented
substitution). Features are computed on COMPLETED D1 bars only; the
label for month M uses data through the last completed D1 bar before
the first cash day of month M (in practice: through month M-1's last
trading day). US500.c provides 6.5+ years of D1 history before 2021,
so EMA200 and the trailing vol median are fully converged for every
label month in 2021-01..2026-04; no burn-in window is needed, and this
is verified (not assumed) in Phase 1.

Features (per label date):
  trend_rel = (close / EMA200(close) - 1) x 100, in percent.
  vol20     = std (ddof=1) of the last 20 D1 log returns x sqrt(252)
              x 100 (annualized percent).
  vol_med   = trailing median of vol20 over the prior 252 completed
              D1 bars strictly before the label date (causal; no
              full-sample statistics anywhere).

Taxonomy (evaluated in this order):
  RANGE      : |trend_rel| < 1.0                    (regardless of vol)
  UP-CALM    : trend_rel >= +1.0 AND vol20 <= vol_med
  UP-VOL     : trend_rel >= +1.0 AND vol20 >  vol_med
  DOWN-CALM  : trend_rel <= -1.0 AND vol20 <= vol_med
  DOWN-VOL   : trend_rel <= -1.0 AND vol20 >  vol_med

Threshold rationale (pre-declared, not fitted): the +/-1.0% EMA200
band gives a monthly taxonomy a non-trivial RANGE bucket (a +/-0.5%
band would starve it on this tape); the median split is the simplest
causal two-state vol classifier. Skew/kurtosis features are declared
OUT of the taxonomy (small-rule discipline; not computed, not
reported, not available for rescue).

Every cash day in month M inherits month M's label. Labels and
features are logged to regime_labels.csv BEFORE any P&L is joined to
them (artifact ordering enforces causality).

4. ROTATION MAPPING (fixed; two pre-declared arms, both reported)

Structural reasoning, fixed in advance from documented behavior only:
ORB is a displacement strategy whose documented regime evidence (the
orb_regime HMM study) says its winners historically sat in LOW-vol
conditions and high-vol filtering DEGRADED it -> ORB belongs in CALM
months, not VOL months. SP2L is a volatility-event strategy (spike and
gap ARE vol events) -> it is the natural VOL-month family. RANGE
months by construction lack both the calm-trend displacement ORB wants
and the vol events SP2L wants -> risk-off. No per-regime child P&L was
consulted to build this mapping, and none may be consulted to change
it.

  ARM P (complementary rotation, PRIMARY):
      UP-CALM   -> ORB only
      DOWN-CALM -> ORB only
      UP-VOL    -> SP2L only
      DOWN-VOL  -> SP2L only
      RANGE     -> no trading
  ARM Q (vol-gated stack, SECONDARY):
      UP-CALM   -> ORB + SP2L
      DOWN-CALM -> ORB + SP2L
      UP-VOL    -> SP2L only
      DOWN-VOL  -> SP2L only
      RANGE     -> no trading

Weights: every enabled child trades at exactly 1.00 lot (as
validated); caps per day: 1.00 lot per child, 2.00 lots total
(structurally inert with two children - verified, not tuned). Arm Q
differs from Arm P only by also running SP2L in calm months.

Rotation frequency: MONTHLY, decisions at month boundaries. A weekly
variant is declared OUT of scope (not computed) to keep the mechanism
slow and the analysis single-armed per schedule. State: the controller
is MEMORYLESS beyond the current month's label - no trailing-P&L
gating, no hidden state, no ML, no feedback loop. (This is the
deliberate opposite of the coordinator, whose daily trailing-Sharpe
gate produced its Sharpe lift and its concentration failure.)

Anti-swap rule: the mapping may NOT be inverted or reassigned after
results are seen (the same rule that rejected the HMM inverse filter).
Both arms are evaluated as declared; neither can be replaced by the
other's better result post hoc beyond the pre-declared
"at least one arm passes" pass condition.

5. PHASE 1 - OFFLINE REGIME-ROTATION QUALITY

Procedure: compute regime labels for 2021-01..2026-04 (regime_labels.csv
written first). Join child daily P&L streams (reproduction gate: exact
child totals/Sharpe/DD vs documented values, portfolio-study
tolerances - abort on failure). For each day, apply the arm's mapping:
a disabled child's PnL that day is treated as zero (simulated no-trade
- the child's own trades are simply not taken); enabled children's
PnLs sum to the ensemble day PnL. Every disabled-child-day is tallied.

Metrics per arm: Sharpe (mean/std ddof=1 x sqrt(252), zero-filled
common universe), max drawdown from the true cumulative curve, total
net, traded days, per-regime Sharpe/net/day-count, yearly net and
shares, correlation of ensemble daily PnL vs each child and vs static
S1/S2.

PHASE 1 GATES (R1-R4; an arm passes only if ALL hold):

  R1  Sharpe > 0 AND strictly greater than every child's Sharpe
      (> max(0.337, 0.275)) AND strictly greater than the best static
      portfolio Sharpe (> 0.420).
  R2  Max drawdown <= 11.29% (1.10 x the worst child's 2.00-lot-book
      drawdown, the same bound as the coordinator SPEC).
  R3  Traded days >= 150.
  R4  Year shape: at least 4 of 5 full years (2021-2025) net positive
      AND no single year > 60% of total net AND the 2025 stress case:
      arm 2025 net >= 0 OR arm 2025 net strictly better than EVERY
      baseline's 2025 net (i.e., > -228.03 pts, the best baseline
      2025). 2026 partial: reported only.

PASS = R1-R4 for arm P or arm Q -> Phase 2. Both fail -> STOP at
REPORT_REGIME_ROTATION: no Phase 2, no EA, no mapping changes. A null
result is a complete deliverable.

6. PHASE 2 - MT5 IMPLEMENTATION (only on pass)

Implementation model: a RegimeController master EA on one chart,
embedding faithful copies of both children's signal/exit logic with
validated parameters (the frozen SP2L file itself is never edited;
the embedded copy must reproduce the child's validated trade set
exactly when ungated - automatic G-gate failure otherwise). External
toggle scripts (attach/detach EAs monthly) are the documented
fallback only if embedding proves infeasible; the choice is made
before any Phase-2 code, with cause recorded.

MT5 constraints: one EA per chart; per-child magic numbers
(MAGIC_BASE + i); regime decisions and per-trade child attribution
logged to regime_days.csv (label, features, enabled children per day,
month decision). The EA computes labels ITSELF from D1 bars
(copy_rates, completed bars only, same thresholds/threshold history
conventions as Phase 1) - no Python-side schedule file is imported at
runtime.

PHASE 2 GATES:

  G1  Consistency: MT5 ensemble Sharpe within +/- 0.15 (absolute) and
      MT5 max drawdown within +/- 20% (relative) of the offline
      Phase-1 numbers for the same arm over the same window
      (Model=0 yearly batches, 2021-01..2026-04; tolerances sized by
      the documented SP2L cross-tape residuals).
  G2  Execution integrity: zero trades by a disabled child on any day;
      MT5 month-decision schedule matches the offline reference on
      >= 95% of months with every mismatch classified; attribution
      complete (every trade to exactly one child).

G-failure rejects the controller; children remain individually as
validated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK RULES

  - Child EAs stay frozen: no entry, exit, lot, or parameter changes
    under this SPEC, in Phase 1 or Phase 2.
  - Labels, taxonomy thresholds, mappings, arms, and gates are fixed
    before any rotation metric is computed. Post-hoc variants are
    labeled EXPLORATORY, cannot rescue a failed gate, and cannot be
    promoted silently.
  - No mapping inversion after seeing results (anti-swap rule; HMM
    inverse precedent). No per-regime child-P&L peeking to redesign
    the mapping; per-regime tables are OUTPUTS, never inputs.
  - All label statistics are causal (completed D1 bars only; trailing
    median; no full-sample quantiles). Burn-in adequacy is verified,
    not assumed.
  - A null result (rotation not demonstrably better than children and
    static baselines on the pre-declared gates) is a complete
    deliverable: SPEC, GATE, labels, stats, report.
