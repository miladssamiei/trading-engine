WHAT FAILED AND EXACTLY WHY (one classified root cause)

G1 DRAWDOWN CLAUSE: MT5 max DD 2.73% vs offline 1.80% -> relative
difference 0.512 > 0.20. FAIL.
G2 ZERO-DISABLED-TRADES CLAUSE: 31 SP2L trades on days the OFFLINE
schedule disables both children. FAIL.

Both failures trace to ONE month out of 64 - May 2025:
  - Offline label (terminal DB US500.c D1): trend_rel -0.982% -> RANGE
    (0.018pp inside the +-1.0% band) -> both children disabled.
  - MT5 label (tester-served US500.c D1): trend_rel -1.023% -> DOWN-VOL
    (0.023pp outside) -> SP2L enabled.
A 0.041pp difference in the EMA200 distance, from the tester's
secondary-symbol D1 feed differing slightly from the terminal's
database - the same documented two-tapes class that SP2L's own
validation flagged (secondary data sources disagree at sub-point
scale). Every other feature agrees to 3 decimals (2025-05 vol20 50.52
== 50.52, vol_med 12.90 == 12.90; 2025-04: -2.035 vs -2.115; 2025-06:
3.843 vs 3.80). SP2L's offline May-2025 loss (-101.19) therefore
entered the MT5 book; it explains the ENTIRE net gap (MT5 +750.83 vs
offline +852.02) and the entire DD gap. Nothing else diverged.

Verdict under the GATE as written: G1 FAIL (DD clause), G2 FAIL
(disabled-trade clause) -> the RegimeController is NOT validated on
this tape. Per the stop rules: no production use, no rescue attempts,
no relabeling; children remain individually as validated (ORB
standalone; SP2L with its own caveats).

Honest notes on what the gates measured here:
- The controller's MECHANISM is verified faithful (exact child
  reproduction, exact gated subset, 0 self-violations, 98.4% schedule
  match, Sharpe within 0.092). What failed is label-input fidelity for
  one borderline month in the tester environment, not controller logic.
- The DD clause's +-20% of a 1.80% baseline is +-0.36pp - inherently
  knife-edge: any single month's inclusion/exclusion can move it. The
  gate was pre-declared exactly so; it stands as written.
- A legitimate re-test path exists (not a rescue): run the same EA
  where the label source matches the offline one (tester/broker feed
  with terminal-identical US500.c D1, or a live/demo account whose
  terminal serves the same history the offline labels used). That is
  future work outside this SPEC and does not change this verdict.
