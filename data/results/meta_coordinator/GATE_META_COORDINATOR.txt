GATE_META_COORDINATOR
Meta-coordinator gates for hypothesis #12 (ORB + SP2L ensemble)

Status: written 2026-08-29 before any Phase-1 script. This file is a
verbatim extraction of all E and F gates in sections 4 and 5 of
SPEC_META_COORDINATOR. Nothing here may be relaxed after ensemble
output exists. Any new gate is exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 GATES (OFFLINE ENSEMBLE OVER EXISTING CSVS)

Let arm A be equal-notional weights and arm B be causal equal-risk
weights, both using the same meta-rules R1-R3.

E1. Sharpe dominance
    For each arm (A and B) compute daily PnL over the common day
    universe, zero-filled, and Sharpe as mean divided by standard
    deviation (ddof=1) times sqrt(252).
    Gate:
        Sharpe > 0
        AND Sharpe strictly greater than Sharpe of every child (with
            two children this means > max(0.337, 0.275))
        AND Sharpe strictly greater than the corresponding ungated
            static arm from the prior portfolio study:
            Arm A must beat S1's 0.420.
            Arm B must beat S2's 0.418.

E2. Drawdown bound
    Compute true max drawdown of the ensemble cumulative PnL curve at
    the 2.00-lot book size.
    Worst child drawdown at the same book size is
    max(2 x 5.13%, 2 x 4.17%) = 10.26%.
    Gate: ensemble max drawdown <= 1.10 x 10.26% = 11.29%.

E3. Activity floor
    Gate: number of days with at least one trade in the ensemble
    >= 150.

E4. Yearly profit shape
    Over full years 2021-2025:
        At least 4 of 5 years net positive
        AND no single year contributes more than 60 percent of total
            net PnL.
    2026 is partial; it is reported but does not count toward the
    4-of-5 rule.

PHASE 1 PASS CONDITION
    An arm passes Phase 1 if it satisfies E1-E4.
    Phase 1 passes overall if at least one arm (A or B) passes all
    E-gates.
    If both arms fail, the project must stop at REPORT_META_COORDINATOR:
    no Phase 2 code, no EA.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 GATES (MT5 EXECUTION), ONLY IF PHASE 1 PASSES

F1. Consistency between offline and MT5 ensemble
    Run the master EA in MT5 over the same window in Model=0 batch
    mode, with meta-rules identical to Phase 1 and trailing statistics
    computed from MT5's own closed-trade history.
    Gate:
        MT5 ensemble Sharpe is within +/- 0.15 (absolute difference) of
        the offline Phase-1 Sharpe for the same arm.
        AND MT5 ensemble max drawdown is within +/- 20 percent
        (relative difference) of the offline Phase-1 max drawdown.
    These tolerances are sized by the documented SP2L cross-tape
    residuals, not chosen loosely.

F2. Execution integrity
    Gate: all of the following must hold over the test window:
    - No "double-fills" attributable to the coordinator (no single
      child signal resulting in more than one unintended position).
    - Each trade is attributed to exactly one child via magic numbers;
      no trade is left unattributed.
    - Risk caps R3 are never violated on any day: total lots per day
      and per child stay within declared limits; this is verifiable via
      coordinator_days.csv versus positions.
    - Gating decisions in MT5 (child allowed/blocked per day) match the
      Python Phase-1 reference on at least 95 percent of days; every
      mismatch must be classified and explained.

PHASE 2 PASS CONDITION
    An arm is validated in MT5 if both F1 and F2 hold for that arm.
    If F-gates fail, the meta-coordinator is not validated; children
    remain individually as before and the coordinator is rejected.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STOP AND HONESTY RULES

    If Phase 1 fails E-gates for both arms, no Phase 2 scripts or EAs
    may be written. REPORT_META_COORDINATOR is the final deliverable.

    If Phase 2 fails F-gates, the meta-coordinator cannot be used in
    production; any further work on it is exploratory and cannot change
    the failed verdict.

    No child strategy's entries, exits, or parameters may be altered
    under this SPEC. The embedded child modules must reproduce each
    child's validated trade set exactly when gating is disabled;
    failure of this check is an automatic F-gate failure.

    Any variant meta-rule or weighting scheme added after seeing
    results must be labeled exploratory and cannot rescue a failed arm.

END OF GATE_META_COORDINATOR
