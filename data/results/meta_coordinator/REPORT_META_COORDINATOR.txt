WHAT THE COORDINATOR DID (mechanics, from ensemble_daily_pnl.csv)

Meta-rules as pre-declared: R1 activation (causal trailing 126-day
Sharpe > 0 AND trailing DD < 8%; 2021 H1+H2 = 126-day ungated burn-in),
R2 conflict veto, R3 caps (structurally inert with two children).

- R1 gated 912 of 2,486 post-burn-in child-days (36.8%): ORB blocked
  491 days, SP2L 421. The drawdown leg of R1 never fired (0 days) -
  every gating decision was the trailing-Sharpe condition.
- R2 vetoed 67 conflict days (both children trading opposite net
  directions); 601 days had both children active.
- Arm B (causal equal-risk weights) behaved almost identically to Arm A
  (the 1.00-lot cap binds for the lower-vol child, keeping weights near
  equal) - arms differ by < 0.01 Sharpe and ~8% of net.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HONEST READING

1. The Sharpe/DD improvement is real and large (+50% Sharpe over the
   best static combination, drawdown roughly halved vs static S1,
   same net). Trailing-Sharpe gating did exactly what it is designed
   to do: it kept the book out of each child's multi-month losing
   stretches (ORB 491 days, SP2L 421 days skipped; 2024 flipped from
   -53 static to +106 gated; 2025 loss trimmed from -488 to -378).
2. It did NOT change the profit's shape, and shape was the gated
   criterion: the profit is still a 2022-2023 machine (2023 alone =
   73%, slightly WORSE than static S1's 71.9% concentration), 2025 is
   still negative, and the SPEC named 2025 as the stress case a
   passing claim had to survive. The E4 concentration guard exists
   precisely to catch "better Sharpe by trading less on the same
   one-good-year stream" - and that is what it caught.
3. The coordinator's effective behavior is a coarse regime filter
   ("skip a child after a losing half-year"), not a diversification
   mechanism; with only two eligible children there is no agreement
   information to exploit (67 conflict days out of 601 co-active days)
   and no third vote to break shape concentration.
4. Per the stop rules: no Phase 2 code, no master EA, no exploratory
   T-window or bound variants (barred as rescues). The children remain
   exactly as validated (ORB standalone; SP2L with its own caveats).
   A future re-test has two legitimate paths, both outside this SPEC:
   post-2026-04 data where the children's regime mix differs, or a
   genuinely third validated child - neither exists today.
