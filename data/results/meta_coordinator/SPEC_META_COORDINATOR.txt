SPEC_META_COORDINATOR
Meta-coordinator EA on US500: causal day-level ensemble of validated
native strategies (hypothesis #12, project hypothesis numbering)

Status: SPEC written 2026-08-29 BEFORE any code or run. The companion
GATE_META_COORDINATOR (artifact list, section 7) must be created as a
verbatim extraction of every E and F gate in sections 4 and 5 BEFORE
any Phase-1 script runs. Nothing here may change after ensemble output
exists; additions are exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GOAL, MECHANISM, DISTINCTNESS

The coordinator combines already-validated native EAs into one
meta-strategy. Each child EA stays UNCHANGED as a signal/exit
generator; the coordinator decides, once per trading day, whether each
child may trade and at what weight. The question: can simple, causal,
pre-declared ensemble rules produce more stable performance than any
single component or than static combination?

Distinctness, explicitly:

  - NOT a new raw-tape pattern. No price-pattern edge is invented or
    searched here; all edges are inherited from child strategies.
    The coordinator never reads tick or bar data.
  - NOT a CAIRO-style ML ensemble. No model is trained, no fitting, no
    checkpoint bridge; coordination uses fixed rules over daily child
    aggregates (trailing Sharpe gating, conflict veto, risk caps).
  - NOT exit re-optimization. Exits live inside each child and may not
    be altered by the coordinator or by this SPEC. A child whose exit
    proves inadequate is handled in its own SPEC, never here.

Prior result this SPEC must confront honestly: the static combination
of these same two children was ALREADY tested
(data/results/portfolio_orb_sp2l/, 2026-08-27): diversification real
(Sharpe 0.337 -> 0.420 at 1+1 lots) but NOT compelling - 2023 = 77.5%
of combined profit, both legs lost 2025 together, all portfolios
bottomed the same week, recommendation was ORB alone. The coordinator
differs by adding CAUSAL DAY-LEVEL GATING on top. Its burden is
therefore heavier than "beat the children": the gated ensemble must
also beat the UNGATED static combinations (its own baselines) before
it justifies existence. A null result is expected to be a live
possibility and is a complete deliverable.

2. INPUTS AND DATA

Instrument: US500 only (US500-tick / US500.c mapping, unchanged).
Extension to XAUUSD or other symbols is OUT OF SCOPE for all gates and
is not pre-declared.

Eligible children - the admission rule is strict and mechanical:
a child must have (i) a native MQL5 EA, (ii) a completed
Python-vs-MT5 reconciliation with zero unexplained residuals, and
(iii) a walk-forward pass of its own S-gates (positive net expectancy
and Sharpe, >= 150 trades, temporal concentration pass). As of this
SPEC exactly TWO strategies qualify:

  CHILD 1 - ORB (opening range breakout)
    EA: ea/ORB_Strategy.mq5. Stream: MT5 net-of-spread trades
    data/results/orb_2021_present/mt5_trades.csv (447 trades,
    +470.67 pts, Sharpe 0.337, maxDD 5.13% at 1 lot).
  CHILD 2 - SP2L spike/gap
    EA: ea/SP2L_SpikeGap.mq5 (frozen by project rule - see section 6).
    Stream: MT5 net trades
    data/results/sp2l_2021_2026/mt5_trades.csv (1,343 trades,
    +281.84 pts, Sharpe 0.275 on the common day universe, maxDD 416.71
    pts at 1 lot). Its documented fragility (tester-tape sensitivity;
    XAUUSD point-convention band) TRAVELS WITH IT into every ensemble
    statement.

Explicitly NOT eligible (fail their own gates, no validated EA):
VWAP mean reversion (edge < costs), VWAP trend-pullback (Step-3 G3
fail), H1/H2, H2-morning, FVG, Bollinger MR, session transfer, both
momentum studies. If any strategy passes its own gates later, it may
be added ONLY via a pre-declared amendment to this SPEC, never
mid-analysis.

Data granularity: the coordinator operates on DAILY aggregates of
child trades - PnL_i(d), trade count, net direction exposure -
zero-filled over the common day universe (the ORB session-day universe
from the portfolio study, 1,369 days, 2021-01..2026-04). It never sees
intraday prices. Execution remains intraday inside the children;
coordination is defined at day level only, using information available
BEFORE the day's session open (cash-day boundary = the decision
point; all trailing statistics use days strictly before d).

3. CORE VARIABLES AND META-RULES (complete set; nothing else)

Variables (all causal, computed once per day d before the open):

  PnL_i(d)     child i's net-of-spread daily PnL (points, $1/pt/lot),
               zero-filled on the common universe.
  S_i(d; T)    trailing Sharpe of child i over the T = 126 cash days
               strictly before d (mean/std ddof=1 x sqrt(252), on
               zero-filled days).
  DD_i(d; T)   trailing drawdown of child i's cumulative PnL over the
               same window, in % of the $10,000 base.
  SIG_i(d)     signal presence: child i has >= 1 trade on day d
               (known from the child's own backtest stream offline;
               in MT5 from the child's pending signals at the day
               boundary - section 5).
  DIR_i(d)     child i's net direction exposure on day d: +1 if net
               long (long PnL weight larger), -1 if net short, 0 if
               flat or perfectly balanced.

META-RULES (fixed; three rules):

  R1 - ACTIVATION GATING. Child i is allowed to trade on day d iff
       S_i(d; 126) > 0 AND DD_i(d; 126) < 8% of base. During the first
       126 cash days of the tape (burn-in, 2021 H1+H2) NO child is
       gated - each trades at its baseline weight - because trailing
       statistics do not yet exist. (Project burn-in convention: 2021
       absorbs the warm-up.)

  R2 - CONFLICT VETO (two-child instantiation). If both children trade
       on day d (after R1) with OPPOSITE net directions
       (DIR_1 x DIR_2 = -1), BOTH are blocked that day: the book has
       no net directional information and only doubles cost exposure.
       Days with one child trading, or both trading the same net
       direction, pass through. NOTE ON THE GENERAL FORM: with only
       two children, a literal "trade only when >= 2 agree" rule would
       leave a degenerate sample; the conflict-veto is the declared
       two-child instantiation. If a third child ever joins, the
       literal >= 2-agreement rule becomes a pre-declared exploratory
       variant - not a rescue.

  R3 - RISK CAPS. Per-child weight never exceeds 1.00 lot (children
       run exactly as validated); total concurrent exposure never
       exceeds 2.00 lots; total book size each day = sum of allowed
       weights (constant 2.00 lots when both children are active, so
       results are comparable with the static portfolio baselines).
       With two children R3 is structurally inert (1 + 1 = 2) - it
       exists for future children and is verified, not tuned.

WEIGHTING ARMS (not rules; both run and both report):

  A - EQUAL NOTIONAL: w_i = 1.00 lot per allowed child (the static
      S1 baseline plus gating).
  B - CAUSAL EQUAL RISK: w_i(d) proportional to 1 / sigma_i(d; 126)
      (trailing daily-PnL std), normalized so allowed weights sum to
      the number of allowed children, each capped at 1.00 lot
      (R3). The static (full-sample) version of this was portfolio
      S2; Arm B makes it causal.

No other rules, weights, or windows may be introduced after Phase-1
output exists. T = 126 and the 8% drawdown bound are fixed here and
are not searched.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PHASE 1 - OFFLINE ENSEMBLE QUALITY (existing CSVs only; no MT5)

Procedure: replay the common day universe chronologically. Before each
day d, compute S_i, DD_i, sigma_i causally; apply R1 to both children;
determine SIG_i/DIR_i from the children's own MT5 streams; apply R2;
assign weights per arm A/B; ensemble PnL(d) = sum over allowed
children of w_i(d) x PnL_i(d). Every blocked child-day is tallied
(why: R1-negative-Sharpe, R1-drawdown, R2-conflict).

Metrics per arm: Sharpe (sqrt(252), ddof=1, zero-filled common
universe), total net, max drawdown from the true cumulative curve,
win rate (days), days with >= 1 trade, per-child contribution (net and
blocked-day counts), correlation of ensemble daily PnL vs each child
and vs the ungated static combinations S1/S2/S3 of the portfolio
study.

Comparators (all at the same 2.00-lot total book where applicable):
each child alone (ORB, SP2L); the ungated static combinations
(portfolio S1 equal-notional, S2 equal-risk, S3 ORB-75/SP2L-25 risk);
buy-and-hold ex-financing (documented benchmark row, reuse, no
recompute).

PHASE 1 GATES (E1-E4; all must hold for at least one arm):

  E1  Ensemble Sharpe > 0 AND strictly greater than EVERY child's
      Sharpe (with two children this means > max(0.337, 0.275)) AND
      strictly greater than the corresponding ungated static arm
      (A beats S1's 0.420; B beats S2's 0.418).
  E2  Ensemble max drawdown (true curve, 2.00-lot book) <= 1.10 x
      the worst child's drawdown at the same book size
      (worst = max(2 x 5.13%, 2 x 4.17%) = 10.26%; bound 11.29%).
  E3  Days with at least one trade >= 150.
  E4  At least 4 of 5 full years (2021-2025) net positive AND no
      single year > 60% of total net PnL. 2026 partial: reported only.

PASS = E1-E4 for arm A or B -> Phase 2. Both arms fail -> STOP at
REPORT_META_COORDINATOR; no MT5 work, no EA. Honest stop-and-report is
a complete deliverable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PHASE 2 - MT5 EXECUTION DESIGN (only if Phase 1 passes)

Implementation model: ONE master EA on one chart ("MetaCoordinator"),
embedding faithful copies of each child's signal/exit logic as
internal modules with their validated parameters unchanged. The
frozen ea/SP2L_SpikeGap.mq5 file itself is never edited (project
do-not-modify rule); the copy lives inside the master EA. Alternative
models (multiple EAs + inter-EA communication, chart events) are
rejected: single-EA embedding avoids the communication/permission bug
class entirely.

MT5 constraints: one EA per chart; one magic number per child family
(MAGIC_BASE + i) so every position attributes to its child; trades.csv
logging in the collector-compatible schema with a child-strategy
column; the coordinator's own daily decision log (allowed/blocked/
weights/trailing stats) written to a coordinator_days.csv.

Coordination in MT5: identical meta-rules, but trailing statistics
S_i, DD_i, sigma_i are computed from the terminal's OWN closed-trade
history (deal history filtered by child magic), not from Python files;
the same T = 126 and 8% bound; decisions at the cash-day boundary
before the first child may trade. Day-boundary and DST handling reuse
the existing session helper conventions (ORB/SP2L code base).

Reconciliation: full Python-vs-MT5 pass per the established standard -
the Python reference reproduces the OFFLINE Phase-1 ensemble exactly
(gating decisions included); MT5 runs in Model=0 yearly batches over
2021-2026-04; every Python/MT5 divergence classified (entry/exit
level, timing, count) with the documented tester-tape residual class
expected for the SP2L child (~10% trade-set differences, knife-edge
OHLC inequalities - classify, don't chase). An additional master-vs-
child integrity check: with gating disabled, the embedded child
modules must reproduce each child's own validated trade set exactly.

PHASE 2 GATES:

  F1  Consistency: MT5 ensemble Sharpe within 0.15 (absolute) and MT5
      max drawdown within 20% (relative) of the offline Phase-1
      numbers over the same window. (Tolerances sized by the SP2L
      child's documented cross-tape residuals, not chosen loosely.)
  F2  Execution integrity: zero double-fills attributable to the
      coordinator; 100% of trades attributed to exactly one child;
      R3 caps never violated on any day (verifiable from
      coordinator_days.csv vs positions); gating decisions in MT5
      match the Python reference on >= 95% of days, every mismatch
      classified.

F-gate failure -> the ensemble is NOT validated; null report; the
children remain individually as they are.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK RULES

  - No child's exits, entries, or parameters are altered by this SPEC
    or by the coordinator. The SP2L EA file stays frozen; embedding a
    copy is not modifying it, and the copy must reproduce the
    original's trades exactly (F-gate check).
  - All rules, windows, weights, and gates are declared BEFORE any
    ensemble computation. Post-hoc variants are labeled EXPLORATORY,
    cannot rescue a failed gate, and cannot be promoted silently.
  - Trailing statistics are strictly causal (days strictly before d;
    the 126-day burn-in is absorbed in 2021, which reports no gated
    result and is excluded from nothing else).
  - Correlations near the children are expected by construction (the
    ensemble CONTAINS the children); the informative comparisons are
    vs the ungated static combinations, and any claim of "new
    robustness" must survive the 2025 joint-loss year specifically
    (the known failure mode of this pair).
  - A null result (ensemble not demonstrably better than children and
    static baselines) is a complete deliverable: SPEC, GATE, Phase-1
    stats, report.

8. SCOPE NOTES

One instrument (US500 CFD), one tape window (2021-01..2026-04), two
eligible children today. The ensemble cannot manufacture an edge the
children lack; it can only reweight their existing streams causally.

compelling diversification, 2025 joint loss) is the bar the coordinator
must clear, and the 2025 joint-loss year is the specific stress case
every passing claim must address.
