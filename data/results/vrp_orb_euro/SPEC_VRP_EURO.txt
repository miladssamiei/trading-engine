SPEC: VRP-CONDITIONED EUROPEAN SESSION BREAKOUT ON US500 - PHASE 1 (HYPOTHESIS #43)
Declared: 2026-09-23, before any code for this study existed.

0. CONTEXT
  - Session-transfer companion of the #31 VRP-ORB screen: tests whether the
    VRP-conditioned edge (if any) also expresses in the EUROPEAN session of
    the same US500 tape, using the Frankfurt/London handover [03:00, 03:15)
    ET opening range instead of the NY cash 09:30 range.
  - Population: the 171 Arm A (VRP > trailing P50) DATES realized as trade
    dates in the #31 artifact
    data/results/orb_vrp_filter/orb_vrp_filtered_trades.csv (ny_date column).
    These are the only dated Arm A records available; the in-sample
    conditioning lineage of #31 is inherited unchanged (#31 overall verdict
    FAIL on G-VRP3 stands). Null result pre-accepted.

1. DATA
  1.1 Tape: data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv (1,791,504 M1
      bars, epoch-UTC open-time Bid OHLC; verified artifact, full 24h
      custom-symbol tape). All wall-time logic in America/New_York.
  1.2 Day unit: the Arm A ny_date; a date participates iff it has >= 1
      printed M1 bar in [03:00, 03:15) ET (minute-of-day [180, 195)).

2. OPENING RANGE (EUROPEAN OPEN)
  2.1 Euro_OR_High = max High, Euro_OR_Low = min Low over printed M1 bars
      with ET minute-of-day in [180, 195).

3. SIGNAL GENERATION
  3.1 Signal window: M1 bars STRICTLY between 03:15 and 09:30 ET
      (minute-of-day in [196, 569], open interval per project convention).
  3.2 LONG candidate: first bar with Close > Euro_OR_High; SHORT candidate:
      first bar with Close < Euro_OR_Low; independent per-direction first
      breakout, max 1 long + 1 short per day; dropped candidates (degenerate
      risk, no-entry) do not re-arm their direction.
  3.3 Entry: the Open of the next PRINTED M1 bar after the signal bar (entry
      may land at/after 09:30; the walk continues either way). No next bar
      before the day's last session bar -> no-entry drop.
  3.4 Stop: opposite OR boundary (LONG SL = Euro_OR_Low, SHORT SL =
      Euro_OR_High). Initial risk = |entry - SL| in points; risk <= 0 is
      degenerate and dropped.

4. EXCURSION WALK AND ECONOMICS (NET)
  4.1 Walk from the entry bar (inclusive) through the last printed M1 bar of
      the date with ET minute-of-day <= 959 (the 16:00 ET cash close
      boundary). Early exit when a walk bar TOUCHES the stop: LONG exits if
      Low <= SL at the SL price; SHORT if High >= SL at the SL price.
      HONEST-FILL RULE: if the touching bar OPENS beyond the stop, fill at
      the open (flag STOP_GAP; #40/#42 convention). Otherwise TIME exit at
      the close of the last session bar.
  4.2 MFE/MAE in points over the walk bars (entry bar through exit bar,
      exit bar's full bar included - M1 so the approximation is one minute).
  4.3 Gross exit PnL points = direction * (exit - entry). Net = gross - 0.51
      (verified US500 CFD round-trip spread floor; no swap - intraday).
  4.4 Net Sharpe = per-trade mean/std(ddof=1) of Net PnL x sqrt(252)
      (#31 family convention).
  4.5 Overlap with NY ORB (descriptive): same-date direction agreement vs
      the #31 Arm A NY-cash trade (BUY/SELL), mean Net PnL for agree vs
      disagree, and the share of Euro trades whose exit printed strictly
      before 09:30 ET (resolved pre-NY-open).

5. GATES (frozen verbatim in GATE_VRP_EURO.txt)
  G-EU1 total executed trades >= 50.
  G-EU2 Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
  G-EU3 day-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 20260924) P(mean Net
      PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
  G-EU3 operationalization (frozen): pooled-mean day-block bootstrap over
      NY-date blocks (resample dates with replacement; pooled mean = sum of
      block sums / sum of block counts), rng seed 20260924, 10,000 resamples
      (#36/#38 family convention).

7. HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK RULES
  - Causality: the Euro OR is fully formed before the signal window opens;
    signals use only the signal bar's close vs precomputed levels; entries
    at printed next-bar opens; stops evaluated on completed bars only.
  - The Arm A date set is in-sample conditioning realized through #31's
    trade dates; every metric inherits that optimism. This study does not
    re-litigate #31 (its FAIL stands).
  - No parameter search: OR window, signal window, stop rule, 0.51 cost are
    frozen archetype values.
  - Null result is final for this artifact; verdicts quote frozen gates only.
