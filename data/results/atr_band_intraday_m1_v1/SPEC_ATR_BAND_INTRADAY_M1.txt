HYPOTHESIS 61: INTRADAY ATR LOWER-BAND LIMIT TOUCH WITH M1 PATH AUDIT
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. PROVENANCE AND MECHANISM
Hypothesis 22's fixed long rule, Close(M5,i-1) - 3.1 x ATR14(M5,i-1),
has positive bar-model expectancy on both US500 and XAUUSD. Its source
code fills at a Bid-bar low touching that band and assumes an exact
limit fill, often followed by an exit on the same M5 bar. A later
FTMO replay used market entry at the bar open, which does not test the
intended limit order. The present study is a NEW, predeclared fidelity
and intraday test: a buy limit must be reachable on the Ask side at
the instrument's documented median spread, entry/exit are walked on
M1, and all trades are forced flat within a fixed same-day session.
This is motivated by the known artifact; it does not change or reopen
the frozen H22 or FTMO verdicts. Existing full-tape exposure makes any
PASS historical and non-virgin. Both assets and all parameters below
are fixed now, with no parameter or side search.

2. VERIFIED TAPES AND PRICE CONVENTION
US500-tick: M5 data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv and M1
data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv; spread 0.51 point.
XAUUSD-tick: M5 data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m5.csv and
M1 data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv; spread 0.41 gold
price unit. Both are Bid OHLC exports from imported ticks. Convert
epoch UTC to America/New_York. Build no M15 bars. Exact M1 printed
minutes only; never forward-fill missing minutes. Skip dates without
the specified entry-session opening M5 bar or exact exit minute.
US500 session: eligible entry M5 opens [09:35,15:50] ET, forced exit
at exact printed 15:59 M1 close. Cash day needs a 09:30 M5 bar.
XAUUSD session: eligible entry M5 opens [08:20,13:20] ET, forced exit
at exact printed 13:29 M1 close. Gold day needs an 08:20 M5 bar.
These are fixed liquid-session choices, not optimized from results.
One long position maximum per asset; assets are independent.

3. CAUSAL INDICATOR AND LIMIT ORDER
Use the source script's ACTUAL Wilder ATR seed so the fidelity change
isolates execution: TR[0] = High[0]-Low[0], later TR = max(H-L,
abs(H-Cprev), abs(L-Cprev)); ATR[14] = mean(TR[0:15]); for k>14,
ATR[k] = (13*ATR[k-1]+TR[k])/14. ATR is computed across the full
M5 tape, including overnight gaps. For eligible M5 bar i, after
bar i-1 completed, buy-limit Ask level L = CloseBid[i-1] - 3.1
x ATR[i-1]. Order is active for printed M1 bars with open epoch
times in [M5open[i], M5open[i]+300 seconds), then cancelled if
unfilled. No entry on the 09:30 US500 bar, and no outside-session
entry on either asset. Enter only if M1 LowBid + fixed spread <= L.
When the M1 open Bid + spread <= L, treat the entry as immediately
filled at L anyway (no favorable gap improvement). Otherwise treat
the first qualifying low in the minute as the fill, still at L.
The fixed Ask proxy is a Phase-1 approximation; Model=4 real Bid/Ask
ticks are required for confirmation. A signal with no M1 touch is
counted as an unfilled order, not a trade.

4. EXITS, OCCUPANCY, AND COST
Initial stop is L - 1.5 x entry ATR; target is L + 2.0 x entry ATR.
Evaluate Bid M1 bars from the fill minute through the exact forced-
exit minute. Stop first if LowBid <= stop; fill at min(stop, OpenBid)
for a gap through stop on a later minute, or stop for the entry minute.
If stop not hit, target if HighBid >= target; fill at target.
On the entry minute only, a target is allowed if OpenBid+spread <= L
(order filled at the minute open); otherwise defer a target hit on
that minute because its high may have occurred before the limit
fill. Stop still takes priority on the entry minute. If neither
bracket fires, exit at the forced minute's Bid close. Skip new M5
signals while a trade is open; after exit, first eligible new M5
bar is the first whose open is strictly after the exit M1 open.
No trade spans the session boundary; no swap. Gross points = exit
Bid - (L - spread), a Bid-to-Bid reference; base net points =
exit Bid - L, equivalent to gross - spread. Double-cost stress net
= base net - spread on the SAME trades; it is a cost buffer, not a
second fill-probability simulation. Units are points for US500 and
gold price units for XAUUSD, always 1 unit/lot and no compounding.

5. PARTITION AND UNCERTAINTY
For both assets, 2024-01-01 onward by NY entry date is the gated
historical test block; earlier dates are diagnostic. The XAUUSD
tape ends 2026-09-01; US500 ends 2026-04-30. 2026 is partial.
Report all years, long-only trades, samples, unfilled orders, bracket
vs forced exits, hold durations, net mean/total/win rate/max drawdown,
and the fraction of trades exiting in the entry M1 bar. Resample
test-block entry dates with replacement 10,000 times; seed 20260927.
Because two assets are tested, use family-wise one-sided threshold
0.025 for each asset. Report descriptive 95% bootstrap quantiles.

6. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-AB1: At least one instrument has at least 150 executed trades in its 2024-2026 test block.
G-AB2: At least one instrument with at least 150 test-block trades has strictly positive mean double-cost net per trade.
G-AB3: At least one instrument satisfying G-AB1 and G-AB2 has date-bootstrap P(mean base net <= 0) at most 0.025, using 10,000 resamples and seed 20260927.
G-AB4: The same instrument satisfying G-AB1 through G-AB3 has strictly positive base-net total in each complete test-block year 2024 and 2025.
The SAME instrument must satisfy all four for overall Phase-1 PASS.
Otherwise FAIL is final for this artifact. No post-hoc filtering by
instrument year, order time, hold duration, or exit type is allowed.

7. STOP, LIMITS, AND ARTIFACTS
If Phase 1 fails, stop; no MT5 tester. If it passes, a separate
predeclared Model=4 real-tick execution SPEC/GATE is required, with
native Ask limit triggers, order-state reconciliation, >=150 trades,
year-sign agreement, and positive net expectancy. Even a Phase-1
PASS is only an M1 proxy, not a deployable result. Freeze this SPEC,
its verbatim GATE, code, trade CSV, metadata, and REPORT. Always
write the REPORT, including on FAIL. Never edit historical records.
