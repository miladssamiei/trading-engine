CATEGORICAL REGIME-SWITCHING OMNIBUS - PREDECLARED SPEC - 2026-09-27
Question: Do fixed hourly ADX-regime Bollinger mean-reversion and Donchian momentum sub-engines produce positive closed-trade net expectancy/Sharpe on the available GC, NQ and ES proxy tapes, separately at the user's CFD and futures cost floors?
Inputs: GC=data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv, NQ=data/results/virgin_assets/ustech_m1.csv, ES=data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv. Use all available M1 rows, unique ascending UTC epoch seconds. GC/NQ cover 2020-2026; ES covers 2021-2026. These are bid-only CFD-derived proxies, not native CME futures trades/quotes. Hash tapes and evaluator. Six independent runs, with no mixed environment occupancy or optimization.
Costs: CFD GC .41, NQ 1.00, ES .51; futures proxy GC .13, NQ .39, ES .30, each price units per completed round trip and embedded once in fills. Long executes Ask=Bid+cost and exits Bid. Short enters Bid and exits Ask=Bid+cost. No commissions beyond the supplied futures floors and no swap charge; overnight counts are reported. Actual variable spread/slippage and financing are unmeasured.
Grid and signals: Aggregate M1 into epoch-aligned hourly OHLC, fieldwise forward-fill all absent H1 buckets. Indicators use this continuous grid. Entry at the first real M1 open of REAL hour t; all signal values are from completed H1 hour t-1. Prior t-1 may be forward-filled and is counted. One open position total per asset/environment across both regimes; suppress every signal in a held position and its exit hour, without cooldown. Require at least 20 completed grid hours for indicators. Hourly Wilder ATR14/ADX14 use alpha=1/14; invalid ADX or ATR cannot signal. ADX<25 is consolidation, ADX>=25 directional. Regime is fixed on entry and attached to the trade.
Consolidation engine: On H1 t-1, compute SMA20 and rolling population standard deviation20 (ddof=0), bands SMA +/- 2*std. Long when close<lower band, short when close>upper band. Fixed target is the t-1 SMA: long target Bid=SMA, short target Ask=SMA. Structural stop is executed long Ask entry-1*prior ATR14 or short Bid entry+1*prior ATR14 in Ask. Skip an entry if the target is not strictly profitable after the supplied cost or ATR<=0. This is a frozen valid-bracket rule; skipped counts are reported.
Directional engine: At H1 t-1, long when close exceeds the Donchian20 high from t-2; short when close is below the Donchian20 low from t-2. Donchian high/low at t-2 use the inclusive trailing 20 continuous bars ending t-2. Long target Bid=executed Ask entry+1.5*prior ATR14; short target Ask=entry Bid-1.5*prior ATR14. Structural stop is executed Ask entry-1*prior ATR14 long or Bid entry+1*prior ATR14 short in Ask. No trailing stop or time limit.
M1 exits: From the entry M1 onward, long stop trips at M1 Bid low, target at Bid high; short stop trips at M1 Ask high, target at Ask low. Normal stop gap fills at the worse M1 open; target gap fills at the better M1 open. On an M1 touching multiple levels, structural stop wins first, then futures prop guard, then target. A trade still open at the tape end is marked OPEN_END at last M1 close for diagnostic mark-to-market but excluded from closed-trade statistics and gate.
Futures-only prop guard: Entry open-equity is zero net price-unit PnL. Track Peak_Open_Equity as maximum observed favorable M1 high/low net PnL, floored at zero. On each M1, test its adverse low/high against the previous peak minus 6.0*entry H1 ATR14; if crossed, liquidate at that threshold, or worse M1 open on an adverse gap, with reason PROP_LIQUIDATION. Then, only if no exit, update peak with that M1 favorable extreme. This adverse-before-favorable convention avoids assuming the favorable extreme happened first within the same unknown M1 path. No futures guard or other equity rule in CFD runs. This is a per-position proxy, not an account-wide firm rule.
Statistics: For each of six independent environment/asset runs, closed trade count, net price-unit expectancy, total, unannualized per-trade Net Sharpe=mean/std(ddof=1), and moving-block bootstrap P(mean net<=0) using 10,000 trade-order samples, block length five, seed 20260927. Gate per row: >=30 closed trades and Net Sharpe>0; p-value is diagnostic, not a gate. Overall pass only if all six rows pass. Break down count/net/mean by Consolidation versus Directional and by year, plus exact exit counts including PROP_LIQUIDATION on futures rows. Preserve all six trade ledgers, run metadata and plain-text REPORT. Full-tape data were exposed in prior studies, so passes are exploratory and not walk-forward confirmation; no prop challenge pass claim. SPEC/GATE/REPORT immutable; corrections require a new study name. Null results are complete.
