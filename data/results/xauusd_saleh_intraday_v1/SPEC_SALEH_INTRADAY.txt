HYPOTHESIS 70: SAME-DAY XAUUSD DONCHIAN/SUPERTREND BREAKOUT
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND RELATION TO HYPOTHESIS 57
Test whether Hypothesis 57's 4H Supertrend-gated 1H Donchian
breakout with ADX filter earns enough within the same New York
calendar day to cover native gold CFD cost. The prior #57 gold
screen made most profit on multi-day trends (median hold 42h),
so it does not answer the user's intraday request. Its frozen
PASS remains a swing-screen result. This adaptation changes only
entry-hour eligibility and a mandatory daily flat exit. No
parameter, side, signal, indicator, stop, or cost-floor search.
The historical tape has already hosted many studies; any Phase-1
PASS is a selected-screen result and needs later independent
forward confirmation before a live-edge claim.

2. PRICE DATA AND UNCHANGED CAUSAL FEATURES
Read verified native-gold BID M1 bars at data/results/xauusd_onboarding/
xauusdtick_m1.csv, 2337538 rows from 2020-01 to 2026-09-01,
same hashed tape and same 1H/4H continuous-grid conventions as #57.
Use #57 load_ctx() and detect_signals() unchanged, including:
1H Donchian(20) previous completed close outside channel of t-2;
4H NY-wall Supertrend(10,3) last strictly completed anchor;
1H Wilder ADX14(t-1)>=20; ATR14, REAL execution bars only.
No use of any future bar for signal or indicator. Keep original
single-position occupancy and shadow all signals on or before
the current exit bar.

3. INTRADAY ENTRY, EXIT, COST
On each NY civil date, eligible 1H entry opens have NY wall hour
00 through 15 inclusive; hours 16-23 are skipped. Require that
same NY date to have a REAL 15:00-16:00 1H bar; otherwise skip
all candidates that date. Entry remains at REAL bar t BID open
for short, ASK=BID+0.41 for long. Initial and trailing stops are
the exact #57 rules, 4*Wilder ATR14, with stop check on the
entry bar, conservative gap fill and ratchet after each REAL
hour close. At the end of the REAL 15:00-16:00 bar, if no stop
triggered earlier, force close at its final BID price for long
or ASK=BID+0.41 for short. Stops on that same final hour take
priority. This is before 17:00 NY rollover, max hold <16 hours,
same NY entry and exit date; no overnight financing. No stop
change, take-profit, partial, or re-entry on the exit bar.
M1 reconstruction applies a fixed 0.41 round-trip spread and is
optimistic about bar-open entry, queueing, quote spread variation,
and stop level-touch fills. PnL units are gold price units/lot.
Extra-cost stress = reconstructed net minus another 0.41 on
exactly the same trades. Actual tick Bid/Ask must be tested in
a separate Model=4 execution study if this M1 phase passes.

4. PARTITION, INFERENCE, REPORTING
2020-2023 NY entry dates descriptive calibration; 2024-01-01
through 2026-09-01 NY entry dates gated historical block.
Report funnel, counts, realized holding-hour distribution,
gross/net/stress means and totals, win rate, profit factor,
maximum drawdown, side, exit type and calendar-year totals.
One or more trades may occur per date. Bootstrap distinct gated
NY entry dates with replacement 10000 times, seed 20261006,
keeping every trade of the selected date; report P(mean net<=0)
and descriptive 95% quantiles. No random-walk or parameter search.
2026 is partial and reported separately from complete 2024/25.

5. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-SID1: At least 150 executed 2024-2026 trades spanning at least 150 distinct New York entry dates.
G-SID2: Strictly positive mean extra-cost stress net gold units per gated trade.
G-SID3: Date-bootstrap P(mean reconstructed quote net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10000 resamples, seed 20261006.
G-SID4: Strictly positive reconstructed net total gold units in each complete gated year 2024 and 2025.
G-SID5: Every executed trade has same NY entry/exit date and holding time below 16 hours.
All five required for Phase-1 PASS. Otherwise FAIL final for
this artifact; no post-hoc time, side, spread or stop rescue.
