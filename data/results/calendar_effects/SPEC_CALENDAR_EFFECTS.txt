SPEC_CALENDAR_EFFECTS
Calendar structure of US500: unconditional overnight premium and
day-of-week seasonality, two independent tests
(hypothesis #14, project hypothesis numbering)

Status: SPEC written 2026-08-30 BEFORE any code or run. The companion
GATE_CALENDAR_EFFECTS (artifact list, section 9) must be created as a
verbatim extraction of every gate number in sections 2, 6 and 7 BEFORE
the first Phase-1 script runs. Nothing in this document may be changed
after Phase-1 output exists; any addition is exploratory and labeled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GOAL, MECHANISMS, DISTINCTNESS

Two INDEPENDENT, UNCONDITIONAL, calendar-only hypotheses on US500,
defined and gated separately. A null result for either or both is a
complete, expected deliverable.

  HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT: being long US500 from the cash session close to
  the next cash session open captures the bulk of the instrument's
  directional drift - the documented academic "overnight return
  anomaly" (overnight returns dominate cumulative index gains;
  intraday returns are flat to negative in aggregate).

  HYPOTHESIS DOW (day-of-week): average daily returns differ
  systematically by day of week - historically a "Monday effect" of
  lower/negative Monday returns and stronger returns later in the
  week. Recent literature reports the effect weakening or having
  disappeared in many developed markets since the 2000s; this
  hypothesis is explicitly expected to be a LIKELY NULL and is tested
  honestly as such. ONLY the classic Monday claim is gated (section
  6.2); no other weekday rule may be adopted from this study.

Distinct from every prior hypothesis, explicitly:

  - Not session transfer (hypothesis #11): that mechanism required
    EXTREME, percentile-conditioned pre-open/post-close moves (causal
    trailing-252d P80 thresholds) to predict reversal or continuation.
    OVERNIGHT here is UNCONDITIONAL: always long every overnight
    regardless of the size of any prior move. No percentile filters,
    no conditioning variables.
  - Not multi-horizon momentum (hypothesis #5): that tested
    trend-following EMA signals with weekly/monthly rebalancing over
    multi-day/week horizons. Both hypotheses here are
    calendar/session-structure effects with NO trend or momentum
    signal of any kind.
  - Not regime rotation (hypothesis #13) and not the meta-coordinator
    (hypothesis #12): no child strategies, no gating rules, no
    ensemble machinery, no D1 regime labels. Both hypotheses are
    single, static, unconditional rules over the whole tape.
  - Not ORB/VWAP MR/SP2L/trend-pullback: no intraday structure is
    consulted for any signal. The only inputs are session open and
    close prices and the NY calendar.

2. DATA AND TIME DEFINITIONS

Instrument: US500 index CFD (US500-tick / US500.c mapping, unchanged
from all prior work). No new data is collected: both hypotheses use
only the verified session-day universe and the verified D1 data from
prior work.

Primary data: verified M5 export
data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv (364,330 bars,
2021-01..2026-04, byte-exact vs terminal in prior studies). Cash open
and cash close prices come from this file. Verified D1 cross-checks:
data/results/momentum_multihorizon/us500c_d1.csv (2014-06..2026-08)
and data/results/momentum_multihorizon/us500tick_d1.csv (the verified
tick-derived D1 used in daily-close work).

Timezone: epoch UTC converted to America/New_York (wall minutes =
hour*60 + minute of the bar OPEN). Same convention as ORB/VWAP/SP2L.

Trading calendar: a date is a CASH DAY if a bar with wall 570 (09:30)
exists on that NY date. Verified baseline: 1,370 cash dates
2021-01..2026-04 (session-transfer reproduction, re-verified below).
Holidays and evening-only dates are excluded automatically (no 09:30
bar). No other calendar filter exists.

Cash session: [09:30, 16:00) NY, verbatim from ORB/VWAP/session-
transfer work. Cash open = first bar OPEN at 09:30 (wall 570). Cash
close = last bar CLOSE before 16:00 (the 15:55 bar close).

Day universes: descriptive statistics use all 1,370 cash dates; every
P&L/Sharpe series is zero-filled on the common 1,369-day ORB
session-day universe (portfolio-study convention). The difference is
tallied in the funnel, never silently dropped.

Day eligibility: an overnight span from cash day d to the next valid
cash day d+1 exists only when both days have a cash open and a cash
close; weekend/holiday gaps are INCLUDED as a single overnight span
per the existing conventions (session transfer treats cross-day
signals the same way). The last cash day of the tape has no span and
is excluded by construction (tallied).

PHASE 1 PRE-GATES (PG1-PG3, reproduction sanity; failure = abort with
a data problem, not a hypothesis result):
  PG1  1,370 cash dates reproduced exactly.
  PG2  the momentum-study swap anchor reproduced exactly: US500-tick
       D1 buy-and-hold swap = -$55,360.00 (-20 x (1,656 + 4 x 278
       Wednesdays)).
  PG3  full-tape close-to-close buy-and-hold from cash closes
       reproduces the documented D1 buy-and-hold figure within the
       documented first-bar residual (34.66%..34.69% band).

3. CORE DAILY VARIABLES

  r_ovn(d)    = ln(cash_open(d+1) / cash_close(d))   signed log
                overnight return, every valid consecutive cash-day
                pair (d+1 = the next valid cash day; weekend/holiday
                gaps included as one span).
  r_intra(d)  = ln(cash_close(d) / cash_open(d))     comparison
                benchmark ONLY, never traded.
  r_cc(d)     = ln(cash_close(d) / cash_close(d-1))  close-to-close
                log return with d-1 = the PREVIOUS valid cash day.
                This is the DOW return definition: close-to-close,
                the classic day-of-week literature convention, used
                consistently throughout.
  weekday(d)  = NY calendar day of week of date d (Mon..Fri; US500
                has no weekend cash session).

All percent figures in CSVs are 100*x for log returns (documented
form in the headers). No other variable is computed for any signal.
No thresholds, no lookback windows, no percentile rules exist in this
SPEC by design - the mechanisms are unconditional.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HYPOTHESES (signal definitions, both pre-declared)

HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT - UNCONDITIONAL OVERNIGHT PREMIUM
  Signal: unconditional. On every valid cash day d, hold 1 lot long
  from cash close(d) to cash open(d+1). No entry filter, no direction
  filter - always long, every single overnight.
  Phase 1 is already a defined round trip; no exits are needed (the
  position is mechanically closed at the next cash open).
  Claim tested: overnight returns carry the directional drift on THIS
  instrument and tape, and survive the documented financing cost.

HYPOTHESIS DOW - DAY-OF-WEEK SEASONALITY (CLASSIC MONDAY CLAIM ONLY)
  Signal: unconditional, calendar-only. For each weekday (Mon..Fri),
  compute the mean and distribution of r_cc(d) across the full tape.
  Pre-declared claim: ONLY the classic Monday effect is gated -
  mean(r_cc | Monday) < pooled mean(r_cc | Tue..Fri), i.e. "Monday
  underperforms the rest of the week". Any other weekday-specific
  rule discovered after seeing the data is exploratory and cannot be
  adopted (section 8).

The two hypotheses are independent tests; each is gated separately,
and each can fail or pass without affecting the other.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. COSTS AND FINANCING (both conventions, never cherry-picked)

Costs: 0.8 pt round-trip spread, $1/pt/lot, $10,000 base, 1 lot, no
compounding - the project cost model. Each overnight round trip costs
0.8 pt. The DOW tradeable version costs one 0.8 pt round trip per
contiguous held block (section 6.2), charged on the block's entry
day.

Financing: the SAME swap treatment used in the multi-horizon momentum
study, BOTH readings reported everywhere, never cherry-picked:
  CONVENTION A (documented): swap_long -20 $/day/lot, swap_short
       +2 $/day/lot, Wednesday x5 (rollover3days=5 read as
       Wednesday-swap x5 - the reading behind all prior financing
       figures). Charged on the close-of-day position for the coming
       night: a position held over a Wednesday night is charged 5
       days; every other night 1 day.
  CONVENTION B (strict MT5 points): US500.c point = 0.01, so the
       strict reading charges -20 x 0.01 = -$0.20/day/lot (100x
       smaller), same Wednesday-x5 structure.
Weekend spans (Friday close -> next Monday open) count as held nights
with NO separate weekend multiplier - the weekend financing lives
entirely in the Wednesday-night x5, exactly as in the momentum
study's B&H accounting (PG2 reproduces that anchor). Every reported
statistic shows ex-financing, Convention A net, and Convention B net.

Only long positions occur in this SPEC; the swap_short rate never
applies but is stated for completeness.

6. PHASE 1 - DESCRIPTIVE QUALITY (no tuning, no exits)

6.1 HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT

Procedure:
  - Compute r_ovn(d) for the full tape; tally the funnel (valid
    pairs; excluded boundary days are never silently dropped).
  - Summary statistics: n, mean, median, std, Sharpe = mean/std
    (ddof=1) x sqrt(252) (252 = overnight periods per year), win
    rate, skew - gross, net of 0.8 pt round trip, and net of
    financing under BOTH conventions.
  - The SAME statistics for r_intra(d) as the comparison benchmark,
    to confirm or refute the "overnight carries the drift, intraday
    is flat/negative" claim on THIS specific instrument/tape.
  - Year-by-year overnight Sharpe and net PnL: gross (before costs),
    net of costs, and net of financing under both conventions;
    2021-2025 full years, 2026 partial reported only.
  - Bootstrap: day-block bootstrap, 10,000 resamples of days (each
    overnight return is a one-day block), of the mean overnight
    return; a 10-day moving-block bootstrap is reported alongside as
    robustness (non-gating).

PHASE 1 GATES - OVERNIGHT (O1-O4; ALL must hold to proceed):

  O1  Gross mean(r_ovn) > 0 AND day-block bootstrap (10,000
      resamples) P(mean <= 0) <= 0.10. (Tighter than prior 0.40
      bars: a much higher-frequency, unconditional claim with much
      larger n.)
  O2  Net-of-financing Sharpe > 0 under BOTH financing conventions
      (A documented AND B strict-points). Surviving under only one
      convention is a FAIL, not a pass - no convention-shopping.
  O3  Year shape under EACH convention separately: at least 4 of 5
      full years (2021-2025) net-of-financing positive AND no single
      year > 60% of total net. 2026 partial: reported only.
  O4  Net-of-financing Sharpe > every individually validated
      single-instrument strategy already in the project, i.e. >
      0.337 (ORB, the current best; SP2L is 0.275 on the common
      universe), under EACH financing convention separately. Failure
      of O4 = the hypothesis is reported as a NULL RESULT: being
      merely positive does not justify adoption.

PASS = O1-O4 -> Phase 2 (section 7). Any failure -> null write-up for
HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT in REPORT_CALENDAR_EFFECTS and STOP for that
hypothesis: no Phase 2, no EA. (HYPOTHESIS DOW is gated
independently and is unaffected.)

6.2 HYPOTHESIS DOW

Procedure:
  - Compute r_cc(d) for the full tape, tagged by weekday(d).
  - Per weekday: n, mean, median, std, Sharpe = mean/std (ddof=1) x
    sqrt(252), win rate.
  - Rank weekdays by mean return; report day-block bootstraps
    (10,000 resamples) per weekday: P(mean <= 0) for the best
    weekday and P(mean >= 0) for the worst weekday (descriptive,
    NOT gates). Welch t-test reported alongside as descriptive.
  - Test the classic claim: delta = mean(r_cc | Monday) - pooled
    mean(r_cc | Tue..Fri). Report whether the classic pattern HOLDS
    (delta < 0, P(delta >= 0) <= 0.10), is REVERSED (delta > 0,
    P(delta <= 0) <= 0.10), or is ABSENT (neither), on the
    day-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples preserving weekday
    labels). Do NOT search for "whichever weekday looks best": only
    the Monday claim is gated.
  - Robustness (non-gating): the D1-derived close-to-close series
    from data/results/momentum_multihorizon/us500tick_d1.csv is
    computed and reported alongside; the cash-close series remains
    authoritative.

PHASE 1 GATES - DOW (D1-D3; ALL must hold):

  D1  The pre-declared classic effect holds: mean(r_cc | Monday) <
      pooled mean(r_cc | Tue..Fri), with day-block bootstrap (10,000
      resamples) P(no effect) <= 0.10 (i.e., P(delta >= 0) <= 0.10).
  D2  The pre-declared tradeable version - FLAT over the Monday-
      earning span, LONG the rest of the week: hold 1 lot from cash
      close(d) to cash close(d+1) for every valid cash day d with
      weekday(d+1) in {Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri}; flat otherwise (the only
      skipped spans end on Monday - the r_cc(Monday) spans, weekend
      gaps included; positions close at the last valid cash close of
      the week or the tape end, whichever is first). One 0.8 pt
      round trip per contiguous held block. Net Sharpe (costs +
      financing) must EXCEED the net Sharpe of the unconditional
      buy-and-hold-equivalent baseline (1 lot always long over every
      night between consecutive valid cash days, no turnover costs,
      same conventions) on the same tape, under BOTH financing
      conventions. Both series zero-filled on the common 1,369-day
      universe, sqrt(252).
  D3  The effect is present in at least 4 of 5 full years
      (2021-2025), not concentrated in 1-2 years: per-year delta < 0
      in >= 4 of 5 full years AND the tradeable version's yearly net
      is positive in >= 4 of 5 full years AND no single year > 60%
      of its total net (the project year-shape clause; this project
      has repeatedly found "one good year" false positives). 2026
      partial: reported only.

If D1 fails (which the literature suggests is likely - the effect has
been reported fading/vanishing in recent decades), STOP IMMEDIATELY:
HYPOTHESIS DOW is written up in REPORT_CALENDAR_EFFECTS as a clearly
labeled null result, and D2/D3 are NOT run. No tradeable design is
computed beyond what D1 needs, and no weekday-specific rule
discovered after seeing the data may be adopted.

Pass for DOW = D1-D3. On DOW pass, any Phase-2 execution design is
OUT OF SCOPE of this SPEC and requires its own pre-declared SPEC
amendment (section 9), exactly like any new hypothesis.

7. PHASE 2 - OVERNIGHT EXECUTION (only if O1-O4 pass)

Execution design (pre-declared parameters, no search):
  Entry: market order at or just before the cash close (the 15:55 M5
  bar close), 1 lot long.
  Exit: market order at or just after the next cash open (the 09:30
  M5 bar open), the overnight window end.
  Implementation form: a scheduled script OR a simple EA on an M5
  chart; the choice is made BEFORE any Phase-2 code with cause
  recorded (the regime-rotation embed-vs-toggle rule). If an EA, the
  project order conventions apply verbatim: NormPrice tick-grid
  rounding on every order and close, DST-aware session code, a magic
  number reserved for this study.
  Realistic execution costs: total round trip = 1.2 pt (0.8 pt
  spread + 0.2 pt per side slippage), replacing the Phase-1 0.8 pt
  cost in all Phase-2 recomputations.
  Gap-risk gate (pre-declared): X = 3.0%. No single overnight loss
  (cash close(d) to cash open(d+1), gap included) may exceed 3.0% of
  the $10,000 account = $300 = 300 points at 1 lot. ONE violation =
  Phase-2 FAIL regardless of every other metric (tail gap risk is
  not captured by daily Sharpe alone).
  Phase-2 gates: O1-O4 recomputed net of realistic execution costs
  and with actual gap outcomes (O1's gross mean is cost-invariant
  but re-verified on the Phase-2 tape), plus the reconciliation
  gates below.

PHASE 2 GATES (G1-G2, project standard):
  G1  Consistency: Phase-2 execution Sharpe within +/- 0.15
      (absolute) and max drawdown within +/- 20% (relative) of the
      offline Phase-2 reference numbers over the same window
      (Model=0 yearly batches, 2021-01..2026-04).
  G2  Execution integrity: >= 95% of the expected overnight schedule
      present (one round trip per valid overnight span), zero
      off-schedule trades, every trade attributed, every mismatch
      classified.

G-failure rejects the Phase-2 implementation; the Phase-1 findings
stand as reported.

8. HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK RULES

  - TWO hypotheses are tested in this SPEC, not one. Every bootstrap
    p-value is reported raw AND Bonferroni-adjusted for 2 tests
    (adjusted p = min(1, 2 x raw)). Gates are evaluated on the raw
    p-values as written above; a hypothesis that passes a gate raw
    but fails adjusted is explicitly labeled in the report as
    fragile under the multiple-testing adjustment. The adjustment is
    reported and flagged, never used to relax or tighten a gate post
    hoc.
  - Swap/financing conventions are applied exactly as previously
    documented (section 5), both readings reported, never
    cherry-picked. No convention-specific rescue exists.
  - Both hypotheses are unconditional static rules - no fitting, no
    optimization, no percentile thresholds, no lookback windows to
    tune. No filter or conditioning variable may be added after
    Phase-1 output; any such addition is exploratory and cannot
    rescue a failed gate.
  - ONLY the classic Monday claim is gated for DOW; no other
    weekday-specific rule may be adopted from this study. No
    combination of OVERNIGHT and DOW (e.g., "hold overnight only on
    Tuesdays") may be tested under this SPEC - that would be a new,
    third hypothesis requiring its own pre-declared SPEC amendment.
  - A null result for either or both hypotheses is a complete,
    expected, and valuable deliverable. The literature itself flags
    DOW as likely fading and OVERNIGHT as cost-fragile for
    individual instruments; finding either negative on US500 CFD is
    informative, not a failure of the research process.
  - Scope statement (one instrument, one tape, 5.3 years, session
    definitions as specified) appears in every report.
