REPORT_CALENDAR_EFFECTS
Phase 1 verdict for hypothesis #14 calendar effects
(HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT and HYPOTHESIS DOW)

Status: written 2026-08-30 after the first Phase-1 run, per
SPEC_CALENDAR_EFFECTS.txt and GATE_CALENDAR_EFFECTS.txt (PG1-PG3,
O1-O4, D1-D3 pre-declared). BOTH HYPOTHESES FAIL PHASE 1 and are
reported as NULL RESULTS. No Phase 2, no EA, no adoption. Per the
SPEC, a null result for either or both is a complete, expected, and
valuable deliverable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCOPE

One instrument (US500 index CFD, US500-tick / US500.c mapping), one
verified tape (2021-01..2026-04: 1,656 M5 dates, 1,370 cash dates,
1,369 overnight spans / close-to-close days), cash session
[09:30, 16:00) NY verbatim from ORB/VWAP work, $10,000 base, 1 lot,
no compounding, 0.8 pt round-trip spread, $1/pt/lot. Financing under
BOTH project conventions: A documented swap_long -20 $/day/lot
Wednesday x5, B strict MT5 points -0.20 $/day/lot Wednesday x5.
Both hypotheses are unconditional static rules - no fitting, no
thresholds, no lookback windows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA REPRODUCTION (PG1-PG3) - ALL PASS

  PG1  1,370 cash dates reproduced exactly.
  PG2  swap anchor reproduced exactly: 1,656 dates, 278 Wednesdays,
       -$55,360.00 = -20 x (1,656 + 4 x 278).
  PG3  buy-and-hold reproduction: D1 close-to-close +34.6852% and
       first-open anchor +34.6601% (the documented +34.66) both
       inside the 34.66..34.69 band. Third reading (M5 first cash
       close 15:55 -> last close) +35.1138% is outside the band and
       is classified, not used: it drops the pre-first-cash-day D1
       bar (weekend Globex) - the documented first-bar open-vs-close
       convention residual.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT - NULL RESULT (O1-O4 FAIL)

3.1 Descriptive (n = 1,369 spans, always long cash close -> next
    cash open):

  overnight: mean +1.084 pts (+0.017%/span), median +2.491, std
  31.064, Sharpe 0.554, win rate 55.15%, skew -0.343.
  intraday benchmark (same days, not traded): mean +1.403 pts,
  median +2.891, std 41.659, Sharpe 0.535, win rate 54.35%, skew
  +0.663.

  The anomaly's premise is REFUTED on this instrument/tape before
  any gate is consulted: intraday returns are NOT flat/negative in
  aggregate (mean and Sharpe are comparable to overnight, mean
  slightly larger), and the overnight premium is a modest, positively
  skewed-but-left-tailed leg (negative skew: occasional gap losses).

  Gross total +$1,484; net of 0.8 pt round trip +$389 (Sharpe
  0.145). Financing totals: Convention A -$49,460, Convention B
  -$494.60. Net totals: A -$49,071 (Sharpe -12.74), B -$106
  (Sharpe -0.04).

  Yearly gross (full years): 2021 +559.65 (Sh 1.73), 2022 -740.31
  (-1.33), 2023 +56.15 (0.18), 2024 +1063.62 (2.31), 2025 +613.74
  (0.90); 2026 partial -68.61. Yearly net-B: +261.06 / -1039.91 /
  -241.45 / +764.82 / +315.13 (3 of 5 positive); net-A negative in
  every full year (-8.4k..-10.3k).

3.2 Gates:

  O1 FAIL. Gross mean > 0 holds, but the day-block bootstrap
  P(mean <= 0) = 0.1552 > 0.10 (10-day moving-block 0.1465;
  Bonferroni-adjusted 0.3104). The overnight premium on this tape is
  not significant at the pre-declared bar.
  O2 FAIL. Net-of-financing Sharpe must be > 0 under BOTH
  conventions: A -12.74, B -0.04. Under the documented convention
  the financing cost (14x the gross edge) destroys the premium;
  under strict-points the strategy is break-even-negative. No
  convention-shopping: one positive reading would not have saved it.
  O3 FAIL. Convention A: 0 of 5 full years positive. Convention B:
  3 of 5 positive but total net negative (share clause degenerate).
  O4 FAIL. Net Sharpe must exceed 0.337 (ORB) under both
  conventions: A -12.74, B -0.04. Being positive was never enough.

  Verdict per GATE: HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT is a NULL RESULT. STOP: no
  Phase 2, no EA, no gap-risk-gate evaluation. Consistent with the
  literature's "cost-fragile overnight premium" expectation - here
  it is not even significant gross, and the documented financing
  convention alone is decisive.

4. HYPOTHESIS DOW - NULL RESULT (D1 FAILS -> STOP)

4.1 Descriptive (close-to-close r_cc, n = 1,369 days):

  Mon +0.1215% (n=273, median +0.1797, Sharpe 2.16, win 61.9%),
  Tue +0.0029% (n=276), Wed +0.0871% (n=276), Thu -0.0156% (n=275),
  Fri +0.0481% (n=269).

  Ranked by mean: Mon > Wed > Fri > Tue > Thu. Monday is the BEST
  weekday and is positive - the classic "Monday effect" (Monday
  lowest/negative) is REVERSED on this tape: delta = Monday mean
  minus pooled Tue-Fri mean = +0.0910pp; bootstrap P(delta >= 0) =
  0.9266; the reversal one-sided bootstrap P(delta <= 0) = 0.0734
  <= 0.10 (distinguishable at the pre-declared 0.10 bar).
  Descriptive per-weekday: Monday P(mean <= 0) = 0.0142 - the best
  weekday is also the only one distinguishable from zero. These are
  descriptive outputs; per the SPEC, only the classic claim is
  gated and NO weekday-specific rule discovered after seeing the
  data may be adopted.

4.2 Gates:

  D1 FAIL. mean(r_cc | Monday) < pooled mean(r_cc | Tue..Fri)
  required; observed +0.0910pp (P(no effect) = 0.9266 >> 0.10).
  STOP RULE TRIGGERED: HYPOTHESIS DOW is written up as a null
  result immediately. D2 and D3 were NOT RUN (per GATE: no tradeable
  design is computed beyond what D1 needs).
  The reversal (Monday outperformance) is a possible NEW hypothesis
  that would require its own pre-declared SPEC amendment; it cannot
  be adopted under this SPEC, and this report does not pre-commit
  to it.

5. HONESTY AND ANTI-LEAK NOTES

  - TWO hypotheses were tested in this SPEC. Every bootstrap
    p-value is reported raw and Bonferroni-adjusted for 2 tests
    (O1: 0.1552 -> 0.3104; D1: 0.9266 -> 1.0). No gate passed raw,
    so no fragility label is triggered, but the adjustment is
    reported as pre-declared.
  - Both financing conventions are reported everywhere; no
    convention-specific rescue exists and none was attempted.
  - No filter, threshold, or conditioning variable was added after
    Phase-1 output; no combination of OVERNIGHT and DOW was tested
    (a third hypothesis would require its own SPEC amendment).
  - Both null results are complete deliverables: the overnight
    premium is not significant and is financing-dominated on US500
    CFD; the Monday effect is absent/reversed in 2021-2026, matching
    the post-2000s fading documented in the literature.
