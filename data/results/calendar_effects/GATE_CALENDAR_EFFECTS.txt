GATE_CALENDAR_EFFECTS
Calendar-effects gates for hypothesis #14 (OVERNIGHT and DOW)

Status: written 2026‑08‑30 before any Phase‑1 script. This file is a verbatim extraction of every gate number in SPEC_CALENDAR_EFFECTS sections 2, 6, and 7. Nothing here may be relaxed after Phase‑1 or Phase‑2 output exists. Any new gate is exploratory and labeled.

    Pre‑gates (data reproduction sanity — failure means a data problem, not a hypothesis result)

PG1. Cash‑day count
1,370 cash dates (2021‑01..2026‑04) must reproduce exactly from the verified M5 export.

PG2. Swap anchor reproduction
US500‑tick D1 buy‑and‑hold swap must reproduce exactly: −$55,360.00 (= −20 × (1,656 + 4 × 278 Wednesdays)).

PG3. Buy‑and‑hold reproduction
Full‑tape close‑to‑close buy‑and‑hold from cash closes must reproduce the documented D1 buy‑and‑hold figure within the documented residual band (34.66%..34.69%).

Failure of PG1, PG2, or PG3 aborts the study as a data‑integrity issue; no Phase‑1 hypothesis result may be reported until all three pass.

    Phase 1 gates — HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT (O1–O4; all must hold)

O1. Gross drift and significance
Gross mean(r_ovn) > 0
AND day‑block bootstrap (10,000 resamples) P(mean ≤ 0) ≤ 0.10.

O2. Financing robustness
Net‑of‑financing Sharpe > 0 under BOTH financing conventions (A documented AND B strict‑points).
Passing under only one convention counts as FAIL — no convention‑shopping.

O3. Year shape
Under EACH financing convention separately:
At least 4 of 5 full years (2021–2025) net‑of‑financing positive
AND no single year contributes more than 60 percent of total net.
2026 partial is reported only, not counted.

O4. Dominance over existing strategies
Net‑of‑financing Sharpe > 0.337 (the current best validated single‑instrument strategy, ORB) under EACH financing convention separately.
Failure of O4 means the hypothesis is reported as a NULL RESULT even if otherwise positive — being merely positive does not justify adoption.

Pass condition: O1–O4 all hold → proceed to Phase 2 (section 4 below).
Any failure → null write‑up for HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT in REPORT_CALENDAR_EFFECTS; STOP for this hypothesis only (no Phase 2, no EA). HYPOTHESIS DOW is unaffected and gated independently.

    Phase 1 gates — HYPOTHESIS DOW (D1–D3; all must hold)

D1. Classic Monday effect
mean(r_cc | Monday) < pooled mean(r_cc | Tue..Fri)
AND day‑block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, weekday labels preserved) P(delta ≥ 0) ≤ 0.10.
STOP RULE: if D1 fails, HYPOTHESIS DOW is written up immediately as a null result in REPORT_CALENDAR_EFFECTS. D2 and D3 are NOT run. No weekday‑specific rule discovered after seeing the data may be adopted.

D2. Tradeable version beats buy‑and‑hold
The pre‑declared rule (flat over Monday‑ending spans, long the rest of the week) must have net Sharpe (costs + financing) that EXCEEDS the net Sharpe of the unconditional buy‑and‑hold‑equivalent baseline, under BOTH financing conventions, on the same zero‑filled common‑universe series (sqrt(252) annualization).

D3. Year consistency
Per‑year delta < 0 in at least 4 of 5 full years (2021–2025)
AND the tradeable version's yearly net is positive in at least 4 of 5 full years
AND no single year contributes more than 60 percent of its total net.
2026 partial is reported only, not counted.

Pass condition: D1–D3 all hold. On DOW pass, any Phase‑2 execution design is OUT OF SCOPE of this SPEC and requires its own pre‑declared SPEC amendment — it may not be built under this SPEC.

    Phase 2 gates — OVERNIGHT execution (only if O1–O4 all pass)

Gap‑risk gate (pre‑declared, checked first, hard stop):
X = 3.0 percent. No single overnight loss (cash close(d) to cash open(d+1), including any gap) may exceed 3.0 percent of the $10,000 account (= $300 = 300 points at 1 lot).
ONE violation = Phase‑2 FAIL regardless of every other metric.

G1. Consistency
Phase‑2 execution Sharpe within ±0.15 (absolute) of the offline Phase‑2 reference Sharpe
AND Phase‑2 max drawdown within ±20 percent (relative) of the offline Phase‑2 reference max drawdown
over the same window (Model=0 yearly batches, 2021‑01..2026‑04).

G2. Execution integrity
At least 95 percent of the expected overnight schedule present (one round trip per valid overnight span)
AND zero off‑schedule trades
AND every trade attributed
AND every mismatch classified.

Pass condition: the gap‑risk gate AND G1 AND G2 must all hold. Failure of any one rejects the Phase‑2 implementation; the Phase‑1 findings stand as reported, unchanged.

    Multiple‑testing and honesty rules

    Two hypotheses (OVERNIGHT and DOW) are tested in this SPEC. Every bootstrap p‑value must be reported both raw and Bonferroni‑adjusted for 2 tests (adjusted p = min(1, 2 × raw)).

    Gates above are evaluated on the RAW p‑values as written. A hypothesis that passes a gate on raw p but fails on the Bonferroni‑adjusted p must be explicitly labeled FRAGILE UNDER MULTIPLE‑TESTING ADJUSTMENT in the report. The adjustment is reported and flagged, never used to relax or tighten a gate after the fact.

    No convention‑specific rescue is allowed for financing (section 5 of SPEC).

    No filter, threshold, or conditioning variable may be added to either hypothesis after Phase‑1 output exists; any such addition is exploratory and cannot rescue a failed gate.

    No combination of OVERNIGHT and DOW may be tested under this SPEC.

    A null result for either or both hypotheses is a complete, expected, and valuable deliverable.

    Stop rules summary

    PG1/PG2/PG3 fail → abort as data problem; fix and rerun before any hypothesis result is reported.

    O1–O4 fail (any one) → HYPOTHESIS OVERNIGHT null result; no Phase 2 for OVERNIGHT.

    D1 fails → HYPOTHESIS DOW null result immediately; D2/D3 not run.

    D1–D3 all pass → DOW Phase‑2 requires a new, separate pre‑declared SPEC amendment; nothing may be built under this SPEC.

    Gap‑risk gate, G1, or G2 fail in Phase 2 → OVERNIGHT Phase‑2 implementation rejected; Phase‑1 OVERNIGHT findings stand as the final result.
