HYPOTHESIS 67: EURUSD POST-LONDON-FIX DOLLAR REVERSAL
Declared 2026-09-25 before this study's code or Phase-1 run.

1. EXTERNAL MECHANISM AND RULE
Krohn et al., Foreign Exchange Fixings and Returns around the Clock,
Journal of Finance 2024, https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13306,
report that the U.S. dollar tends to weaken after the 16:00 London
FX fix in their 1999-2019 sample; the published post-fix window is
16:00 London to 17:00 New York. This test takes the fixed direction
(BUY EURUSD) after the five-minute fix window and closes one hour
before the New York FX rollover to avoid its spread and financing.
This is a new external mechanism, not a retuning of prior event or
US500 calendar failures. The literature result does not establish
profitability on this broker CFD after current quote costs.

2. DATA AND CALENDAR

askPrice, bidPrice, and source sha256

Construct every Monday-Friday Europe/London calendar date from
2020-01-02 through 2026-09-18 inclusive, independently of prices.
Entry target = date at 16:05:00 Europe/London; exit target = the
corresponding same London calendar date at 16:00:00 America/New_York.
Convert both with IANA time zones; do not assume a fixed UTC offset.
This yields a same-day roughly four- to five-hour hold and avoids
17:00 New York rollover even in London/NY DST mismatch weeks.

3. EXECUTION PROXY AND COST
For each target take the first valid quote at or after target and
within 10 seconds inclusive. A valid row has finite positive Ask
and Bid with Ask >= Bid. Exclude and count invalid rows in sampled
windows; skip and log dates missing either quote. No fallback window,
stops, targets, news exclusions, or day/month/volatility filters.
Enter long at Ask and exit at Bid; quote-net pips =
(exitBid-entryAsk)/0.00010. Midpoint gross pips =
(exitMid-entryMid)/0.00010. Actual quote spread is already charged.
Extra-cost stress net pips = quote-net pips - 0.3, a separate
latency/slippage buffer equal to the user's EURUSD spread floor.
First-quote fills still omit rejection and market impact; CFD quote
history is an execution proxy, not proof of fillability.

4. PARTITION, INFERENCE, AND REPORTING
2020-2023 are descriptive only; 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-18 is
the untouched rule's gated chronological block. The rule comes
from published 1999-2019 research; no parameters are selected on
this broker tape. Report per-year trade count, midpoint and quote-
net and stress means/totals, win rate, spread distribution, hold
duration, maximum drawdown, and missing-quote funnel. Keep 2026
partial separate from complete 2024 and 2025. Bootstrap gated
London dates with replacement 10000 times, seed 20261003, and
report P(mean quote-net <= 0) and descriptive 95% quantiles.
One trade per date means date and trade bootstrap units coincide.
2024-2026 dates were already accessible to this research project;
call the test a predeclared historical replication, not virgin OOS.

5. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-LFD1: At least 150 executed EURUSD trades on distinct 2024-2026 London dates.
G-LFD2: Strictly positive mean extra-cost stress net pips per executed trade in the gated block.
G-LFD3: Date-bootstrap P(mean executable quote net <= 0) at most 0.05, 10000 resamples, seed 20261003.
G-LFD4: Strictly positive executable quote-net total pips in each complete gated year 2024 and 2025.
All four are required for Phase-1 PASS. Otherwise FAIL is final
for this artifact. Do not rescue failure by reversing direction,
changing exit time, excluding dates, or changing cost assumptions.
