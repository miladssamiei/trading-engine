SECTION 0. SCOPE AND STATUS

0.1 This is a Phase-1 signal-quality measurement (MFE/MAE excursion study) of
a video-specified setup: liquidity-sweep candles at prior swing highs/lows on
the USA30 M15 tape. No trade simulation, no costs, no walk-forward, no Phase
2 authorization. Null results are complete deliverables.
0.2 downstream_model_allowed=false, live_execution_allowed=false regardless
of verdict. USA30 carries the 2.28 pt verified RT spread; any future
execution study must clear it - this screen measures raw excursions only.

SECTION 1. DATA SOURCING AND PREPARATION

1.1 Tape: data/results/usa30_onboarding/usa30tick_m5.csv (verified Dukascopy
USA30 M5, epoch-UTC). Window: STRICTLY 2025-07-01 00:00 UTC .. 2026-09-01
00:00 UTC (the 100%-covered onboarding-audit window). The runner verifies
first/last bar inside the window and reports bar counts.
1.2 Aggregation M5 -> M15: epoch floor to 900 s buckets; open = first,
high = max, low = min, close = last of the bucket's M5 bars. Buckets with no
M5 bars do not exist (no gap filling); buckets with 1-2 bars are kept and
their count distribution is reported. Bars sorted, duplicate epochs dropped.
1.3 Session (video: 12:00-19:45 Iran Time; Iran is permanently UTC+3:30):
setup candles are evaluated only if the M15 bar OPEN time is within
[08:30, 16:15) UTC. Swing tracking and ATR run on the FULL continuous M15
tape (all hours).
1.4 ATR: 14-period Wilder ATR on the M15 tape: TR[0] = high-low;
ATR[13] = mean(TR[0..13]); ATR[i] = (13*ATR[i-1] + TR[i]) / 14. The signal
bar t uses ATR through bar t inclusive (normalization only).

SECTION 2. STATE MACHINE - SWING HIGHS AND LOWS

2.1 Definitions (strict inequalities; a doji Close == Open is NEITHER green
nor red and breaks both patterns): bar is green if Close > Open, red if
Close < Open.
2.2 A Swing High forms at bar i when bar i-1 is green and bar i is red;
level = max(High[i-1], High[i]). A Swing Low forms at bar i when bar i-1 is
red and bar i is green; level = min(Low[i-1], Low[i]).
2.3 Invalidation (breakout): a Swing High is invalid once any subsequent
bar CLOSES strictly above its level; a Swing Low once any bar CLOSES
strictly below its level. Invalidation is checked from bar i+1 onward
(bar i's own close cannot exceed its level by construction).
2.4 Tracking and ORDER OF OPERATIONS at each bar i: (a) FIRST evaluate the
trigger with the swing lists as valid at the close of bar i-1 (a level must
be intact when price returns to it - the LS candle's own close can break
the level AFTER the sweep is counted); (b) THEN update the lists with bar
i: append swings formed at i, remove swings invalidated by Close[i], trim
each side to the 3 MOST RECENTLY FORMED valid swings.
2.5 The swept swing must be strictly previously established: it is in the
bar-(i-1) list, i.e. formed at a bar < i. The LS candle itself is never its
own swept swing.

SECTION 3. THE LS SETUP CANDLE (TRIGGER)

At each session bar t compute Body = abs(Close-Open), UpperWick =
High - max(Close,Open), LowerWick = min(Close,Open) - Low.
3.1 Bearish (short signal): UpperWick > Body AND UpperWick > LowerWick AND
LowerWick <= 7*Body AND High[t] >= level of at least one valid Swing High
in the tracked 3-list.
3.2 Bullish (long signal): LowerWick > Body AND LowerWick > UpperWick AND
UpperWick <= 7*Body AND Low[t] <= level of at least one valid Swing Low in
the tracked 3-list.
3.3 The two shapes are mutually exclusive (wick dominance). One signal per
bar at most. Recorded per signal: swept level (most recently formed swept
swing), number of swept levels, direction, ATR14[t].

SECTION 4. EXCURSION WALK (MFE/MAE)

4.1 Entry price = exact Open of bar t+1.
4.2 Walk forward over M15 bars j from t+1 while OpenTime[j] < 20:00 UTC of
the signal's UTC date (16:00 NY-class end-of-day). The 19:45 bar (closing
20:00) is included; the 20:00 bar is not.
4.3 Long: MFE = max(High[j]) - entry; MAE = entry - min(Low[j]). Short:
MFE = entry - min(Low[j]); MAE = max(High[j]) - entry. All in raw points,
then normalized: MFE_atr = MFE / ATR14[t], MAE_atr = MAE / ATR14[t].
4.4 Signals whose entry bar opens at/after 20:00 UTC of the signal date, or
with no walkable bars (tape edge), are dropped from the analysis set and
counted in the funnel (they have no excursion by construction).

SECTION 5. GATES (verbatim in GATE_LS9_LIQUIDITY_HUNT.txt)

G-A1 SAMPLE SIZE: total excursion-valid signals >= 150.
G-A2 EXCURSION EDGE: for every threshold tau in {0.5, 0.75, 1.0} ATR and
for the aggregate set AND each direction separately:
mean(max(MFE_atr - tau, 0)) > mean(max(MAE_atr - tau, 0)).
(9 comparisons; all must hold.)
G-A3 DAY-BLOCK BOOTSTRAP: 10,000 resamples (numpy default_rng(20260921))
of UTC-date blocks with replacement over the aggregate signal set; the
fraction of resamples with mean(MFE_atr - MAE_atr) <= 0 must be <= 0.20.
OVERALL: PASS iff G-A1..G-A3 all PASS. PASS is a descriptive screen pass
ONLY (in-sample, single window, no costs, no walk-forward); FAIL is final
for this artifact.

SECTION 6. HONESTY RULES

6.1 MFE and MAE are NOT simultaneously achievable (no exit logic exists);
raw excursions overstate any tradable edge. USA30's 2.28 pt RT spread is
NOT subtracted anywhere in this screen.
6.2 All mechanical choices are declared: doji handling (2.1), trigger-then-
update order (2.4), 3-swing recency cap, strict inequalities, the [08:30,
16:15) UTC session, the 20:00 UTC walk bound, dropped no-walk signals (4.4).
6.3 Single window (2025-07..2026-08), in-sample, no multiple-testing
correction beyond the declared grid; no re-opening of closed families; no
live or downstream authorization.
