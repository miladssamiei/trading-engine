DAILY CONNORS RSI(2) INDICES AND FTMO 2-STEP SCENARIO - PHASE 1 SPEC
Frozen 2026-09-28 before evaluate/connors_rsi2_daily_mc.py exists or any results are run.

Purpose: Fixed daily RSI2 strategy screen on the specified index BID proxy tapes, followed by a conditional empirical Monte Carlo scenario. The historical indices have been studied previously in this project, so this is exploratory, not independent or MT5 validated. Null results are complete outcomes. A Monte Carlo positive EV is not a purchase recommendation.

SOURCE AVAILABILITY: Use US500 data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv and US100/USTECH data/results/virgin_assets/ustech_m1.csv. A verified native GER40.cash bid/ask tick-derived M1 source exists at data/results/ger40_native_source/ger40cash_native_tick_m1_2022_2026.csv, but it is broker native, not the requested Dukascopy source, and the request gives no GER40 cost or EUR-to-USD normalization. No verified GER40 Dukascopy M1/D1 tape was found. Therefore apply the owner's US500/US100 fallback; report GER40 as DATA UNAVAILABLE FOR REQUESTED SOURCE, not as a tested null. Never silently transfer a US index spread or USD point value to GER40. If the owner supplies a cost/source decision later, use a separately frozen study.

DATA AND D1 CLOCK: Ingest unique sorted finite well-formed BID M1 OHLC. USTECH tick_volume=0 is a vendor-onboarding schema placeholder, not a filter. Construct New York wall-clock sessions [previous day 17:00, current day 17:00), correctly handling DST by local wall-time date. Include only Monday-Friday session-ending dates with at least 300 recorded M1 bars; keep only actually observed sessions, never weekend or missing-day synthetic bars. D1 OHLC is first M1 open, max high, min low, last close. The session is considered completed at its 17:00 NY end even if its final quote was earlier. Indicators update only then. Preserve true M1 opening timestamps for fills. Entry at the first available observed M1 open at or after the signal session end, before the next valid D1 session end; report any delay. If no such bar, skip. Fixed round-trip ASK-BID floor is US500 0.55 points or US100 1.66 points, embedded once through long ASK entry and BID exit. Swap=0 in the stated swap-free/swing scenario; count NY-date overnight positions and disclose that actual account financing is unverified.

STRATEGY: Long only. D1 SMA200 and SMA5 use 200/5 observed completed trading sessions, respectively. Wilder RSI2 is seeded by mean gains/losses of the first two completed D1 changes; 0/0 RSI=50, gain-only=100, loss-only=0. Wilder ATR14 is seeded by the first 14 D1 true ranges. A completed D1 close>SMA200 plus RSI2<10 creates the primary signal. RSI2<5 is separately predeclared sensitivity. One open position per asset; signals whose scheduled entry falls on/before that asset's exit M1 are shadowed. The two assets may hold positions simultaneously, and portfolio concurrency is reported. Entry occurs only after the completed D1 signal; no same-close fill.

EXITS: At each subsequent completed observed D1 session, a close>SMA5 creates Exit A. Execute at the first available M1 BID open at/after that session end. Exit B also creates an exit after N=3,5,7 completed trading sessions since the signal session, whichever comes first. Exit C uses Exit B plus a fixed BID emergency stop at actual entry ASK minus 2.5*signal D1 ATR14, checked on every observed M1 bar after entry and through a scheduled exit bar. On a stop gap, exit at min(stop, current M1 BID open); stop takes precedence if its level and scheduled exit coincide in the same M1. If no scheduled exit quote exists by end of tape, mark OPEN_END and exclude from closed-trade gates. No slippage beyond floor. An emergency stop is not imposed on A or B. For every exit, reference R is initial 2.5*ATR14 in price units, including the entry spread in realized net PnL. Thus A/B can lose more than -1R and C can gap beyond -1R. Net R=net points/reference R. M1 BID high/low from entry through exit provide per-trade MAE/MFE; on a scheduled exit M1 only its open is included, while a stop M1 includes its observed high/low. This overstates path precision because M1 within-bar order is unknown; stop-first is conservative.

FIXED VARIANT GRID: For each asset run RSI<10 and RSI<5 under A, B3/B5/B7 and C3/C5/C7 (14 cells per asset, 28 total). Primary account scenario is predeclared as RSI<10, C5 on BOTH assets, chosen for the available catastrophic stop and five-session cap; do not substitute a better performing cell after results. Report all cells, even no-trade or negative cells. Store one complete ledger per cell, plus primary pooled portfolio concurrency.

TRADE STATISTICS: Closed count, net win fraction, net profit factor, gross and net expectancy in index points, mean net R, average observed-session holding days, MAE/MFE distributions (mean, median, p90 magnitude, all in R), unannualized per-trade net Sharpe=mean/std(ddof=1), and MBB P(mean net<=0) from 10000 resamples of five consecutive trades with replacement and seed 20261010. Fewer than five trades have undefined p; fewer than two or zero dispersion have undefined Sharpe. Annual entry-cohort count and net points for 2020-2026. Save source and script hashes.

MONTE CARLO POOL AND CLOCK: Use only primary C5 RSI<10 CLOSED trades whose entry lies in the common US500/US100 source overlap ending by the US500 tape end; pool trade-weighted, preserve asset identity, each trade's actual net R, daily open-equity path, and empirical inter-entry calendar-day gaps. Reference R is 2.5 D1 ATR14. For a sampled historical trade, build a path indexed by Europe/Prague calendar days: daily adverse BID-low mark and end-of-day BID-close mark in R, with final close mark replaced by actual net R; the day's adverse mark is at least as bad as its closing mark. Use up to the observed maximum path duration without truncation. In synthetic paths draw trade templates and inter-entry gaps independently with replacement; allow US500/US100 overlap but cap one open trade per asset and two new entries per day. Reject/shadow an entry that violates occupancy. At each simulated day, calculate the conservative combined worst open equity by adding both positions' daily adverse marks as though their worst minutes coincide; this is an upper bound on joint adverse movement, not true synchronized intraminute equity. Daily end realizes trades that end on that template day. Use $10k fixed initial balance and fixed $50/$100/$150/$200 cash per reference R; no compounding. This synthetic day sequence represents empirical spacing/duration, not actual future calendar dates or calendar news.

FTMO TWO-STEP MODEL: Exactly 10000 paths for EACH risk profile, seeded independently from 20261010. Phase 1 starts at $10000, passes on closed balance >=$11000 AND four distinct simulated opening days AND no open trades, fails if worst open equity <=$9000. Phase 2 resets to $10000, passes on closed balance >=$10500 with four opening days and no open trades, same static loss. At each simulated day start, the daily equity floor is the midnight closed balance minus $500; a day's conservative worst open equity <= that floor fails. This approximates FTMO's 00:00 CE(S)T balance reset and includes open P/L; it is not tick-exact. If both floors breach, record BOTH inclusively and a unique BOTH category; static and daily failure rates may overlap. Check loss before profit pass. Each phase stops after at most 3650 synthetic calendar days or 2000 closed trades; unresolved is a reported no-pass. Continue Phase 2 only after Phase 1 pass.

FUNDED / EV SCENARIO: After both phases pass, reset to $10000, run the same daily/static equity guards for a fixed first-payout window of 30 CLOSED sampled trades, and require positive closed profit and no open positions; wait until at least 14 calendar days from the first funded entry for reward eligibility. If account breaches either loss guard or profit<=0 at the window, first payout=0. Otherwise gross receipt=0.80*funded closed profit+$175 fee refund. The user-specified $175 is assumed purchase fee, not a verified current price. Net EV per purchased challenge=mean(gross receipt across all original 10000 paths)-$175. Report Phase 1 pass from purchase, Phase 2 conditional and unconditional, funded-status unconditional, first-payout probability, static-only/daily-only/both failure counts per phase, median/p90 calendar days and trade counts to funded status conditional on achieving it, receipt and EV. A phase that cannot resolve within the cap is not treated as a pass. No account action is authorized by the model.

RULE CONTEXT: Official FTMO 2-Step objectives checked 2026-09-28: 10%/5% targets, static 10% maximum loss, 5% initial-capital daily loss limit recalculated at midnight CE(S)T from current-day-start balance and applied to equity, four trading days per evaluation phase; first 2-Step reward can refund the fee, standard split 80%, reward claim at/after day 14. Sources: https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/ ; https://ftmo.com/en/faq/is-the-entry-fee-refunded/ ; https://ftmo.com/faq/how-do-i-withdraw-my-profits/ . This study's worst-day template, combined worst-minute assumption, time mapping, fixed cost, zero swaps and synthetic reward window are approximations, not FTMO certification.

GATES (verbatim copied to GATE_RSI2_DAILY.txt):
G1: Every asset/variant cell requires at least 150 closed trades for a standalone passing verdict.
G2: Every asset/variant cell requires positive net expectancy in points and positive mean net R after its retail cost floor.
G3: Every asset/variant cell requires unannualized per-trade net Sharpe > 0.
G4: Every asset/variant cell requires MBB P(mean net <= 0) <= 0.05 with 10000 resamples, block length 5, seed 20261010.
G5: Every asset/variant cell requires no negative net entry-cohort total for a full source calendar year (US500 2021-2025, US100 2020-2025); partial 2026 is disclosed, not gated.
G6: The primary two-asset strategy passes Phase 1 only if BOTH primary C5 RSI<10 asset cells pass G1-G5; the 10,000-path Monte Carlo is a diagnostic regardless of gate outcome and cannot overturn a failed historical gate.
G7: Monte Carlo validity requires 10000 original paths per risk, payout count <= funded count <= Phase 1 pass count, no payout on a failed or unresolved path, and EV=mean(gross receipt)-175 within numerical tolerance.
