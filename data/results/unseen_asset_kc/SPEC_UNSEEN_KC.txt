HYPOTHESIS #54 SPEC: UNSEEN-ASSET KELTNER CHANNEL BREAKOUT REPLICATION (4h execution / D1 17:00 ET anchor)
Study ID: unseen_asset_kc
Date frozen: 2026-09-24, before any evaluation code was written or run.
Status: frozen. Corrections or re-measurements require a NEW study name.
Lineage: #50 (cross_asset_kc_breakout) FAILED overall but passed on XAUUSD;
#51 (xauusd_kc_oos) delivered a bounded PASS whose disposition explicitly
named "a genuinely unseen-instrument/time OOS replication" as one of two
compliant follow-ups. This study IS that path, owner-tasked: the #51
mechanics are frozen verbatim and run on three instruments whose tapes
have never been touched by any study in this program - USTECH (Nasdaq-100
CFD-style index), BTCUSD, ETHUSD. No parameter is re-fitted; nothing about
these tapes informed #50/#51.

1. DATA
1.1 Tapes (standard schema time,open,high,low,close,tick_volume; time =
    epoch-UTC seconds; onboarded 2026-09-24 by
    evaluate/virgin_assets_onboarding.py from Dukascopy-style M1 BID bar
    exports in C:\Users\Milad\download; source sha256 pinned in
    data/results/virgin_assets/onboarding_stats.json; monotonicity and
    M1->M5 consistency HARD-ASSERTED there; 0 integrity drops on all
    three tapes):
    - USTECH: data/results/virgin_assets/ustech_m1.csv  (2,936,100 bars, points)
    - BTCUSD: data/results/virgin_assets/btc_m1.csv     (3,260,989 bars, price units)
    - ETHUSD: data/results/virgin_assets/eth_m1.csv     (3,375,260 bars, price units)
    All three span 2020-01-01 00:00 UTC .. 2026-09-23 23:59 UTC.
1.2 Timezone America/New_York (EST/EDT) for all wall-time logic.
1.3 COST FLOORS ARE OWNER-SUPPLIED ESTIMATES, NOT MEASUREMENTS (declared):
    the M1 BID exports carry no ask side, so no spread distribution
    exists. Round-trip floors: USTECH 1.0 points, BTCUSD 15.0 price
    units, ETHUSD 2.0 price units; subtracted once per trade from gross.
    Gate margins smaller than plausible floor error are flagged by also
    reporting gross-side statistics in the report.
1.4 tick_volume is 0 on all three tapes (vendor export carries no
    volume); it is a schema placeholder, never interpreted downstream.

2. MULTI-TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION (identical machinery to #50/#51)
2.1 1h base: M1 epochs floored to 3600s slots; open=first, high=max,
    low=min, close=last per slot; reindexed to the FULL continuous 3600s
    grid first..last slot; missing hours forward-filled OHLC = prior
    close, volume = 0 (synthetic flat bars). NY wall 1h boundaries align
    exactly with the epoch grid.
2.2 4h execution bars: NY WALL buckets [00:00-04:00), [04:00-08:00), ...
    built from the continuous 1h grid; DST-transition buckets span 23 or
    5 real hours; buckets contiguous. n4 = wall - (wall mod 4h).
2.3 D1 anchors: NY WALL buckets [17:00 -> next 17:00) from the continuous
    1h grid (dkey = wall - ((wall - 17h) mod 24h)); D1 close = last 1h
    close of the bucket; the D1 bar COMPLETES at its 17:00 ET boundary.
    For a 4h bar t the mapped D1 input is the last bucket whose key is
    <= (4h bucket key - ((key - 17h) mod 24h) - 24h) - strictly completed
    BEFORE the 4h bucket; never the bucket containing t.
2.4 Crypto tapes print weekend minutes, so BTCUSD/ETHUSD have few
    synthetic bars; USTECH closes Fri..Sun (327 weekend closures in the
    onboarding census) and its weekends are forward-filled flats - the
    #50/#51 declared flat-bar consequences apply unchanged there.

3. STATE MACHINE (frozen #50/#51 mechanics; all inputs strictly completed)
3.1 D1 anchor trend: EMA(50) on D1 closes (ewm span=50, adjust=False;
    first 49 values NaN). Bullish iff D1 Close > EMA(50); Bearish iff
    D1 Close < EMA(50); equality = neither.
3.2 4h execution channels: EMA(29) on 4h closes (first 28 NaN); Wilder
    ATR(29) on 4h H/L/C (seed = mean of first 29 TRs, RMA after);
    Upper = EMA29 + 2.77*ATR29; Lower = EMA29 - 2.77*ATR29.
3.3 Armed at 4h bar t iff prior-bar Close, EMA29, ATR29, Upper, Lower
    (all t-1) are non-NaN AND the mapped D1 bar exists with non-NaN
    EMA(50). Warm-up (first ~29 4h bars and first 49 D1 buckets,
    i.e. roughly Feb 2020) is absorbed inside the CAL block.

4. SIGNALS, EXECUTION AND EXIT
4.1 At the open of each 4h bar t while flat:
    LONG  iff Close(t-1) > Upper(t-1) AND mapped D1 is Bullish;
    SHORT iff Close(t-1) < Lower(t-1) AND mapped D1 is Bearish.
    Entry = Open(t), market. Max ONE open trade per instrument; trades
    occupy [entry_bar, exit_bar]; signals while open are counted
    (ignored-while-open) and not acted on.
4.2 Initial stop: SL = Entry - max(|Entry - EMA29(t-1)|, 1.5*ATR29(t-1))
    for LONG; Entry + same for SHORT.
4.3 EMA-riding trail: on every 4h bar j of the walk (entry bar included),
    AFTER that bar's exit checks, SL ratchets to EMA29(j) - LONG:
    SL = max(SL, EMA29(j)); SHORT: SL = min(SL, EMA29(j)); only tightens;
    NaN EMA29 skipped.
4.4 Exit walk (long case; short mirrored), checks in order per bar j:
    a) Open(j) <= SL -> exit at Open(j), SL_GAP (honest gap fill);
    b) Low(j) <= SL -> exit at SL, reason SL (worst-case same-bar
       default: stop checked before any ratchet);
    c) else apply 4.3 and continue.
    No take-profit. If nothing hits by the final 4h bar, exit at its
    close, reason FORCED_EOD, included in results.
4.5 MFE/MAE recorded over [entry..exit] in instrument units, descriptive
    only. R multiple = side*(Exit-Entry)/initial_risk_distance.

5. ECONOMICS
5.1 Gross = side*(Exit - Entry) (scale 1.0 for all three; units: USTECH
    points, BTCUSD/ETHUSD price units). Net = Gross - cost floor (1.0 /
    15.0 / 2.0), one round trip per trade.
5.2 Net Sharpe (per-trade convention): mean(Net)/std(Net, ddof=1)*sqrt(252)
    over executed trades of the instrument.

6. TEMPORAL BLOCKS (REPORTING ONLY - never gated)
6.1 One continuous walk per instrument over the full tape; each trade is
    assigned a block by its ENTRY bar NY wall date:
    CAL [2020-01-01, 2023-12-31] / OOS [2024-01-01, present].
6.2 The report shows both blocks (n, mean, Sharpe, sides, exits, yearly)
    and the CAL->OOS degradation direction, mirroring #51's regime-
    stability presentation. Gates are evaluated on the FULL tape only:
    the entire tape is unseen data for this mechanic, so the full tape IS
    the replication test; the split is diagnostic, not a gate.

7. PHASE 1 GATES (applied INDEPENDENTLY to each instrument, on the FULL
   TAPE; frozen verbatim in GATE_UNSEEN_KC.txt)
    G-REP1: Total executed trades >= 30.
    G-REP2: Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
    G-REP3: Moving-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=5 trades,
           seed 20261002) P(mean Net PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
    VERDICT RULE: an instrument PASSES only if G-REP1 AND G-REP2 AND
    G-REP3 all PASS. OVERALL PASS only if ALL THREE instruments PASS. Any
    FAIL is final for this artifact; corrections require a new study
    name. Null results are complete, expected deliverables.

8. OUTPUTS (data/results/unseen_asset_kc/)
    SPEC_UNSEEN_KC.txt (this file), GATE_UNSEEN_KC.txt (written before
    the run), REPORT_UNSEEN_KC.txt (funnel, full-tape economics, CAL/OOS
    block degradation, gates), unseen_kc_signals.csv (all instruments,
    asset + block columns), run_meta.json (sha256 lineage of tapes and
    script, funnel, gates, verdict).

9. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
9.1 Mechanics, parameters and walk logic are copied verbatim from #51
    (itself frozen from #50): prior-bar indicator inputs only, completed
    D1 anchors only, worst-case same-bar stop, honest gap fills, exit
    checks before ratchet. No adaptation to the new tapes beyond the
    declared cost-floor estimates.
9.2 Cost floors are owner estimates (1.3): a pass/fail decided inside
    the floor-uncertainty band is reported as such (gross mean and
    cost-free bootstrap are printed alongside) and does NOT license a
    re-run with friendlier floors.
9.3 Continuous forward-fill consequences declared as in #50/#51: on flat
    runs EMA flattens toward the last close, Wilder ATR decays (channels
    narrow), flat closes cannot print new breakouts, D1 direction
    persists; accepted, no bar-skipping fix. Crypto 24/7 printing makes
    these mostly moot on BTCUSD/ETHUSD; they bind on USTECH weekends.
9.4 Single frozen parameterization; no optimization, no re-runs, no
    post-hoc filtering; sides, years, blocks and instruments reported as
    run. The full-tape gates are in-sample for these tapes by
    construction (first and only touch); the CAL/OOS split and
    year-by-year signs are inspected for regime stability but not gated.
    MFE/MAE are exploratory descriptors, never results. Phase 2 (MT5
    execution) only on overall PASS plus standard walk-forward
    discipline (>=150 trades, year-by-year sign agreement).
