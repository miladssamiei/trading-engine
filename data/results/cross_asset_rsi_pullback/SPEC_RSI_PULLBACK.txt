HYPOTHESIS #52 SPEC: TREND-ALIGNED BOLLINGER BAND PULLBACK (4h anchor, 15m limit execution)
Study ID: cross_asset_rsi_pullback
Date frozen: 2026-09-23, before any evaluation code was written or run.
Status: frozen. Corrections or re-measurements require a NEW study name.
Lineage: fifth cross-asset study. #47-#50 were breakout/trailing paradigms;
#51 delivered a bounded PASS on XAUUSD KC breakout. This study tests a
pullback paradigm: in a strong 4h trend (RSI extreme + Supertrend + very
strong ADX), BUY the 15m lower-Band dip (SELL the upper-Band rally) with a
limit order, harvesting to the opposite band under a wide 6xATR stop.
NOTE: the closed "Bollinger MR" family was an unconditional 15m mean
reversion; this is a 4h-trend-conditioned limit-entry variant with a
different exit paradigm - a new pre-declared mechanism, not a re-run.
Parameterization (RSI 70/30, ST(10,3.0), ADX 40/20, BB(20,2.0), 6xATR stop)
is owner-supplied and frozen as given; no parameter search, one run per
instrument.

1. DATA
1.1 Tapes (verified M1, epoch-UTC time, columns time,open,high,low,close,
    tick_volume; sorted ascending defensively before aggregation):
    - US500 : data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv  (points; cost floor 0.51)
    - EURUSD: data/results/eurusd_onboarding/eurusdtick_m1.csv (pips, 1 pip = 0.0001; cost floor 0.3)
    - XAUUSD: data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv (price units; cost floor 0.41)
1.2 Timezone America/New_York (EST/EDT) for all wall-time logic.
1.3 Cost floors are round-trip, subtracted once per trade from gross.

2. MULTI-TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION
2.1 15m bars: M1 epochs floored to 900s slots; open=first, high=max, low=min,
    close=last, volume=sum per slot. NY wall 15m boundaries align exactly
    with the epoch-900 grid (NY UTC offset is always whole hours).
2.2 Continuity: the 15m series is reindexed to the FULL continuous 900s grid
    from first to last slot; missing bars are forward-filled with OHLC = prior
    close, volume = 0 (synthetic flat bars). This fills no-tick minutes,
    maintenance breaks, holidays and weekends alike.
2.3 4h anchor bars: built FROM the continuous 15m grid by NY WALL-clock
    bucket [00:00-04:00), [04:00-08:00), ... DST-transition buckets span 3 or
    5 real hours; buckets are contiguous. The two series are strictly aligned.
2.4 For a 15m bar t, the 4h input is the most recently completed 4h bar
    relative to the open of t (the wall bucket strictly before the bucket
    containing t); its indicators include that anchor bar's own close.

3. STATE MACHINE (causal indicators)
3.1 4h anchor indicators (on the mapped completed bar):
    - RSI(14) Wilder: RMA-smoothed gains/losses seeded with 14-term means;
      RS=ag/al; RSI=100-100/(1+RS); al=0 -> 100 if ag>0 else undefined.
    - Supertrend(10, 3.0): median m=(H+L)/2; Wilder ATR(10); basic bands
      UB=m+3*ATR10, LB=m-3*ATR10; final bands
      fUB(i)=UB(i) if (UB(i)<fUB(i-1) or C(i-1)>fUB(i-1)) else fUB(i-1);
      fLB(i)=LB(i) if (LB(i)>fLB(i-1) or C(i-1)<fLB(i-1)) else fLB(i-1);
      direction seeded +1 at the first bar with valid ATR10, then
      dir(i)=-1 if (dir(i-1)=+1 and C(i)<fLB(i)); dir(i)=+1 if (dir(i-1)=-1
      and C(i)>fUB(i)); otherwise dir(i)=dir(i-1). Valid from the seed bar.
    - ADX(14) Wilder double smoothing: +DM/-DM tie->0; TR/+DM/-DM
      RMA-smoothed; +DI/-DI; DX; ADX(27)=mean(DX 14..27), RMA after.
3.2 15m execution indicators, evaluated strictly on bar t-1:
    - Bollinger Bands: SMA(20) of Close +/- 2.0*population std (ddof=0).
    - ADX(14) as in 3.1.
    - ATR(14) Wilder (stop sizing).
3.3 Armed: bar t is armed iff BB_U/L(t-1), ADX15(t-1), ATR15(t-1) and the
    mapped 4h RSI, Supertrend direction, ADX4 are all non-NaN.

4. SETUP, LIMIT ORDER AND FILL
4.1 At the open of each 15m bar t while flat:
    - Long setup: 4h RSI > 70 AND 4h Supertrend dir = +1 AND 4h ADX > 40 AND
      ADX15(t-1) > 20. Place a Limit Buy at BB_Lower(t-1).
    - Short setup: 4h RSI < 30 AND 4h Supertrend dir = -1 AND 4h ADX > 40 AND
      ADX15(t-1) > 20. Place a Limit Sell at BB_Upper(t-1).
4.2 Fill rules (same-bar limit fill, honest gap accounting):
    - Long fills iff Low(t) <= BB_Lower(t-1); fill price = min(Open(t),
      BB_Lower(t-1)).
    - Short fills iff High(t) >= BB_Upper(t-1); fill price = max(Open(t),
      BB_Upper(t-1)).
    Equality counts as a touch/fill (declared).
4.3 Orders do NOT persist past bar t if unfilled (one-bar limit validity).
    Setups while a trade is open are counted (ignored-while-open) and no
    order is placed. After an exit at bar j, the next order can be placed at
    the open of bar j+1. A trade occupies [entry_bar, exit_bar] inclusive.
4.4 Funnel counting: setups = flat bars with a true setup (order placed);
    fills = executed trades; unfilled = difference.

5. EXCURSION WALK AND BRACKET EXIT
5.1 ATRsig = ATR15(t-1) (strictly prior, at the setup/entry bar).
5.2 Long: SL = Fill - 6.0*ATRsig; TP = BB_Upper(t-1) (the opposite band).
    Short: SL = Fill + 6.0*ATRsig; TP = BB_Lower(t-1).
    Fixed bracket; no trailing.
5.3 The ENTRY bar itself participates in the walk with worst-case ordering
    (a fill and an exit may occur in the same bar): long - if Low(t) <= SL
    exit at SL; elif High(t) >= TP exit at TP; short mirrored.
5.4 Subsequent 15m bars j > t (long case; short mirrored), checks in order:
    a) Open(j) <= SL -> exit at Open(j), reason SL_GAP (honest fill);
    b) Open(j) >= TP -> exit at Open(j), reason TP_GAP (honest fill);
    c) Low(j)  <= SL -> exit at SL, reason SL (worst-case default when both
       levels are touched in the same bar);
    d) High(j) >= TP -> exit at TP, reason TP.
5.5 If no level is hit by the final 15m bar, exit at that bar's close,
    reason FORCED_EOD, included in results.
5.6 Per-trade record includes MFE/MAE excursions over [entry_bar..exit_bar]
    (long MFE = max High - fill, MAE = fill - min Low; short mirrored),
    in instrument units, descriptive only. R multiple =
    side*(Exit-Fill)/(6.0*ATRsig).

6. ECONOMICS
6.1 Gross = side*(Exit - Fill) in instrument units: points for US500 and
    XAUUSD, pips (x10,000) for EURUSD. Net = Gross - cost floor (0.51 / 0.3 /
    0.41 respectively), one round trip per trade.
6.2 Net Sharpe (per-trade convention): mean(Net)/std(Net, ddof=1) * sqrt(252)
    over executed trades of the instrument.

7. PHASE 1 GATES (applied INDEPENDENTLY to each instrument; frozen verbatim in
   GATE_RSI_PULLBACK.txt)
    G-MR1: Total executed signals >= 30.
    G-MR2: Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
    G-MR3: Moving-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples, block=5 trades,
           seed 20260930) P(mean Net PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
    VERDICT RULE: an instrument PASSES only if G-MR1 AND G-MR2 AND G-MR3 all
    PASS. OVERALL verdict PASS only if ALL THREE instruments PASS. Any FAIL is
    final for this artifact; corrections require a new study name. Null
    results are complete, expected deliverables.

8. OUTPUTS (data/results/cross_asset_rsi_pullback/)
    SPEC_RSI_PULLBACK.txt (this file), GATE_RSI_PULLBACK.txt (written before
    the run), REPORT_RSI_PULLBACK_PHASE1.txt (funnel setups vs limit fills,
    net expectancy, win rate, exit mix), rsi_pullback_signals.csv (all
    instruments, asset column), run_meta.json (sha256 lineage of tapes and
    script, funnel, gates, verdict).

9. HONESTY / ANTI-LEAK RULES
9.1 Setups use only the strictly prior state (t-1 15m inputs, completed 4h
    anchor); same-bar limit fills use only that bar's Open/Low/High with the
    declared honest prices; exits have no intrabar lookahead beyond the
    worst-case same-bar default; gap fills at the open.
9.2 Single frozen parameterization; no optimization, no re-runs, no post-hoc
    filtering; sides, years and instruments reported separately and pooled.
9.3 CONSEQUENCE OF 2.2, declared honestly: on long flat runs the BB bands
    collapse toward the last close and equality-touch fills at the collapsed
    band become theoretically possible; in practice the ADX15>20 gate decays
    below 20 within ~20 flat 15m bars and the 4h ADX>40 gate decays across
    the anchor buckets, suppressing degenerate flat-market fills. Wilder
    ATR/ADX decay across gaps also tightens the 6xATR stop for post-gap
    fills. Accepted under the declared continuity convention; no
    bar-skipping or ATR-freezing fix is applied.
9.4 Phase 2 (MT5 execution) only on overall PASS; in-sample character of
    this screen acknowledged; MFE/MAE are exploratory descriptors, never
    results.
