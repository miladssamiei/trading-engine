ACCOUNT REPLAY
Start $10,000 on every distinct 2024+ NY trade date with a full
180-calendar-day follow-up before the tape's last NY date. Include
trades on start date through day before start+180. For fixed planned
risks of 0.1%, 0.5%, 1%, 1.5%, and 2% of initial balance per trade,
divide dollar risk by initial stop distance from the frozen trade
entry/SL. Fractional size and exact risk-dollar conversion are assumed.
At each Prague date, set daily floor to prior-midnight closed balance
minus $500; static floor is $9,000. Check the worst intratrade equity
mark against both floors before booking the frozen quote-net close.
Check closed equity likewise. Breach takes priority over pass. A pass
requires closed balance >=$11,000 after at least four distinct Prague
entry dates. Report starts, pass, breach, timeout, pass-time median,
and compare against the old close-only summary. The result remains an
optimistic upper bound on actual FTMO execution because first-quote
fills, fractional lots, margin, broker feed, rejection and the one
unresolved exit are not resolved. No after-the-fact risk-grid rescue.
