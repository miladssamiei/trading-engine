HYPOTHESIS 59: US500 CASH-CLOSE HEDGING MOMENTUM
Declared 2026-09-25 before strategy code or any Phase-1 run.

1. MECHANISM AND PROVENANCE
The move from the previous cash close to 15:30 New York time may predict
the 15:30-16:00 move because options dealers and leveraged funds rebalance
near the underlying equity-market close. The precise rest-of-day to last-
half-hour relationship and a hedging-demand mechanism are documented by
Baltussen, Da, Lammers, and Martens, Journal of Financial Economics 142
(2021) 377-403, https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf . Their
evidence is from exchange futures through 2020, not this broker's CFD in
2021-2026. This is a predeclared transfer test, not a replication claim.
The prior project intraday multi-horizon test predicted the next 90 minutes
at all M5 bars; it did not test the fixed 15:30-16:00 hedging interval.
No parameters, windows, direction arms, or exit rules will be selected
after seeing outcomes. Only this single unconditional rule is gated.

2. DATA AND CLOCK
Use the verified Bid OHLC M5 tape at
data/results/orb_2021_present/us500tick_m5.csv. Epoch seconds are UTC;
convert to America/New_York for cash-day and wall-time keys, respecting
DST. A cash day requires a printed 09:30 bar. A usable trade day also
requires exact printed 15:25, 15:30, 15:35, 15:40, 15:45, 15:50, and
15:55 M5 bars. The prior cash day's exact 15:55 close must exist; do
not substitute an older available day's close if the immediately prior
cash day lacks its 15:55 bar. Skip missing/early-close days and record
each reason. The latest tape date is 2026-04-30. No server D1 label is
needed. Do not construct M15 from M1.

3. CAUSAL SIGNAL AND EXECUTION
At 15:30 ET, compare the just-completed 15:25 M5 close with the prior
cash day's 15:55 M5 close. Higher means LONG; lower means SHORT; exact
ties mean no trade. Enter one lot at the 15:30 M5 Bid open, exit at the
15:55 M5 Bid close, and force flat before 16:00. No stop, target,
intraday re-entry, position overlap, leverage change, or overnight hold.
Signal values are known before entry. The 15:30 open is a bar proxy for
an immediately sent market order, not a verified executable fill.
Gross directional points = direction * (exit Bid - entry Bid).
Base net points = gross - 0.51 per round trip; double-cost stress net
= gross - 1.02. The 0.51 figure is the project's verified US500 real-
tick median spread, not a fill guarantee. Additional slippage/market
impact is absent from both calculations; no swap applies to same-day
positions. Report gross, both net conventions, wins, and drawdown.

4. PARTITION AND INFERENCE
Calendar 2021-01-01 through 2023-12-31 is diagnostic calibration;
calendar 2024-01-01 through 2026-04-30 is the frozen test block. All
dates use New York cash dates. No filter or parameter is fitted in the
calibration block. This historical test block has been visible in other
project studies and is not virgin data; it is a fixed temporal check of
this previously untested rule. The 2026 year is partial. Compute the
one-sided probability P(mean base net <= 0) by resampling test-block
cash dates with replacement 10,000 times, seed 20260925; there is at
most one trade per date, so each day is a block. Report the empirical
2.5% and 97.5% bootstrap quantiles as descriptive intervals. Report
yearly results for every year, including the partial 2026 year.

5. GATES, FROZEN BEFORE PHASE 1
G-CH1: At least 150 executed trades in the 2024-2026 test block.
G-CH2: Test-block mean net points under double 1.02-point cost is strictly greater than zero.
G-CH3: Test-block bootstrap P(mean base net <= 0) is at most 0.05, using 10,000 date-block resamples and seed 20260925.
G-CH4: Each complete test-block calendar year, 2024 and 2025, has strictly positive total base net points.
Overall Phase-1 PASS requires all four gates. A FAIL is final for this
artifact; report it regardless. No alternative cost threshold, direction
subset, time window, or entry threshold can rescue a failure here.
