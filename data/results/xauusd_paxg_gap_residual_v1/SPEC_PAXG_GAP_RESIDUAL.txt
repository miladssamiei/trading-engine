MECHANISM AND PREVIOUS EVIDENCE
Weekend tokenized gold and reopened spot gold may temporarily differ
because the weekend token market is thinner and spot gold must absorb
the closed-market news at its reopening. Castello (2026),
https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7174537,
finds rapid convergence of the token/spot deviation once gold trades
again, but does not show which market adjusts or a post-reopen CFD
profit. Test a distinct, fixed implication: if PAXG's Friday-to-
Sunday return still exceeds XAUUSD's Friday-close-to-Sunday-entry
gap, XAUUSD may rise over the next five hours; if it lags, XAUUSD
may fall. H74 tested unconditional token direction; this tests a
cross-market return residual after observing the gold reopening.
The historical family has already been examined, so passing requires
stricter evidence and later independent forward confirmation.
