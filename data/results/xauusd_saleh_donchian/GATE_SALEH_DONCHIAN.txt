GATE_SALEH_DONCHIAN.txt - HYPOTHESIS #57 PHASE 1 GATES (frozen 2026-09-24,
BEFORE the Phase 1 script ran; verbatim extraction from
SPEC_SALEH_DONCHIAN.txt section 7)

Population: all executed trades of the single pre-declared machine pass over
data/results/xauusd_onboarding/xauusdtick_m1.csv (full tape), one
configuration: 4H Supertrend(10,3.0) anchor, 1H Donchian(20) breakout entries
at the 1H open, ADX14 >= 20, 4xATR(14) initial stop, close-based 4xATR
trailing stop, fixed spread 0.41 embedded in fills. Net PnL per trade in
price units as defined in SPEC section 5.

G-SAL1: Total executed trades >= 30.

G-SAL2: Net Sharpe Ratio > 0.
        Net Sharpe = mean(trade Net PnL) / std(trade Net PnL, ddof=1),
        event-indexed per trade, not annualized.

G-SAL3: Moving-block bootstrap P(mean Net PnL <= 0) <= 0.20.
        10,000 resamples, block = 5 trades, seed 20261005.
        Algorithm: n = trade count; per resample draw ceil(n/5) block starts
        uniformly from {0..n-5}, concatenate the 5-trade blocks, truncate to
        n, take the mean; P = fraction of the 10,000 resample means <= 0.
        (If n < 5, P is undefined and G-SAL3 is scored FAIL.)

Verdict rule: OVERALL PASS iff G-SAL1 AND G-SAL2 AND G-SAL3 all PASS. Any
FAIL -> OVERALL FAIL, final for this artifact; no re-runs. A PASS is not a
validated result under validation_discipline and does not authorize Phase 2.
