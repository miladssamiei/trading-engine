ZERO-EDGE MAX AGGRESSION US500 HISTORICAL MONTE CARLO - FROZEN SPEC - 2026-10-02

Purpose
Measure random-entry evaluation outcomes on the verified US500 BID M1 proxy tape under the owner's fixed hypothetical account rules. This is a scenario calculation, not a broker fill validation or a firm-rule assertion. A null, failed or censored outcome is a complete deliverable. Freeze all mechanics before writing or executing the experiment script.

Account and primary evaluation loop
Nominal account USD 50,000; start PnL zero; pass at realized PnL >= USD 3,000 and fail at realized PnL <= USD -2,000. Fixed 50 lots at USD 1 per point per lot (USD 50 per point), no compounding. Every individual trade has 40-point stop distance and 30-point target distance from its executable entry; limit target improvement so profit never exceeds USD 1,500. Primary follows the requested accumulate-trade-outcomes loop, testing account thresholds after each closed trade. It omits floating account breaches; report that limit explicitly and run a secondary equity-aware arm described below.

Theoretical comparators
Owner quoted pass rate 32.65% equals (4/7)^2, the probability of two consecutive wins, with TP +1500, SL -2000 and zero-drift continuous price. It does not solve the requested repeat-until-static-boundary rule: after one win and one loss account PnL is -500, still above -2000. Solve the exact absorbing Markov system on USD 500 units with up step +3, down step -4, boundaries +6/-4 and win probability 4/7. Preflight mathematical calculation gave 40.83151672%. Report both the quoted figure and this correctly matched zero-cost theoretical comparator. The requested theoretical trade win probability is 4/7=57.14285714%. With executable-entry brackets and a 0.51 fixed spread, the continuous zero-drift trade win probability is (40-0.51)/(40+30)=56.41428571%; report this as an idealized friction-only comparator. None assumes historical independent price increments actually have zero drift.

Historical source and random sampling
Use data/results/vwap_2021_2026/us500tick_m1.csv exactly, hash it, require strictly increasing minute-aligned positive valid BID OHLC rows. Epoch UTC to America/New_York with DST. Eligible entries are actual M1 opens in [09:30,15:30) NY. Independently draw an eligible minute uniformly with replacement and an independent 50/50 direction for each successive closed trade in each evaluation. Each trade walks historical real M1 forward from that entry until its first bracket touch. Successive sampled timestamps can move backward in history; this is an IID outcome experiment, not a chronological deployable strategy. Do not generate filled M1 bars or resample an entry because its trade remains open at tape end. No time exit, swap, extra commission, margin constraint or live execution delay is modeled. Holds may extend outside RTH and overnight. Sampling means historical calendar months are not evaluation duration forecasts.

Execution and costs
Long executable entry = entry BID open + 0.51; exit on BID. Short executable entry = entry BID open; exit on ASK=historical BID+0.51. Long stop/target are executable entry minus 40/plus 30; short ASK stop/target are executable entry plus 40/minus 30. Thus nominal net TP is USD 1,500 and nominal net SL is USD -2,000; the spread changes trigger distances and outcome probabilities rather than being charged again to those fills. Gross reference PnL uses BID entry/BID exit, and gross-minus-net must equal USD 25.50 per closed real-cost trade. Stop checks precede target checks on every M1 minute, including entry and any collision. Adverse stop gaps fill at the adverse BID/ASK M1 open; favorable target gaps fill at target with no price improvement. Gap stop losses can exceed USD 2,000. Record collisions, adverse gaps and overnight holds.

Arms and censoring
Primary REAL_COST_CLOSED: 10,000 evaluation paths, fixed 0.51 cost, account boundaries at trade closures as requested. Control ZERO_COST_CLOSED: same 10,000 seeded minute/direction draw schedules, same price tape and fixed brackets, zero spread, to isolate friction from historical drift/geometry. Audit REAL_COST_INTRABAR: same 10,000 draw schedules, plus account equity floor/target during the M1 walk. This arm shortens a trade's effective stop to the account's remaining distance to -2000 and target to remaining distance to +3000 if closer than its individual bracket; adverse account floor takes priority in a collision. Each trade still gains no more than 1500. Seed 20261002. Maximum 200 trade attempts per path; any unresolved trade at tape end or nonabsorbed path at cap is CENSORED, not silently redrawn or called failure. Report pass/10,000 and upper bound (pass+censored)/10,000 separately; report resolved-only rate secondarily and 95% Wilson intervals as conditional Monte Carlo precision, not historical uncertainty.

EV economics
Evaluation fee USD 80, not refunded. Owner-specified payout is 0.80 times USD 2,000 = USD 1,600. Requested conditional-payout EV = 1600*evaluation pass probability -80; this explicitly assumes every passed account reaches and receives that withdrawal. Also simulate an independent funded stage for both real-cost arms with a fresh zero PnL balance, funded target +2000, static floor -2000, same 50-lot 30/40 brackets and risk model, new seed 20261003. Generate 10,000 funded paths, join path i to evaluation i only if evaluation passed, and calculate realized first-payout scenario EV = 1600*joint evaluation/funded pass probability -80. No funded consistency, minimum days, activation fee or payout eligibility rule beyond these provided economic assumptions is inferred. Censored payout cases have explicit lower/upper EV bounds.
